# Expedition leave — the page

The expedition's one-pager, in A3 spirit. It answers four things:

- the goal
- what happened
- what settled it
- what is left

It becomes the record's report.md. That is the page a retro adjudicates,
either applying it as design input or dismissing it to the archive.

## Fields

- What was the goal | The goal in your own words — drift from the minted goal shows here. | required
- What was done | What actually happened. One page forces choosing what counts. | required
- What settled it | What was doubted, what was run, what settled it — evidence, not claims. | required
- What was not done | Open threads and deliberate omissions. "Nothing" must be said outright. | required
- Files | Name evidence files in the frontmatter files: list — each must exist in the record's evidence/ folder. | optional
