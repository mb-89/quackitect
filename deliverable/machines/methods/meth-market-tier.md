## Situation

M8 gate-validation's market_tier field. Everyday iterations skip it. An iteration DECLARED TO MARKET runs it before the gate, after the demonstrations.

## Procedure

Three checks, each ending in a named result:

- COLD-READ. A person who did not build it works the entry documents and the must stories' paths unaided.
  - What they stumble on is a finding.
- MEASUREMENT. Every claim carrying a number is measured in the real environment, not the dev one.
  - Cite the numbers.
- PILOT. One real use, end to end, by the audience it is for.
  - The pilot's record is a report, the same shape as a demonstration's.

The field cites the three results by reference. A check that cannot run says why - a named gap beats a blank.
