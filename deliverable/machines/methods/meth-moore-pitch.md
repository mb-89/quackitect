## Situation

Closing move of the vision draft (M1).

One blockquote forces the five hard choices:

- the audience
- the need
- the category
- the benefit
- the displaced alternative

## Procedure #work

- Fill every slot. An empty <unlike> means the alternative was not faced.
- The <who> must match a value-prop audience.
- The <difference> must survive the prior-art scan.
- Read it aloud against the goal system. If the pitch and the top goal
  disagree, one of them is wrong.
