## Situation
AFTER the vision packet is drafted (never before - it tests, not generates). M1's pressure-test state.

## Procedure #work
- Write the one-page press release from the to-be world's ship day: headline, what shipped, who benefits how, a customer quote.
- Write the FAQ a skeptic would ask: why now, why us, what breaks, what it costs, what was given up.
- Every question the FAQ cannot answer cleanly is a finding: fold it back into the vision or the risk log before the gate.

## Sources
Amazon working-backwards practice; v1 method sheet pr-faq.

## THE HOSTILE HALF IS WRITTEN BY A DIFFERENT HAND

The hand that wrote the pitch cannot write the attack on it. It knows every
reason the pitch is right, and that is exactly the knowledge an attacker must
not have.

SO SPAWN A SEPARATE ADVERSARIAL HAND FOR THE HOSTILE QUESTIONS. Brief it to
find every reason the thing does not work, and to say which of those are real.
"Review this" invites a summary. The second wording invites a finding.

RECORD WHICH HAND WROTE IT, so a reader can tell an attack from a rehearsal.

MEASURED: one pressure test run this way produced 17 attacks and withdrew two
claims the round had been carrying. A claim withdrawn by its own author is
rare enough to be worth the separation on its own.
