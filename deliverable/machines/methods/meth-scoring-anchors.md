## Anchors (absolute, 0-5)

- 0 - absent. The dimension is not addressed at all.
- 1 - gesture. Addressed in prose only; nothing checkable.
- 2 - partial. Works for the demo path; known holes remain unrecorded.
- 3 - solid baseline. Meets the stated requirement; holes are recorded with homes.
- 4 - prior-art par. Matches what the best comparable external tool does.
- 5 - beyond prior art. Demonstrably better than the named external comparison; the evidence names it.

Rules:

- Scores 4 and 5 REQUIRE a named external comparison. No name, no score above 3.
- The score never moves for effort or intent. Only for what the evidence shows.

## The research-agent recipe

The builder never scores its own work. At a scoring step:

- Spawn a fresh research agent. It gets the criterion, the anchors above, and READ access.
- The brief names 2-3 prior-art references to check against (or asks the agent to find them and cite them).
- The agent returns: score, the anchor line it matched, the prior-art citation, and the falsifying question it asked.
- The builder records the score unchanged. Disagreement is a note for the gate, never a silent edit.
