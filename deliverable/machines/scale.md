# The autonomy scale

FIVE RUNGS, plus blocked. Each rung names a KIND of work, and the rung above
it is the one that decides that work needs doing: ideation commissions
strategic work, strategy commissions tactical, tactics commission
operational, operations commission mechanical.

Set the control to a rung and the agent may do work at that rung and at
every rung below it. The rungs are cumulative on purpose — you cannot hand
over a strategic decision while withholding the mechanical work it implies.

NO STATE IS EVER AUTHORED AS BLOCKED-FOR-WORK, and that is what makes a full
block possible. The gate refuses when a state weighs MORE than the dial, so
mechanical is the least a step can weigh and the blocked setting admits
nothing at all.

BLOCKED IS A CONTROL POSITION, NOT A WEIGHT A STATE MAY CARRY. The other
rungs are both: positions to click, and words to calibrate a state's
`priority:` against. Blocked is only a position. Authoring a state as blocked
would let it run at the blocked SETTING, which is the one thing that setting
promises cannot happen.

THE LADDER IS THE OWNER'S CUT-OVER RULING (2026-08-12): none, then
mechanical, operational, tactical, strategic, ideation — emergency rides
with ideation.

THERE ARE NO NUMBERS HERE, AND THE ORDER OF THE LINES IS THE SCALE (owner
ruling 2026-08-18). The rungs used to carry an anchor each, and a reader met
them in the log, in the details and in every refusal without ever being told
what the range was. The engine still compares ranks, because a gate is a
comparison and always was — it works them out from the ORDER of the lines
below instead of reading them off the page.

SO MOVING A LINE MOVES THE RUNG. Adding one between two others puts it
between them. That is the whole editing rule, and it is the one a person can
hold without knowing an implementation.

BLOCKED IS ALSO A PRIORITY, and it is the same word for the same thing. A
drawing authored `priority: blocked` can never be entered by the agent, at
any setting. The person may always enter it. The archives are drawn that
way.

THE TWO SIDES SIT AT OPPOSITE ENDS, and that is not a contradiction. On the
control, blocked is the position where no rung is pressed. On a state, it is
above every rung. The gate refuses when a state weighs MORE than the dial, so
"nothing gets through" is the bottom of one range and the top of the other.

SO THE ENGINE MATCHES THE RESOLVED RUNG, NEVER THE SPELLING. The list below
resolves a name, its abbreviation and any case alike, so `blocked`,
`Blocked`, `BLOCKED` and `B` all mean the same rung.

IDEATION IS THE DANGEROUS ONE, and it is marked. Every other rung delegates
work that already exists; ideation delegates the CREATION of work, so it has
no natural stopping point and it spends budget until something stops it. The
owner's standing experience is that taking the person out of the loop
degrades the result.

## Levels

- B | blocked — nothing moves without the human
- M | mechanical — what mainly the engine does, carried out as decided
- O | operational — single steps and submitting
- T | tactical — running iterations and expeditions, seeding and delegating sub-work
- S | strategic — seeding iterations, retros, overhauls and planning; delegating downward; draining the backlog
- I | ideation — filling the backlog and producing the new; everything but the axiomatic vision
