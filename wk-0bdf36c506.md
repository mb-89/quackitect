## detail

From the verdict on wk-887e9a126a, the engine archives.

src/engine/find.go builds FindParams{Words, Regex, Limit} for the archive branch and drops Path on the floor. The flag is still declared, still documented as "a glob over paths", and still accepted.

Measured on this tree. se find --archive --regex 'undo pops' --path 'nothing/at/all/*' answers the same one hit as the same search with no --path at all.

The damage is that a narrowed search answers unnarrowed and reads as narrowed. A reader who asks for one folder and is handed the whole archive believes the hits are from that folder, which is worse than being told the flag does not apply.

An archived token is a tag rather than a path, so a glob over paths may genuinely have nothing to say here. Then say it: refuse --path with --archive rather than take it and ignore it.
