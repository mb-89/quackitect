Method and Project

Three drawings of one split: what v1 did with two folders, what v5 holds in one tree today, and the shape where a vehicle is a project that works on itself.

method: rules, guidance, verbs, doorsproject: what the work is about

1. v1: three shapes, one engine

v1 kept the method under product/quackitect/method and the project under spec/. A vehicle vendored the engine and worked on its own tool. A stub was a bare folder with a launcher that found the engine at runtime.

2. v5 today: one tree, both halves mixed

v5 holds the split as two roots, method and work, and lets a work root inherit rules file by file. But this tree is the only method root, and its spec/ folder holds method and project side by side.

3. The shape: a vehicle is a project working on itself

Two folders with fixed jobs. method/ holds what applies to everything. project/ holds what the work is about. A vehicle carries both and works on its own method. A stub carries the project folder and a bridgehead, and takes the method from the vehicle it starts with.

The sort of today's spec/ into the two folders, by the question each item answers: does it hold over any project, or does it say what this one is about?
