## Why it crosses out

A PERSON IS THE CONSUMER AND A PERSON IS OUTSIDE THE SYSTEM. The transformation
does not claim its own result is right, so what it produces is handed to
somebody to read rather than to another function to act on.

NOTHING INSIDE CONSUMES IT, AND THAT IS THE DESIGN RATHER THAN A HOLE. A
consuming function would be the system judging its own transformation, which is
exactly what this mechanism declines to do.

## And that is the winner's sharpest weakness, stated where it lands

A MIGRATION THAT RUNS, SUCCEEDS AND PRODUCES SOMETHING WRONG HAS NO OTHER
SIGNAL. The change is applied, the diff is unstaged, and a person who does not
read it learns nothing.

THAT SITS DIRECTLY AGAINST [[req-a-wrong-act-never-passes-silently]], which this
iteration ranked first of ten. The winner scores 4 on that criterion for its
write jail and its refusals; this flow is the one place it does not refuse.

SO THE FLOW IS NAMED PARTLY TO KEEP THE WEAKNESS VISIBLE. A gap carried in prose
disappears into a paragraph. A gap carried as a flow with a crossing and no
internal consumer is on the page every time the graph is drawn.
