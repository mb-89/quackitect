## What it carries

Every piece of work the session started, in one answer. A finished piece stays
in it with its outcome, so a reader that missed the moment still learns what
happened.

Each entry carries a duration or a plain statement that none can be computed,
and where there is a duration it carries what that duration rests on.

## Why the basis travels with the figure

The measurements behind any duration here are recorded as unreliable, in
`raid-asm-battery-timings-measure-work`.

A reader who can see the basis can discount it. A reader who cannot, cannot,
and will wait on a number nobody computed.

## What it is not

It is not the work itself. `flow-work-under-way` is what the session holds;
this is what a reader is told about it.

It is not a rendering. Nothing here says whether it reaches a screen, and the
screen is outside this iteration's boundary.
