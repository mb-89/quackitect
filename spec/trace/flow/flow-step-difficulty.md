IT IS KEYED TO A COLUMN, not to a row. A probe at i38's probe-assumptions found `draft-vision` spanning three rungs across its change sizes and not monotonically — accepting a standing artifact at major, framing one from nothing at product. A flow carrying one value per row would have been carrying the wrong number for three columns in four.

DECLARED OR DERIVED IS NO LONGER THIS FLOW'S ANSWER, corrected 2026-08-20.
The statement used to end "as the step itself declares it", and this paragraph
called that the whole trade the iteration makes.

WHY IT MOVED. `req-every-matrix-row-declares-its-complexity` was restated at
gate-architecture and stopped naming the declaration. A flow that keeps the
clause re-freezes in the function layer exactly what the requirement layer just
released, and M4 has four candidates that answer it differently.

THE TRADE IS STILL REAL AND IT IS M4'S. A difficulty computed at run time would
fit the work better and could not be replayed. That argument belongs on the
candidates, where it is already made.

IT NEVER ENTERS A RECORD'S DEMANDS. It is a hint about who should drive, not a claim anything rests on, so nothing needs it frozen.
