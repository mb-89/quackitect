## Why it is a flow at all

Two answers have always existed and were never named as something that
crosses. A third answer is what forces the naming.

Three readers consume it: a gate asking whether its feeders are green, the
route drawer asking which hops already pass, and whatever paints a step's
standing for a person.

## The third value is the whole point

Still-being-decided is not passed and not failed. A reader that flattens it
into either one gets a wrong answer rather than a coarse one.

Flattened toward passed, a gate opens on evidence that does not exist.
Flattened toward failed, a walk is refused for doing nothing wrong.

## What it does not say

It does not say what any reader should DO with the third value. That is the
design's, and this flow only demands the value reach them distinctly.
