## Concerns

- It is the only role that can spawn another, because the engine cannot start
  an agent and the harness will not do it unasked. A duty nobody else can
  discharge sits here.
- Nothing stops it walking the record itself. The roster says the walker is the
  only role that calls the pull, and no check enforces that.
- Its own share of the work is invisible while it is happening. Only the retro
  can weigh how much the guide did against how much it delegated.
- It carries context the walker must not inherit, which is the whole reason the
  two are separate hands rather than one agent wearing two names.

## Notes (not load-bearing)

WHY THIS NODE WAS LATE. The roster was ruled at i38 and shipped on 2026-08-21,
naming four agent roles. Only the walker had a node, and that iteration minted
the reviewer. The guide and the researcher were left without one, and i38
logged that absence as a standing issue rather than closing it.

THE MEASUREMENT THAT MAKES IT REAL is the retro's, not a gate's. Every call
records the part its caller played, so the share the guide took is countable
after the fact. A guide doing too much is a signal to fix the system rather
than a refusal in the moment.
