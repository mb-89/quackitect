## CLOSED AS WORK, NOT AS A FINDING — 2026-08-26

AN ISSUE IS SOMETHING EXTERNAL: people we do not have, tooling we cannot get,
infrastructure that is missing. This is deferred work with a clear shape.

NOTHING BELOW IS WITHDRAWN. It becomes a work token when work tokens exist, and
it is ready when the migration is.

## What is missing

THE ROW ASKS FOR TWO THINGS and the structure delivers neither.

- A window, where the pool holds more than the answer's bound admits.
- A statement on the answer that it IS a window, with how many stand.

## Why it is unaddressed rather than at risk

THERE IS NO HINGE TO NAME. A risk is a design choice with a cost; this is a
demand nothing in the structure answers at all.

## Why it is not this iteration's scope

THE ROW SAYS SO ITSELF. It is graded `should` because the pool starts empty on
every clone and nothing breaks while it is small. It becomes a hard demand the
day the migration runs, and the migration is a declared non-goal of this record.

## What it is waiting for

THE MIGRATION, or the first pool large enough to exceed an answer's bound.
Either one turns this from a standing finding into work.
