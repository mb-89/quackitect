## The shape of it

THE OWNER RULED BOTH ARRANGEMENTS LEGAL AND ONE OF THEM IS UNOBSERVABLE. Either
hand may work the lane. Where the guide works it directly, a call exists and can
carry a part. Where the walker relays, there is nothing for the lane to stamp,
because the lane never saw the guide.

SO THE STAMP CANNOT COME FROM THE SERVER IN THE CASE THAT MATTERS MOST. It has
to come from the caller, declaring whose work it is filing. That is the same
position `req-every-call-records-the-model-that-answered-it` is in and it takes
the same mark: self-reported until it arrives from whatever performed the spawn.

## Why expected rather than plausible

THE RELAY IS THE CHEAP PATH AND IT WILL BE THE COMMON ONE. Handing a guide the
lane means giving it the cage rules, the verbs and the reading loop; handing it a
question and typing the answer costs one turn. Nothing makes the cheaper path
declare itself, and the walker is the party being measured.

AND THE SAME SHAPE HAS ALREADY BEEN OBSERVED IN THIS RECORD. Every judgment a
subagent produced this iteration re-entered the lane through the walker, and the
log carries `agent` for all of it.

## What would retire it

A DECLARED ORIGIN ON THE FILING, checked against nothing and marked as claimed —
which is honest — or the value arriving from whatever performed the spawn, which
knows what it started and is not the party being measured. The second is what
takes the mark off, and it is outside this iteration's box.
