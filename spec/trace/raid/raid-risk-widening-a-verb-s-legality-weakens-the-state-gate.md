## What forces the widening

RECORDING THAT A HELPER WAS LAUNCHED IS BLOCKED BY WHERE THE WALK STANDS. The
call that registers a spawned hand rides one verb, and that verb is not legal
in every state.

SO THE REGISTRATION IS REFUSED EXACTLY WHERE A HAND WAS JUST SPAWNED, which is
the only place it is ever needed.

A HAND THAT WAS STARTED IS A FACT ABOUT THE WORLD. Refusing to record a fact
does not make it untrue; it makes the account wrong.

## The story, with no coincidence in it

The verb becomes legal everywhere for the registration argument. A later change
adds an argument to the same verb without asking where that argument should be
legal, and it inherits the widening.

## What the goal conflict ruled

RULED FOR THE NARROWEST POSSIBLE WIDENING. Only the registration argument
becomes legal everywhere. Nothing else the verb carries moves with it.

## What would make this the wrong call

Finding that the narrow widening cannot be expressed — that legality is per
verb and not per argument. Then the honest answer is a separate verb for
registration, not a wider one for everything.
