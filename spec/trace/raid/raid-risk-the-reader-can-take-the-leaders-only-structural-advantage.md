## Closed at reverse-sensitivity, 2026-08-20 — the rival is not a candidate

THE RIVAL THIS ENTRY IS ABOUT CANNOT TAKE ANYTHING.
`cand-the-reader-beside-the-walk` violates two musts, on sentences it wrote
about itself, and a must gates: a line satisfies it or is not a candidate.

- `req-the-machine-names-a-driver-and-starts-nothing` demands the named driver
  be published ON THE PULL. Its picked
  `opt-the-record-is-the-channel-and-there-is-no-separate-publication` says
  "anything wanting the driver before the call is made cannot have it, because
  the record does not exist until the call does."
- `req-every-matrix-row-declares-its-complexity` demands a complexity for every
  applying cell when the matrix is loaded, refusing loudly where it cannot. That
  line obtains nothing at load and rates an unwalked row easy rather than
  refusing.

BOTH PERTURBATIONS IN THE IMPACT FIELD ARE THEREFORE VOID. The graft cannot
happen, and the weighting by damage grades now reads receiver 36 against reader
33 rather than the reverse, because two axes were re-scored after this entry was
written.

WHAT SURVIVES AS A QUESTION RATHER THAN A RISK. If
`req-the-machine-names-a-driver-and-starts-nothing` were read as its no-spawn
half alone, that line is the STRONGEST of the four on it and returns to the
front. The reading is recorded and rejected at reverse-sensitivity, and it is
where the whole decision actually rests — on one clause of one requirement rather
than on any cell of the chart.

SO THIS ENTRY IS CLOSED AND ITS SUCCESSOR IS NOT A REGISTER ITEM. It is the
elimination itself, argued in reverse-sensitivity's own sensitivity field.

## Why the graft is takeable, which is the whole question

THE LEADER'S HOST-SWAP ADVANTAGE IS THAT NOTHING IN OUR TREE NAMES A MODEL. That
is a property of what is published, not of where the publisher stands.

cand-the-reader-beside-the-walk stands outside the walk and reads finished
records. A reader that publishes a rung rather than a model is coherent and stays
itself: what makes the leader the leader is publishing FORWARD from INSIDE the
walk, and the reader does neither before or after the graft.

SO THIS IS NOT THE INCOMPATIBLE CASE. The two eliminated candidates cannot take
it — holding the roster is what they are — and the reader can.

## The fallback

DECLARE THE WINNER WITH THE GRAFT ALREADY TESTED, which is what
graft-onto-the-winner exists for and which runs next. If the graft holds, the
declared winner should be cand-the-reader-beside-the-walk and the margin is no
longer one point on one axis.

IF THE GRAFT IS REFUSED — and there is a real reason it might be, since a reader
that names a rung has to publish a vocabulary it cannot enforce — then the
declaration stands at the leader, and this entry is the record that the lead was
one point wide and that a weighting alone reverses it.

WHAT WOULD RETIRE THIS ENTRY: a cost axis. Both candidates on the front spend
nothing on the walk, and neither was chosen for it. An axis that measured spend
would separate them on the thing the record exists for, rather than on one point
of host portability.
