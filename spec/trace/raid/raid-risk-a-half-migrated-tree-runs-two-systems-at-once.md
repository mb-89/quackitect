## The hinge

THE SURFACE ASSUMES TOTALITY. A bubble showing three means three things are
owed here. If some of what is owed still lives in an unconverted form, the
bubble is quietly wrong and looks exactly like a right one.

THE MIGRATION IS WIDE. Every state of every machine, every evidence field in
every form, and every method card with steps in it.

## Why it is graded plausible

ONE ORDINARY EVENT PRODUCES IT: the round runs out of time with the engine
converted and some method cards not, or the other way round. That is the usual
shape of a wide migration and it needs no coincidence.

## What closes it

TWO ANSWERS AND ONLY ONE IS CHEAP.

A CHECK THAT REFUSES A MIXED TREE. Every state either hands out tokens or it
does not, and a tree where both are true goes red. That makes the partial
state visible instead of plausible-looking.

OR A COUNT THAT SAYS WHAT IT DOES NOT COVER, which is weaker, because a
footnote on a number is read less often than the number.
