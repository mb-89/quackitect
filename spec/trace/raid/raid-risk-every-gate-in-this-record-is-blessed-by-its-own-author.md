## Why it is recorded rather than argued about

THE OWNER SET IT UP THIS WAY, in their own words this session: raise the
autonomy, place the gates yourself, and let only blockers stop the walk. The
dial stands at strategic and the stop-at at blockers only.

So this is not a rule being broken. It is a known property of an unattended
run, and it is written down because the record's readers cannot see the dial.

## What it actually costs

A GATE IS A COMPARISON AGAINST AN OUTSIDE VIEW. Blessed by its author, it
still runs every mechanical check the engine holds — the field checks, the
conditions, the ripple — and it loses only the part a second person brings.

WHAT THE MECHANISM STILL CATCHES: an unanswered field, a broken reference, a
claim whose feeder moved.

WHAT IT CANNOT CATCH: a scope that is wrong, a size that is too small, a
red-team round that argued with a weak version of the objection.

## What reduces it, and what does not

- REDUCES IT: writing the steelman before the attack, and naming a
  kill-criterion on every gate. Both are in the form and both are cheap.
- REDUCES IT: a fresh-eyes subagent at verification, which that state's own
  guidance demands.
- DOES NOT REDUCE IT: more careful self-review. The failure mode is not
  carelessness, it is a missing second view.

## What would close it

The owner reading the gate briefs, or a later record's red team opening this
one. Until then it stands open, and every gate in this record inherits it.
