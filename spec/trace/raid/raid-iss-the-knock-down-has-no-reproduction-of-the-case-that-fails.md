## What is established

THE KNOCK-DOWN FIRED TODAY, and the engine named the whole chain. `author-tests`
was re-signed, and the next pull refused to route with this:

> specify-build's own content still passes. It is down because author-tests was
> RE-SIGNED after it, so it answered older ground.

It named the root, the path from it, and the exact call that fixes it. That is
the mechanism working, end to end, with a reader who could act on it.

## What is not

WHICH CASE FAILS. The work token says one case worked and one did not on the same
day, and that the guard therefore fires only sometimes. It records the symptom
and not the shape.

THE TOKEN'S OWN PHRASE POINTS AT DIRECTION: "whichever way the walk happens to be
pointed". That suggests the guard's answer depends on the standing target, which
would make it a routing question rather than a claims question. It is a lead and
not a finding.

## Why nothing was built for it

CHOOSING A FIX AGAINST AN UNKNOWN CAUSE IS THE MISTAKE THIS ROUND ALREADY MADE
ONCE. A plausible cost was found and code was changed before anything measured
whether it was the cost, and the change was reverted once measured.

THE SAME REASONING APPLIES HERE with less evidence, not more. One case is known
to work and the failing one has no reproduction.

## What would close it

A REPRODUCTION FIRST. Re-sign a feeder with the walk pointed away from the
dependant, and again with it pointed at the dependant, and see whether the two
answers differ. That is two calls, and it settles the direction the token
suspects.

THEN THE FIX, aimed at what the reproduction shows.

## What must not happen

THE CHUNK MUST NOT BE MARKED BUILT. It is chunked in this round's drawing, and
the honest standing is that its cause is unknown rather than that its work is
done.
