## Which three

- `the-drawer-tells-deciding-from-failed`
- `a-record-that-holds-still-to-measure-against`
- `a-score-cell-can-say-it-has-no-evidence`

The fourth added chunk, the knock-down, was not built at all and carries
[[raid-iss-the-knock-down-has-no-reproduction-of-the-case-that-fails]].

## How it happened

THE FOUR WERE CHUNKED LATE. The first build drawing listed exactly the four
things already built, so the four owed items were invisible from inside it. A
cold reviewer found them by reading the phase brief instead.

BY THEN THE WALK STOOD PAST THE STATE THAT WATCHES REDS. Building them was the
owner's ruling — nothing leaves scope — and the order could not be undone.

## They are CHECKED and UNREDDENED, which is not the same as untested

EACH OF THE THREE CARRIES A GUARD. What none of them carries is proof that its
guard can fail, because none was watched failing.

SAYING "UNTESTED" WOULD TELL A LATER READER THERE IS NOTHING THERE, and there is.
A reviewer corrected this entry on exactly that word, and the correction matters:
the two standings call for different work.

WHAT IS LOST is the one thing observing the red buys — evidence the check is
asserting something that was not already true. That is a real loss and it is why
this entry stands rather than the boxes being ticked.

## What would close it

EACH CHECK SHOWN FAILING ONCE: break what it guards on purpose, watch the red,
revert, and record it.

THAT IS THE STANDARD WAY TO PROVE A GUARD CAN FAIL, and an earlier version of
this entry called it theatre while prescribing it two lines later. It is not
theatre. It is the only thing that separates a guard from a sentence.

## The cause worth carrying past this round

A DRAWING WRITTEN AFTER THE WORK CANNOT SHOW WHAT WAS DROPPED. That is recorded
against the drawing itself, and it is what put these three on the wrong side of
the red.
