## Why it is an assumption rather than a design question

THE MECHANISM IS NOT THE PROBLEM. Resolving a collision is ordinary and every
vendoring arrangement answers it the same way: a person decides, once, in the
usual manner. git-subtree's manual says exactly that.

WHAT IS ASSUMED IS THE PERSON. Specifically, that the cost of resolving is
small enough, often enough, that they keep paying it. Nothing about the
mechanism guarantees that, and nobody here has watched anyone do it.

AND THE EVIDENCE WE HAVE POINTS THE OTHER WAY, mildly. The reason this product
does not already vendor anything is the same reason: the earlier attempt used a
dependency link because copying and reconciling looked like work. People route
around reconciliation.

## Why it is people rather than environment

THE SWEEP ASKS SIX SOURCES and this is the one that answers the PEOPLE
question. It is about skill, patience and setup rather than about what the
software does.

IT IS ALSO THE ONLY ENTRY IN THIS REGISTER THAT CANNOT BE SETTLED BY READING
CODE, which is worth saying plainly after a session in which most of them
could.

## Probe

THERE IS NO CHEAP MECHANICAL ANSWER, and pretending otherwise is how an
assumption becomes decoration. Three probes are possible and they differ in
cost and honesty.

- ASK ONE. The audience is real - stk-vehicle-owner's node records the owner
  correcting an agent who assumed otherwise. One conversation with somebody who
  holds a vendored dependency today, about what they do when an update
  conflicts, answers more than any measurement here.
- COUNT THE COST. Once a mechanism is chosen, measure how many collisions a
  realistic month of upstream change produces against a realistic month of
  local change. A number under a handful is a different product from a number
  over a hundred.
- WATCH THE FIRST ONE. The first real copy is the first evidence. It is the
  slowest probe and the only conclusive one.

WHAT ANSWERS IT: a copy's owner taking a second update after a collision. That
is the whole test, and nothing before it counts.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT EARLY: a chosen mechanism whose collision cost is paid in
files rather than in lines. Nobody reconciles a hundred files by hand twice.
