## What was measured

EVERY WORK ITEM IN THE TREE CARRIES AN EMPTY DIFFICULTY. Counted across both
homes, 18 of 18 at the time of the gate review, and every one of them was
withheld for the same reason.

THE OFFER IS NOT AT FAULT. `offer` withholds an unrated item on purpose, and it
reports the withholding rather than dropping it in silence. The design says so
in the same breath: difficulty is read, never decided there.

THE SOURCE IS EMPTY. Difficulty is published by the sizing, which reads the
rigor matrix. No row in that matrix carries a complexity for any column, so the
sizing publishes nothing and every mint stamps an empty field.

THE GUARD THAT WOULD HAVE CAUGHT IT IS OFF. The matrix refuses to load with an
unrated active cell only when its own page carries the line that turns that
refusal on. That line is absent, so the corpus loads clean with nothing rated.

## Why this is an issue rather than a risk

IT HAS ALREADY HAPPENED, in the present tense, on every look. A risk is
something that might; this is the state of the system today.

## What it is not

IT IS NOT A REASON TO INVENT RATINGS. Grading how hard a step is belongs to
whoever owns the matrix. A ratings pass authored to make a mechanism light up
would make the offer confident and wrong, which is worse than an offer that
plainly hands back nothing.

## What closes it

THE MATRIX CELLS CARRY A COMPLEXITY, authored by the hand that owns them.

THEN THE LOAD-TIME REFUSAL IS TURNED ON, so the next unrated cell is caught at
the door rather than a milestone later.

AND ONE MEASUREMENT SETTLES IT: a position asked for work returns at least one
offered item to a hand at the matching rung.
