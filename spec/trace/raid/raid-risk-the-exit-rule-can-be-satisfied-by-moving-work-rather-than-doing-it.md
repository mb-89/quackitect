## The two destinations

THE PENDING BUCKET DOES NOT BLOCK, by definition. It was specified for the
backlog, and the owner said outright that it makes little sense on a state. But
nothing written says an work token a state owed may not be put there.

A USER'S OWN BUCKET IS BORN HOLDING WHAT WAS DROPPED ON IT. Whether it blocks
the state it sits beside has never been stated either way.

## Why this is graded fatal rather than corrosive

STRIKE THE RULE AND DESCRIBE THE PRODUCT TRUTHFULLY. It would be a filing
system for work tokens, not a machine that holds a walk to them. The sentence
changes, so the grade is fatal by the scale's own test.

## Why it is graded plausible rather than expected

IT NEEDS ONE ORDINARY EVENT AND NO COINCIDENCE: a hand facing a token it does
not understand, and a destination on screen that accepts it. That story has no
"and also" in it.

WHAT KEEPS IT OFF `expected` is that neither destination is documented as legal
for a state's own work tokens. The gap is silence rather than permission.

## OWNER RULING — who judges the move

THE ATTACK IS ACCEPTED AS TRUE. In principle a token can always be moved. The
question the design has to answer is not whether a move is possible; it is WHO
JUDGES IT.

THE ANSWER IS THE GATE, in three steps.

- DEFERRING A TOKEN RECORDS AN OPEN POINT. The token goes to the state that
  will do it later, and the move is written down rather than silent.
- OPEN POINTS ARE VISIBLE TO THE GATE. They arrive at its review as a list,
  beside everything else it weighs.

THE WORD IS OPEN POINT AND THE OWNER RULED IT. A gate review discusses open
points and then decides whether to close with them standing. That is what the
phrase means in a review, and it is the right word for it.

THE OLD MEANING IS NOT A CLASH BECAUSE IT IS BEING DELETED. Today an open point
is an unresolved node in the decision graph, and this round removes the
narration system that produces them. The name is free.
- THE GATE DECIDES WHETHER TO ACCEPT EACH MOVE. Accepted, the walk carries on.
  Not accepted, the gate does not pass.

SO A MOVE IS A DEFERRAL SOMEBODY SIGNS FOR, not an escape. The exit rule stays
exactly as written, and the judgment sits at the gate, which is where every
other judgment about a phase already sits.

WHERE DEFERRED ITEMS ARE STORED IS NOT SETTLED, and does not need to be at this
stage. That is a design question rather than a motivation question.

THE ENTRY IS MITIGATED. Both halves of the ruling now stand, and the naming
collision is fixed. What follows records how it got there.

- THE SECOND HALF IS NOW GIVEN. A user's own bucket dropped on a state does
  not sit beside it. Its tokens move INTO the state and become that state's
  own, so they block exactly as any other token there does. The bucket itself
  is not kept; it is emptied by the drop and gone.

  THAT IS THE WANTED BEHAVIOUR RATHER THAN A CONCESSION. It is how a person
  adds work to a state. There is no lingering container with unclear blocking
  rules, because there is no lingering container.
- THE TERM WAS QUERIED AND THE OWNER KEPT IT. A reviewer flagged that open
  point already names a decision-graph node. The owner ruled that a gate review
  discusses open points, that the word is right for it, and that losing the old
  meaning is fine because this round deletes the system producing it.

AND THE MITIGATION IS CIRCULAR WHILE ONE HAND HOLDS THE GATE. Routing every
move to the gate makes the gate the only guard, and a sibling entry grades this
record's gates as authored and judged by the same hand, at expected. Naming the
second hand that accepts or refuses a move is part of closing this.

## What closes it

A RULING WITH TWO PARTS, and the owner has asked to discuss it.

- Whether an work token a state owed may leave that state's input or output
  bucket at all, other than by being settled.
- Whether a user's own bucket blocks the state it is shown on.

IF MOVEMENT STAYS LEGAL, then what records the move, and who reads that record,
have to be named in the same breath.

## Where it came from

The red team at the motivation gate named this as its kill criterion, and found
it met on the artifacts as they stood. It is met by an undecided question rather
than by a wrong decision, so a ruling clears it without a redesign.
