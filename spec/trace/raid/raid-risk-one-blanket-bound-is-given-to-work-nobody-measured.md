## What was actually built

EVERY ENTRY CARRIES A BOUND, and every one of them carries the same default.
Nothing in the tree passes a measured figure yet.

THE ROW DEMANDED THE MECHANISM, NOT THE FIGURE. Its own words: choosing the
bound well is a measurement, set where the work is understood.

## Why this is a risk rather than a defect

THE WORST OUTCOME IS BOUNDED. An expiry no longer closes an entry for good: a
real ending replaces it, and the entry is put back in front of a reader. A
handle that says the process is there is never expired at all.

SO WHAT IS LEFT IS A WRONG ANSWER IN A WINDOW. Between the bound passing and
the work reporting, a reader is told the wait expired.

## Where it will bite first

A REGISTERED HAND, because it has neither handle nor number. A subagent working
past thirty minutes is exactly the case the sweep cannot ask about, and long
hands are ordinary rather than rare.

## What closes it

A BOUND CHOSEN PER KIND OF WORK, from what that kind has actually taken. The
provenance field already distinguishes the two, so the register can tell when
this stops being true without anyone remembering to look.
