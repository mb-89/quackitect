## Probe

WALK ONE STATE UNDER WORK TOKENS AND COUNT THE GAPS. For every piece of work
that reached a terminal status, ask whether an in-work mark was ever recorded
against it.

THE FAILING SHAPE IS A JUMP. Minted, then finished, with nothing between.

COMPARE AGAINST THE CALL LOG, which records the work anyway, so the two can
disagree and the disagreement is the answer.

IT NEEDS THE THING BUILT, so this is a before-and-after probe rather than a
check that can run today.

## Why the old machinery is not evidence for the new

THE NARRATION SYSTEM WAS OBEYED BECAUSE IT REFUSED. Five clauses policed it,
and the toll bit when the log went quiet. Measured over one window: 199 of
1,233 calls were narration, which is what a policed work token looks like.

NOTHING POLICES THE MARK. A hand that acts without marking is not refused, and
the account it produces is thinner rather than red.

SO THE COMPARISON IS NOT LIKE FOR LIKE. The old rate was produced under
compulsion and the new one will not be.

## Why it is graded plausible

ONE ORDINARY EVENT PRODUCES IT: a cheap hand told to say what it is starting,
which is exactly the shape the smaller-model journey is written against.

## What falsifies it

A WALK WHERE EVERY SETTLED WORK TOKEN CARRIES AN IN-WORK MARK. One run
settles it in the good direction; one gap settles it in the bad.

## What is not assumed here

NOTHING SAYS THE MARK MUST BE POLICED. Making the mark automatic on the act
that takes the work is one honest answer, and it would dissolve this entry
rather than satisfy it. That is the design's to choose.
