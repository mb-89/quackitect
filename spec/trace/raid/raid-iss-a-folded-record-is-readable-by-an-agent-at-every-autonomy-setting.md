## CLOSED AS WORK, NOT AS A FINDING — 2026-08-26

TWO SHAPES OF FIX ARE NAMED BELOW and both are ours to build. Work with a known
fix is not an external issue.

NOTHING BELOW IS WITHDRAWN. It becomes a work token when work tokens exist.

## What is open

THE RULE IS THAT AN AGENT BROWSING THE ARCHIVE IS REFUSED, at every autonomy
setting, with the person-only rule named.

THE WINNER PUTS THE ARCHIVE IN GIT. Reading history is an ordinary lane act, and
the git allowlist carries `show`, so the folded content is reachable without
going through the archive door at all.

## Why the door is not enough

THE GUARD IS ON A VERB AND THE CONTENT IS SOMEWHERE ELSE. Refusing the browse
verb stops the front way in and leaves the back way open. That is not a new
mechanism failing; it is an old mechanism that only ever guarded one path.

IT WAS ALREADY SCORED, AT 2, at the graft state, with this reason. So it was
seen. What is new here is that nothing was filed against it, so the score was
the whole of the record.

## Why it does not gate

THE ROW IS `should`, graded corrosive. A design failing it is still a candidate,
and the comparison already priced it.

SAYING THAT IS NOT DISMISSING IT. The rule erodes silently by design, which is
what its own removal note says would happen.

## What would close it

TWO SHAPES, and neither is chosen here.

- GUARD THE CONTENT RATHER THAN THE DOOR. The concealment mechanism the
  benchmark guard already uses hides paths from the lane, and it works on the
  ref-reading path as well as the working tree.
- DROP THE RULE AND SAY SO. A rule enforced on one path of two is a rule that
  reports a guarantee it does not have, and an honest removal beats that.

## The trigger

AT THE BUILD STEP THAT WRITES THE FOLD, and at any state that touches the
archive door.
