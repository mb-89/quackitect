THE GROWTH WAS DELIBERATE, not drift. The owner ruled that one record holds
what needs their judgment and this one holds what does not, and eight already
ruled items moved here as a consequence.

TWO THINGS HOLD IT. The left-out list names five exclusions and binds. The
change size is major, which compiles a machine that plans the build rather
than improvising it.

WHAT IS STILL OPEN is the order. The argument on record is that the renderer
collapse comes first, because every other piece draws through it, and building
it last would mean building each surface twice.
