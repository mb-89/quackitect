## The perturbation that found it

The reverse graft asks the weight hunt's question the other way round. Not
"what if a criterion moved", but "if a rival could take what the leader does
better, does it win".

THE LEADER HOLDS TWO STRENGTHS, read off the score table rather than chosen.

- 5 on `req-call-answers-in-one-second`, the only 5 in the table, from the
  rider delivering completion inside answers the caller was already getting.
- 3 on `req-a-slowness-signal-never-shortens-the-wait`, from reporting on a
  clock plus unasked completion.

BOTH COME FROM ONE CELL: `opt-the-account-rides-every-answer`.

## What happens when the runner-up takes it

`cand-the-measured-answer` scores 4, 4, 2. It takes the rider and nothing else
changes, because the rider sits on the telling row and the basis pair sits on
the estimate row. They do not compete for a cell.

WITH THE RIDER IT WOULD SCORE 4, 5, 3.

THE LEADER SCORES 2, 5, 3.

That is domination: at least as good on every axis, better on one. Not a trade,
and not a close call.

## Why this is not simply "graft it then"

IT MIGHT BE, AND THAT IS THE POINT OF THE TRIPWIRE. The graft state exists for
exactly this. What makes it worth a register entry rather than a note is that
the convergence has already run and declared a seat.

IF THE GRAFT RUNS AND TAKES THE RIDER, the winner is the measured answer with a
rider, which is the fifth curve build_chart named as legal and left undrawn.

IF THE GRAFT DOES NOT RUN, the record declares a winner that a two-cell change
to its runner-up would have beaten outright, and nothing downstream would ever
notice.

## The fallback

Declare the winner as computed, and let the graft carry the rider onto it. The
graft is the sanctioned door and it is one state away.

WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN is re-running the convergence with this finding in hand.
That is scoring with the answer visible, which is the poisoning the whole
weights-before-candidates order exists to prevent.

## What would retire it

The graft state recording which cells moved, and a re-read of the score table
against the grafted winner showing no rival dominates it.
