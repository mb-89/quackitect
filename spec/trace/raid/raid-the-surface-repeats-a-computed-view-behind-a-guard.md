## The decision in one line

THE SURFACE REPEATS AND DOES NOT DERIVE, AND NOTHING ELSE MAY DRAW. Two
mechanisms, adopted together, on one surviving surface.

## How it was reached, including how thin it was

THE COUNT TIED TWO TO TWO between the guarded collapse and the repeater
surface. The tie broke on severity: the wrong-act axis is graded fatal and the
panel axis crippling.

THE ENGINE'S OWN PERTURBATION HUNT PUT THE RIVAL'S DEFICIT AT ZERO, with two
single-point swings available. Either one would have moved the seat.

THAT IS WHY THIS IS NOT RECORDED AS ONE CANDIDATE BEATING ANOTHER. The two
answer different questions — where the view is decided, and what stops a
second surface being written — and the graft takes both. The tied count was
the option box drawing a pair as a choice.

A SECOND HAND RE-SCORED THE GRAFT with clean context. Two axes up, two
unmoved, one down.

## Rejected options

- opt-the-host-tree-view-replaces-the-custom-drawing — REJECTED. It lost every
  axis it could be compared on: 2 against 5 on showing the machine, 1 against
  4 on controls, 2 against 3 on the filter. It survived on the front only
  because two of its cells were unscorable, and incomparability is not a
  virtue. Its own note also records the owner's field experience against it.
- opt-one-renderer-with-two-outputs — REJECTED, and never drawn as a
  candidate. It buys a second destination the owner has ruled obsolete
  residue, so it pays for versatility nobody wants.
- KEEPING TWO SURFACES ON PURPOSE — REJECTED BY OWNER RULING rather than by
  analysis (2026-08-23). Recorded here because a reader deserves to know the
  alternative was removed rather than beaten.

## Consequences

WHAT THIS BINDS FROM NOW ON.

- THE VIEW MODEL IS AN INTERFACE. Every widget's needs must be expressible in
  it, and a widget that needs something it does not carry may not reach around
  it. That is the whole discipline and it is also the whole cost.
- THE ENGINE OWNS PRESENTATION DECISIONS it does not own today, including
  which control is grey and why.
- ONE EXPORTED ENTRY POINT EMITS WIDGETS, and the exemption list for fixtures,
  diagnostics and vendored components is part of the design rather than an
  afterthought.
- LATENCY GETS WORSE BEFORE IT GETS BETTER. The second hand scored this graft
  2 where the base scored 3, because derivation moves inside the timed path.

## The cost that is already real

THE ONE-SECOND BUDGET IS ALREADY BREACHED. The requirement's own detail
records surface requests at 3468, 3966 and 4026 milliseconds, before anything
moves. This decision makes an already-broken budget worse rather than breaking
a healthy one, and that distinction belongs on the record rather than in
somebody's memory.

## What would overturn this

- THE COMPUTED VIEW MEASURING FAST ENOUGH would not overturn it; it would
  remove the only objection.
- THE GUARD'S PREDICATE PROVING UNDEFINABLE would remove half of it, and the
  fatal axis with it.

Both are carried as assumptions with probes.
