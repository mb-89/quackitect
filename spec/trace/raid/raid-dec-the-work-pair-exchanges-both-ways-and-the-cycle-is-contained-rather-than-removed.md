## What was decided

THE PAIR STAYS TWO-WAY. The offer reads the store's work, and names back the
work a hand took so the store can record the take.

## Why it cannot simply be removed

WHOEVER HANDS WORK OUT MUST REPORT THAT IT WENT. The report has to reach the
element that writes, because only one element writes.

SO THE CYCLE MOVES, IT DOES NOT VANISH. Routing the report through the walk
engine makes the pair walk-against-store instead, over a longer path, and the
walk already reads the store for the leaving judgment.

## Rejected options

THE OFFER WRITES THE TAKE ITSELF. Rejected: it puts a second writer on the work,
and one writer is what makes the merge surface countable. That property is what
[[raid-dec-the-position-owns-its-work-and-the-merge-cost-is-accepted]] rests on.

THE TAKE IS NEVER RECORDED. Rejected here and kept as a live option elsewhere.
It genuinely removes the cycle, and it also removes the mark that
[[req-work-says-when-a-hand-is-on-it]] asks for. It is named in
[[raid-risk-work-taken-by-a-hand-that-dies-has-no-path-back]] as one of two ways
to fix stranded work, so the build may still reach it — from that direction,
with that row's demand in hand, rather than to tidy a metric.

ROUTE THE REPORT THROUGH THE WALK. Rejected: it relocates the cycle to a wider
pair and adds a hop, which is worse on both counts.

## Consequences

THE STRUCTURE METRIC STAYS AT FIVE TWO-WAY PAIRS rather than four. The target is
zero and this record does not reach it, deliberately.

THE CONTAINMENT IS THE BENEFIT. A cycle between two elements of one group, both
new, both this record's, is the cheapest place for one to sit. Neither end
reaches outside the work group.

A LATER RECORD MAY STILL CLOSE IT, through the never-record-the-take option, and
that path is written down above rather than left to be rediscovered.

## Why this is a decision and not a follow-up line

THE STATE'S OWN CARD RULES ON IT. A nonzero structure number is worked in the
deck, or back at the structure, and never typed about. It was typed about first,
and the cold review caught that. This node is the correction.
