## What the spawn did and did not buy

THIS ENTRY IS NOT A COMPLAINT THAT NO SECOND HAND RAN. Two ran, where the
method asks for none, and both found things the author had not.

WHAT IT BOUGHT: reasoning nobody else did. Three claims were corrected that had
been fixed in one document and left standing in two others.

WHAT IT DID NOT BUY, and it is three things.

- THE REVIEWERS NEVER READ THE ARTIFACTS. Each read a summary the author wrote
  into its brief. Anything left out of that summary could not be attacked,
  because it did not exist as far as the reviewer knew.
- THE AUTHOR SCOPED THE ATTACK. The second was told to go after the answers
  rather than the design. That is a sensible brief and it is also the author
  choosing the search area.
- THE AUTHOR GRADED THE RESULT. Which findings were real, which entered the
  register, and what damage grade and likelihood each carried.

## What the correction rate says

SEVENTEEN QUESTIONS WERE ASKED BY AN ADVERSARY AND THREE CLAIMS CHANGED: a
withdrawal, a concession, and a reworded ruling. That is roughly one finding per
six questions.

NOTHING SUGGESTS THE UNASKED QUESTIONS HAVE A LOWER DEFECT RATE. The honest
prior is the same rate over an unbounded set.

## Why it is graded expected

THE CONDITION ALREADY HOLDS. The walker count for this record is zero by
deliberate choice, and the phase has no second author. Only the timing of the
next miss is open.

## What the phase did right, recorded so the grade is not read as blame

TWO ADVERSARIAL PASSES WERE RUN when the method asks for none. Both were told to
attack the load-bearing parts. Both returned findings that changed the artifacts,
and the second named its own commissioning as part of the problem.

THE SYCOPHANCY GUARD HELD in at least one measurable place: the delta says out
loud that the storage shape is the one five surveyed systems moved away from.

## What closes it

A REVIEWER THAT DID NOT AUTHOR, at a gate, briefed by somebody other than the
author. The register already carries a sibling entry about every gate in a record
being blessed by its own author, and this is the same seam one step earlier.

THE FIX WAS TESTED AND IT WORKS. A third hand was spawned and told to read the
record itself. The lane answered its first call. It read nine evidence files,
four register entries and five engine files, and it checked four of this
phase's citations against the source rather than trusting them.

SO THE EARLIER EXCUSE WAS FALSE. The author had written that a spawned hand
might not reach the lane verbs and embedded a summary instead. That claim was
never tested and it is wrong, which means the input dependency in the first two
passes was avoidable at the time.

WHAT THE THIRD PASS FOUND that the briefed ones could not: three of four
corrections the phase said it had made were still standing in the tree, and one
claim the phase said it verified in the engine rests on a comment rather than on
the code.

A SECOND PARTIAL FIX is that the pressure test's own method should name a
separate hand, which today it does not. Captured as a note, with the evidence
that the argument for it already stands one state earlier and nothing acts on
it.
