## What closed it

THE RIVAL HAS NO ADVANTAGES LEFT. `cand-the-folder-answers-for-itself` scores
zero pluses against two minuses, so minus two. Ten of the twelve axes are ties.

IT HELD THREE PLUSES WHEN THIS ENTRY WAS MINTED, and every one of the three
turned out to be a scoring artefact rather than a design advantage.

- THE INSTALL AXIS went to the leader by graft, and that was a real change to
  the design rather than a correction.
- THE SETUP FLOOR and THE OPEN CLOCK were each scored again across BOTH lines
  by one hand. Both came back equal.
- STOPPING BEFORE A PARTIAL INSTALL was a minus resting on a candidate sentence
  that had already been corrected. Rescored, it became a tie.

SO NEITHER TAKEABLE CELL REACHES THE SEAT. Either alone leaves the rival at
minus one. Both together leave it at nought, which does not BEAT the datum.

## The condition, as it stood

THE LEADER'S MARGIN WAS ZERO. The rival tied the datum at nought and lost the
seat only because Pugh requires a rival to BEAT it rather than match it.

TWO CELLS WERE ONE POINT FROM CLOSING, both where the rival scored below the
leader, and both remain takeable at no cost to anything the rival wins on.

- `req-a-wrong-act-never-passes-silently`, 2 against 3. The only FATAL axis here.
  Swap the rival's root cell for ownership stated and acknowledged.
- `req-fresh-product-starts-empty`, 4 against 5. Swap its withholding cell for
  the include list. Probe P3 measured that both producing acts already run our
  own filter, so the change lands in one function.

NEITHER REQUIRED THE CRITERIA TO BE WRONG. That is what made it credible rather
than a perturbation.

## The audit trail, kept because a margin this narrow is worth one

THE SAME DECISION WAS REACHED FOUR TIMES BY DIFFERENT ROUTES.

- FIRST, at converge-pugh: three pluses against three minuses, nought.
- THEN the graft took the rival's install cell for the leader. Two of the
  rival's pluses became ties, and the total read minus one.
- THEN the stop-before-partial axis was settled across both lines. One of the
  rival's minuses became a tie, and the total read nought again.
- THEN the two axes the rival still led on were scored twice more. Once by a
  hand told a separate reading had found the mechanisms identical, and once by
  a hand told nothing at all. Both runs returned the same four numbers.

THE BLIND RUN IS WHY THIS IS BELIEVABLE. The first run was asked a leading
question and its answer favoured the incumbent, which is exactly when a result
should be distrusted. The blind run found a genuine difference in commitment on
the open clock and still scored the two lines equal, with its reasons.

THE RECOUNT IS A SCRIPT RATHER THAN ARITHMETIC IN A PARAGRAPH.
`scratchpad/pugh-recount.mjs` parses the stored table, applies each
rescore with its reason, and prints every axis with its sign.

## What this does NOT settle, and it is the larger finding

TEN OF TWELVE AXES ARE TIES. These criteria barely separate these two designs.
The chart's own cut struck 99 rows of 115 as non-differentiating and kept
twelve; twelve differentiate on two.

THE SEPARATIONS THAT MADE IT LOOK OTHERWISE WERE ARTEFACTS OF SCORING ONE
CANDIDATE AT A TIME. Nobody ever read an axis ACROSS the set, so two hands could
put the same mechanism two points apart and nothing caught it. That is filed as
note-3302bd1644f0 for the retro, with two candidate fixes.

A DECISION RESTING ON TWO CELLS IS NOT A COMFORTABLE ONE. It is defensible, and
both cells are the leader's central argument rather than incidental: a wrong act
never passing silently is the only axis on the chart graded FATAL, and what a
produced copy carries is the include list.

## What would have caught this earlier

NOTHING WOULD HAVE CAUGHT THE FLIP. The reverse graft that found it was added to
the method on 2026-08-19, the same day, at the owner's ruling. Before that, the
question was only ever asked of the winner and only ever after the declaration,
where a flip is a reopen rather than a sentence.

THE SCORING INCONSISTENCY IS A DIFFERENT MATTER, and it would have been caught
by anybody reading one row of the table across all three candidates and asking
whether the numbers agreed. Nobody did, because the table is filled column by
column and read the same way.
