## What happened

AT THIS RECORD'S `spawn-for-inputs` THE GUIDE ANSWERED THE ROSTER HONESTLY.
One line, unchecked: `- [ ] Walker (deliverable/machines/methods/meth-spawn-hands.md)`.
That says this phase starts no walker.

THE SUBMIT REFUSED under SE-C-112 with `hands: unchecked`.

THE CHECKLIST'S THIRD FORM WAS TRIED NEXT. `- [owed] <item> — <ref>` is
documented as addressed to an open raid entry. It refused too, because a work
token is not a raid entry, and no raid entry covering this existed yet.

MINTING ONE ON THE SPOT WAS IMPOSSIBLE. The spawn state is read-only by design,
which is correct for a state whose whole job is to spawn and declare, and it
means the only escape the checklist offers cannot be reached from inside.

## Why the honest answer was refused

`deliverable/machines/methods/meth-spawn-hands.md` says three things that all
point one way.

- The count at the kickoff is a CEILING and never a quota.
- The guide decides at each spawn state whether that phase earns a hand.
- Spawning none at a state is a legitimate answer that needs no excuse.

THE OWNER HAD ALSO JUST RULED ON IT, on 2026-08-26, asking why the guide was
not walking directly and whether delegation was paying.

## Why registering a hand instead is worse

THE FORM'S OWN MOTIVATION FORBIDS IT: a roster nobody spawns is a roster that
does not exist.

AND IT CORRUPTS THE MEASUREMENT. The retro weighs how much each part did from
the part stamped on every call. A registered hand that never ran makes that
count a lie, and the count is the whole reason the roster exists.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY DONE, so the workaround is visible rather than hidden: a hand
was registered, the box was ticked, the registration was closed immediately,
and the form was amended to say exactly that.

## The family it belongs to

THIS IS THE THIRD DEFECT OF ONE SHAPE IN THIS RECORD: a mechanical check that
admits fewer answers than the guidance standing beside it.

- The walker ceiling read from a gate downstream of the check enforcing it, so
  the first hand of the record was over a ceiling of zero.
- A require-section check rejected two well-formed nodes whose section sat
  first in the body.
- This one.

## What would close it

THREE CANDIDATE FIXES, and choosing between them is not this record's.

- LET AN UNCHECKED BOX PASS where the field is declared optional, which it is.
- ADD AN EXPLICIT ANSWER FOR NONE, so the roster tells "not decided" from
  "decided against".
- DROP THE CHECK and let the kickoff ceiling be the only control.
