## The same shape, three times

A JUDGED EVIDENCE PART IS SETTLED BY THE HAND HOLDING THE STATE. The refusal
bites on inaction, when a token is left open. It does not bite on a self-serving
close, because several terminal statuses accept one.

COMPLEXITY IS RAISED BY THE HAND THAT FOUND THE WORK HARDER. A hand able to
detect that reliably would also have been able to detect that it was filling a
form in wrong, which is the incident this round exists for.

THE PERSON-NEEDED FLAG IS SET BY THE WORKING HAND. That is the owner's ruling
and it is deliberate.

## Why the three are one entry rather than three

THEY WOULD BE MITIGATED THE SAME WAY. Any non-self signal closes all three: a
script that answers, a second hand that reviews, or an outcome the engine can
observe. One entry, one concern.

## What is already true and worth keeping

MECHANICAL TOKENS ARE NOT AFFECTED. A token whose evidence is a script settles
by running it, and the hand cannot assert past that.

THE READING PROBES ARE NOT AFFECTED EITHER. An input token minted from a
reading requirement is settled by the probes, unchanged.

SO THE EXPOSURE IS THE JUDGED PARTS ONLY, which is narrower than the attack
implies and wider than nothing.

## What closes it

NAME A NON-SELF SIGNAL FOR EACH OF THE THREE, or record that the judged case is
knowingly left to the hand and say what counts it afterwards.

THE PERSON-NEEDED CASE HAS A CHEAP HALF-ANSWER ALREADY: count the flags a
session raises, the way forced stops are counted today. A measured rate is what
turned an earlier guard from a guess into a fix.
