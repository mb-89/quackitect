## The trigger names READING now, and this round is why

IT SAID CONDITIONS AND VERDICTS. An owner ruling then made a hop the sweep walks
OVER stop owing its reading, and that is a rigor mechanism of the same family:
the reading proof exists so a hand cannot claim to hold a document it has not
read.

A REVIEWER FOUND THE GAP. The ruling is sound and it was landed with its safety
argument written down, but no standing trigger reached it — so the NEXT change of
that shape would have had nothing watching.

ONE CLAUSE RATHER THAN A SIBLING ENTRY, because it is the same risk: a change
made to go faster removing something the rigor rests on. Two entries would split
one watch in half.

## Why it stands

THIS ROUND IS CHARTERED TO MAKE THE WALK FAST, and the cheapest way to make any
walk fast is to check less. That pull is real and it is present in every item
of this round's scope.

THE RULING THAT KEEPS IT SHUT was made at this round's vision state: the walk
gets fast by not redoing what it already did, never by doing less. A hop that
has already passed keeps its verdict. A hop nobody has judged is still judged,
at full strength.

## The direction that is safe, and the direction that is not

THE EXISTING DEBT ON THE ROUTE DRAWER ERRS THE SAFE WAY. It reads a step that
is still deciding as failed, so it refuses a walk that has done nothing wrong.
That wastes work and never lets anything through.

THE FIX MUST NOT REVERSE THAT ERROR. Teaching the drawer the third word makes
it stop refusing a deciding step. It must not also make it stop refusing a
FAILING one, and those two live one line apart.

## The check that keeps it honest

ANY CHANGE HERE LEAVES THE SAME STATES GREEN FOR THE SAME REASONS. A speed-up
that turns a red hop green has broken the goal it was serving.

THAT IS TESTABLE RATHER THAN A HOPE. A hop whose conditions fail must still
fail after the change, and a hop still deciding must be distinguishable from
both.
