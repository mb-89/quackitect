## Why it stands

Three defects put outside one record's boundary, all still standing, and together they name a shape: a tested rule aimed at a function the product never calls. The count drawn beside a position sums plain integers, while a function returning a value, a basis and a stated absence sits unused. An ordering field is declared, parsed and written, and no producer anywhere supplies one, so a numbered list comes out shuffled. And the strength field lands on none of the items at all.
