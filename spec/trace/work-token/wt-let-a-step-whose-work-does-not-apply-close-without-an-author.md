## Why it stands

Let a step whose work does not apply close without an author writing prose. One onboarding step is skipped by design when there is nothing waiting for it, and the ruling behind that is explicit. The skip only reaches the entry condition, so the position still opens its form and still demands every required box. A hand then writes several paragraphs saying that nothing needed doing. Either the form should follow the condition and stand down with the step, or its boxes should stop being required when the condition says the work is absent.

## When it comes back

ready when a round takes the evidence forms or the onboarding row
