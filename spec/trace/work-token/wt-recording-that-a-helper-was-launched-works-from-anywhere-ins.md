## Why it stands

Recording that a helper was launched works from anywhere, instead of depending on which step the walk is standing on. Today that recording rides one verb, and several steps forbid that verb, so the launch goes unrecorded and the helper never shows on the board. Two faults sit here and only one is mechanical: the driver can also simply forget, and nothing asks. Closing the forgetting half means every launch going through the cage rather than the host, which is larger and wants the owner.
