## Why it stands

A red run cannot say what is red. The last full pass reported five broken cases and printed the names of three. One of the three compares entire position objects, so its difference report unrolled every field of every position, prose included, and ran past a hundred and forty thousand characters. The runner cut itself off there and the last two names never appeared. The exit code says something is wrong and the report says nothing about what. Repeating the pass repeats the silence, and a person cannot act on it. Two repairs suit. Bound what a single comparison may contribute to the summary, so no one case can crowd out the others. And narrow that comparison to the field it actually asks about instead of the whole shape.
