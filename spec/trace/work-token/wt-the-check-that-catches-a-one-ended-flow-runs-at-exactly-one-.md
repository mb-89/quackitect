## Why it stands

The check that catches a one-ended flow runs at exactly ONE position and nowhere else, so four broken ones reached trunk and sat there until a later round tripped over them. It is a standalone program wired as a single leaving script, and it appears nowhere in the sweep that walks the whole corpus. A corpus-wide law with a position-local trigger bills whoever happens to walk that position, and never bills the author who wrote the break.

## When it comes back

ready when the whole-corpus consistency sweep is next opened — the check becomes a sweep rule running over everything on every write, and the leaving script becomes a second look rather than the only one
