## Why it stands

When something a signed answer rests on moves, the machine sends a hand to look at what the movement actually touched, and a checkpoint rules on the finding. It does not grey out everything downstream on the assumption that all of it fell. The owner's framing: we do not kill ourselves because we are afraid of dying.
