## Why it stands

Ungradeable and unreadable ledger entries reach the shared branch through two open holes. The scenario walk creates an at-risk item whose likelihood field holds commentary rather than a value, so running it manufactures cleanup for a later step. Separately, no check rejects a severity word outside the published vocabulary at write time. Seventeen historical items carrying invented words survived until an unrelated leaving condition three milestones downstream demanded they be legible, which charged one walk for other walks' debts.
