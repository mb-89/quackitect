## Why it stands

The compare-card form field's empty-pool case gets checked against the reference-list template's rules instead of its own, so it refuses ordinary compare-card content and only accepts a bare "none" line. Needs either its own empty-set handling in the compare-card validator, or the reference-list acceptance documented as the real contract for that case.
