## Why it stands

Settle whether every host must reach one shared engine, or may each spawn its own. The editor connects one way and an unattended box connects another, and nothing anywhere records that as a decision rather than an accident. The answer was given once in chat and lives only in the call trail, which the rules themselves describe as a trail and not a record.

## When it comes back

ready when somebody decides whether one shared server is a hard requirement or an editor convenience, and writes that decision into guidance
