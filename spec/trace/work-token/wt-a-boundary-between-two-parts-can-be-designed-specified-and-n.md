## Why it stands

A boundary between two parts can be designed, specified and never built, and both checks pass over the hole. The step that plans the build carries two evidence fields and a submit law of three checks, and NONE of them compares the plan against the boundaries the designs declare. So a design can claim to realise a boundary while no chunk in the plan builds it. THIS IS EXACTLY WHAT COST ONE ROUND 43% OF ITS CALLS: seven chunks were planned, none of them connected the new store to the walk, and the whole thing shipped inert.

## When it comes back

ready when a major round reaches the step that plans the build chunks — or sooner if somebody builds the cheap half, which is a sweep after the plan signs listing every boundary a design names and no chunk does
