## Why it stands

The editor panel is the single place that counts, and any other renderer is incidental. A file the panel never draws may not exist. The test is not whether something is listed but whether the panel actually pulls it. Twenty-one stray emitters get sorted on that one question. Nothing may remain that lets an agent argue from a page the owner cannot see.
