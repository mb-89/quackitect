## Why it stands

An ordering of decision criteria is computed from a damage grade and then trusted without a look. A person can override it, and nothing obliges them to. So the ranking that settles which design wins may be decided by arithmetic nobody read. Wanted is a required confirmation of the leading entries, taken before any winner is picked.
