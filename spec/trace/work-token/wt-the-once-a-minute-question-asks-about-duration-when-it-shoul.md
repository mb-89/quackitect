## Why it stands

The once-a-minute question asks about DURATION when it should ask about MATCH. One of three proposed nudges shipped: the question now quotes back the four words naming what the hand claims to be doing, which is what makes it answerable at all. The other two are absent. Nothing asks about a write made while nothing is in hand, and nothing compares the files being edited against the ones the work started on. The item carries no list of what it touched, so the cheaper signal would have to come from the call log each time.
