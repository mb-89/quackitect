## Why it stands

Strictly downstream of another open question, and unjudgeable until that one lands. IF every piece of work proved its own failure first, the position whose only job is observing that failure might have nothing left to do. The argument bites only once the premise holds, and it does not: nothing carries a check today. IT IS A CONVERSATION THE OWNER WANTED, never a deletion they ordered, and that difference has to travel with it.

## When it comes back

ready when the check-per-token idea is decided — judging this before its premise exists would be a verdict over unfinished work, which is exactly what the method forbids
