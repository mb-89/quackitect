## Why it stands

The history reader stops at the newest file and misses everything older. Today's trail split in two while a look-back was in progress: an archive appeared holding thirteen hundred entries and the live file began again at nothing. A count taken afterwards from the live file alone returned a hundred and three where fourteen hundred stood, and produced a summary table of one line rather than thirty-three. Nothing about that answer looked wrong. The same class of silence is already written up on the method page from a different cause, where sixty-eight entries came back against two thousand eight hundred. Two repairs. Have the reader take every archive sitting beside the live file, oldest first. And warn on the page that one window can span several files, so a person doing it manually goes looking for them.

## When it comes back

ready when a round takes the call history reader, and it blocks any honest mining before then
