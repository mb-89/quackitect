## Why it stands

Two mechanisms still depend on a local web server, although it is widely believed that the dependency was taken out. The person's whole viewing surface is served over it, and a probe answered with a valid handshake. The guard stopping two engines from opening one project folder also works by holding a network address derived from that folder's path. Removal is therefore two jobs rather than one, and the second job must replace the only thing keeping two processes off a single project.
