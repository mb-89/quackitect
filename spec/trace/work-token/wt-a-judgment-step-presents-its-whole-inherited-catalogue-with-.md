## Why it stands

A judgment step presents its whole inherited catalogue with no indication of ownership. One session met a hundred and nine pairs where roughly ten belonged to it, concluded that answering them would set weights binding on every future decision matrix, and halted to ask permission it did not need. Marking which entries arrived with this record, either in the offered set or in the surrounding help, removes the misreading.
