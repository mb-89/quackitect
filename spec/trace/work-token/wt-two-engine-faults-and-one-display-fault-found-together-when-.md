## Why it stands

Two engine faults and one display fault, found together when a step above the walk showed as unfinished. First, a machine rebuilt in the middle of a walk let the token pass a step that owed a signature, and nothing stopped it. Second, the completion call raises a plain error carrying no clause number and no instruction, which this lane's own law forbids everywhere else. Third, the board draws an impossible order without complaint — an unfinished step sitting above a running one — rather than saying it cannot be true.

## When it comes back

ready when an engine round opens on the walk's own faults, or when the panel iteration i4 starts
