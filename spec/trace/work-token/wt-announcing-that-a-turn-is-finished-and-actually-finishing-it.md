## Why it stands

Announcing that a turn is finished and actually finishing it should be a single act. The guard only accepts a named reason after it has already pushed back once, which forces a four-beat sequence: the agent says it is done, gets refused, names its reason, and says it again. Anything worked on during that refused beat directly contradicts what the person was told a moment earlier. The ordering exists so that naming a reason cannot become the routine exit, and that aim is worth keeping. Check the reason against the closed list of legitimate endings instead, and let it be given up front. The writing rule for the agent is narrower: having announced an ending, nothing else may happen before the act that ends it.
