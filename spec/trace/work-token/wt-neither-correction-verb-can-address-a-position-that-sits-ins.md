## Why it stands

Neither correction verb can address a position that sits inside a nested machine. One strips a qualified id down to its last segment and looks that up in the top machine only; the other passes it through untouched. Both throw on anything the top machine does not declare. Two behaviours are owed and neither exists: resolve a fully qualified id, and resolve a bare one wherever exactly one position answers to it.
