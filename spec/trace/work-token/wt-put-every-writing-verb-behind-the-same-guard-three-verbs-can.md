## Why it stands

Put every writing verb behind the same guard. Three verbs can write a file and only one of them runs the check that refuses a write leaving the corpus unreadable. The one the guidance recommends for ordinary editing is among the two that skip it, so following the advice is what gets past the guard. Whichever verb a hand reaches for should meet the same refusal, and the check belongs in the one place all three pass through rather than in each of them.
