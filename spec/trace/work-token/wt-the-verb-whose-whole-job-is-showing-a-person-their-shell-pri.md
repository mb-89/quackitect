## Why it stands

The verb whose whole job is showing a person their shell prints no power controls, while the verb that aims the walk prints them. That is backwards: a reader asking to look gets less than a reader asking to move. The engine already works the block out and hands it to the picture, so what has to change is which text renderer includes it.
