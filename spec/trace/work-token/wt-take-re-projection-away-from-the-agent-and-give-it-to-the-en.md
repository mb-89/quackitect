## Why it stands

Take re-projection away from the agent and give it to the engine. Today an edit to a source document leaves the files generated from it behind, the preflight goes red at the next verdict, and a hand has to remember to call a verb. The owner ruled it should work the other way: one file declares what is generated and from what, the engine notices when a declared source is edited, and it regenerates on its own. When that lands, no agent calls a projection verb at all.

## When it comes back

ready when an engine round takes the generated-file layer, and before the next hand-made edit to a source document
