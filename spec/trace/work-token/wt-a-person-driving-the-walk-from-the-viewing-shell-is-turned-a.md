## Why it stands

A person driving the walk from the viewing shell is turned away with no stated cause. The engine holds a full account of every block it issues: the rule number, the gap between what was demanded and what arrived, and a ready-made call that would clear it. All of that reaches an agent and none of it reaches the person. The standing law is that anything blocking owes a way forward, and today that law is honoured for one of the two hands only.
