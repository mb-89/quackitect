## Why it stands

Stop a spawned helper from moving the position its parent is standing on. Every attached agent shares one walk, so a helper that pulls drags the parent somewhere it never asked to go, and this was seen for real with three readers running at once. The cheap half is one line in the spawning card telling a read-only helper which verbs are forbidden. The thorough half refuses those verbs outright for anything that did not start the session.

## When it comes back

ready when somebody picks between warning a spawned reader off the walking verbs and refusing them mechanically
