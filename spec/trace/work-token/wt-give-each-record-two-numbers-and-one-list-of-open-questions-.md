## Why it stands

Give each record two numbers and one list of open questions, then route by them. The first number says how hard the thing is, drawing on how much design remains as well as how much building. The second says how much the rest of the system depends on it. Sorting by both splits the queue three ways: what a machine finishes alone, what it finishes once the owner has answered the open questions up front, and what needs somebody watching while it runs.

## When it comes back

ready when the next seeding round opens, since the owner asked for it to shape the records seeded after this retro
