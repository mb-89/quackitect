## Why it stands

Move the dated backstory out of the pages the machine loads into every prompt. Better than twenty dated attributions still sit inline across the binding rules, the refusal register and three craft cards, and each one is paid for on every turn of every session. The rule stays where it is; the account of who decided it and when moves to a page linked once.

## When it comes back
