## Why it stands

Evidence produced during the first two milestones is never written to history. No state in that stretch declares the version-control verb, and the walking rules tell the agent it never has to reach for one. Everything therefore sits unsaved until a much later checkpoint. That is invisible on durable hardware and fatal on ephemeral hardware, where an interrupted session loses every form it signed. Closing it means an automatic checkpoint at each signature or each gate.

## When it comes back

ready when a disposable box is the normal host rather than the exception
