## Why it stands

Half of this is fixed and half stands. Excluding the desk by name is gone, and the code records that it had no requirement behind it. A second guard remains: a position drawn as finished owes nothing, so any position that turns finished the moment it is entered can never owe anything at all. Boot and the desk both behave that way, and somebody watching a boot sees nothing appear. Two repairs are available and the owner has to pick — derive what is owed earlier in the entry, or let a read-only position past that guard.
