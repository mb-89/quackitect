## Why it stands

Half of the owner's ruling landed. The RULE that decides a checkpoint's approval now lives in one method, and the guard over standing work is called from there, so there is one place where the decision is made. What remains is three separate readers of the approval key in the incoming payload — two in the session and one on the surface. That is not a second decider, but it is three places a shape change has to be made, and the owner asked for one.

## When it comes back

ready when the retro or an engine round decides whether the two remaining payload readers fold into the single session method
