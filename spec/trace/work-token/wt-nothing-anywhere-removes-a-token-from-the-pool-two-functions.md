## Why it stands

Nothing anywhere removes a token from the pool. Two functions read it and one adds to it, and no third exists. Since i63 the situation is slightly worse rather than better: work a hand left unfinished is now carried INTO the pool when its position closes, so the pile has a new inlet and still no outlet. THE OWNER SAID THIS NEED NOT BE DECIDED YET, so it waits on them rather than on code. It is the same hole the routing table ran into.

## When it comes back

ready when the owner rules between the two they named: a sweep that files old work into the archive, or forbidding work that outlives every record
