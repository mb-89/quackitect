## Why it stands

Three closes were wrong and a person found all three by looking at a screen. No check found any of them. A second-reader mechanism exists in this system, but only at a milestone checkpoint — nothing looks at a single piece of work. The one path that closes work takes a status and a non-empty reason and asks nothing else. THE INSTRUMENT IS PROVEN AND POINTED ELSEWHERE: the checkpoint reviewer caught the same shape twice in one round, on a different unit.

## When it comes back

THE RULING IS MADE, at the 2026-08-28 look-back. They are TWO mechanisms and they are built separately.

A second reader is a person's judgment on a close. A proof carried by the work is a check the machine runs.

Conflating them buys neither. The judgment cannot be automated, and the check does not need a person.

Ready when the record takes it up.
