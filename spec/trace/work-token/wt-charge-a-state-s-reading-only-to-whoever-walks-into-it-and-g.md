## Why it stands

Charge a state's reading only to whoever walks into it, and give every guidance card a short form beside the long one. Reaching one door at the switchboard cost eight documents where that door wanted two, because the loop clears the demands of every neighbouring door before it will open any of them. The switchboard is the genuine exception: it advises about everywhere, so it wants the short forms of everything and the long form of nothing.
