## Why it stands

Nothing counts how often each kind of design node is used, or each frontmatter attribute. So nobody can say which kinds are dead, which attributes nothing reads, and which pairs mean the same thing under two names. The schema declares the legal edges and a list names the known kinds, and neither is a census. IT IS ONE SCRIPT over the design folder, and the owner asked for the answer rather than for a rebuild.
