## Why it stands

Each story a person could act out gets a check that drives the whole system the way that person would, and minting those checks is automatic rather than remembered. The owner ranks them above the small isolated checks. Two things fell over on 2026-08-24 while every small check stayed green: the tooth that holds a turn open stopped biting, and two positions of a dial did nothing when pressed. Neither is reachable by a check that only reads one function's output. "It needs a real machine" is not accepted as a reason to skip one.
