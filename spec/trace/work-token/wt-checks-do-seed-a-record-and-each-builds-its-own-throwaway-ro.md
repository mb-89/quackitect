## Why it stands

Checks DO seed a record, and each builds its own throwaway root, which is exactly the reason a path resolving through the real session slipped past every one. That pattern repeats across at least five of them. WHAT IS MISSING IS A SACRIFICIAL RECORD sitting where the product itself looks, openable at any moment and safe to ruin. Today nobody exercises the machinery without walking a genuine round, so faults surface in real work rather than in a fixture.

## When it comes back

ready when the next defect is found by walking the live record rather than by a check — that is the cost this removes, and it will keep recurring until somebody builds it
