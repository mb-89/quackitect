## Why it stands

The verb meant to show the person their shell still asks the host to open a web address that was deleted. Requesting it answers not-found, and only two paths are recognised now, the root not among them. The launch is fire-and-forget, so nothing anywhere reports the failure. That same dead address is written to the error stream at every start, phrased as an invitation to visit. Choosing a new destination for it belongs to the owner, who is designing the editor extension that supersedes it.
