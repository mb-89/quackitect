## Why it stands

Starting a session costs a handful of exchanges rather than dozens. Three costs stack today: material is served that this kind of session will never use, a large answer is handed back one slice at a time even though the machine had just sent the whole thing, and a source file carrying an unfinished merge stops the opening outright instead of reporting it. The owner raised all three on 2026-08-25.

## When it comes back

ready when the opening sequence is next measured, and the owner's target is twenty seconds
