## Why it stands

Notice when a fetched page is a wall rather than the thing asked for. Several real sites answer with a success code and a body that is a robot challenge, a consent barrier or an age gate, and the tool reports that as an ordinary result. A researcher then cites a page nobody read. Inspect the body, and say plainly that the content was withheld.
