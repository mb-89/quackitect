## Why it stands

A retro's future-scope findings land in a pile nothing delivers, and a record's opening step never draws them down. The drain still mints onto trunk and stops there; its own field description says so. There is no placement at an opening step, no pending bucket on a record, and no sorter. THIS RETRO IS THE PROOF: 172 tokens were routed to owners on paper and not one could be moved, because the door out does not exist.
