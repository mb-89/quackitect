## Why it stands

A person who confirms having finished a document does not thereby satisfy the demand for it. The one proof the machine accepts is a set of anchored fill-in-the-blank answers, which measures a machine reader and not somebody who has simply finished the page. The cause was measured: the form holds its own list of confirmed labels, while the demand consults a different store, and confirming writes the first and never the second. A third store does exist and does credit some confirmations, so the seam is narrow. Until it closes, a person is halted at the first document on any route. That is where an attempt to drive this system without an agent ended.
