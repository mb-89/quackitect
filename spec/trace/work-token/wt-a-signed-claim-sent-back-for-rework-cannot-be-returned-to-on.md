## Why it stands

A signed claim sent back for rework cannot be returned to once it sits behind where the walk stands, because the engine draws forward edges only. The driver must leave through the escape hatch to get there, which pushes people toward quietly correcting a claim they ought to have sent back. The honest act is the expensive one. Three candidate repairs, in rising size: warn at the moment it happens, land the walk on the step that was sent back, or draw a path backwards. Separately, the explaining verb cannot address a step inside a nested machine, unlike its neighbour.

## When it comes back

ready when an iteration opens on the walk machine or on the correction verbs
