## Why it stands

Stop the machine's own prose teaching its design history to whoever is reading it. A person needs what is true and what to do, never how the mechanism came to exist. Two greeting lines were already trimmed on that principle. The same habit sits untouched wherever the engine writes words for a reader.

## When it comes back

ready when somebody sweeps the same pattern through every surface that carries embedded prose; the two greeting lines already cut are the template
