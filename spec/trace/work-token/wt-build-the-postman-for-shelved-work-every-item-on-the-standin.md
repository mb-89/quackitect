## Why it stands

Build the postman for shelved work. Every item on the standing pile carries a sentence saying when to bring it back, and no code ever reads those sentences. Somebody has to remember, and nobody does. Three items are overdue right now by their own wording. The starkest one asked to be woken by the round that has just been walked end to end; it was never woken. Two others have said they are ready for days while pointing at rounds nobody opened. Finding all three took me a few lines of throwaway code inside this session, which is the measure of how cheap the missing mechanism is. The right trigger is creation: when a round is brought into being, everything whose wake-up sentence names it should surface then and there. This failure has been counted before, on a different round, at sixteen items.
