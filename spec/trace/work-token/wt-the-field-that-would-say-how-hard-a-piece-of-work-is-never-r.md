## Why it stands

The field that would say how hard a piece of work is never reaches disk. It is declared on the item and written as an empty string by the one path that mints, so 33 of 33 items on disk carry it not merely blank but absent. The two timestamps beside it ARE written, so the duration half is free the moment the other half is filled. Together they would turn every finished item into one calibration point for estimates, which nothing currently produces.

## When it comes back

ready when the routing side settles what a strength rating is for, since the same empty field blocks both and filling it twice would be two answers to one question
