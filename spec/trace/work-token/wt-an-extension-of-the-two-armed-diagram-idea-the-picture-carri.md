## Why it stands

An extension of the two-armed diagram idea. The picture carries two registers at once: touched nodes within the arms, and beside them whatever each milestone turned out. How far the colour descends is the progress reading, so a single image answers what changed, what came out of it, and how far it got. Where a whole product is being shown rather than one iteration, no walk exists to read, so book chapters take the place of the turned-out items and every element becomes a door into what it names. Two questions stay open. How progress is worked out without a walk. And whether a milestone still owing an output draws an empty slot or draws nothing.
