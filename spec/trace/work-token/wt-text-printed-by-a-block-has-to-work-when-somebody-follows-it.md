## Why it stands

Text printed by a block has to work when somebody follows it. One block instructs anyone missing a freshly built capability to restart the serving process, promising the capability will be there afterwards. Watched on 2026-08-25: the restart happened, the walk began again from the entrance, and the capability was still not callable. The probable cause is that the client asks what it may call once, at connection time, and never asks again. Establish whether anything short of a fresh session refreshes that inventory. Where nothing does, the text must name the session restart. Until then it should hedge rather than promise, because an instruction nobody can carry out is worse than none.
