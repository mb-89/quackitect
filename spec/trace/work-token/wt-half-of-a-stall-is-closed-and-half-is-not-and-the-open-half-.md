## Why it stands

Half of a stall is closed and half is not, and the open half has exact steps written down for making it happen again. The checker does now read the verdict left on disk, and a case covers it. What still happens: a route drawn over several steps refuses with a phrase meaning the program never ran, while a single-step aim over the same ground passes. Nothing has been walked at that moment, so the route is judged before any of it begins — which points at how routes are drawn rather than at the checker already repaired.

## When it comes back

ready when the walk next re-enters a long record after a restart and the multi-hop sweep refuses, or when a session reaches the repair position with engine time to spend
