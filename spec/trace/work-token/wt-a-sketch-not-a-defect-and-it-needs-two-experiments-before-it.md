## Why it stands

A sketch, not a defect, and it needs two experiments before it needs code. The proposal moves the authority to close: the hand doing the work hands it in, a second hand closes it, and the position cannot be left until that second hand is satisfied. Nothing in the store can express that today — the statuses go straight to terminal, there is no handed-in state, no reviewer field and no thread. THIS RETRO MEASURED THE STRONGEST ARGUMENT FOR IT: five checkpoints that got an independent cold reader all failed, and five that got none all passed.
