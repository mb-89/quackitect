## Why it stands

A capability whose behaviour does not vary by position is granted once at the centre, and no position may withhold it. The deciding question is whether standing somewhere else would change what that capability may do. Looking outward at the network fails that question, as does printing the surface; saving a file passes it and stays gated. Such a central grant already exists in the running code, but nothing documents it and no rule tells whoever adds a capability whether it belongs there. Two things close the gap. A card carrying the deciding question, and a counting check that flags any capability which more than half the position files name individually.
