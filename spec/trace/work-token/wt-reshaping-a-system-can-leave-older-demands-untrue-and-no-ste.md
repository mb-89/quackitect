## Why it stands

Reshaping a system can leave older demands untrue, and no step in the walk asks which ones. One iteration lost most of a milestone lane to a constraint that was never a demand at all: a single write destination, treated as though it were law. It outlived four steps, an adversarial pass and a demand check before a single question dissolved it. Wanted is a step emitting one row for every standing demand the work collides with, plus a verdict on each — keep, reword or drop — carrying its reason and its author. Telling a law apart from one way of building it is the whole point.
