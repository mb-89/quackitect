## Why it stands

Four hand-rolled caches stand in the engine with four different keys and no shared mechanism. One counter exists, watching a single function. So there is nothing general to wrap a function in, and nothing that could find the functions worth wrapping. THE SWEEP IS THE HARDER HALF AND THE MISSING ONE: without an instrument that counts how often one operation asks for the same unchanged thing, any list of candidates is a guess.

## When it comes back

ready when the sweep has an instrument — counting repeated calls over unchanged inputs is the half with no mechanism, and the decorator is pointless without it
