## Why it stands

Rule on whether a hand may ask a question while it builds. The verb that runs checks is forbidden at every building position and at the checking position too. A hand there has no sanctioned way to learn whether the edit it just made works. In one round that produced two outright rejections and three uses of the declared exception, each time invoking the test runner directly, each time with the same justification. So the practice already exists and only the permission is missing. Two endings are honest. One says the craft is deliberately blind at those positions, and the written instructions should say so plainly enough that nobody keeps reaching. The other admits the verb where the work is. Today's arrangement is neither: rejected by the machine, performed anyway through the side door.
