## Why it stands

The bookkeeping around the work stops producing two in every five refusals. Over one measured window of 2,124 calls, 41 of 102 typed refusals came from the progress-reporting mechanism rather than from the work itself. The commonest shape is a one-line summary naming three things: on one kind of entry the engine quietly rewrites it into a list, and on another it refuses outright, and nothing tells a reader which kind they are on. A fifth of all failures in the same window carried no clause code at all, so the ranking this step depends on cannot see them.

## Measured again on 2026-08-26, and both halves got worse

A SECOND WINDOW, 1,897 calls, one round walked end to end.

THE CLAUSELESS SHARE ROSE FROM A FIFTH TO NEARLY TWO THIRDS. 121 calls failed and 77 of them carry no clause code, so the ranking this step depends on cannot see 64 percent of its own subject. At least six distinct clause codes fired in that window and appear nowhere in the grouping.

THE THREE-PART SUMMARY IS NOW THE TOP NAMED CAUSE. It fired 15 times, ahead of every other code. Its neighbour, which refuses closing an item over an unclosed child, fired 11.

SO 26 OF THE 44 CLASSIFIED FAILURES CAME FROM THE PROGRESS BOOKKEEPING rather than from the work, which is a higher share than the earlier window's two in five.

AND THE VOLUME IS THE LARGER HALF. The bookkeeping verb accounts for 686 of the 1,897 calls, 36 percent of everything, against 282 of the walking verb. That is 2.4 bookkeeping entries per step of walking, over a stretch of about five and a half hours, which is above the pace the contract asks for.

## The owner ruled on this, 2026-08-28

REMOVE IT RATHER THAN MAKE IT LEANER. The tokens talk now: a token taken says
what began and a token settled says what happened, so the bookkeeping the toll
was built to force is already written.

WHAT SURVIVES IS ONE NUDGE, at about five minutes, asking whether the token in
hand is still the work. Its shape is in
[[wt-the-once-a-minute-question-asks-about-duration-when-it-shoul]].

THE MEASURED PRIZE IS ON THIS ITEM ALREADY: 41 of 102 typed refusals in one
window came from the progress-reporting mechanism rather than from the work.
Removing it removes those refusals outright.
