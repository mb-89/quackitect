## Why it stands

The bookkeeping around the work stops producing two in every five refusals. Over one measured window of 2,124 calls, 41 of 102 typed refusals came from the progress-reporting mechanism rather than from the work itself. The commonest shape is a one-line summary naming three things: on one kind of entry the engine quietly rewrites it into a list, and on another it refuses outright, and nothing tells a reader which kind they are on. A fifth of all failures in the same window carried no clause code at all, so the ranking this step depends on cannot see them.

## When it comes back

ready when the narration system is next opened, and the owner has already asked for it to become leaner
