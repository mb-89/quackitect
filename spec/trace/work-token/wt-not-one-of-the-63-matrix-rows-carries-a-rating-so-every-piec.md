## Why it stands

Not one of the 63 matrix rows carries a rating, so every piece of work minted from them comes out unrated, and the offer that routes work by strength has nothing to route on. The engine short-circuits the whole check while ratings are absent, so nothing goes red and nothing explains why the offer hands out nothing. The code is right to wait, because grading those cells is a person's judgment and no agent may take it. THIS IS A DECISION BLOCKED ON SOMEBODY, not a defect.

## When it comes back

ready when the person who owns the matrix has rated its active cells — the README line is the last act and it arms the load-time refusal by itself
