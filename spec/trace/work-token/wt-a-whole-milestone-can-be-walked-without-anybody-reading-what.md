## Why it stands

A whole milestone can be walked without anybody reading what the round is actually for. The summary written at the opening checkpoint reads as the complete scope, so every position below measures itself against the summary and nobody opens the record's own statement. Scanning every position's entry demands, exactly ONE mentions the record at all, and that one is incidental. Neither the cheap repair nor the real one exists anywhere.
