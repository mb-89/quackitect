## Why it stands

A serving process that dies and returns puts the walk back where it stood, not at the entrance. On 2026-08-25 one call failed with a transport error and the next answered from the very beginning, costing a wasted entry. Both the checklist and the proof of what had been read survived, so the position alone was lost. Why it died at all is the second half: nothing had asked it to restart.
