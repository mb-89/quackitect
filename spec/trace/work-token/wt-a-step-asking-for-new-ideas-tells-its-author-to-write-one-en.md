## Why it stands

A step asking for new ideas tells its author to write one entry per idea, and never shows what already stands. Two entries minted a year apart now sit on one chart saying nearly the same thing, which inflates how large the design space appears. Wanted is a form that lists what already exists for the questions in scope, so a writer meets the duplicate before making it. A ruling on whether those two particular entries are really one thing is owed as well.
