## Why it stands

Nothing holds a round's logged dangers against the demands it wrote, so a danger can be logged and answered by nothing at all. The checkpoint where that would be caught carries NO FIELDS AT ALL BY A STANDING RULING, which is what makes this a decision rather than an edit — adopting it reverses that ruling. It also has a price the finding names itself, and the two have to be put to the owner together, because taking one without the other is not honest.

## When it comes back

ready when the owner accepts two things together: that the requirements checkpoint takes a field back, and that adding one makes every record already holding that checkpoint owe its evidence again
