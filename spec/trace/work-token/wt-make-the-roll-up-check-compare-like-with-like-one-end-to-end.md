## Why it stands

Make the roll-up check compare like with like. One end-to-end case asserts that a container's total equals a sum built by filtering boxes on their name. The container totals everything beneath it, including positions nested inside a record; the filter keeps only the records themselves. Anything held at a nested position lands in one figure and not the other. The consequence is that the suite turns red exactly when somebody has work open where they are standing, which is the ordinary condition. A suite that reddens because a person is working teaches its reader to look away. Either the sum should descend as far as the total does, or the two should be brought level another way.
