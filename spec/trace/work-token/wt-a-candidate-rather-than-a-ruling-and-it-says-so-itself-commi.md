## Why it stands

A candidate rather than a ruling, and it says so itself. Commit as a phase opens, read the difference as it closes, and a reviewer then knows exactly what to look at instead of scoping itself. Nothing anywhere implements it. TWO THINGS WOULD HAVE TO BE SETTLED FIRST: how a phase walked twice behaves, and which point the difference is measured from on that second walk. It matters because it is the alternative to the positions somebody is about to delete.

## When it comes back

ready when the round that removes the positions whose only job is starting a hand opens — this exists so that round finds the alternative rather than only the deletion
