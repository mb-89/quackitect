## Why it stands

Re-entering work that already passed walks straight over it. Nothing is re-answered, nothing signed is re-litigated, and no material is demanded again on the way through. The cause is a mismatch of lifetimes: proof that something was read belongs to the connection, while the signatures belong to the positions themselves, so a fresh connection re-owes reading for positions that are already finished. The owner has raised the cost of this twice, and ruled that a route skipped forward owes no reading along the part it skipped.

## When it comes back

ready when somebody re-enters a record after a restart, which happens most days
