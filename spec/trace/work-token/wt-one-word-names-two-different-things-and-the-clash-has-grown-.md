## Why it stands

One word names two different things and the clash has grown rather than shrunk. It means the set of positions a walk currently holds, and it means a piece of work — both live, in code, in checks and in the binding rules, across 455 uses in 98 files. An older figure of 41 was taken over prose and code together and must never be quoted again. Nothing was renamed. TWO DELIBERATE ACTS WOULD SETTLE IT: reword the loose mentions so the walk's sense is unmistakable, then choose a name for the check that pins that sense and for the design section behind it.

## When it comes back

ready when the next corpus pass runs, or sooner if a reader confuses the walk's own position set with a piece of work
