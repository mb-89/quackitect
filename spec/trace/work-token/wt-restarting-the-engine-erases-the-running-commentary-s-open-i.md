## Why it stands

Restarting the engine erases the running commentary's open items, and the next entry naming one is refused for pointing at something unknown.

That refusal reads exactly like a typing error, sending the author hunting for a mistake nobody made. The item was genuine when written, and only the restart destroyed it.

Two repairs look sound. Carry the commentary across a restart, as the aimed destination already survives one. Or make the refusal say a restart cleared the list, ending the hunt in a single line.

Seen on the very first call after a restart.
