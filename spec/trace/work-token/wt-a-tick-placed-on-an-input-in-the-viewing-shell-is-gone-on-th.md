## Why it stands

A tick placed on an input in the viewing shell is gone on the next visit, and an entry the person had already confirmed shows up unconfirmed again. Ticking is how a person puts a small judgment on the record, so losing it throws that judgment away. Either it was never stored, or it was stored and never read back, and from outside the two look the same. Whichever holds, the control cannot be trusted, which makes every list of them useless for the job they exist to do.
