## Why it stands

Track down whatever edits stored records while the suite runs. Ninety-six of them moved during one run. Every one of those moves is the same: a frontmatter key that held nothing now holds an explicit null. The two spellings mean the same thing to every reader, so no behaviour changed. What did change is whether a comparison can be believed. Anyone reading the history afterwards sees edits attributed to a person who made none. Two suspects: a case round-tripping stored text, and the engine restamping in passing. Prove which, then make the writer leave a file untouched unless its meaning would differ.
