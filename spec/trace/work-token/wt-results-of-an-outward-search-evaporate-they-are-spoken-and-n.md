## Why it stands

Results of an outward search evaporate. They are spoken and never stored, so no later step can quote them, link them or mine them.

Two repairs stack. Make saving the result part of returning it. Then write the standing method governing such a pass: who orders it, where its product is kept, what backing every assertion owes, and how somebody finds it afterwards.

Several rules for that method were already paid for in hard currency. Verify the outward verbs are open where the walk stands before beginning. Require backing per assertion. Flag whatever rests on a digest rather than a fetched page. Make each result state what it disproves. Refuse a progress line where results belong.

Two documents already in this repository covered the topic and went unread.

## When it comes back

ready when any record next orders outward investigation, because the loss happens on the first such pass and cannot be recovered afterwards
