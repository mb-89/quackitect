## Why it stands

Every position on the hand-back dial does something when a person selects it. The two that interrupt least are included, so somebody who wants the machine to run on can ask for exactly that. Reported live by the owner, and part of it may have been repaired since; the first job is to press all four and see.

## When it comes back

ready when somebody next works the mirror's stop-at control, or when the two quiet notches are exercised end to end
