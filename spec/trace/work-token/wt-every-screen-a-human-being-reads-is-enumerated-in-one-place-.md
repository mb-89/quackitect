## Why it stands

Every screen a human being reads is enumerated in one place, findable by a reader and by an automated check alike. Whatever is absent from that roll is not one of them. This comes before any speed work, because nothing unnamed can be put under a stopwatch. Each entry then carries its own timing instrument, so anyone can ask what took how long.
