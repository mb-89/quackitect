## Why it stands

The cage instructions move out of every helper's opening text and into the launch itself. Three helpers were measured at roughly 230 to 300 words each, and the identical four-line cage block took about a fifth of every one. Dropping it outright is not an option, since a helper lacking it will grab at its native verbs and come back empty-handed.
