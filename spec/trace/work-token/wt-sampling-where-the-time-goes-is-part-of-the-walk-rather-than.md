## Why it stands

Sampling where the time goes is part of the walk rather than something somebody remembers to do. Twice an agent has narrowed a sluggish route by inserting printed lines one at a time, when the runtime ships a sampler that names the whole chain in a single run. The owner asked for it on both occasions.

## When it comes back

ready when a slow path is next chased, and the owner has now asked for it twice
