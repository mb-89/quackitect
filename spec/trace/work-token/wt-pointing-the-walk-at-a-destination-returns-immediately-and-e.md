## Why it stands

Pointing the walk at a destination returns immediately, and every bit of computing moves to the moment the agent asks for its next step. An older design bundled the two on purpose, to stop an agent re-deciding hops one at a time, so the new split has to keep solving that. A third word for this act appears in the engine's own answers and in no one's vocabulary; it gets a definition or it goes.

## When it comes back

ready when the walk-speed iteration is seeded
