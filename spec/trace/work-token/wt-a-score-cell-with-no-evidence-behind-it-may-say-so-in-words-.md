## Why it stands

A score cell with no evidence behind it may say so in words, and the ranking maths then treats that cell as absent rather than as a value. Today the column demands a figure, so honest silence gets rounded to nought, and nought reads as worst in class. That can knock out a contender over words nobody ever put on the page.
