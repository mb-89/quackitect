## Why it stands

A parked item whose moment has arrived announces itself, rather than waiting to be remembered. Ninety-two stand in the pool and at least three name a moment already past; one of those names a round that has since started, run and shipped. The mechanism is cheap: where a re-entry line mentions a record, compare it with that record's standing and raise the match while the next round is composed. Three is a floor and not a total, because the search that found them read wording alone.

## The owner ruled on the shape, 2026-08-28

IT IS TWO MECHANISMS, NOT ONE ANNOUNCEMENT.

- THE RETRO DISTRIBUTES backlog items to the records and states that own them.
  It is a step of the method rather than something an agent remembers.
- THE KICKOFF PULLS. Once a record's substeps are created, everything assigned
  to that record comes into it.

NOT AT SEEDING. Seeding is the creation of M0 and nobody can wait on it,
because nobody knows in advance whether an iteration will be seeded at all.
The full correction is on
[[wt-sixteen-parked-items-name-one-record-as-the-moment-they-shou]].

THE ANNOUNCEMENT NOW EXISTS AND IS THE SMALLER HALF. `se_survey` returns a
`passed_moments` block naming every backlog item whose condition points at a
record that has shipped or been abandoned. That tells a reader; it still does
not move anything by itself.
