## Why it stands

Make the absence of a second pair of eyes visible. A round can be walked from end to end by one hand alone and leave no trace that this happened. The freshly measured case: a whole round, a thousand logged calls, and not a single helper announced. The checking position took eighty-eight of those calls, and its own written instructions call for eyes that have not seen the work before. Afterwards the archive reads as though everything was intended that way, because no field anywhere asks the question. The repair is to let a position declare the help it expects, so finishing with none is an answer somebody chose rather than a silence. A neighbouring item complains that one checkpoint advertises a reviewer it never demands; that is about the wording of one page, while this is about the record having nowhere to say what happened.
