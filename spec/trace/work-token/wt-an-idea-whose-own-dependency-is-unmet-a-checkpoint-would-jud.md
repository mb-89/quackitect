## Why it stands

An idea whose own dependency is unmet. A checkpoint would judge every piece of work finished in its phase against what actually changed in the tree, rather than against a form somebody filled in. Nothing links a piece of work to a change today — the item carries no diff, no review and no delta field — and reading changes between two points is a capability another record owns. It belongs inside that record rather than standing alone.
