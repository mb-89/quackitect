## Why it stands

The largest of three linked ideas: retire the programs that run when a position is left, and turn what each of them checked into work the position owes on entry. It is load-bearing today across the compiler, the session, the matrix and fourteen matrix rows, so nothing about this is small. The hardest case is boot, whose five checks run before any record is bound — work needs somewhere to stand, and at boot there is nowhere.

## When it comes back

ready when the check-per-token pair is decided and after the whole-system sweep has run — this is the largest of the three linked ideas and rests on both
