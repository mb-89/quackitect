## Why it stands

The engine keeps hold of everything it launches and asks each one, on a fixed interval, whether it is still there. Anything that stops answering gets ended. Inspecting a stored record can only ever guess about something that has gone quiet; asking the thing itself does not guess. The check at start-up stays as a backstop for what a dead engine left behind.
