## Why it stands

Standing at a machine's final position, with a document still owed for the place beyond it, the walking verb blocks and points at itself as the way through. Following that advice returns the identical block, forever. The document loop never starts, because at a closing position the verb hands back work rather than a page to take in. Opening the file by hand does credit it and does break the circle, but that route is mentioned only as an aside. Either the verb should serve the page there the way it does everywhere else, or its advice should name the direct read together with the exact location. Two calls were thrown away on 2026-08-25 before the advice was distrusted.
