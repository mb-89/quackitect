## Why it stands

The rule that every must-level story owes a worked showing of itself can be checked by a machine, and nothing checks it until the last milestone. The function that answers it already exists and runs at two positions: once with the must half switched off, and once at the final approval with it on. So the first anybody hears of a missing one is at the end, where fixing it is dear. The position that authors the checks covers demands in both directions and never looks at stories. ONE ROUTING LINE MOVES IT EARLY.
