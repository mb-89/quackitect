## Why it stands

Some tool in our chain strips YAML comment blocks out of the rigor matrix rows, silently, leaving the tree dirty.

WHAT WAS LOST. Three blocks went. Two of them recorded why two particular positions may re-place the prompt layer, a lesson bought painfully in an earlier round. The third was a quoting change on a statement.

NOTHING BROKE, and that is the danger. The grants themselves survived, so the checks stayed green. A later reader would simply have found two positions handing out an unexplained permission, with no way to recover the argument behind it.

A TOOL THAT ERASES COMMENTS ERASES REASONS. Find which one does it before it runs again.

The blocks were restored by hand. Nothing stops the next run removing them.
