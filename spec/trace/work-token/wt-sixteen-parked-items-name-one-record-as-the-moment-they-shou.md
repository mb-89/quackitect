## Why it stands

Sixteen parked items name one record as the moment they should come back. That record was opened, walked and finished on 2026-08-24, and not one of them was collected. Each now needs judging afresh: taken into something, or given a condition that can still come true. The deeper half is that a condition naming a record has to fire when the record is SEEDED, instead of waiting for somebody to read the pool at exactly the right hour.

## The owner corrected the deeper half, 2026-08-28

THE SENTENCE ABOVE IS WRONG WHERE IT SAYS "SEEDED". Seeding is the creation of
M0 and nothing more. Nobody can wait on it, because nobody knows in advance
whether an iteration will ever be seeded at all.

THE SUBSTEPS ARE SEEDED AT THE KICKOFF. That is the moment a record actually
acquires the steps that could carry work, so that is where the hook belongs.

SO IT IS TWO MECHANISMS RATHER THAN ONE, and they meet in the middle.

- THE RETRO DISTRIBUTES. It reads the backlog and writes an owner onto each
  item that has one. Done by hand for 352 items on 2026-08-28; the method has
  to carry it so the next retro does it unasked.
- THE KICKOFF PULLS. Once a record's substeps are created, everything in the
  backlog assigned to that record is pulled into it.

WITHOUT THE SECOND HALF THE FIRST IS DECORATION. A place written on an item is
just a word until something reads it at the moment the record opens.

## The sixteen are placed, and the mechanism is not built

All sixteen were rerouted on 2026-08-28 and each now names a live owner. That
settles this item's first half by hand. The mechanism above is what stops the
same sixteen accumulating again, and it is not built.
