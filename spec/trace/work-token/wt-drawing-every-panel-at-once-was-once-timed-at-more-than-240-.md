## Why it stands

Drawing every panel at once was once timed at more than 240 seconds here. The surface now serves a single panel per request and keeps a timing record for two of them, which narrows the cost without ever measuring the whole. No panel is cached. THE MEASUREMENT IS THE HARD PART: the draw has to run somewhere other than the server's own loop, or taking the figure freezes every other surface for exactly as long as the figure says.
