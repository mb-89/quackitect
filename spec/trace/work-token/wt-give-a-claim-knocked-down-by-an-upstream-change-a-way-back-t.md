## Why it stands

Give a claim knocked down by an upstream change a way back that the walk can see. When something a state depended on moves, that state's signed claim stops standing. The walk then offers neither the filled form to read nor a way to submit it, so the position looks finished and behaves as though it is not. The way out exists and is a separate verb nobody is pointed at. Either the pull should hand the form straight back with what changed, or the block should name the verb in its own remedy.

## When it comes back

ready when a round takes revalidation or the pull's shaping
