## Why it stands

The chart a step fills draws every question and every line held anywhere in the repository, not only what belongs to the open iteration. Counted once at twenty-six rows where nine belonged. That defeats the instruction saying each line speaks to every row, since a line has nothing to say about rows from elsewhere. The owner called it a fault, and separately asked for a whole-repository view of its own, so narrowing the one must not delete the other.

## When it comes back

ready when the narrowed chart and a standing whole-repository view are agreed as two separate things
