## Deck

A method card tells me to do seven things. It is prose, and prose does not
refuse. I can do five of them, write the evidence, and leave. Nothing
objects, and nobody finds out until much later, if ever.
|||
THIS FAILED EXACTLY THIS WAY EARLIER TODAY, which is why the round exists.
Four items in scope were settled without being built, the owner caught it from
outside the machine, and their words were: "if you close an item like this,
then I think it's done and it's not done. I have no visibility on that."

---

I have just entered a state. Its method card carries seven marked steps.
Two documents are demanded before it can be worked, and I have read one of
them in this session already.
|||
OBSERVED, at more than seven. Entering `gate-implementation` handed over ELEVEN
marked steps, each minted from a heading on the gate's own card.

---

The state hands me what it owes. One work token for the document I have not
read, and none for the one I have — that proof is already on file, so
nothing asks me twice.
|||
THE CREDIT WORKS AND THE ASYMMETRY WAS NOT WATCHED. After a reload, twelve
documents were owed and each was served once. Once a document's three probes
were answered it was never asked again, including across a later route that
demanded the same card.

WHAT WAS NOT OBSERVED is a single state minting one incoming token while
skipping a second because that one was already proven. The credit was watched
suppressing the ASK; it was not watched suppressing the MINT.

---

Seven more arrive, one per marked step of the method, each carrying that
step's own guidance in its body and what it owes as evidence beneath it.
|||
OBSERVED. 329 marks stand across 76 of 87 method cards, every one on a line the
compiler accepts, and no marked part swallows another. Each token minted from a
mark carries that step's own guidance in its body.

---

Three more arrive for the results this state must produce. What used to be
fields on a form I filled at the end are now work tokens I am handed at the
start, and the state counts them the same way it counts everything else.
|||
OBSERVED. `run-demos/carry-a-finding-without-stopping` handed over `Fill built`
as a token at entry, and `sweep-consistency` handed over `Fill swept`. Both are
fields on a form and both arrived as work, counted like everything else.

---

I work them. As each is settled its evidence goes INTO it. There is no
second act of writing the state's evidence somewhere else, because the
finished work tokens are the evidence.
|||
PARTLY OBSERVED, AND THE SECOND ACT STILL HAPPENS. Every token settled tonight
carried its evidence in the settle comment. But the state's evidence FORM is
still filled separately, so the field and the token both get written.

THAT IS THE GAP THIS SLIDE NAMES and this round did not close. The tokens are
the narration; the form is still the claim.

---

I reach the sixth and I think the seventh does not apply here. I cannot
simply not do it. I close it as skipped and write why in one line, because
every close carries a reason.
|||
OBSERVED, in its own form. One token tonight was settled `carried` rather than
`done`, with the reason on the item: the sweep budget, routed to the retro on
the owner's word. SE-C-149 refuses any close that is not `done` and carries no
reason, so the reason is mechanical rather than a habit.

---

One of the seven is not mine to close. It carries a flag saying the work
needs a person, so I stop there. I consult no list of acceptable reasons to
end a turn, because the reason is a fact written on the work.
|||
BUILT AND NOT EXERCISED TONIGHT. `person_only` stands on every token's face and
SE-C-150 refuses an agent settling one, with no override argument. No
person-only token stood in this record's path, so the stop it produces was not
observed here.

---

The person answers and that work token settles. I did not have to judge
whether stopping was allowed, and they did not have to guess why I stopped.
|||
NOT OBSERVED, for the same reason as the slide above: no person-only token
stood in the way. What WAS observed is the neighbouring case — the owner
answered in chat five times and each answer became a token settled in the same
state, so nobody had to guess what the answer was for.

---

I try to leave with one still open. The state will not let me. It is not a
reminder and not a warning — the state cannot be left while anything in it
is unsettled and unmoved.
|||
OBSERVED, REPEATEDLY, AND WIDENED TONIGHT. The hold was earned four separate
times: a submit refused over one unchecked box, a state refused to serve on a
placeholder drawing, a join refused while a branch was owed, and this gate's
own eleven steps held it shut.

AND IT NOW HOLDS A WHOLE RECORD. A gate refuses its thumb while any state
inside the record still holds open work, and emergency does not lift that one —
which is the hole the owner walked through this morning.

---

I left the state having done every step or said out loud why not. The
method stopped being advice and became the shape of the work, and the
failure that opened this round cannot happen the same way again.
|||
TRUE FOR THIS WALK, AND THE PROOF IS THAT THE SAME FAILURE WAS CAUGHT TWICE
MORE. Both times the machine refused rather than a person noticing: once at the
unchecked box, once at the placeholder drawing.

WHAT IS NOT PROVEN is that it cannot happen in a DIFFERENT way. The gate now
holds over open work; it does not hold over work nobody opened. A step that
never becomes a token is still invisible.
