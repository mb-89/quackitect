## What it does

It is a file, and its reader is the second half of this element.

ONE ENTRY PER DEPARTURE. Each names the module and carries a reason written by
whoever needed the departure.

THE READER REFUSES AN EMPTY REASON. That is a change from what stands: the
current reader IGNORES a bullet with no reason, which its own documentation
states at
[deliverable/machines/widget-exemptions.md](deliverable/machines/widget-exemptions.md)
line 30.

## Why it is central rather than at the site

Both shapes were on the chart and the central one won on one axis: coverage.

A SCATTERED LIST TURNS ONE ANSWER INTO TWO PASSES. Gathering what the departures
are, then judging them. The winner answers completeness from one place, which is
what [[req-sweep-covers-every-drift-class]] asks for.

WHAT IT PAYS is locality. The reason sits away from the code it excuses, and
somebody moving a module has to remember the list exists. The sweep is what
notices when they do not.

## Why the reason is prose and not a citation

A DEPARTURE CITING A DECISION NODE WAS DRAWN AND MEASURED. It is stricter, it
was ruled that way in an earlier version of this product, and it lost on three
axes to two.

The measurement and its reasons are on
[[raid-dec-a-departure-carries-a-written-reason-that-cannot-be-left-empty]]. The
short version: there are no decision documents in this tree to cite, so on day
one a legitimate departure could not be recorded at all.

## What crosses its boundary

- From [[el-door-write-guard]] — an appended entry, through the refusal's
  remedy.
- To [[el-door-rule]] — the recorded departures, read as a set.

## What nothing here buys

A REASON WORTH READING. No machine can check that, and no candidate in this
record claimed one could.

What this element buys is that the easiest path stops producing a non-answer.
[[raid-asm-an-author-refused-at-write-time-states-a-usable-reason]] is the named
kill criterion and its sample is one line; this is what makes a larger sample
possible.
