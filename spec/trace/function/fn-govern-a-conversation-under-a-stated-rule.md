## Rationale

This is the overall function, and its unit of governance is a CONVERSATION
rather than a technology.

That wording is the carving finding made structural. Cockburn's hexagonal
architecture paper (HaT Technical Report 2005.02) states it plainly: "A port
identifies a purposeful conversation." His worked failure is a weather system
whose four interfaces were "identified and discussed by technology"; the fix
was "to architect the system's interfaces by purpose rather than by
technology".

The falsifier run over 64 disk sites landed on the same answer from the other
side. Thirty of those 64 share one shape — read, change, write back a claim or
a form — so the object that pays is a claim writer and not a disk facade.

The root therefore says "a conversation", not "the disk". Everything beneath
it is one step of holding that conversation to its rule.
