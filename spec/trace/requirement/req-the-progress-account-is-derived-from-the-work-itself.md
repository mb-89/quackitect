## Detail

THE TWO STRUCTURES BECOME ONE. The open and closed records the work already
carries produce the graph the update ops build by hand today.

THE BEFORE SIDE IS MEASURED AND IT IS THIS ROUND'S TO COLLECT. Sixteen per
cent of calls, and 59 consecutive refusals on the worst measured walk. If
neither figure moves, the design did not do what the actual says it should.

THE GUARDS GO WITH THE MACHINERY. A guard that counts narration refuses for
a reason that is not about the work, which is exactly what the 59 refusals
were.

WHAT REPLACES THEM. A guard reading real pieces of work refuses for real
reasons or not at all.
