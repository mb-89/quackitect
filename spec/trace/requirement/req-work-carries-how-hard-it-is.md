## Detail

THE MARK ITSELF IS ALREADY REQUIRED, at matrix-row grain, by
req-every-matrix-row-declares-its-complexity and
req-a-milestone-takes-the-maximum-complexity-over-its-rows. What is new here
is two things: the grain becomes the piece of work, and the mark FILTERS the
offer rather than only publishing a named driver.

IT IS A ROUTING KEY AND NOT AN ESTIMATE. Owner ruling at the kickoff gate.
The mark decides which hand gets the work; it says nothing about how long the
work takes.

TWO LADDERS SURVIVE AND TWO GO. Complexity and autonomy remain. Reading and
the five-name rung are removed entirely.

ONE AXIS NOW CARRIES WHAT TWO CARRIED BEFORE, and whether it can is an open
assumption named at the kickoff gate. This row states the demand; the
mapping is the design's to write.

NO SURVEYED SYSTEM COVERS THIS PURPOSE. Human teams never had a reason to
route work by which worker could understand it.
