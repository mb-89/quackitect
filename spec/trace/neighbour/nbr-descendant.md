## Why this neighbour is new

THE PRODUCT HAS NEVER HAD CHILDREN IT KEPT TRACK OF. `RUNME.ps1 --export` at
lines 57-155 already produces a copy today, but it produces a FORK: a fresh
repository with one commit and no relationship afterwards.

SO THE NEIGHBOUR EXISTS AND THE RELATIONSHIP DOES NOT. That is the whole
subject of the iteration that minted this node.

## Interface

OUTWARD, AT TWO MOMENTS ONLY, and both are acts somebody performs.

AT SPAWN: the whole product leaves, renamed. The name is ONE FACT - RUNME.ps1
lines 28-33 record that `brand.json` at the root holds it and nothing below
spells it out - so a descendant renames itself by writing one file.

AT UPDATE: whatever the parent changed since, offered to a descendant that
asks. From the parent's side this is publishing rather than pushing. It cannot
reach a descendant to deliver anything, and does not know one exists.

## What the parent knows about a descendant

NOTHING, BY CONSTRUCTION. There is no registry, no callback, no telemetry and
no list. A descendant on a colleague's machine is invisible here.

AND THAT IS A REQUIREMENT RATHER THAN A HAPPY FACT, which is a correction this
iteration's own pressure-test surfaced. "We could not find you if we tried" is
only true while nothing ever tries. The clause is owed at write-requirements.

THE AUDIENCE ASKED FOR IT FIRST. [[stk-vehicle-owner]]'s concerns already state
it in both directions: what the engine writes must never land inside their
tree, and what they write must never land inside the engine's.

## What this neighbour is not

NOT A PEER MACHINE. [[nbr-peer-machine]] is another machine running this same
product with no lineage. A descendant carries lineage and a different name.

NOT A USER. [[stk-vehicle-owner]] is the person who holds a descendant. The
person is a stakeholder and the copy is a neighbour, and conflating them is how
a boundary picks up intentions it cannot have.

NOT THE MIRROR OF [[nbr-parent-engine]]. The two nodes describe the same
relationship from opposite ends, and the ends are NOT symmetric: the parent can
be reached for an update and the descendant can never be reached at all.
