## Interface

What crosses it, and nothing else: seed stubs (an iteration exists),
claim files (an iteration is taken, by machine id and time), and the
owner's own pushes of landed work.

The engine pushes exactly two things as machinery acts - the seed stub
and the claim - under the surgical relaxation of the never-push rule
(owner ruling 2026-08-11). Work never pushes itself.

Offline, the remote is simply absent: a claim is taken locally and
reconciles at the next push, desync knowingly accepted.
