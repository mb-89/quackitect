## Why this one

It is the only curve that attacks polling at its root rather than making
polling cheaper.

Every other candidate assumes the caller asks. This one removes the reason to
ask: a caller working steadily learns the work finished inside an answer it
was getting anyway.

## How it works

THE OTHER CELL ON EVERY ROW BUT ONE. This is the line drawn to make the
comparison possible, and it shares almost nothing with the other three.

- THE HANDBACK is the predecessor's acknowledge-then-report shape rather than
  the separation in time.
- THE STANDING was the standard's state word. IT IS NOW THE CLOSED SET OF
  THREE, grafted at M5.
- THE ESTIMATE was a duration computed at the ask with no basis beside it. IT
  NOW CARRIES ITS BASIS, grafted at M5, and the late computation is kept inside
  that cell.
- THE TELLING is the rider, and the uniform listing beside it, grafted at M5.

THE HANDBACK IS THE ONLY ROW STILL WHOLLY ITS OWN, and it is the row this
candidate was drawn to differ on.

THIS PROSE ONCE READ "the measured answer, plus a rider", which was wrong on
all four rows. Corrected at i51's evaluate-set, where the scoring agent found
the picks and the prose disagreeing. The picks were and remain authoritative,
and they are what was scored.

The rider itself: Every lane answer carries a small note when
something is running or something changed, and carries nothing when there is
nothing to say.

The verb that asks still exists, for a caller that wants to know now.

## The seams

THE RIDER MEETS THE ANSWER IT RIDES ON, and that seam is the only one that
matters here. Every other cell on this line inherits its seams from candidates
already composed.

THREE RULES HOLD THE RIDER IN PLACE, and each exists because of a way this
candidate fails without it.

- IT APPEARS ONLY WHEN THERE IS SOMETHING TO SAY. An answer that carries
  "nothing out of sight" on every call is a field nobody reads, and a field
  nobody reads is a field nobody notices changing.
- IT CARRIES A COUNT AND A CHANGE, NEVER THE ACCOUNT. What is running, and what
  finished since the caller last heard. The account itself stays behind the
  verb that asks for it.
- IT NEVER GROWS WITH THE WORK. Three jobs and thirty produce the same rider
  size. A rider proportional to the work is the answer-size problem coming back
  through a door nobody was watching.

THE PRODUCT'S OWN HISTORY IS THE ARGUMENT FOR THE THIRD RULE. Big results were
moved to disk and are paged back by reference precisely because answers grew.

WITH THE STANDING WORD. THIS ARGUMENT IS SUPERSEDED and is kept because a
superseded argument is evidence about how the decision moved.

IT ONCE READ: this is the one line taking the standard's state word rather than
the closed set, and the seam is a real difference, because a state word admits
new values later without every reader changing.

THAT CUT BOTH WAYS and the note said so: admitting new values later is what
lets a reader silently ignore one, which is the failure the closed set makes
loud.

THE GRAFT RULED ON IT AT M5 and took the closed set. The re-scoring agent
confirmed that this cell was the whole reason the fatal-graded axis sat at 2,
and the score moved to 3 when it changed.

WITH THE ACKNOWLEDGE-THEN-REPORT SHAPE. The predecessor's once-a-minute
progress bound and the rider are the same promise from two directions: the
caller learns something without asking. This is the only line that takes both,
and a design taking one without the other has half a mechanism.

## What it costs

EVERY ANSWER GROWS, and this product already fights answer size. It moved big
results to disk and pages them back by reference for exactly that reason.

SO THE RIDER MUST STAY TINY, and nothing enforces that. A rider that grows is
the problem the reference mechanism was built to solve, coming back through a
door nobody was watching.

## What it leans on

Everything the measured answer leans on, plus a discipline rather than a
mechanism.

THE SIZE CONSTRAINT IS THE WEAK POINT. It is a rule about how much a rider may
carry, and this product's own history says rules about answer size are the
ones that get broken.

## What it buys that nothing else does

The vision asked for this in one sentence and no option node existed for it
until the transformation finder reached it.

It is also the only candidate that catches the guided caller's failure: work
that finished while the caller was waiting out a next-ask time it was given.
