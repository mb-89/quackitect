## What it leans on

THE MEASURED HEADER FAILURE. A change landed in one surface and was invisible
in the other because each built its header from different parts.

THE ENGINE ALREADY COMPUTING MOST OF THE ANSWER. The pull resolves position,
greyness and conditions today, so the view model is an extension of work that
exists rather than a new layer.

THE ASSUMPTION THAT AN EDITOR VIEW CAN BE PASSIVE ENOUGH. A host that demands
its own layout decisions leaves a seam this candidate promises to close.

## Why this one

IT ATTACKS THE CAUSE RATHER THAN THE COUNT. Two surfaces were only dangerous
because each derived its own answers. A surface that derives nothing cannot
disagree with anything.

THE MEASURED FAILURE FITS IT EXACTLY. A header change landed in one surface
and was invisible in the other, because each built its header from different
parts. A repeater has no parts to build a header from.

## How it works

THE ENGINE RESOLVES THE VIEW, not the data behind it. Which control is grey,
why, what the label reads, which row is highlighted — all computed once,
server side.

THE SURFACE DRAWS AND ROUTES INPUT, and holds no position of its own.

## The seams

THE SEAM IS THE VIEW MODEL, and it is the whole candidate. Everything above it
is engine and everything below it draws.

WHAT CROSSES IT IS RESOLVED, NEVER RAW. A colour, not a condition. A label,
not an id to look up. The moment the surface has to derive one field from
another, the seam has leaked.

INPUT CROSSES THE OTHER WAY AS AN INTENT, not as a state change. The surface
says which control was pressed; the engine decides what that means.

THE RISK LIVES AT THE EDGES. A widget whose needs the model does not carry
will be tempted to reach around it, and one such reach turns the repeater back
into a deriver.

## What it costs

THE VIEW MODEL BECOMES AN INTERFACE, and every widget's needs must be
expressible in it. A widget that needs something the model does not carry
cannot quietly reach around it, which is the point and also the pain.

IT MOVES PRESENTATION DECISIONS INTO THE ENGINE, which does not own them
today.

## What it does not do

IT DOES NOT PREVENT A SECOND SURFACE from being written. It makes one
harmless instead of impossible, which is a different bet.
