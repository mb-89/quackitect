## Why this one

It is the smallest curve through the box that still ends the measured failure.
Nothing it picks can produce a wrong answer, because it makes no claim about
the future.

## How it works

A walk attempt starts the leaving judgment and answers at once. The step's
standing becomes one word from a closed set of three, and every reader learns
that third word.

One listing answers for every piece of work out of sight, whatever started it.
Each entry says what kind of work it is and whether it is still running.

No entry carries a duration.

## The seams

TWO CHOSEN CELLS MEET, AND THE JOIN IS THE WHOLE ARCHITECTURE.

THE DEFERRED VERDICT PRODUCES THE THIRD STANDING VALUE. It is not that the two
were chosen to fit; the separation in time CREATES a moment where a step is
neither passed nor failed, and the closed word is the only thing that can hold
it. Pick the first cell and the second is forced.

THE LISTING DOES NOT HOLD STEPS, and that seam is the one a reader gets wrong.
A step with a leaving judgment running is not an entry in the work listing. The
judgment's PROCESS may be, if it was spawned; the step is not.

SO TWO THINGS ARE TRACKED IN TWO PLACES ON PURPOSE. The step's standing lives
on the step. The work out of sight lives in the listing. A design that merges
them has made every state a job, which is a different product.

WHAT CROSSES BETWEEN THEM is one direction only: starting the judgment may
create a piece of work in the listing. Nothing in the listing ever changes a
step's standing, because the standing is settled by the verdict rather than by
the process.

## What it costs

THE CALLER STILL GUESSES ITS INTERVAL. It asks, sees `running`, and picks a
number out of the air. That is polling with fewer calls rather than no
polling.

The work token that raised this named the estimate as half of what it wanted,
so this ships a partial answer and would have to say so.

## What it leans on

The deferred verdict, which is unprobed for the leaving-check path and probed
for the nearest analogue. The closed word, which costs every existing reader a
change.

It leans on nothing about measurement at all, and that is the whole point of
it.
