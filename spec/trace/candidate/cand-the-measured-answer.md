## Why this one

Every cell it picks was either probed on this machine or is a rule the product
already applies somewhere else.

It answers the whole work token rather than half of it, and the part that
looked expensive turned out to be a division.

## How it works

The walk half is the quiet handback, unchanged.

On each ask, the report computes elapsed time against the fraction of the
work's own countable unit that is finished. The figure and what it was computed
from are two fields, and an entry with no basis reports no figure.

The figure may go up between two asks, and the design says so rather than
hiding it behind a counter that only falls.

## The seams

THE BASIS FIELD IS WHERE THIS CANDIDATE'S WHOLE WEIGHT SITS, and three seams
meet on it.

WITH THE ESTIMATE. The two fields must move together or the design has kept
the precision and lost the point. A basis field that goes stale while the
number updates is worse than no basis at all, because a reader checks it once
and then stops checking.

SO THE COMPUTATION PRODUCES BOTH OR NEITHER. They are one return value, not
two fields somebody remembers to fill.

WITH THE LISTING. Every entry has the pair, including entries that cannot
estimate. A shell command's entry carries an empty duration and a basis saying
why, rather than being a different shape from a battery's entry.

THAT UNIFORMITY IS WHAT THE LISTING CELL BUYS. One shape for every kind of
work means the no-estimate case is an ordinary value rather than a special
case, and a caller writes one piece of handling.

WITH THE HANDBACK. Nothing crosses. The leaving judgment's own progress is not
reported as a duration on the step, because the step is not an entry in the
listing. This candidate inherits that seam from the quiet handback unchanged.

## What it costs

FOUR OPTIONS WHERE THE QUIET HANDBACK PICKS ONE, on the same row. That is more
to build and more to get wrong.

AND IT CAN BE WRONG IN A WAY THE QUIET HANDBACK CANNOT. A figure is a claim
about the future. The measurement says it errs long and converges, which is
the safe direction, and it is still a claim.

## What it leans on

ONE MEASUREMENT, ON ONE MACHINE, ON ONE KIND OF WORK. The linear model was
replayed against this session's own battery: 1.44 at a tenth through, 1.11 at
halfway, 1.01 at three quarters, over-predicting throughout.

It does NOT lean on the timings the register calls unreliable. Those measure
summed case durations including waiting; this counts files against a wall
clock, and they are different instruments.

It leans on a shell command having nothing to count, which was observed rather
than assumed.
