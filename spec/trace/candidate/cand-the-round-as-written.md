## Why this one

IT IS THE KICKOFF'S OWN DESIGN, and a comparison that omits the thing being
proposed says nothing. Everything else on the chart has to beat it or explain
why it does not.

WHAT IT TRADES AWAY is measured rather than argued: 22,080 to 27,738
work token files across the records already on trunk, against 1,821 trace
nodes standing today.

## How it works

ENTERING A POSITION IS WHERE EVERYTHING HAPPENS. The method's marked steps
are read, the reading already proven is subtracted, and one file is written
for each work token that remains. The position holds the list. Leaving is
refused while any of them is open.

THE SEAM THAT MATTERS IS THE ENTRY. Every other act in the cluster reads what
the entry wrote, so the entry is both the hot path and the single point where
a wrong derivation becomes permanent. Nothing downstream can repair it.

WHAT STAYS UNCHANGED IS MOST OF THE SYSTEM. The walk, the record's life, the
account and the surface are untouched except that they now read work tokens
where they used to read form fields.

THE SECOND ENTRY IS THE HARD HALF. An work token carries an identity stamped
when it was made, and a reworded method card is matched against those
identities rather than against its own headings.

## What it costs

THE FILE COUNT IS MEASURED AND IT IS THE HEADLINE. 320 to 402 files per
record, and 22,080 to 27,738 across the 69 records already on trunk, against
1,821 trace nodes standing today. The corpus grows twelve to fifteen times
over, and every one of those files is a merge surface.

THE PER-HOP COST IS NOT KNOWN, and that is an honest not-known rather than a
blank. Minting sits on the hot path of every entry into every position, and
no measurement exists.

MAKE, REUSE OR BUY. All make. Nothing existing writes one file per derived
work token, and the two shipped systems that came closest both refused to.

THE FAILURE MODE THAT DECIDES. A second entry that matches wrongly either
orphans finished work or mints a duplicate beside it, and both are silent.

## What it leans on

THAT ONE MARKED HEADING BECOMES EXACTLY ONE WORK TOKEN. Untested, and the
probe for it has not run.

THAT MINTING FITS THE PER-HOP BUDGET. Unmeasured.

THAT A FOLDER OF TENS OF THOUSANDS OF FILES STAYS WORKABLE. Measured, and the
measurement says no.
