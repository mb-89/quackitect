## Why this one

IT IS THE LINE THE OUTSIDE WORLD ACTUALLY RUNS, taken whole. Debian will not
build a package whose source differs from what was declared. Aviation applies a
directive to a modified aircraft anyway and demands a described alternative back.
European standards ship the list of who departed as a chapter of the standard
itself. None of that is theory and all of it is decades old.

WHAT IT TRADES AWAY IS COMFORT. The copy's owner stops being somebody who edits
their own product and becomes somebody maintaining a declared set of changes
against a base, which is a different and less pleasant job. Every source in the
survey that demands declaration also demands it BEFORE the edit, not after.

AND IT NEEDS SOMETHING SOFTWARE USUALLY LACKS. Every mechanism here works
because a body with standing can reject a filing. Without one, "file your
reason" degrades into a required text field, and the whole line becomes
ceremony.

## How it works

THE COPY NEVER EDITS WHAT IT RECEIVED. Every change it makes exists only as a
named, described, separately stored patch, and the set of those patches is the
inventory of what the copy made its own. Nobody computes it, because the changes
have no other representation.

EACH PATCH CARRIES A REASON FROM A FIXED VOCABULARY, and the change and the
reason point at each other so neither can be found without the other. A patch
with no reason is not a patch the machine will apply.

WHEN UPSTREAM MOVES, A CLOCK STARTS. Every departure touching what changed gets
a deadline with exactly two legal answers: I have conformed, or here is my
declared difference and what I intend to do about it. Silence is a fault, not a
default, which is the inversion the whole line turns on.

AND HAVING CHANGED SOMETHING DOES NOT EXCUSE THE COPY FROM AN UPSTREAM FIX.
Where the copy's change blocks the given instructions, it owes back a described
alternative reaching the same end — unless it can show its change already
removed the problem, which releases it.

RESOLUTION KEYS ON IDENTITY rather than on path, and an override replaces a
named part of an artifact while being able to invoke the version it replaced, so
upstream's changes to the untouched parts still arrive. The driven tree records
WHICH copy drives it and at what version, never where that copy sits.

THE SEAM THAT HOLDS IT TOGETHER is that every one of those mechanisms produces
or consumes the same thing: a declared departure with a reason and a version.
Remove the declaration and the clock has nothing to fire on, the proposal duty
has nothing to name, and the identity match has nothing to report against.

WHAT STAYS is the lane, the walk and the trace corpus.

THE EXECUTABLE STILL FINDS ITS OWN METHOD, AND THAT IS NOT THIS LINE'S ANSWER
TO THE DRIVEN TREE. The two are different questions and this node used to run
them together, which read as picking self-location when the picks say otherwise.

- RUNNING INSIDE ITS OWN COPY, the system finds its method by walking up from
  its executable, exactly as the predecessor does.
- DRIVING A TREE THAT CARRIES NO METHOD, the tree records WHICH copy drives it
  and at what version. That is `opt-the-tree-names-what-not-where`, and it is
  this line's pick on that row.

### And a tree with no record is not a driven project

A TREE CARRYING NO SUCH RECORD IS NOT A DRIVEN PROJECT. The system refuses to
come up in it, and names the record it looked for and did not find.

THERE ARE THREE STATES, NOT TWO, and the middle one is the easy one to miss.

- NO RECORD. Not a driven project. Refuse, and say what was looked for.
- A RECORD THAT RESOLVES TO NOTHING, on a machine that has never seen the named
  copy. Refuse, naming the identity rather than the absence.
- A RECORD THAT RESOLVES. Come up.

## What it costs

THE PRICE IS PAID BY THE COPY'S OWNER, AT EVERY EDIT. They stop being somebody
who edits their own product and become somebody maintaining a declared set of
changes against a base. Every source in the survey that demands declaration
demands it BEFORE the edit, not after.

BUILD IS LARGE BUT UNUSUALLY WELL SPECIFIED. Every element has a shipped
reference implementation to read: a patch-series format, a tagging vocabulary
accepted as a specification in 2012, a machine check that refuses an artifact
with no stated reason, and a notification protocol with a deadline and a defined
answer set. Nothing here has to be invented, which is rare on this chart.

THE WORST CASE THAT DECIDES VIABILITY is that there is no body with standing to
reject a filing. Every mechanism borrowed here works because a regulator, a
commission or a technical board can say no. Strip that out and "file your reason"
becomes a required text field, filled with "needed for our use case" within a
month.

THE FAILURE MODE THAT DECIDES is therefore not technical. It is that the
vocabulary decays into one catch-all category and the whole apparatus becomes
ceremony while continuing to pass its own checks.

AND ONE COST IS PAID EVERY TIME UPSTREAM MOVES, whether or not the change
matters. A deadline fires on every departure touching a changed region, so noise
rises with the source's pace rather than with the significance of what changed.

MAKE, REUSE OR BUY: make, but from published designs rather than from nothing.

## What it leans on

THAT THE MACHINE ITSELF CAN BE THE AUTHORITY. Without a person who may reject,
the only available rejecter is the engine, refusing on rules it can check — the
category is not in the set, the pointer does not resolve, the patch does not
apply cleanly. That is much smaller than what the source domains have, and
whether it is enough is untested.

THAT A CLOSED VOCABULARY OF REASONS CAN BE WRITTEN FOR METHOD ARTIFACTS AT ALL.
Building regulation permits three categories, and they say nothing about this
domain. What the categories are here is an unmade decision, and the option that
proposes the mechanism deliberately does not make it.

AND THAT THE COPY'S OWNER ACCEPTS THE WORKING HABIT. This is the assumption most
likely to fail in practice and least likely to be caught by any check, because a
person who finds the discipline intolerable does not report it — they route
around it, and the inventory silently stops being true.
