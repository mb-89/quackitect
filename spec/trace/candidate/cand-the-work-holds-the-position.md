## Why this one

IT IS THE ONLY LINE THAT CHANGES THE SHAPE OF THE RELATION rather than the
shape of the storage. Every other candidate argues about where a piece of work
is kept; this one argues about which end holds the list.

WHAT IT BUYS THAT NOTHING ELSE DOES. One piece of work can hold two positions
open, which a list cannot express at all. Placing work needs no cooperation
from the destination. Rewording a method card reaches every open work token
at once, because nothing was copied.

WHAT IT TRADES AWAY. Nothing can be answered by reading one position, so
every question is a query over everything. It also rewrites the round's exit
rule rather than narrowing it, because blocking becomes a property.

## How it works

NO POSITION KEEPS A LIST. Each piece of work points at the positions it holds
open, at the guidance it asks for, and at whatever it waits on. A position is
leavable when nothing blocking points at it.

BLOCKING IS A PROPERTY RATHER THAN THE DEFINITION. Most work reports and a
few pieces refuse the exit, which makes the harmless finding an ordinary case
rather than an exception the system carries specially.

THE GUIDANCE IS A POINTER, NEVER A COPY. Rewording a method card updates what
every open work token asks for, everywhere, at once, and the re-entry problem
disappears because nothing was copied to reconcile.

READINESS COSTS PER DECLARATION. Everything open is offered unless it named
something it waits on.

THE SEAM THAT MATTERS IS THE QUERY. Nothing can be answered by reading one
position any more, so the whole candidate rests on asking a question of
everything cheaply. That is the same seam the trace graph already crosses,
where a requirement names its use case and the use case keeps no list.

## What it costs

THE WIDEST BLAST RADIUS OF THE FOUR. It changes which end holds the relation,
so every reader of what a position owes changes with it.

IT REWRITES THE ROUND'S EXIT RULE rather than narrowing it. A rule written as
though every work token blocks becomes a rule about the blocking kind, and
that is an owner-visible change rather than a footnote.

WHAT IT BUYS THAT NOTHING ELSE DOES. One piece of work holding two positions,
which a list cannot express at all, and placing work that needs no cooperation
from its destination.

MAKE, REUSE OR BUY. Make, but the shape is already shipped inside this
repository: the trace graph points upward and nothing keeps a downward list.

THE FAILURE MODE THAT DECIDES. A query over everything that turns out not to
be cheap. Then every count a surface draws becomes the expensive question.

## What it leans on

THAT A QUERY OVER EVERYTHING CAN BE MADE CHEAP. The trace graph is evidence it
can, at 1,821 nodes. At 22,000 it is unmeasured.

THAT THE OWNER ACCEPTS THE EXIT RULE BEING RESTATED. That is a ruling rather
than a risk.

THAT A HARMLESS FINDING LEFT UNHELD DOES NOT ROT. The register already carries
a demand that a carried finding names an open entry, and this candidate leans
on that demand being kept.
