## Why this one

IT IS THE ROUND'S DESIGN WITH EVERY MEASURED OBJECTION ANSWERED. The volume
probe capped the live population at one record's worth. The prior-art sweep's
two shipped counter-examples both objected to clutter, and folding at close
removes the clutter they objected to. The per-hop budget is answered by
deriving late, and the count by an index.

WHAT IT TRADES AWAY is the history of how a closed record was worked. Only
the result survives, so nobody can ask afterwards which work tokens were
closed as duplicates.

## How it works

ENTERING A POSITION COSTS ALMOST NOTHING. It records that the position is
open and derives no work token at all. The first question about what is owed
pays for the derivation, and only for the part it asked about.

THE FILES ARE FOR A PERSON AND THE INDEX IS FOR THE SYSTEM. Both exist, and
the files are the truth. The index is keyed to their content, rebuilt when
they move, and every count, filter and offer is answered from it. A
disagreement is reported rather than resolved quietly.

A HAND HOLDS AN AUTHORISATION TO ACT, and holding one is what makes the act
legal. That is what refuses the exit: a position with authorisations still out
is demonstrably unfinished, and nothing has to compare two lists to find out.

AT CLOSE, EACH SETTLED WORK TOKEN FOLDS INTO THE EVIDENCE and its file goes.
The archive keeps what was learned rather than the bookkeeping that produced
it.

THE PEN IS ABSORBED. A stray and a parked item are pieces of work whose place
is nowhere yet, so capturing one and placing one are acts this cluster already
performs.

THE SEAM THAT MATTERS IS THE CLOSE. It is the one moment where two
representations of the same fact exist at once, and getting it wrong loses
work rather than slowing it.

## What it costs

THE MOST MACHINERY OF THE FOUR. Derivation on demand, an index with its own
invalidation, an authorisation on every act, and a fold at close. Four
mechanisms where the round as written has one.

THE FILE COUNT IS CAPPED AT ONE RECORD'S WORTH, measured at 320 to 402. The
vault figure of 22,080 to 27,738 does not apply, which is the whole purchase.

MAKE, REUSE OR BUY. Mostly make, and the index has real prior art the
architecture milestone can lean on.

THE FAILURE MODE THAT DECIDES. The index disagreeing with the files, silently.
The corpus already rules that the derived copy loses every disagreement, so
the rule exists; what does not exist yet is the reporting.

## What it leans on

THAT AN INDEX KEYED TO FILE CONTENT STAYS HONEST. There is prior art and no
measurement here.

THAT FOLDING AT CLOSE LOSES NOTHING ANYBODY NEEDS LATER. Untested, and it is
the one irreversible act in the candidate.

THAT THE PEN'S PRIVACY RULE SURVIVES BEING ABSORBED. The register carries
that rule as fatal, so a design that absorbs the pen and forgets it reopens a
fatal row.
