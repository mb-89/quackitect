## Mechanism

THE AXIS HAD TWO POINTS AND WANTED THREE. The seed names a driver per milestone;
opt-name-the-driver-per-state-not-per-milestone goes finer. Nothing went coarser,
and a granularity axis with only its fine half explored is not an axis.

ONE DECISION PER RECORD, MADE WHERE THE CHANGE SIZE IS ALREADY BLESSED.
gate-kickoff already rules the change size for the whole record — this iteration
was blessed major there. Reading a driver off that single ruling costs nothing
new: no per-row declaration, no reduction, no republication, and
reduce-a-milestone-to-one-difficulty disappears entirely.

IT IS NOT THE INCUMBENT. Today nobody names anything and whatever hand the human
started keeps walking. Naming it once at kickoff is a real decision made by the
machine, recorded, and comparable against what actually answered.

WHAT IT COSTS: the spread that raid-risk-a-submachine-maximum-drags-easy-items-onto-an-expensive-walker
names, at its worst. A record blessed major puts the strong hand on all fifty-three
states including the ones that read a file and stamp a form. If the milestone
maximum wastes, the record maximum wastes more.

WHEN IT WOULD WIN ANYWAY: if measurement shows the per-row difficulties inside a
record are mostly the same, the finer options buy nothing and this one is the
cheapest thing that is still a decision.
