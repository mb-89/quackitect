## Mechanism

WHAT IF THE SHOWING DOES NOT EXIST, for the parts that are files? The panel
stops rendering them. The files are still there.

WHO DOES ITS JOB INSTEAD? THE ENVIRONMENT. The person's own editor already
opens, searches, diffs and follows links in a folder it has open, and after
this delta the machine-state folder is one of those. That is the third
trimming outcome: somebody else's system already solves this and we were
reimplementing.

THIS OPTION ONLY EXISTS BECAUSE OF THE COLLAPSE. Before it, the folder sat
above what the editor had open, so nothing outside could show it and drawing
was the only way. Moving it changed which answers are available, and that is
the kind of second-order effect a trimming pass is for.

WHAT MUST NOT BE TRIMMED, and the line is sharp. Anything the engine COMPUTES
rather than stores: where the walk stands, which states are green, what is
walkable this minute, the drawn machine. None of that is a file, so no editor
can show it.

WHAT IT COSTS. The reading loop and the notes both have a structured verb
serving them precisely because a raw read loses ordering and paging. Handing
those to a file tree gives that back up, which is why this iteration's own
rule is to hide what has a door and serve what does not — this option is that
rule applied to the DRAWN surface rather than to the lane.

WHAT IT BUYS. Less surface to build and keep in step, and a person who can use
tools they already know on the parts that are just text.
