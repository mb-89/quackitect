## Mechanism

BOTH DEMANDS WERE ASSUMED TO APPLY AT ONE MOMENT and they never did. What a
position owes has to be EXACT. It does not have to be exact at the instant of
entry — it has to be exact when somebody asks what is owed.

SO ENTERING COSTS ALMOST NOTHING. It records that the position is open.
The first question about what is owed pays for the derivation, and only for
the part it asked about.

WHAT IT COSTS. The count a surface draws becomes the expensive question rather
than the cheap one, and a position nobody has asked about has no work tokens
to show. That is the exact opposite of what the round's own count demand
wants, so the two have to be reconciled in the design rather than assumed
compatible.

WHAT IT BUYS. The per-hop budget stops being a risk. This is the same
separation the corpus already made for its own claim checking, where the
exhaustive check moved from opening a form to submitting it.
