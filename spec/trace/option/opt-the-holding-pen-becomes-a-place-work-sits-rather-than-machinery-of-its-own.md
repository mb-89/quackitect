## Mechanism

A STRAY, A PARKED ITEM AND AN WORK TOKEN ARE THE SAME SHAPE. Each is
something somebody owes, each carries a statement and a re-entry condition,
and the only real difference is that two of them have no position yet.

SO THE PEN IS A PLACE, not a machine. Capturing a stray puts a piece of work
somewhere called nowhere. Placing it later moves it, which is the act the
work cluster already performs. Nothing separate mints, holds or offers.

WHAT IT BUYS. One vocabulary instead of two, and the register's own naming
rule is satisfied rather than argued with. It also makes the pool and a
position the same kind of destination, which is what routing work already
assumes.

WHAT BREAKS. The pen has its own rules that the work cluster does not: a raw
capture may carry anything private, and what leaves it must be authored
rather than pasted. Those rules have to move with it, and a design that
absorbs the pen and forgets them reopens a fatal register row.

IT IS A TRIM OF A CLUSTER THIS ROUND DID NOT SET OUT TO TOUCH, which is
exactly why the finder asks about every cluster rather than the interesting
one.
