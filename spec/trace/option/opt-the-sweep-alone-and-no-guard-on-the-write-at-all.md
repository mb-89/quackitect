## Mechanism

One caller instead of two. The rule is stated once and read once, by a pass
that walks the tree and reports.

WHAT IT DELETES. Half the requirement that one rule be read by two callers, the
whole write-time refusal, and with them the assumption that every write adding
a departure passes through a path-carrying verb — which is the assumption this
record could not probe, because the shell carries no path.

SO IT DELETES A HOLE RATHER THAN A CAPABILITY, and that is the case for it.
The write-time guard was never going to see a shell write. A sweep sees every
write however it arrived, because it reads the tree rather than the act.

WHAT IT COSTS. The window. A departure written today is caught whenever the
sweep next runs, and the author who could have explained it has moved on. The
whole argument for refusing at write time is that the reason is only cheap
while the author is still there.

WHAT MAKES IT REAL RATHER THAN A STRAW MAN. The two precedents in this tree
already work this way. widgets.ts silently ignores a bullet with no reason and
trace.ts reports a finding rather than refusing. Neither refuses a write today,
so the sweep-only design is what actually ships here, and the write-time half
is the addition.

THE CHEAPEST DESIGN ON THE CHART, and the one the record has to argue against
rather than around.
