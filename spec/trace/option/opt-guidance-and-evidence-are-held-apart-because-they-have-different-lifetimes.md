## Mechanism

THE TWO HALVES HAVE DIFFERENT OWNERS AND DIFFERENT CLOCKS. What a step asks
for belongs to the method, is shared by every record, and changes when
somebody rewrites a card. What a step produced belongs to one record, is
never shared, and is frozen the moment it is signed.

SO HOLD THEM APART. The work token carries a pointer to its guidance rather
than a copy of it, and holds only the answer. Rewording a method card updates
what every open work token asks for, everywhere, at once.

WHAT IT BUYS. The re-entry problem shrinks: nothing has to be reconciled
because nothing was copied. It also answers the round's own demand that
settled work IS the evidence, since the half that remains is exactly the half
a record keeps.

WHAT IT COSTS. A piece of work stops being readable on its own. Opening the
file shows an answer and a pointer, and a person who wants to know what was
asked has to follow it. That is a direct cost against the readable-text
demand, paid in one hop rather than in unreadability.
