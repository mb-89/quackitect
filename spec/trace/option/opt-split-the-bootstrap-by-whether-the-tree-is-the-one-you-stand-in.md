## Mechanism

THE RULE FOUND A SEAM THIS ITERATION MADE. The bootstrap cluster is named for
acting on a whole tree FROM OUTSIDE IT. `bring-the-product-up` was added to it
on 2026-08-19 and does not do that. It runs inside the folder somebody has open,
every time they open it.

## What changes together

FIVE MEMBERS MOVE WHEN THE TREE MOVES. Producing a project, producing a vehicle,
taking an update, reporting what a vehicle changed, standing a product up. Each
reaches in from outside. Each changes when the shape of a tree changes.

## What changes apart

ONE MEMBER MOVES WHEN THE ENTRY POINT MOVES. This iteration is the evidence
rather than the argument. The entry point changed, and exactly one function in
this cluster changed with it. The other five were untouched.

## What it costs

THE HAZARD IS GENUINELY SHARED, and splitting means solving it twice. All six
fail by leaving something that looks finished and is not. The cluster node
already names that price: refuse-or-complete is the kind of rule solved well
once and badly twice.

THE SPLIT ALSO STRANDS A FLOW. `bring-the-product-up` consumes
`flow-scaffolded-product`, which `stand-up-a-product` produces. That edge would
cross the new boundary.

## What it buys

THE COUPLING CLASS BECOMES TRUE AGAIN. Right now half of the binding holds for
one member and the other half does not, which is a cluster that cannot state its
own rule without an exception.

IT ALSO SEPARATES TWO CLOCKS. Five members run at the birth or the update of a
tree. One runs every single time a person opens a folder, and carries a
five-second bound nothing else here carries.

## It contends with another option on this chart

[[opt-the-arrival-is-the-bootstrap-with-nobody-present]] MOVES SEVEN FUNCTIONS
INTO THIS CLUSTER. This one moves one out. They are not exclusive, and taken
together they say the same thing twice: the binding force here is when the act
runs and who is watching, not which tree it touches.

TAKEN TOGETHER THEY REDRAW THE CLUSTER ENTIRELY, which is a bigger move than
either alone and should be judged as one.
