## Mechanism

TODAY THE RULE IS A SENTENCE. A weaker driver owes a recorded reason, nothing
asks for one, and a walk with no reason looks exactly like a walk that needed
none.

MAKE THE ABSENCE UNREPRESENTABLE. The block publishes a name; the record of a
walked state carries what actually drove it AND, where that differs downward,
why. A state whose record carries neither is not a valid record.

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT FROM CHECKING IT. A check runs somewhere and can be
skipped, disabled or never written — the standing risk entry says exactly that
about the current design. A shape the record cannot hold fails at the write,
in the same way frontmatter the reader cannot parse fails at the write today.

WHAT IT DEPENDS ON, AND THIS IS THE HONEST LIMIT. Knowing that the driver was
weaker needs knowing what actually drove, which is self-reported and carries an
open assumption. SO THE UNREPRESENTABLE SHAPE IS "no statement at all" rather
than "a false statement". It closes the silence and not the lie.

THAT IS STILL WORTH HAVING. The failure this design fears is a cheap driver
talking itself into staying cheap, and silence is how that happens. A required
statement makes it a deliberate false claim instead of an omission, which is a
different act and a much rarer one.
