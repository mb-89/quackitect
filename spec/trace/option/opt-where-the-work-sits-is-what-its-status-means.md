## Mechanism

ONE PLACE PER STATUS, and the piece of work sits in exactly one of them.
Open, in work and each terminal kind are locations rather than field values.
Nothing edits a status; something moves the work, and the move is the record.

WHAT SURVIVED THE TRANSLATION. The count a position owes stops being computed
at all — it is how full a place is. A person reading the board sees the shape
of the work without opening anything, which is the whole purchase of the
strip bay.

WHAT DID NOT SURVIVE, and it is the honest half. A strip board assumes one
room and one team looking at it. This system runs in clones that are apart
for hours, and two hands moving the same strip in two rooms is a conflict a
physical board cannot have.

IT ALSO COLLAPSES A SEPARATION THE ROUND ASKED FOR. The register demands that
place and status stay independent, because routing work out of scope changes
where it is without changing what it is. This option fuses exactly those two,
so taking it means reopening that row rather than quietly breaking it.
