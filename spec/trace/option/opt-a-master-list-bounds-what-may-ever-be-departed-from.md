## Mechanism

Two lists, one derived from the other, and the direction of travel is fixed.

The master list is published by whoever made the type and approved by an
authority. It defines the universe of things that may be inoperative for
dispatch at all. An operator derives their own list from it, and that list may
demand earlier rectification, or forbid a dispatch the master permits. It may
never permit one the master forbids.

WHAT SURVIVES THE TRANSLATION, AND THIS SYSTEM ALREADY HAS THE TWO LEVELS. The
shipped method is the master. A driven project has its own tree and its own
rules. Today a project could grant itself any exception it liked, and nothing
in the method would know.

So the master list would name the kinds of departure that are ever legal, and
a project could narrow that set but not widen it.

WHAT IT BUYS. A rule that survives being copied. Everything else on this chart
governs one tree, and this is the only mechanism found that governs a rule as
it travels.

WHAT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT TRANSFER. The approving authority, again. Aviation's
master list is approved by a regulator through a named board process. Ours
would be approved by whoever edits the method, which is the same hand that
edits the project in most sessions.

WHAT IT COSTS, AND IT IS THE REASON THIS MAY BE PREMATURE. It is machinery for
a problem nobody has reported yet. One driven project exists in this system's
own record, and no departure has travelled between trees. Building the two
levels before the one level has been used once is the failure the trimming
finder exists to catch.
