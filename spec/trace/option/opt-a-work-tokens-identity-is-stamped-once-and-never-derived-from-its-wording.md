## Mechanism

THE CONFLICT SITS BETWEEN TWO LEVELS. At the level of the WHOLE, a method
card is prose somebody rewrites freely, and that is the point of it. At the
level of the PART, an work token has to be the same thing across a second
entry, or work already done is lost.

SEPARATE THEM BY LEVEL. The identity belongs to the part and is stamped once,
when the work token first exists. The wording belongs to the whole and may
change without limit. The marked step is recorded as a pointer the work token
holds, never as the key it is looked up by.

WHAT IT COSTS. Something has to survive between entries to carry the
identity, so the option that stores nothing cannot take this one. It also
needs an answer for a step that is genuinely DELETED, where the honest move
is to close its work token as obsolete rather than to drop it.

WHAT IT BUYS. Re-entry stops being a matching problem, which the register
already names as the hard half.
