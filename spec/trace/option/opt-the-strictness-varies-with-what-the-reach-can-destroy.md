## Mechanism

The rule reads a second attribute off the reach itself and sets its own
strictness from it.

- A REACH THAT DESTROYS gets a refusal at write time and a departure that needs
  a reason.
- A REACH THAT ONLY OBSERVES gets a report and nothing more.

WHY THE ATTRIBUTE IS DAMAGE RATHER THAN KIND. Reading and writing is the
obvious split and it is the wrong one. A read of the wrong tree is how a
verdict comes back about a file nobody meant, which the corpus already carries
as its own requirement. What actually separates the cases is whether the reach
can leave the tree different afterwards.

WHAT IT BUYS. The common case gets cheap without the rule getting weaker, which
is the heuristic this record swept and could not otherwise satisfy. Most of the
398 measured disk sites are reads.

WHAT IT COSTS. A second attribute is a second thing to get wrong, and getting it
wrong is silent in the dangerous direction. A destructive reach mis-graded as
observational is exactly the departure nobody was asked to justify.

THE CORPUS ALREADY HOLDS BOTH SIDES OF THIS ARGUMENT as standing requirements
— one saying reads and writes take separate rules, one saying a single rule
covers both. This option is the third position: one rule whose bite varies,
rather than one rule or two.
