## Mechanism

THE CARD IS THE PERMISSION. A hand cannot act on a position unless it is
holding one, and holding one is what makes the act legal. The description of
what to do rides along, but it is not what the card IS.

WHAT SURVIVED THE TRANSLATION. The exit rule stops being a check somebody
runs at the end. A position with cards still out is a position whose work is
demonstrably not back, and nothing has to compare two lists to find that out.
It also gives the difficulty mark real teeth: a card a hand may not hold is a
door that will not open, rather than an item it was asked not to take.

WHAT DID NOT SURVIVE. Kanban caps work in progress by printing a fixed number
of cards, and that half does not transfer. Our work tokens are not
interchangeable, so a cap would block a position rather than smooth its flow.
The authorisation transfers; the quota does not.

WHAT IT COSTS HERE. Every act on a position has to name the card it is acting
under, which is a change to how calls are made rather than to how work is
stored. That is a wider blast radius than the other options in this cluster.
