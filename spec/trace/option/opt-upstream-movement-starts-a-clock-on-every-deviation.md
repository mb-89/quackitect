## Mechanism

READ AS A PROTOCOL, the treaty's second sentence is the design. When an
international standard is amended, a State that does not amend its own rules to
match "shall give notice to the Council within sixty days of the adoption of the
amendment to the international standard, or indicate the action which it
proposes to take."

FOUR PROPERTIES FALL OUT OF THAT SENTENCE.

- A CLOCK STARTS on the upstream change, not on anybody noticing.
- EVERY ADOPTER MUST RESPOND, whether or not the change affects them.
- THE LEGAL ANSWER SET IS EXACTLY TWO: I have conformed, or here is my declared
  difference and what I intend to do about it.
- SILENCE IS A FAULT. It is not read as agreement, and it is not read as
  nothing-to-report.

AND THE CENTRE BROADCASTS. The Council then notifies every other State of the
difference that now exists. A departure is not a private fact.

WHAT A FILING CARRIES, per the same regime: the affected provision by number,
the reason for non-compliance, a concise description of the difference, and the
State's intentions for future compliance. That last field is the interesting
one, because it distinguishes a departure somebody means to keep from one they
mean to retire.

## What transfers

THE INVERSION OF THE DEFAULT, which is the whole value. Every other mechanism in
this iteration's survey treats an override that still applies cleanly as fine.
This treats an upstream change as a question that must be answered, and the
absence of an answer as the failure.

THAT ANSWERS A HAZARD NOTHING ELSE CATCHES. An override can apply perfectly and
be wrong, because what it overrides changed underneath it for a reason the copy
never saw. Loss is loud in most systems. Staleness is loud in almost none.

AND THE ASYMMETRY IS WORTH KEEPING. Differences are categorised, and one
category is MORE EXACTING — the adopter is stricter than the standard. A
stricter departure and a looser one are not the same risk and should not be
recorded identically. CENELEC draws the same line in prose, treating more
demanding and less demanding national requirements as separate cases.

## What breaks in translation

THE SIXTY DAYS IS A PERIOD, NOT A MECHANISM. It exists because aviation
regulators are public bodies coordinating across a hundred and eighty-eight
States. Copy the structure — a defined window, a required response, a stale
mark — and discard the number.

THE ENFORCEMENT DOES NOT COME FOR FREE. Article 38 works because ICAO audits
States on their filings under a standing oversight programme. Strip out the body
with standing to say "you did not answer" and the clock degrades into a
notification nobody reads.

AND A COPY OF THIS PRODUCT HAS NO COUNCIL. The treaty's broadcast step assumes a
centre that sees every adopter, which the isolation rule here forbids. What
survives is the per-copy half: the copy's own machinery marks its own departures
stale and demands an answer. What is lost is every other copy learning about it.

THE ONE PLACE IT COULD BE HARDER THAN THE ORIGINAL. A treaty cannot stop a State
flying; it can only record the breach. Software can refuse. A copy that has not
answered for a touched departure could be blocked from taking the update at all,
which is stricter than anything the source domain does.
