## Mechanism

The group is declared as its own thing, with a name and members. A departure
points at the group.

WHAT IT FIXES. A flat per-site list repeats itself, and a repeated reason is
the exact symptom
raid-asm-a-demanded-reason-is-a-considered-reason worries about. Twelve sites
in one area with one true reason should be one entry, not twelve copies of a
sentence.

WHAT IT COSTS, AND IT IS THE REASON THIS IS NOT OBVIOUSLY RIGHT. A group is a
second thing to keep true. A member joins the group by being added to it, so a
new file lands inside a granted exception without anybody judging it — which
is the not-exempt default failing quietly, and this record has a requirement
against exactly that.

THE TENSION IS REAL AND IT IS THE CELL'S ARGUMENT.
req-absence-from-the-exemption-list-means-not-exempt says silence never grants.
A group grants by membership, and membership can be silent.

A NARROWER FORM SURVIVES BOTH. Let the group name its members explicitly, one
line each, and forbid a pattern. The repetition then falls on the member list
rather than on the reasons, which is where repetition costs nothing.
