## Mechanism

THE FORM IS ALREADY THE CHECKLIST. It is derived from the position's own note,
a required field already refuses the submit, and a position cannot be left
while one is empty. Nobody authors the list. That is four of the round's
demands, shipped, today.

WHO TAKES OVER. The walk does, through judging a claim. Nothing new is built,
and the five acts this round named collapse back into the one that already
exists.

WHAT BREAKS, and it is exactly four things. A form field cannot be moved to
another position. It cannot be counted from outside the position without
opening it. It cannot be withheld until something else settles. It cannot be
refused to a hand whose mark is too low.

WHY IT BELONGS ON THE CHART ANYWAY. It is the cheapest thing that could
possibly work, and the method says the null option is regularly the best one
there. A candidate set without it cannot show what the other options are
buying, and the four breaks above are the price list.
