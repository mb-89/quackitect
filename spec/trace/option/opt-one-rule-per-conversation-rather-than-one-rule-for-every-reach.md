## Mechanism

Four rules rather than one. Each names its own governed set, its own departure
list and its own strictness.

WHY THE DIVISION IS NOT ARBITRARY. The neighbours walk already found the seam.
Four conversations came out of seven neighbours, and a rule per conversation
cuts exactly there rather than at a line somebody drew.

WHAT IT BUYS. The rules can differ where the conversations differ, and they do
differ. The predecessor ruled two opposite ways on two rules for stated
reasons — correct the history for one, exempt it for the other — which is only
expressible if a rule is a thing rather than the thing.

It also lets the four be built one at a time. The version door has few callers
and no history, so it could be strict from day one while the byte door is
still ratcheting down from 79 modules.

WHAT IT COSTS, AND IT IS EXACTLY WHAT THE RECORD SET OUT TO AVOID. Four rules
is four places a reader must look, four grammars to keep aligned, and four
chances for the shapes to drift apart. The requirement that one rule be
expressed once was written against precisely this.

THE TENSION IS BETWEEN TWO OF THIS RECORD'S OWN FINDINGS. Carve by purpose says
the conversations are four. Express a rule once says the expression is one.
Both can hold only if one rule ENGINE reads four rule DECLARATIONS, which is a
third thing neither finding named.

THAT THIRD READING IS THE ONE TO SCORE, and it is why this cell is on the chart
rather than being folded into the door-naming requirement.
