## The contradiction it breaks

Hiding history from an agent makes the measurement honest and makes ordinary
work impossible. Every verb that reaches history would need its own hiding
rule, and three such rules already disagree in this codebase.

## Why time dissolves it rather than trading it

Nothing is HIDDEN. The rewind commit is a moment, and everything before it is
fully readable. The agent's ordinary work is untouched; only the future is
absent, which is exactly the condition the original walk was in.

## Mechanism

One ancestry test against the rewind commit, applied where a commit or a ref
is resolved. A commit that is an ancestor resolves. Anything else does not.
