## What it collapses

The distinction between a benchmark run and an ordinary iteration. Both are
walks. One happens to stand on a rewound tree and discard its output.

## What it buys

A real iteration could then be measured with the same instrument, which is the
one thing that would let the honesty assumption be probed — it needs a real
walk and a benchmark walk at the same size and model.

## What it costs, and it is the reason this may lose

The two concealments only make sense on a rewound tree. Unifying the walk means
the measuring is universal and the concealing is conditional, which is two
degrees of freedom where the design currently has one.

## Mechanism

The cost derivation and the conditions stamp attach to any walk. The rewind,
the ceiling and the concealment attach only to a bound run.
