## Rationale

THE FIVE MOMENTS THE OWNER SPLIT APART LAND BACK TOGETHER, and that is the
check that the split was sound. They were separated because each is its own
act with its own refusal. They cluster because they pass one thing along:
routing opens the record, holding gives it a worktree, landing puts it on
trunk, closing rules on it, and the archive holds it still.

Splitting them was about naming. Clustering them is about coupling. Both can
be right at once, and a partition that could not show that would be hiding
something.

ANSWERING WITH TESTS IS THE SIXTH, AND IT IS THE TEAR CANDIDATE. Its verdict
is what landing refuses to proceed without, so the sequence coupling is real.
It has no other tie to the cluster. A candidate that allocates testing
somewhere else pays one thin link and nothing more.
