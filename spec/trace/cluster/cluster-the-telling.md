## Rationale

THREE OPTIONS ALREADY BELONGED TO THIS CLUSTER and it was never declared. Each
is an alternative shape for the same answer.

SO THIS NODE IS WRITTEN FROM WHAT THEY SAY, not from a fresh judgment. The
grouping already existed in the corpus; only its declaration was missing.

## What holds it together

EVERY MEMBER SHAPES WHAT COMES BACK from work that has not finished. The caller
sees only this, so the choice is the whole interface.

The alternatives disagree about three things.

- WHAT THE OBJECT IS. One operation object for every kind of long work, against
  a shape per kind.
- WHERE IT RIDES. On every answer, against only on the answers that carry work.
- WHETHER IT SAYS WHEN TO ASK AGAIN. Naming the next ask makes polling a rule
  rather than a guess.

## Why same-external-interface

THE MEMBERS DO NOT SHARE CODE. They share the surface a caller reads, and two
of them cannot both be that surface at once.
