## Rationale

SIX FUNCTIONS, AND FOUR OF THEM ARE JOINED BY FLOW. Stating the rule feeds
enumerating and judging. Enumerating feeds judging. Recording a departure feeds
judging. That is a tight block and the matrix shows it as one.

THE OTHER TWO ARE JOINED BY POLICY, NOT BY FLOW, and saying so is part of the
partition rather than a hole in it.

- REFUSING A DEPARTURE produces a flow that leaves the system, so nothing
  inside consumes what it makes. It reads the same rule as the rest and it
  fires on the same act.
- THE ROOT is the context box. Every flow it names crosses the boundary, which
  is what a root is for.

WHY THEY ARE NOT SEPARATED. Every one of the six is inert without the stated
rule, and none may act on a governed thing the rule does not name. That is one
policy, held six ways, and it is the same argument the holding-pen cluster
makes for its own two members.

WHAT WOULD BREAK IT. A second rule whose departures are judged differently
would split this into one cluster per rule, and the flow block would go with
whichever rule it belongs to. Nothing else in the partition moves.
