## Rationale

FIVE ACTS TOUCH ONE THING, and that thing is the piece of work itself.
Minting makes it, placing moves it, offering decides whether a hand may take
it, settling closes it, and counting says how many stand. Four of the edges
between them carry the same flow.

IT IS NOT THE RECORD'S LIFE. That cluster follows a RECORD from routing to
archive. This one follows a single work token inside a position, and a record
holds many of them at once. The two lifecycles nest rather than coincide.

THE TEAR CANDIDATES ARE THE TWO THAT FACE OUTWARD. Counting hands its figure
to the surface, and settling hands its result to the judgment and to the work
account. A candidate that puts either somewhere else pays one thin link and
keeps the other four together.

WHY IT IS NEW RATHER THAN AN EXTENSION OF AN EXISTING GROUP. Before this
round nothing in the system modelled a piece of work as a thing that can be
pointed at, moved and counted. The acts did not exist, so no cluster could
have held them.
