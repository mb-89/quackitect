## Rationale

TWO OPTIONS ALREADY BELONGED TO THIS CLUSTER and it was never declared. Both
rule on the same moment.

SO THIS NODE IS WRITTEN FROM WHAT THEY SAY, not from a fresh judgment. The
grouping already existed in the corpus; only its declaration was missing.

## What holds it together

EVERY MEMBER RULES ON ONE MOMENT: the call returning while the work goes on.
Everything the caller can do next follows from what it was handed there.

The alternatives disagree about two things.

- HOW SOON. Acknowledging inside a second, then reporting on a beat, against
  holding the call until there is something to say.
- WHAT IS OWED. A judgment at the leaving, against a judgment at the asking.

## Why same-policy

THEY SHARE NO SURFACE AND NO DATA. They share the rule that governs one
transition, and a transition can follow only one rule.
