## Rationale

TWO OPTIONS ALREADY BELONGED TO THIS CLUSTER and it was never declared. Both
rule on the same field, from opposite ends.

SO THIS NODE IS WRITTEN FROM WHAT THEY SAY, not from a fresh judgment. The
grouping already existed in the corpus; only its declaration was missing.

## What holds it together

EVERY MEMBER RULES ON ONE WORD. A reader takes it in at a glance, so a word
that means two things costs more than a longer report would.

The alternatives disagree about two things.

- WHETHER THE VOCABULARY IS CLOSED. One closed word means a reader learns the
  whole set once.
- WHETHER IT CARRIES READINESS. A word marking what is listed but not yet
  usable puts availability inside the standing rather than beside it.

## Why same-external-interface

THE MEMBERS ARE ALTERNATIVE VALUES FOR ONE PUBLISHED FIELD. Choosing either
settles what every reader of that field may assume.
