## Rationale

SIX OPTIONS ALREADY BELONGED TO THIS CLUSTER and it was never declared. Every
one of them is an alternative rule for the same field on the same answer.

SO THIS NODE IS WRITTEN FROM WHAT THEY SAY, not from a fresh judgment. The
grouping already existed in the corpus; only its declaration was missing.

## What holds it together

EVERY MEMBER RULES ON ONE FIGURE. The time a running piece of work still needs,
and what the reader is allowed to conclude from it.

The alternatives disagree about four things.

- WHETHER TO GIVE ONE. Shipping with no estimate at all is one of the options.
- WHAT SHAPE IT TAKES. A single point against a window.
- WHEN IT IS COMPUTED. At the ask against at the start.
- WHETHER IT MAY GROW. An estimate that only falls is a promise, not a belief.

## Why same-policy

THEY SHARE NO DATA AND NO ORDER. What they share is the rule being chosen: pick
one and the others are excluded, because a single field cannot follow two rules.
