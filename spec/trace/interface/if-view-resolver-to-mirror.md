THIS IS THE INTERFACE THE DECISION TURNS ON, and it was missing from the first
decomposition. Every other crossing this round minted runs INTO the resolver;
without this one the element had no way to deliver what it computes.

WHAT CROSSES IT IS RESOLVED AND NEVER RAW. A colour rather than a condition, a
label rather than an identifier to look up, a row already marked as selected.

IT IS THE ONE MOST LIKELY TO DECAY. A widget whose needs the model does not
carry is a gap in the model, and the temptation at that gap is to reach around
it rather than widen it.
