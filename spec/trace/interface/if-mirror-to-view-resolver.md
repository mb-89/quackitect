THE RETURN HALF, and without it the resolver is a one-way feed no act can
reach.

WHAT DOES NOT CROSS IT: any claim about what changed. The surface says a
control was pressed; deciding what that means is the engine's job, and a
surface that decided it would be deriving again.

THE REPAINT RIDES THIS PAIR. An act crosses here, the engine resolves the new
view, and it comes back over if-view-resolver-to-mirror. That is one round
trip rather than two, which is what the repaint requirement asks for.
