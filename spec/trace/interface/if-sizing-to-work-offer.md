THE MARK IS A ROUTING KEY RATHER THAN A LABEL, and this crossing is where it
does its routing.

WHAT IT CARRIES. One difficulty per piece of work, and the rung that difficulty
resolves to.

WHAT THE OFFER NEVER DOES: decide the difficulty. It reads the published figure
and filters on it.

IT SITS ON THE HOT PATH. Every ask for work reads this, which is why the sizing
publishes its answer rather than being asked to compute one.

FAILURE BEHAVIOUR: an unmatched rung names itself and publishes no strength, so
the offer withholds the work. Guessing that a hand is strong enough is the one
thing this crossing must not do.
