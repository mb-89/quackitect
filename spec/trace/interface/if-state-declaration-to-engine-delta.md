The crossing that stops a reload from moving the folder underneath a walk.

## What crosses

THE RESOLVED ANSWER, not the declaration file. The engine-delta takes what the
declaration decided rather than reading and deciding again, because a second
reader is exactly what the corpus decision removed one element over.

## Why it matters more here than it looks

AN ENGINE THAT RELOADS ONTO A CHANGED DECLARATION would otherwise pick up a new
folder while a record is bound to the old one. The branch-independence probe
measured that the folder is per checkout; nothing measures what a reload does to
it, and this crossing is where that question lives.
