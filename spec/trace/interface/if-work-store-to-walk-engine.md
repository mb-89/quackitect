THE STORE REPORTS AND THE WALK JUDGES. The split is deliberate.

WHAT IT CARRIES. Which work reached a terminal status, which moved elsewhere
and where it went, and the stated reason on any close that was not plain
completion.

WHY MOVED IS NOT A FAILURE. A position stops owing work that went elsewhere.
The report says so plainly rather than leaving the walk to infer it.

WHAT THE STORE NEVER DOES: refuse the leave. It reports what stands. The
judgment belongs to the walk, where every other leaving condition is already
weighed.

FAILURE BEHAVIOUR: work still open makes the report incomplete rather than
absent. The walk refuses on a report, never on silence.
