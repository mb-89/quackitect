THIS IS THE INTERFACE THE DECISION TURNS ON. What crosses it is resolved and
never raw: a colour rather than a condition, a label rather than an identifier
to look up.

IT IS ALSO THE ONE MOST LIKELY TO DECAY. A widget whose needs the model does
not carry is a gap in the model, and the temptation is to reach past it
instead of widening it.
