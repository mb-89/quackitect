THE SECOND ENTRY IS THE HARD HALF, and this crossing carries it.

WHAT IT CARRIES. The marked steps as they now read, each beside the identity
stamped when its work was first made.

WHY IDENTITY RATHER THAN HEADING. A card whose wording changed still names the
same step. Matching on the heading orphans finished work, or mints a duplicate
beside it. Both failures are silent, which is what makes the rule worth writing
down.

FAILURE BEHAVIOUR: a step whose identity is absent from the standing work is
new, and is minted. One present in the work and absent from the card is
orphaned, and the store reports it rather than deleting it.
