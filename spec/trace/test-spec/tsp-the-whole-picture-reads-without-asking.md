## Scope

THE CLAIM IS ABOUT READING, NOT ABOUT DATA. That the counts are computed
correctly is a test's question and already has one. This asks whether a person
learns where the work is by looking.

IT IS A DEMONSTRATION AND NOT A TEST because the pass is a person's answer to
a question, given from a screen, within a few seconds. No fixture can be that
person.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY OUT. Whether the counts are RIGHT, which the served
payload's own cases carry. This asks whether they are LEGIBLE.

## Approach

THE DESIGN METHOD IS SCENARIO-BASED, and the extreme is ignorance: the observer
must be somebody who has not walked this record.

THE LEVEL IS ACCEPTANCE. The pass is what they can say without help.

DEPTH IS ONE PASS, timed. The story's own word is "glance", so a slow correct
answer is a fail on the thing being asked.

## Procedure

FOUR STEPS, and each names what is WATCHED as the pass.

- ONE. Open the machine of a record that is part way through. OBSERVED: the
  states are drawn and each carries its counts without anything being clicked.
- TWO. Ask the observer to name one state that still owes work, and say how
  much. OBSERVED: they answer from the drawing alone, and the answer matches
  the store.
- THREE. Ask which states are finished. OBSERVED: they answer from an empty
  count rather than by interpreting a colour, and nothing has to be explained
  to them first.
- FOUR. Ask how much is waiting with no home. OBSERVED: they find the backlog
  beside the states, with its own count, and do not go looking in a folder.

STEP THREE IS THE ONE THE STORY EXISTS FOR. A colour already said whether a
state was finished. Only an empty count says it without anybody deciding what
the colour means.

## What this demonstration cannot settle

WHETHER IT SCALES TO A BIG MACHINE. This record's own machine draws 64 states.
A machine several times that may be legible per state and illegible as a whole,
and one sitting cannot tell.

AND IT CANNOT BE PERFORMED BY THE AGENT AT ALL. Looking at a screen is the
person's act here, by the standing rule that the agent does not look at one
unasked. Every run of this procedure needs somebody else.
