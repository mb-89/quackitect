## Scope

THE CLAIM THAT PROSE CANNOT MAKE. A method card can tell a walker to do seven
things and cannot refuse when it does five. This demonstration asks whether the
refusal is real.

IT IS A DEMONSTRATION AND NOT A TEST because the thing being watched is a WALK
choosing to move on and being stopped. A fixture can prove the guard computes;
only a walk can show it binding a hand that wanted to leave.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY OUT. Whether the right steps were marked on the card,
which is the card author's job. This asks only whether marked steps bind.

## Approach

THE DESIGN METHOD IS SCENARIO-BASED, and the extreme is a walker in a hurry.
The run is performed at the end of a long session, with the walk trying to
reach a gate.

THE LEVEL IS ACCEPTANCE, and the pass is what the walker could NOT do.

DEPTH IS SEVERAL PASSES, because one refusal proves a guard and several prove
that it is not one special case.

## Procedure

FIVE STEPS, and each names what is WATCHED as the pass.

- ONE. Enter a state whose method card carries marked steps. OBSERVED: the
  steps arrive as work, one per mark, each carrying that step's guidance.
- TWO. Try to submit the state's form with one required item unanswered.
  OBSERVED: the submit is refused and the refusal NAMES the item, rather than
  saying the form is incomplete.
- THREE. Answer that item with a reason rather than an answer. OBSERVED: the
  refusal stands. An excuse is not an answer, and this step is the one that
  separates a guard from a reminder.
- FOUR. Try to leave the state with a work token still open. OBSERVED: the walk
  does not move, and the refusal names the work.
- FIVE. Settle or move every token, then leave. OBSERVED: the walk moves, and
  what each token carried is readable afterwards without opening a transcript.

STEP THREE IS THE ONE THE STORY EXISTS FOR. Steps one, two, four and five
describe careful bookkeeping. Only step three shows the machine refusing
something that reads as diligence.

## What this demonstration cannot settle

WHETHER A STEP NOBODY MARKED IS CAUGHT. The guard holds over work that EXISTS.
A method step that was never marked mints no token, holds nothing, and is
invisible to every part of this procedure.

AND WHETHER A PERSON-ONLY STEP STOPS THE TURN CLEANLY. That path is built and
needs a person-only item standing in a walk's way, which this run may not
produce.
