## detail

`voice.SentencesIn` cuts after every full stop.
So `.md`, `.txt` and `$.model.classify` each read as several sentences.

A paragraph naming three file extensions counts as six sentences and breaks a limit of six.
A sentence naming a version counts as short and passes a limit it should break.

Both limits the group adds rest on this split.
Measured here: the design note `spec/design_input/prose-under-control.md` reports two paragraphs over the limit, and both are one sentence naming a path.

## done when

- a full stop inside a word does not end a sentence, decided by a Go test over `SentencesIn` with `.md` and `$.model.classify`
- a full stop before a space and a capital does end one, decided by the same test
- an ellipsis and a decimal number do not end one, decided by the same test
- the count over `spec` falls, decided by running the paragraph counter before and after
