## detail

The owner asks how long `se format` and `se lint` take over the tree.
A call under the battery's own step times goes in front of it.

`util/checks/battery.sh` runs the checks and reports each step.
A voice breach that reaches the tip today is found by nobody until a person looks.

Putting the two verbs at the head of the battery makes the prose a build failure, the same as a test.

## done when

- the time `se format` takes over the tree is measured and written on this token, decided by timing one run from the root
- the same for `se lint`, decided by the same method
- the two verbs run as the first steps of the battery where the pair takes under a minute, decided by `util/checks/battery.sh` and reading its step list
- the battery fails where a voice breach stands, decided by running it over a fixture holding one
