## detail

`se status` says it answers the rules in force. Its value block carries `guards.*`, `limits.*`, `panel.shown` and `work.queue_filter`, and no `control.*` at all.

So a rung a person just set does not appear. Unbind, god, ask, ideation, finish-up and stop-everything are all invisible there.

Measured in September 2026. The owner set `UNBIND=ON` and the engine recorded `UNBIND is now unbound` with ok true. `se status` answered the same before and after.

The rung is the thing a person changes by hand, and it is the one thing the status omits.

## done when

- `se status` names every control and the rung each is at, decided by running it and reading the answer
- a control changed by a keyword shows its new rung on the next status, decided by a Go test setting one and reading the answer
- the binding rung appears beside the controls, decided by the same reading
