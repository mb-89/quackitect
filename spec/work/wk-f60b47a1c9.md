## detail

Voice rule 10 says to follow the technical English rules and points at `[[spec/guidance/ASD-STE-100]]`.
That file does not exist.
Voice rule 6 points at `[[spec/guidance/stakeholders]]`, which does not exist either.

Both links ship in `spec/guidance/voice.md`, in `.github/copilot-instructions.md` and in `.claude/output-styles/quackitect.md`.
So two of the fourteen voice rules reach nothing, in every prompt this project hands an agent.

ASD-STE100 Issue 9 holds 53 writing rules and about 900 approved words.
It is free to obtain and restricted to redistribute, so the wording here is this project's own.

## approach

`spec/guidance/ASD-STE-100.md` is written as a guidance note, so it carries Motivation, Actionables and Discussion.
Its actionables are the STE rules this project takes, in this project's words, each one a thing a writer does.
It names the standard as its source and copies no text from it.

The mechanical ones move on to `spec/config/voice-rules.json`, where a pattern can hold them.
The rest stay here, where a person applies them.

`spec/guidance/stakeholders.md` is the same shape, and the owner says what it holds.

## done when

- `spec/guidance/ASD-STE-100.md` exists and validates, decided by `se lint`
- it names ASD-STE100 as its source and quotes no text from the standard, decided by reading it
- each rule a pattern can hold is named in `spec/config/voice-rules.json`, decided by reading both files
- `spec/guidance/stakeholders.md` exists and validates, decided by `se lint`
