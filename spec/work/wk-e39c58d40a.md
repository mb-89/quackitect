## detail

The guidance `Discussion` chapters narrate incidents.
`spec/guidance/voice.md` tells of a measurement written twice and a file compressed against its cap.
`src/cage/cloud-runner.md` tells of two sessions that said they were current and were wrong.

Each is a real thing that happened, and each teaches.
Each also sits on a surface a reader comes to for what to do now.

A rationale is the note kind for an argument, and its `why` chapter is the one place declaring past tense.
So each of these becomes a rationale, and the chapter that told it names the link.

## approach

The work is a pass over `spec/guidance` and `src/cage`, one chapter at a time.
A chapter narrating an incident becomes a rationale under `spec/rationale`, with `explains` naming the file it came from.
The chapter keeps one present-tense sentence stating the rule, and adds `For details, see [[link]]`.

A rationale allows 250 words in `why`, so a chapter carrying two incidents becomes two rationales.
Voice rule 9 already asks for this shape, so this applies a rule the tree carries.

The guidance chapters run 618 lines against 161 rules today, and the owner rules that ratio acceptable.
This changes what those lines say.

## done when

- every `Discussion` chapter that narrates an incident names a rationale by link, decided by reading each chapter under `spec/guidance`
- each new rationale carries `explains` naming the file its chapter is in, decided by `se lint`
- no chapter keeps a narrated incident in place, decided by the same reading
- the tense check passes over `spec/guidance`, decided by `se lint` over the tip
