## detail

Every schema section declares a tense.
`src/engine/schema.go` reads it at line 76 and line 227, and nothing else touches it.
So the field is written in three schemas and enforced nowhere.

Two sections declare the past: the `why` chapter of a rationale, and the `Discussion` chapter of guidance.
Every other section declares the present and gets no help holding it.

The owner rules that the surface is present tense, and that a rationale is where the past lives.

## approach

`ValidateNote` gains a tense reading beside the word caps it already applies.
A present-tense section is refused where a sentence opens in the past: `was`, `were`, `had`, and a verb ending in `ed` after a subject.

The rule is a heuristic, so it says what it found and names the sentence.
A section declaring `past` is left alone.

`Discussion` keeps `past` on the owner's ruling, because a chapter about a real incident is history and reads as such.
What moves out of `Discussion` is the next token.

## done when

- a present-tense section carrying a past-tense sentence is refused, decided by a Go test over one such note
- the refusal names the sentence, decided by reading the message in that test
- a section declaring `past` passes the same sentence, decided by the same test
- every note under `spec` passes, decided by `se lint` over the tip
