## detail

Level 0 proposed that a notification may travel as a message carrying no content. It says only that new bytes exist, and a reader goes and reads them.

Losing one costs latency and nothing else, because the reader still has its own clock underneath. That is what makes the message cheap to get wrong.

## what we gain

A viewer that hears about a new record line rather than discovering it on its own schedule. The content never travels, so nothing about the message can leak what the line said.

## what breaks without it

Every reader finds out on its own clock, and the window lags what the engine already knows. The lag is invisible until somebody watches the panel while an agent works.

## appetite

Small. A message with no body, and a reader that already knows how to read.

## what we do not know

Whether anything is slow enough today to be worth it. Nobody has measured the lag between a line being written and the panel drawing it.
