### Why the third row exists

THE BOOTSTRAP CLUSTER CARRIES TWO ABSTRACTIONS RATHER THAN ONE, and separating them was the useful move.

THE FIRST ROW IS ABOUT DELIVERY: how a later improvement reaches something already handed over.

THE THIRD ROW IS ABOUT ACCOUNTING: how anybody says what each holder changed. Those are different problems with different domains, and collapsing them would have sent the search to the wrong fields. Aviation answers the first and says almost nothing about the second. Textual scholarship answers the second and has no mechanism for the first at all.

AND THE PRIOR-ART FINDER ALREADY SHOWED THEY COME APART. The system that computes a copy's changes attaches no meaning to them, and the system that carries rich meaning cannot compute anything.

### What the law sweep settled, in its own vocabulary

THE TERMS OF ART ARE STATIC AND DYNAMIC INCORPORATION BY REFERENCE. Dynamic is also called ambulatory, prospective or rolling. That is precisely the fork this iteration has been circling, and it has a name and a literature.

AND THE CONSTITUTIONAL OBJECTION TO DYNAMIC ADOPTION IS THE PART THAT DOES NOT TRANSFER. A legislature adopting whatever a private body writes NEXT has delegated law-making to people the electorate cannot remove. Kansas struck down adoption of the National Electrical Code on that ground in 1919, and United States federal agencies are forbidden it outright: incorporation "is limited to the edition of the publication that is approved. Future amendments or revisions of the publication are not included."

A SOFTWARE TEAM PINNING A DEPENDENCY HAS NO SUCH CONSTRAINT. It pins for reproducibility and blast radius, never for consent. So the vocabulary carries and the reasoning does not, and an argument here that static adoption is somehow more legitimate would be importing a premise that does not hold.

### The failure mode, named by the domain itself

UNCITRAL'S MODEL LAW STATUS TABLE lists a hundred and twenty-seven jurisdictions with a year and a single version bit, then tells the reader that "the legislation of each State should be considered in order to identify the exact nature of any possible deviation from the model in the legislative text that was adopted."

THAT IS A PROVENANCE RECORD WITH NO DEVIATION RECORD. It says which version you are on and nothing about what you changed, and hands the reader a hundred and twenty-seven forks to diff by hand.

IT IS ALSO EXACTLY WHAT A VENDORING MANIFEST DOES. The prior-art finder found the same shape in software and did not have a name for it. The name is now available.
