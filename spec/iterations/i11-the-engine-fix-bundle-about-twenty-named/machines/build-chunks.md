# The build plan

Twelve chunks over one surface: what a lane call costs.

## THIS PLAN IS WIDE WHERE i34'S WAS DEEP, and the difference is real

i34 HAD ONE CHAIN OF EIGHT because each chunk removed the thing the next would
trip on. Nothing here works like that. Nine of the twelve depend on nothing at
all, because they touch different verbs on the same lane.

SO PARALLEL BUILDERS ARE GENUINELY USEFUL HERE, which is the first time that
has been true for a while. `pull-payload`, `mirror-buttons`, `narration-grace`
and `promotions-are-own-record` cannot collide.

## The two pairings, and neither is negotiable at build time

TWO CHUNKS EACH CARRY WORK THAT MUST NOT SHIP HALF-DONE, and both are on the
register with their reasoning.

- `the-bucket` holds THREE pieces: the permission, the guard, and the
  close-side reader. A permission whose guard is missing is strictly weaker
  than the unchecked box it replaces — `- [ ]` reads as unfinished, `- [owed]`
  reads as dispositioned.
  [[raid-risk-an-owed-item-without-a-guard-ships-a-known-defect]]
- `test-verb` holds BOTH clauses. The refusal alone strands 428 wasted polls.
  The answer alone removes the accidental deterrent — a battery currently costs
  a handoff plus ten calls of watching — and leaves the judgment that was wrong
  five times in one day.
  [[raid-dec-blocking-and-the-battery-refusal-ship-together]]

## What each chunk turns green

Six reds were authored and watched failing, run `test-msvqis6r-25`, 0 of 6
passing.

- `the-bucket` turns three green: the owed item refused with no open entry, the
  well-formed owed item accepted, and the close refusing while one stands.
- `test-verb` turns two green: the battery refused outside verification, and a
  scoped run answering its caller instead of handing back a job id.
- `deletion-names-dependents` turns one green: the delete naming a node that
  cites it IN PROSE. That case is written so a graph-only implementation still
  fails, because the graph sees roughly a fifth of what a deletion breaks
  ([[raid-asm-the-trace-graph-holds-every-reference]], probed false in part).

SIX CHUNKS TURN NO AUTHORED RED GREEN and each says why. `pull-payload`,
`lane-shape`, `walk-repairs` and `narration-grace` are measured rather than
asserted — their pass lines are calls per state and bytes per answer, and
verification takes the measurement. `mirror-buttons` is judged by the owner
using it, which is stated rather than dressed up as a number.
`promotions-are-own-record` and `pipe-refused` are refusals, and the honest
place to prove a refusal is the case that provokes it — both get one during
their own chunk.

## Three chunks came from the walk demonstrating the defect

`engine-runs-the-red`, `promotions-are-own-record` and `pipe-refused` were not
in the seed. All three were ruled by the owner at observe-red, after the state
hit each one live: observe-red could not use se_test, specify-build refused on
another record's promotions, and a shell fallback ate its own output.

[[raid-dec-the-engine-runs-the-red-and-owns-its-own-promotions]] carries all
three with their rejected options.

## The audit goes last, and depends on three chunks

`audit-the-twenty` waits for `pull-payload`, `walk-repairs` and `lane-shape`,
because several of the fourteen unchecked defects live in exactly those files.
Reading them first would mean reading them twice.

THE RULE IT CARRIES: anything already fixed is struck WITH its evidence, and
anything live but outside this surface is RECORDED, not fixed. That second half
is what keeps the bundle closable — without it the plan grows every time
somebody reads it.

## The tests move with the chunk that breaks them

NO SEPARATE TEST-REWRITING CHUNK, same as i34 and for the same reason. A chunk
that leaves the suite red for a later chunk to repair cannot be judged on its
own, and the plan would then have one step whose whole job is fixing the others.
