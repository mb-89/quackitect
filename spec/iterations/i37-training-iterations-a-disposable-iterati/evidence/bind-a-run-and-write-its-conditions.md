## anything_else

THE READ RATCHET IS THE BEST THING THAT HAPPENED IN THIS CHUNK, and it is worth saying why rather than just that it passed.

I WROTE FIVE DIRECT READS AND BELIEVED ALL FIVE WERE FINE. The ratchet does not ban them — ninety-nine are legitimate — it only refuses to let the number rise without somebody deciding it should. Being made to write the reason down is what showed that two of the five were reading NOTES, which the door exists to serve, and a third was re-reading a manifest that already has exactly one reader for a documented reason.

SO THREE OF FIVE WERE WRONG AND NOTHING ELSE WOULD HAVE CAUGHT THEM. `tsc` was clean, biome was clean, and every behavioural test passed. The design was right and the plumbing was wrong, and only a check that asks for a JUSTIFICATION rather than a result could tell.

THAT IS DIRECTLY THIS ITERATION'S SUBJECT. A benchmark measures what a walk COSTS, and a ratchet like this one is a cost the machine imposes on purpose: it makes an agent stop and argue. The number it would produce — a slow state, several refusals, a fix — looks identical to drag. It is not drag. Any report that ranks states by cost will rank this kind of state badly, and the report has no way to tell the difference.

THAT LIMIT BELONGS ON THE RECORD RATHER THAN IN A HEAD. It is not a defect in the design; it is what `WHAT IS MEASURED IS THE MACHINE'S DRAG, NEVER QUALITY` means in practice, and this is the first time this walk has met an example of it that cuts the other way.
