It sits behind two dependencies, which means it is also the chunk most likely to be reached late and rushed.

ONE THING THE CHUNK LIST DELIBERATELY DOES NOT CONTAIN. There is no chunk for authoring a scenario pool, a sandbox package, or fake test fixtures. All three were struck by owner ruling at the kickoff, because the archive already holds real design inputs and a re-walked iteration writes real tests against the real tree. The build is smaller than the original brief by exactly that much, and that is the design working rather than scope being lost.
