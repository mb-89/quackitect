# The demonstrations, three must stories

## Why three, and why each earns a person

ALL THREE STORIES ARE GRADED MUST, and the reason is shared: the value prop
they refine is priority must, and the pass lines behind them are zero-target
or all-of-them. A target like that cannot be shown by sampling. It is shown by
a demonstration that the failure does not occur on a real pass.

THE THIRD ONE EARNS ITS PERSON DIFFERENTLY, and the difference is worth
stating rather than blurring. Its pass line IS instrument-readable: a clock and
a list of interfaces answer it with no watcher needed. What the demonstration
adds is whether the DENOMINATOR is real — whether the modelled list covers the
interfaces a person or an agent actually touches, and not merely the ones that
were easy to model. That is a judgment, and it is why a person is still owed.

## What each one is looking for

THE CONTROL STORY looks for silence. A decline that emits nothing is
indistinguishable from a control that was never pressed, and the panel's own
history records the emergency rung and the shutdown row failing exactly that
way before the stop-at notch did it again. Three sightings, no check.

THE SLOW-CALL STORY looks for the opposite failure of the same rule. Not
silence but noise: a signal so loud or so discouraging that the person
abandons a wait worth finishing. The motivation gate's own prior-art scan
argued against our first reading of honest, and the narrowed ruling is what
this demonstration tests — never leave a person guessing, and do not mistake
that for publishing a faithful completion percentage.

THE SPEED STORY looks for a denominator with holes in it. Thirteen boundaries
are modelled and twelve of them have no calls attributed to them, so today the
instrument can only speak for one crossing. A demonstration that reads the
clock on a list nobody has checked for completeness proves less than it looks
like it proves.

## What this drawing is not

IT DOES NOT SAY WHEN. Two of the three need a person at this machine and one
of them needs people who are not us, and all three sit in
raid-debt-ten-checks-wait-on-a-person-or-a-second-machine with what each
actually needs. This drawing says WHAT is owed and in what shape; the debt
entry says who can discharge it.

NO STEP DEPENDS ON ANOTHER. The three passes are independent, so a person with
one hour can take any one of them without the other two being scheduled first.
