# Evidence form / scope-non-goals

## current_situation

The delta is framed and the proposition it serves is named. The register holds five entries.

This state says how much of the gap this iteration takes on, and what it deliberately leaves.

The kickoff already wrote a pulled-in and a left-out list. This state re-reads that list rather than re-deriving it, and sharpens it now that the baseline is known.

## scope

FOUR THINGS ARE IN, and the first two are one design rather than two.

1. ONE CALL REPORTS EVERY PIECE OF WORK RUNNING OUT OF SIGHT. Shell jobs and test jobs in one answer. Today they live in two tables, `jobList` for the shell and `testVerdicts` for tests, and no verb reads both.

2. EACH ENTRY CARRIES A TIME REMAINING WITH ITS BASIS NAMED. Not a rate, and not the previous run's wall clock reported at start. Where no basis exists the entry says it cannot estimate.

3. A LEAVING CONDITION NEVER FREEZES THE WALK. The exit script starts, the pull answers at once, and the verdict arrives on a later call. This is the part that moves the walk's hop-completion contract, and it is why the change size is `major`.

4. THE ENGINE PICKS TESTS THAT ANSWER FOR A CHANGE. The two `decideScope` branches that fall back to the whole battery for a diff nothing maps to are narrowed, and the fallback says plainly when no test answers rather than running every test.

AND THE REFUSAL THAT POINTS AT THE WRONG LIST IS FIXED WITH THEM. `JOB_UNKNOWN` today hands back `se_run {jobs: true}` as its remedy, which cannot hold the test job the caller asked about. A remedy that answers a different question is a defect in the work under this iteration's hands, not a stray.

TWO DEFECTS FOUND ENTERING THE RECORD ARE ALREADY IN, under the standing engine-improvements goal. Ten rigor-matrix gate rows carried a literal backslash-n where newlines belonged, and a record with no pin never refreshed the machine frame it walks on. Both are patched and both are owed a test.

ONE CORRECTION LANDED AT LOG-RISKS and is in scope as the same kind of thing. A register entry carried a likelihood word the scale does not have.

## non_goals

SEVEN THINGS ARE OUT, each with the reason it is out rather than merely unlisted.

- WORK STARTED BY ANOTHER SESSION OR ANOTHER CLONE. One agent works one clone, so the report covers this lane's own jobs. Reaching further would need a shared store this product deliberately does not have.

- THE MIRROR'S PRESENTATION OF THE JOB LIST. The goal is one lane call. What a screen does with the answer is a separate question with a separate reader.

- RETIRING `se_run {jobs: true}`. It stays until the unified report has proven itself. Removing the old door before the new one is walked through is how a working path is lost.

- REPAIRING THE BATTERY'S TIMING INSTRUMENT. `raid-asm-battery-timings-measure-work` stands open and is cited as a risk against the estimate. Absorbing it would double the iteration and is not needed for an estimate that names its own basis.

- A PROGRESS BAR FOR THE AGENT. A condition script already emits `##progress done total label` for the mirror. Reading those lines back into the report is a tempting extra and belongs to whichever record takes the presentation question.

- MEASURING WHAT POLLING COSTS. Nobody has counted how often an agent polls a running job. The iteration does not lean on that number, so it does not owe it.

- A DEFERRED VERDICT FOR ANYTHING BUT SCRIPTS. Entry conditions, form checks and the corpus guards stay synchronous. Widening the deferred shape beyond the exit script would move the contract further than the goals need.

THE HARD EDGE IS THE THIRD GOAL. Everything else here is additive and could be cut without breaking what is left. The non-freezing exit cannot: it changes what green means, so it is either done properly or not started.
