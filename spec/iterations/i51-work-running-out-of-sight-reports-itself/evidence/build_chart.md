## anything_else

THE ROWS WERE WRONG UNTIL THE ENGINE REFUSED THEM, and that is the finding worth keeping.

Every finder wrote its options into `cluster-the-walk` or `cluster-the-record-life`, because those are the clusters partition-functions settled and the option template asks for a cluster.

THOSE ARE FUNCTION CLUSTERS, NOT CHART ROWS. A function cluster groups what passes data to what. A chart row groups things that are ALTERNATIVES to each other. The two coincide often enough that nobody notices, and here they did not.

THE REFUSAL CAUGHT IT MECHANICALLY: every candidate picked twice in both clusters. Nothing in the method prose would have.

WHAT THE RE-CUT PRODUCED: four rows where two stood, and a box holding 72 combinations where the old shape held nine. The space got bigger by being described properly.
