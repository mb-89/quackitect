## current_situation

Forty-eight quality scenarios stand and the declared architecture is walked through every one.

SEVEN MOVE. Two are newly at risk, two worsen, one narrows, one is answered outright, and one is more exposed than it was.

GATE-ARCHITECTURE READS THIS NEXT, and three findings go to it.

- [[raid-ar-walk-resumes-from-repo]] is graded fatal and is new. What a fresh session does with a step it finds still deciding is undecided, and M7 has to decide it.

The walk does not close register entries; naming it here is how it reaches the party that does.

TWO FITNESS CANDIDATES ARE NEWLY FLAGGED and feed M7's author-tests. The flag is written on both requirement nodes.

NOTHING SHAKES THE CHOICE. No finding here reopens converge-pugh, and no finding ripples a requirement back to M3. Every scenario the walk touched already existed.

### On the sycophancy guard

THIS ARCHITECTURE IS WORSE THAN THE ONE IT REPLACES ON TWO AXES, and the walk says so before it says anything good: a step's standing stops being derivable from the tree, and a per-call read appears that scales with running work.

WHAT IT BUYS IS ONE THING: the pull stops freezing on a leaving check. That is the iteration's goal and the trade is deliberate, not free.
