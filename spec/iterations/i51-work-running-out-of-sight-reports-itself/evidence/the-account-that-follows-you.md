THE RIDER MEETS THE ANSWER IT RIDES ON, and three rules hold it in place. Each exists because of a way this candidate fails without it.

- IT APPEARS ONLY WHEN THERE IS SOMETHING TO SAY. An answer carrying "nothing out of sight" on every call is a field nobody reads, and a field nobody reads is a field nobody notices changing.
- IT CARRIES A COUNT AND A CHANGE, NEVER THE ACCOUNT. What is running, and what finished since the caller last heard. The account itself stays behind the verb that asks for it.
- IT NEVER GROWS WITH THE WORK. Three jobs and thirty produce the same rider size.

THE THIRD RULE IS THE ONE WITH HISTORY BEHIND IT. This product moved big results to disk and pages them back by reference precisely because answers grew. A rider proportional to the work is that problem returning through a door nobody was watching.

WITH THE STATE WORD, and this is a real difference rather than an inherited seam. A state word admits new values later without every reader changing. A closed set of three does not.

THAT CUTS BOTH WAYS, and the note says so. Admitting new values later is exactly what lets a reader silently ignore one, which is the failure the closed set makes loud.

WITH THE ACKNOWLEDGE-THEN-REPORT SHAPE. The predecessor's once-a-minute progress bound and the rider are the same promise from two directions: the caller learns something without asking. This is the only line taking both, and a design taking one without the other has half a mechanism.

THE ROUGH COST CHECK. The three rules are the cost. None is a mechanism; all three are discipline, and this product's own history says rules about answer size are the ones that get broken.

## anything_else

THIS CANDIDATE IS THE ONLY ONE WHOSE COSTS ARE ALL DISCIPLINE.

Every other line pays in mechanism: a field to compute, a value to represent, a rule to derive. Those can be got wrong once and then tested.

THE RIDER'S THREE RULES CANNOT BE TESTED THE SAME WAY. Nothing fails when a rider grows a little. It fails after several iterations of growing a little, by which time the reason for each addition is on record and the aggregate is not.

THAT IS WORTH SAYING BEFORE THE SCORING rather than after. A candidate whose risk is drift will score well on everything measurable, and the evaluation should know that going in.
