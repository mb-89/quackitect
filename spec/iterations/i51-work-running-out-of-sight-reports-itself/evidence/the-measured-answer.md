EVERY SEAM ON THIS CANDIDATE MEETS AT THE BASIS FIELD.

- WITH THE ESTIMATE. The duration and its basis must move together, or the design has kept the precision and lost the point. A basis that goes stale while the number updates is worse than none, because a reader checks it once and then stops. SO THE COMPUTATION RETURNS BOTH OR NEITHER — one return value, not two fields somebody remembers to fill.
- WITH THE LISTING. Every entry carries the pair, including entries that cannot estimate. A shell command's entry has an empty duration and a basis saying why, rather than being a different shape from a battery's entry.
- WITH THE HANDBACK. Nothing crosses. The leaving judgment's own progress is not reported as a duration on the step, because a step is not an entry in the listing. This candidate inherits that seam from the quiet handback unchanged.

WHAT THE UNIFORMITY BUYS, and it is the rough feasibility check this state owes. One shape for every kind of work means the no-estimate case is an ordinary value rather than a special case, so a caller writes one piece of handling rather than two.

THE COST CHECK IS PROPORTIONAL AND NO MORE. The estimate itself was measured at find_by_probing, so the expensive-looking cell is a division over numbers already written down. The basis field is a second value on the same return.

## follow_up

Two compose states remain, then the cut and the evaluation.

One seam is a build constraint rather than a description. The duration and its basis come from one computation returning both, and a design that fills them separately has reintroduced the drift the pair exists to prevent.

## anything_else

THE SEAM WRITING CHANGED WHAT THIS CANDIDATE IS, slightly and usefully.

Drawn on the chart, it reads as the quiet handback with a number added. Written as seams, the number is not the addition — the PAIR is, and the pair is one value.

THAT MAKES IT CHEAPER THAN IT LOOKED and harder to get wrong in a way that matters. Cheaper because there is one thing to compute rather than two. Harder to get wrong because two fields filled independently is exactly how a basis goes stale.

AND IT SHARPENS THE COMPARISON WITH THE QUIET HANDBACK. The difference between them is not "does it estimate". It is whether every entry in the listing carries a pair of fields, one of which is often empty.
