# The candidate drawing

One compose state per line drawn on the chart at build_chart. Four lines were
drawn there, sampled along the one axis that reorganises everything else: how
much the product volunteers about the future.

- THE QUIET HANDBACK volunteers nothing. The walk stops freezing, one listing
  holds every kind of work out of sight, and no entry anywhere carries a
  duration. It cannot be wrong, because it makes no claim about what has not
  happened yet.
- THE MEASURED ANSWER volunteers a figure when asked, and volunteers its own
  uncertainty with it. The duration and what it was computed from are two
  fields, and an entry with an empty basis carries no figure at all.
- THE GUIDED CALLER volunteers a figure and an instruction. The linear estimate
  measured on this machine, plus the entry saying when it is worth asking
  again — which is the only cell on the chart that helps a piece of work with
  nothing to count.
- THE ACCOUNT THAT FOLLOWS YOU volunteers without being asked. The
  predecessor's acknowledge-then-report shape, the standard's state word rather
  than a closed set, a duration recomputed on every ask, and a small rider on
  every lane answer so the caller never has to ask at all.

They are independent and all four hang off start; the join waits for every one.

## What each state writes

It writes back into the candidate note its line already created, and mints
nothing:

- How it works - the whole architecture, and especially the seams between the
  chosen options.
- What it costs - rough feasibility checks, proportional and no more.
- What it leans on - what has to be true for it to work.

Nothing here is scored. Judging stays at evaluate-set, because an agent that
knows the score while it writes the description writes a description that earns
the score.

## Three lines share two cells, and that is the finding rather than a defect

The quiet handback, the measured answer and the guided caller all take the
separation in time on the handback row, and the closed word on the standing
row.

Trimming asked what happens without either. Nothing takes over from the
deferred verdict, and without the third standing value the deferred verdict
becomes unbuildable.

So a compose state should describe those two seams rather than re-argue them.
The fourth line takes the other cell on both rows precisely so the comparison
has something to compare.
