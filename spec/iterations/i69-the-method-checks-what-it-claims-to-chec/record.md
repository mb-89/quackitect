# i69-the-method-checks-what-it-claims-to-chec

## Goal

The method checks what it claims to check: the checks that do not exist get built, the checks that are wrong get fixed, and the suite is proved able to notice a mechanism going missing.

## Rough vision

Done looks like a green board that means something. The measured fact behind it: five working parts were deleted one at a time and every one of over sixteen hundred cases still passed, so today a whole green board says only that nothing crashed. Around that sit eighteen standing findings of the same shape. A check runs at one position and nowhere else, so four broken flows reached trunk. A rule fires wrongly whenever its section opens a body. A boundary can be designed, specified and never built with both checks passing over the hole. A step accepts an empty result without inspecting what it claims to inspect. The suite itself carries standing failures nobody counts. Each is the method asserting a guarantee it does not hold.
