## current_situation

NO CODE. A spike produces an answer, and this one produced a count and five ids.

EVERY STRUCK STATEMENT WAS READ, ninety-nine of ninety-nine. The statements were pulled as one frontmatter key across the whole requirement folder, joined to each row's rank and its quoted cut reason, then judged against what the three designs commit to. Nothing was sampled and nothing was judged from a name.

A CLEAN CONTEXT DID THE READING. It was given the three designs, the cut, and the rule that a row is wrongly struck only where at least two designs commit to DIFFERENT behaviour. It was not told what the earlier check had found.

### What it returned

FIVE MORE ROWS STRUCK WRONGLY, on top of the two the gate found by sampling ten.

- `req-scaffold-from-template`, rank 14, above the line.
- `req-extension-replaced-reported`, rank 58.
- `req-method-reuse-is-vendoring`, rank 86, and named as the weakest.
- `req-purpose-recorded-at-begin`, rank 91, and the strongest on merit.
- `req-begin-says-own-window`, rank 108.

ALL FIVE SHARE ONE BLIND SPOT. Nobody checked the bootstrap cluster. The third design picks a cell that deletes the installer AND the scaffold, and every row describing scaffolding was struck as untouched.

### A second defect, and it is worse than the one being probed

THREE ROWS CARRY THE REASON "vehicles, untouched" AND NONE IS ABOUT A VEHICLE. Three genuine vehicle rows sit nearby carrying the same reason.

A REASON WAS COPIED DOWN A BLOCK. Judging from a name at least looks at the row. This did not.

### One of the five did not survive scoring

A SECOND CLEAN HAND SCORED ALL FIVE across the three designs and DISAGREED ON ONE. `req-begin-says-own-window` scores nought for every design, because the word window appears in none of them and in none of the cells they pick.

BOTH HANDS ARE RIGHT AND THE SEAM IS WORTH NAMING. The third design does behave differently: it removes the moment at which the statement would be made. That is a behaviour difference, and it is not a score difference, because no design addresses the clause at all. A row can differentiate and still tie.

### What it does not change

### What it makes worse

THE DISCRIMINATION PROBLEM. Twelve axes separated the two leaders on two. A thirteenth they tie on makes it two of thirteen.

EITHER THE CRITERIA DO NOT DISCRIMINATE, OR THE CUT REMOVES THE ROWS THAT WOULD. This spike cannot tell which. It only looked at rows that were cut, and nobody has asked whether the twelve that survived were the right twelve.

### What the retro inherits

THE ECONOMICS RUN THE WRONG WAY, and this spike is the proof. Striking a row costs one line. Reading its statement costs one script and one pass over a folder, which is all this spike was, and it found five errors nobody had seen.

A CHEAPER GUARD WOULD CATCH THE PASTED REASONS WITH NO JUDGMENT AT ALL: a cut whose reason does not quote the row's own statement is unverified by construction, and can be marked so mechanically.

THE SEEDING CARRIED NO FALLBACK. All three spikes were seeded with a question and a timebox and nothing about what to do if the answer came back badly. That is recorded on each experiment node rather than only here.
