## current_situation

THREE ELEMENTS HAD NO DESIGN SPEC AND ALL THREE WERE THIS ITERATION'S OWN. Twenty-two elements, thirty-two specs, and the three uncovered were exactly the three i9 minted.

ZERO ARE UNCOVERED NOW, and no spec names no files.

### The three specs

- THE STATE DECLARATION. Two facts and nothing else: what the folder is called, and which files have a structured door. Five consumers are generated from it, and the staleness check that guards them is no longer tidy work.
- THE ONE CORPUS READER. Deletion over reconciliation, with the cost named: every form's node list comes from the whole corpus afterwards, measured at 359 ms cold and 11 to 13 ms stamped against a five-second bound.
- THE INSTALL PREFLIGHT. Complete to a named standard of five properties, with the open tension declared rather than netted away: carrying the remediation means the person installs the tool, and the setup floor demands the command do it.

### The chunk machine, and the lenses that shaped it

NINE CHUNKS. Three lots that run in parallel, and each later chunk leans on ONE earlier chunk rather than three.

SPINE FIRST is the main lens. The declaration is the thinnest slice everything else crosses: the consumers, the exclusion split, the root check and the move all read it. Building it first exercises every seam that matters.

RISK FIRST shapes what sits early inside the spine. The root check carries the only FATAL axis on the chart, and the move carries twenty-six hard-coded sites that no rename reaches. Neither is left to the end.

PARALLEL FLOW gives the other two lots. The corpus reader depends on nothing here and fans out on its own. So does the preflight. Neither touches the declaration, and that is why they are separate strands rather than steps in one chain.

THE BRANCH PIN IS THE ONLY DEEP EDGE. It waits on the move, because a test that the folder resolves to one place across a branch switch cannot run before the folder has moved.

## follow_up

### What the build inherits

EVERY CHUNK NAMES ITS OWN CLAIM, and the test specs authored one state earlier name what each must prove. Nothing is copied between them.

ONE PROMOTED SPIKE ENTERS AS A STARTING CHUNK. The eleven recorded search queries land in the move, where re-running them is the check that nothing was missed. The old folder name returning nothing is the pass.

THREE TREES MUST BE SEARCHED BY HAND during the move, because version control ignores them and no sweep will look: the build output, the machine-state folder itself, and the workbench. The folder's own contents matter most, because a generated client script sits inside it carrying its own path.

### What the order deliberately does not do

IT DOES NOT RENAME OR RESHAPE ANYTHING WHILE MOVING. That is a non-goal on the record, and without it a reviewer cannot tell a relocated file from a deleted one.

IT DOES NOT PRUNE. The owner rules what should not have travelled, after everything is in one place. Their candidates are examples rather than a list.

### Two things the build owes that the design does not settle

WHERE THE INSTALL LINE FALLS: which tools the command installs itself, and which it reports for the person to install. The preflight standard and the setup floor pull against each other, and a second hand found it while scoring rather than the composer while designing.

HOW DEEP THE POST-INSTALL CHECK GOES. Answering the health endpoint is the floor. The requirement's measure is that the demonstration runs on the first attempt.
