## goal_system

INHERITED WHERE UNTOUCHED. The resident vision stands. What follows is this delta's own goals and the conflicts between them.

THREE CONFLICTS, ALL RULED HERE.

### One: legibility pulls against the structured door

THE STANDING GOAL is that some things are read through a door. The call log, the notes and the reading each have a verb serving them with structure, ordering and paging. A raw read loses all three.

THIS DELTA'S GOAL is that the folder stops being invisible. Hiding it wholesale costs three concrete things the record names.

THE RULING: HIDE WHAT HAS A DOOR, SERVE WHAT DOES NOT.

WHY THE CONFLICT WAS NEVER REAL once stated properly. The door rule is about THREE FILES. Hiding the whole directory was a proxy for it, and the proxy over-reached onto everything else in the folder. A proxy that catches more than its rule is not the rule.

### Two: the entry point pulls against the person's consent

THIS IS THE SHARP ONE and it is live rather than theoretical.

ONE GOAL SAYS opening a project gets it everything it needs, with no act of the person's.

THE STANDING INSTINCT SAYS nothing happens to somebody's folder that they did not ask for. The owner acted on it twice in one sitting — they raised automatic seeding and struck it minutes later, and they accepted a restart message rather than a silent reload.

THE RULING: CONVENIENCE INSIDE A PROJECT, CONSENT AT ITS EDGE.

- A folder that is NOT a project is untouched. Nothing installs, nothing starts, nothing is written. It simply does not work there.
- A folder that IS a project was seeded deliberately, and that act is the consent. Inside it, setup happens without asking again.
- ANYTHING THAT CANNOT APPLY SILENTLY ANNOUNCES ITSELF rather than going quiet or forcing a reload.

WHY THE LINE SITS THERE. Seeding is the moment a person says this folder is mine to drive. Everything after is honouring that, and everything before would be presuming it.

A FOURTH BULLET WAS ADDED AT THE M2 GATE AND STRUCK BY THE OWNER THE SAME DAY, 2026-08-19. It made a cloned folder ask once before starting, on the argument that a clone arrives carrying the marks of somebody else's seeding rather than the opener's own decision. Three widely used systems were cited, each keeping a trust decision outside the tree.

THE OWNER'S RULING, in their own words: if it finds the machine-state folder it can work on it, and if it does not it does not work on it, and that is it.

WHY THE ARGUMENT DID NOT SURVIVE, recorded so nobody rebuilds it. All three cited systems guard against a tree that can EXECUTE on arrival - hooks, tasks, scripts - which is why each keeps its trust decision elsewhere. The machine-state folder runs nothing. It holds a call log, notes and session state, and coming up against it starts a caged agent that does nothing until a person types a sentence.

SO THE PROMPT'S BENEFIT WAS BORROWED AND ITS COST WAS REAL. Every clone onto a second machine would have paid a question, against a threat that does not exist here, and this project already runs on two machines.

THE RULING THEREFORE STANDS AS FIRST WRITTEN. Seeding is the consent, and the folder answers for itself every time it is opened.

### Three: the collapse pulls against reviewability

MOVING EVERYTHING AT ONCE is the goal. Reviewing what moved is what makes it safe.

A MOVE THAT ALSO RENAMES AND DELETES CANNOT BE READ, because nothing tells a relocated file from a removed one.

THE RULING: MOVE FIRST, CLEAN SECOND, AND STOP IN BETWEEN. The cleanup is the owner's, over a list, after everything is in one place.

## big_idea

A method that engineering work cannot walk around.

The person says what they need. An agent does the work, and every move it makes is one the system served it — its own tools are switched off, every call is recorded, and what it is allowed to do next is decided by a machine rather than by its own judgment. Where a decision needs a person, the walk stops and waits.

WHAT THAT BUYS somebody deciding whether to use it: the work leaves a record that was not written afterwards. What was decided, what it rested on, and what was still unproven are all captured as the work happened, because the system would not let it proceed otherwise.

RESTATED, NOT AUTHORED HERE. This is the resident vision. This delta does not move it, and the major column asks the field rather than asking for a new answer.

## to_be_world

A weekday morning. The engineer opens a folder in their editor — their own product, not this one.

THE FOLDER IS ALREADY A PROJECT, because they seeded it deliberately once. Nothing happened to it that they did not ask for.

BY THE TIME THE WINDOW HAS PAINTED, the lane is answering. They did not run anything. If something changed that a running window cannot pick up, a small message says so and offers to reload. They click it or they do not.

THEY TYPE WHAT THEY WANT in their own words. Not a command — a sentence. The system tells them what it thinks that is, and what it would cost, and waits.

THE AGENT WORKS. On the side of the screen a list fills in as it goes, so the engineer can see where it is without asking. They read their mail.

IT STOPS AT A GATE. One page: what was decided, what it rests on, what is still unproven, and the dissent it wants recorded. They read four paragraphs, disagree with one, and say so in a sentence. The agent takes the correction and keeps going.

THEY NEVER OPEN A TERMINAL. Not because it is hidden, but because nothing asked them to.

AND AT THE END OF THE DAY the record is already written. Not summarised afterwards from memory — written as the work happened, because the machine would not let the work happen otherwise.

## moore_pitch

FOR an engineer who wants agents to do real work on a real codebase,
WHO needs the result to be defensible afterwards and cannot get that from a chat transcript,
THE product IS A method the work runs inside rather than a tool it uses,
THAT records what was decided and what it rested on as the work happens, and stops for a person wherever judgment is owed.

UNLIKE an agent harness, which gives the model more reach and leaves the discipline to whoever is watching,
OUR PRODUCT switches the model's own tools off and serves it a lane instead — so what it may do next is a decision the system made, not one the model talked itself into.
