### The cheapest rework in the iteration, by two orders of magnitude

TEN FORMS RE-SIGNED IN 120 SECONDS at M4 and M5. Seven fitness flags landing late invalidated a step far upstream, and ten forms between them re-signed between 15:38:54 and 15:40:54. One records that its scores came back byte-identical.

THAT IS THE RECHECK BLOCK DOING EXACTLY WHAT IT EXISTS FOR, and it is worth naming beside the expensive rework above. The difference is that a recheck asks only whether the named change moved the claim, where a reopen asks the question again from nothing.

### What the walk could not establish

- PER-STEP COST IN CALLS OR MINUTES. Every time figure above is a signature-to-signature gap covering reading, chat and owner discussion as well as the step itself. This is the fourth retro running to say so.
- HOW MUCH EACH REOPEN COST. The stamp carries a timestamp and a reason, not a call count. One form's "roughly an hour of writing" is the only figure any form gives.
- WHETHER A REOPENED FORM'S FIRST BODY DIFFERED MATERIALLY. Only the latest body is on disk; earlier ones are in git history nobody read.
- WHETHER THE 19 FORMS WITH NO REOPEN STAMP WERE CLEAN. A form saved and resubmitted without a reopen leaves no mark, so a clean stamp is not proof of a clean first pass.
- THE CLOSE'S SWEEP COUNT. Two gates give different figures over different spans and neither is derivable from the other.
