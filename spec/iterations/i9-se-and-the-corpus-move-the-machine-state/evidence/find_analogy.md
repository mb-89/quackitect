### The three mechanisms described, and what broke in each translation

The table above holds the abstraction and the domains, which is what its columns ask for. The transfers themselves are here, because a transfer with no losses was not a transfer.

### Air traffic control, into the walk

Control of an aircraft moves by a named handover. The releasing controller states it; the receiving controller acknowledges. There is never a moment when two believe they hold it, and never one when nobody does. Ambiguity is prevented rather than resolved afterwards.

WHAT TRANSFERS: whatever starts the system states the tree, and the lane echoes back what it took. Neither side searched and neither guessed.

WHAT BREAKS: both ends there are live people who notice silence. A process does not, so an acknowledgement nobody reads is a hope rather than a handover.

### Emergency egress, into the bootstrap

One standard marker, learned in other buildings, repeated at every decision point, placed where the eye already goes, working without anything being read first.

WHAT TRANSFERS: a person can arrive here in three places — the hosting page, an editor on the folder, a terminal in it — and the way in should be marked in all three.

WHAT BREAKS: exit signs have a legally mandated form and we have none to borrow. It becomes one meaning in three different forms, which can drift.

### Epidemiology, into the query

Many observers count one outbreak the same way not because one of them counts, but because a written case definition fixes the ambiguous middle in advance.

WHAT TRANSFERS: define what a malformed node IS and what every reader must do about it. Two readers of a well-formed node already agreed; they diverged only on the malformed one.

WHAT BREAKS: a case definition is enforced by people trained on it. Code needs a check holding readers to it, and without that check this is weaker than simply having one reader.
