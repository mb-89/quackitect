THE FIRST THING THAT LANDED: THE MINT COST IS HONESTLY UNSETTLED. Its own probe asks for one hop timed twice, with minting and without, and minting does not exist. The script measured one source of three and the experiment now says so in its `faked` field rather than claiming none.

THE SECOND: THE BOUND IS SETTLED AND IT IS NOT THE ONE THIS PHASE USED. The 250 ms binds the FLIP. The requirement's own table puts "the state's own reading, condition scripts and entry duties" on the unbounded side, and minting is an entry duty. What it owes instead is a signal if it runs long. The measured fraction is 0.570 ms and the whole act would have to be some hundreds of times that to matter — which is an argument, labelled one, and not a measurement.

THE THIRD: THE MARK NOW COVERS THE CARD THAT PRODUCED IT. The first decision covered headings only, and the retro's twelve steps are list items. It now marks any line that opens a part, heading or top-level list item, with markdown's own scoping doing the rest.

THREE OVERRIDES ARE LOGGED WITH THIS PASS.

One. THE MINT COST IS UNMEASURED AND UNMEASURABLE UNTIL MINTING EXISTS. This gate's purpose is validating the riskiest assumptions, and one of them cannot be validated yet. Passing over that is the override, and it is passed because the entry itself said so before the phase began.

Two. THE DRAG FALLS AND FORCES AN ARCHITECTURE CHANGE THAT IS NOT DESIGNED. The state machine and the work editor must share one webview. The shape of that sharing is the build's.

Three. 137 CARDS WILL BE MARKED BY HAND, and nothing recovers the intent automatically from cards written before this system existed. That is a pass over a large manual step rather than a measured risk.

WHAT NO LONGER STANDS AS A DISSENT. The record folder is already capped at forty characters, so a residual risk this phase published does not exist. The vault was never a question. The 1,535 ms figure reads two ways because it answers two questions, now separated in the text.

THE PATTERN, SAID ONCE. Every fault at both gates today was a cheap proxy passing for the real thing: a fraction of an act timed and reported as the act, a count taken on convenient text, a scope argument standing in for a probe. Three cold reviews found what three self-reviews did not, and that is the strongest argument in this record for keeping them.
