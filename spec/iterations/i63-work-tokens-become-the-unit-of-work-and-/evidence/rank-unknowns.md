## follow_up

Seven spikes, and they are not equal. Two can reverse the design and five only shape it.

THE TWO THAT CAN REVERSE IT.

The cross-panel drag. Three things must hold and only the first is ordinary: a drag begun in one panel received by another, a payload carrying enough to identify the work, and the receiving panel changing what it shows mid-drag. Nothing in the product drags anything between panels today. A negative answer reopens a signed comparison.

Read evidence outliving its session. The design mints an incoming item only where reading evidence is absent, and the register already records that the evidence does NOT outlive the session. If that stands, every session re-reads everything and the incoming slot is noise rather than work.

THE FIVE THAT SHAPE IT.

Minting on every entry, against the per-hop budget. It is on the hot path and it is this record's sharpest open assumption.

A heading per item, against the retro card. The design says a method's marked headings become its items, and the register already names the retro card as the case that decides whether that is true.

One file per item, at volume, in both neighbours. The repository half is measured: 1,292 files put git add at 1,535 ms. The vault half is not measured at all.

A file path per item, against both platforms. Cheap to answer and silent when it fails, which is the worst combination to leave unprobed.

A position's count, against a real record. The surface assumes a small number a person reads at a glance, and nobody has counted what a real record actually owes.

TIMEBOX: the two reversing spikes get a day each because their answer decides whether the design stands. The five shaping ones get half a day each, and one of them — the path length — is an hour.
