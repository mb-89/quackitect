## follow_up

THE DESIGN ADVANCES WITH ONE ADDITION, and it is the falling spike's.

req-a-card-says-which-of-its-parts-are-work is solution-free: a card must say which parts are work, ordinary headings must survive for everything else, and heading depth must not matter. raid-dec-a-reserved-tag-on-a-heading-line-marks-a-piece-of-work carries the mechanism and its rejected alternatives.

THE BUILD OWES FOUR THINGS FROM THIS PHASE.

The compiler reads the mark rather than inferring from shape. One deliberate pass marks 137 cards, because nothing recovers the intent from cards written before this system existed. The surface handles a rare 23 as well as a common 2. And somebody times the write half of a mint, since only the derivation was measured.

NO MEASUREMENT IS OWED FOR THE VAULT. The owner ruled it a compatibility target rather than the interface, and it handles far more than this design produces.

ONE QUESTION IS LEFT DELIBERATELY OPEN. Whether two webviews can exchange a drag at all. The vendor documentation was not read, nothing this record ships depends on the answer, and a later record that wants the gesture starts by reading rather than building.

THE SAFE DIRECTION IS ALREADY IN THE RULE. An unmarked card mints nothing and says so, so an unconverted card reports zero work rather than inventing some.
