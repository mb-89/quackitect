# Evidence form / heading-per-token

## current_situation

The design assumed a method's marked headings become its pieces of work. The register named the retro card as the case that would decide it, and it was named correctly.

THE BET FAILS, AND ON TWO CARDS RATHER THAN ONE, in opposite directions.

The gate review card carries 15 headings of which about 2 are steps. The rest are rules. Inferring from headings mints thirteen items nobody owes.

The retro card carries 5 headings and 12 steps. The steps are a numbered list under one heading spanning 342 lines. Inferring from headings folds twelve acts into one item.

THE CAUSE IS NOT THE CARDS. A heading in this corpus means a SECTION of any kind: a step, a rule, a rationale, a rejected option. The rule was overloaded on a symbol nobody reserved.

## follow_up

TWO NODES COME OUT OF THIS and they are deliberately separate.

req-a-card-says-which-of-its-parts-are-work is solution-free. It demands that a card say which parts are work, that ordinary headings survive for everything else, and that heading depth not matter. It names no syntax, because a requirement is not the place for one.

raid-dec-a-reserved-tag-on-a-heading-line-marks-a-piece-of-work carries the mechanism, with the alternatives and why they lose. The closest rejected one is real: markdown's own heading-attribute syntax is level-independent and exactly the right shape, and the editor this project writes for does not implement it.

THE BUILD OWES TWO THINGS. The compiler reads the mark. And 137 cards get one deliberate pass to carry one, because nothing recovers the intent automatically from cards written before this system existed.

THE SAFE DIRECTION IS ALREADY BUILT INTO THE RULE. An unmarked card mints nothing and says so, so an unconverted card reports zero work rather than inventing some.
