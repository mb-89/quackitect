## follow_up

THE REVERSE IS THE ONE TO WATCH. Inverting who holds the list makes placing work free and lets one piece of work hold two positions, which the list shape cannot express at all. It also makes every question about a position a query over everything, so it only stands beside something that answers such a query cheaply.

THE SUBTRACTION ARGUES WITH THE ROUND'S EXIT RULE, openly. If blocking is a property rather than the definition, the rule that a position may not be left with work outstanding has to be restated as a rule about the blocking kind. That is a rewrite rather than a narrowing, and the gate should see it as one.

THE DIVISION IS THE CHEAPEST OF THE THREE and pays in one hop of readability. It also shrinks the re-entry problem to nothing, because nothing was copied to reconcile.

NINE OPERATORS CONFIRMED RATHER THAN INVENTED, and that is recorded as the result rather than hidden. An operator that lands on an option another finder already found is evidence the option is not arbitrary.
