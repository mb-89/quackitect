# Report / browse the backlog and decide what happens next

## What the story demands

Everything outstanding is browsable in one table, sortable and filterable, and
acted on there — so deciding and doing are the same gesture.

## THE HEADLINE, AND IT IS THE WEAKEST OF THE FIVE

THE TABLE EXISTS AND WAS MEASURED. THE DECISION WAS NOT MADE IN IT. This is
the story this record demonstrates least, and saying so is the report.

## What was observed

THE TABLE IS SERVED AND IT HOLDS THE WHOLE PILE. Measured on this repo: 395
rows in one pane, grouped four ways, with the backlog as its own group carrying
its own count. Every row is a work token with its statement, its place, its
status and its source.

THE COLUMNS THE STORY WANTS ARE THERE. The view carries statement, place,
bucket, slot, status and source, and the sort, properties, grouping and
filtering controls come from the database card rather than from a second
surface built for this.

IT SHIPS FOLDED SO IT DOES NOT BURY THE PAGE. The backlog and the retro both
arrive closed, and every row under a closed heading arrives hidden rather than
being taken back by a script. That was proven this evening and is now held by
`deliverable/tests/first-paint-folds.test.ts`.

FILING ROWS UNDER A NAME WORKS. The bucket mechanism the story's third slide
describes — drag rows together, name the group — is built and has its own
cases.

## What was NOT observed, and it is most of the story

NOBODY BROWSED THE BACKLOG TO DECIDE ANYTHING TONIGHT. The work this session
did was routed by the owner in chat and by the walk itself. The backlog table
was measured as an artifact, not used as an instrument.

THE FILTER WAS NOT EXERCISED. The story's fourth slide filters on complexity
and priority to get eleven actionable rows. That filter was never run.

SEEDING FROM THE TABLE WAS NOT PERFORMED. The story ends with an iteration
seeded from a bucket found there. Nothing was seeded tonight at all.

## The honest verdict

PARTLY OBSERVED, and the unobserved part is the story's whole point. The
surface is built and correct; the gesture it exists for has not been made by
anybody yet. That is a real gap in this record's evidence and it is named here
rather than implied.
