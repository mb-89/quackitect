# Report / walk a state that will not let me skip a step

## What the story demands

Each step of a method arrives as its own work token that cannot be skipped and
sometimes cannot be closed, so the method becomes the shape of the work rather
than advice beside it.

## How it was performed

By walking this record. Every refusal below was earned by trying to move on,
and every one of them is in the call log.

## What was observed

THE METHOD ARRIVED AS TOKENS, NOT AS ADVICE. Entering gate-implementation
handed over eleven marked steps as work, one per thing the gate demands. None
could be skipped: the gate would not stamp while any stood open.

A STEP REFUSED TO CLOSE ON AN EXCUSE. At sweep-consistency the submit was sent
with one checklist box left open and a reason written beside it. The state
refused: `swept: unchecked — book chapters`. The box was then answered by
looking rather than by arguing, and the surface turned out not to exist at all.

A STATE REFUSED TO BE ENTERED ON A PLACEHOLDER. run-demos would not serve while
its drawing carried the pin's placeholder, so the walk could not report an
empty machine as done.

A READING PROOF COULD NOT BE TALKED PAST. Twelve documents were owed after a
reload, each asking three fill-in-the-blank probes. Three answers missed and
each was named individually, with the ones already right banked.

THE HOLD WIDENED TO THE WHOLE RECORD TONIGHT. A gate now refuses its thumb
while any state inside the record still holds open work, and emergency does not
lift it. Exercised on this very gate.

## What the observation left behind

- `evidence/gate-implementation.md`, stamped only after eleven marked steps
  were settled.
- `evidence/sweep-consistency.md`, whose first submit was refused.
- The call log, holding each refusal with its clause.

## What was NOT observed

THE INCOMING SIDE OF THE STORY, in its strongest form. The story says a state
hands over a token for the document not yet read and NONE for the one already
proven. Reading credit was observed working — the pull stopped asking once a
document was answered — but no state was watched minting exactly one incoming
token while skipping a second. That is a narrower claim than the story makes.
