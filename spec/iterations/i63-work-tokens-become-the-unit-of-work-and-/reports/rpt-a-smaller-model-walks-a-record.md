# Report / a smaller model walks a record

## What the story demands

An engineer sends a night's work to a cloud box on a cheap model, and wakes up
to signed states. Two things have to hold: the machine must decide which hand
each piece of work needs, and a cheaper hand must be able to walk safely.

## THE HEADLINE, BEFORE THE DETAIL

HALF OF THIS STORY WAS DEMONSTRATED AND HALF WAS NOT. The routing that makes a
cheaper hand safe was observed on the shipped system. The cloud run itself was
not performed on this machine tonight. Saying so is the report; a claim covering
both halves would be fabrication.

## What was observed

THE MACHINE NAMES THE HAND, AND THE WALK CARRIES IT. Every spawn state on the
route published the rung its milestone demands, read from the sizing table
rather than from anyone's taste. Observed at five spawn states across this
record: for motivation, inputs, requirements, candidates, architecture,
prototype and implementation. Each one carried the difficulty and the rung into
its own guidance.

A SUBMACHINE TAKES THE MAXIMUM OVER ITS ITEMS, so one walker strong enough for
the hardest step walks them all. Observed in the drawn route, where the spike
and build submachines each published one rung for the whole of their contents.

THE GUARD THAT MAKES A CHEAPER HAND SAFE HELD ALL EVENING. A weaker hand cannot
skip a step, because the step holds shut over its own open work. That guard was
widened tonight to hold a whole record at a gate, and it was exercised on this
very walk: the implementation gate could not be blessed until fix-findings'
four tokens and the gate's own eleven marked steps were settled.

THE SAFETY DECLARATION RIDES EVERY CALL. `named_driver`, `went_weaker` and
`weaker_reason` are accepted on every lane tool, so a walk that went weaker
without a reason is marked rather than hidden.

## What was NOT observed, and it is the larger half

NO CLOUD BOX RAN TONIGHT. This walk ran on the owner's own machine, on a strong
hand, attended for most of its length. So the story's opening sentence — sending
a night's work away and finding signed states in the morning — was not performed
and is not claimed.

WHAT THAT LEAVES UNPROVEN, named rather than glossed:

- That the arrival script places the cage correctly on a fresh box.
- That a cheaper hand actually completes a record end to end without a stronger
  one answering its questions.
- That the states signed by such a run read as honestly as these do.

## What the observation left behind

- The route's own record of the rung at each spawn state, in the walk.
- `gate-implementation` blessed only after every token inside the record was
  settled, with emergency armed throughout.
- The call log, where `part` and `answered_by` stand on every call made
  tonight.

## The honest verdict

PARTLY OBSERVED. The routing half holds and was watched working. The unattended
cloud half is unexercised here and needs a real run on a real box before anyone
may claim it.
