# The demonstration drawing

ONE MUST STORY AND ONE DEMONSTRATION. `sty-the-machine-picks-the-hands` is
this iteration's only `must`, and its slides are the forward half: the machine
names the hand before the work.

`sty-read-the-record-and-ask-who-did-what` IS A `should` AND GETS NO DEMO.
Its evidence half is filled from the shipped record rather than from a
performance, which is the law's own division.

## What the demonstration must do, and what would make it a lie

IT RUNS AGAINST A SERVER BUILT FROM THIS CODE. The lane server this iteration
was walked through predates the build and does not know the `as` argument
exists. Performing the demonstration against it would demonstrate the absence
of the feature and read as a pass.

IT RATES A CELL BY HAND FIRST. Nothing in the shipped matrix is rated — that is
the standing debt — so the demonstration declares one, walks to that step, and
reads what comes back. A demonstration that skipped the rating would show a
pull with no `hand` field and prove only that the field is optional.

IT ENDS AT THE DELEGATION, not at the publication. The story's fourth slide is
whoever is driving reading the name and acting on it, and the acting is a
walker handing the step to a stronger hand. A report that stops at the pull has
performed half the story.
