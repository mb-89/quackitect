# The candidate drawing

One compose state per line drawn on the chart at build_chart. Four lines were
drawn there, sampled along the one axis that reorganises everything else:
whether our tree holds a roster and names a driver, and whether the thing
deciding sits inside the walk.

- THE SEED MADE TOTAL keeps both. A difficulty declared by hand on the matrix
  cell, riding the compiled state; the milestone maximum; a mapping split into a
  rung ladder and a model roster that move on different clocks; a named fallback
  pool; totality checked when the column compiles; publication on change; the
  call record carrying the named driver beside the one that answered; a sampled
  audit.
- THE DERIVED LADDER keeps both and removes the declaration. The difficulty is
  computed from what a state's own cell already makes it do, named per state
  rather than per milestone, the rung names an intensity as well as a worker,
  and the claim that the rung matters is tested by walking sampled steps twice.
- THE READER BESIDE THE WALK takes the decider out of the walk entirely. A
  separate reader tails the finished record, derives each state's difficulty
  from its own reopen history, writes into the record which is also the channel,
  and holds no path to act.
- THE RECEIVER DECIDES gives up the roster. A two-part difficulty and a rung
  name and never a model, the no-match a returned value rather than a silence, a
  record with neither a driver nor a reason not a valid record, and an
  acceptable over-driving rate named in advance because that is the only half of
  measuring that lives on our side of the boundary.

They are independent and all four hang off start; the join waits for every one.

## What each state writes

It writes back into the candidate note its line already created, and mints
nothing:

- How it works - the whole architecture, and especially the seams between the
  chosen options.
- What it costs - rough feasibility checks, proportional and no more.
- What it leans on - what has to be true for it to work.

Nothing here is scored. Judging stays at evaluate-set, because an agent that
knows the score while it writes the description writes a description that earns
the score.

## Two cells sit on more than one line, on purpose

The complexity riding the matrix cell and the compile-time totality check are
picked by both the seed and the derived ladder. Probe 4 found the path they ride
already compiled, so they are the cheap repairs any candidate keeping a mapping
would take. A compose state should describe the seam, not re-argue the choice.
