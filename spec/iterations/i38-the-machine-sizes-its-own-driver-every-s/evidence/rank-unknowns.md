# Evidence form / rank-unknowns

## current_situation

The register view holds the ranking and this form does not restate it. What is decided here is which unknowns get a timeboxed spike.

### Three seeded, and the first decides whether the other two matter

THE HOT CORNER OF THE OPEN REGISTER, by damage times likelihood, is crowded with entries this change does not touch — inherited debt across ten checks waiting on a person, a package proof run by hand, a marker that cannot be committed as the vision describes it. NONE OF THEM IS THIS ARCHITECTURE'S TO SETTLE, and the pick method's third filter says an entry a standing mechanism already exercises gets no second probe.

WHAT IS LEFT AFTER THAT FILTER is small, and it is exactly the set this iteration created or made load-bearing.

#### 1. Can a receiver act on what we publish

`raid-dep-the-payoff-waits-on-a-weak-model-being-able-to-boot-at-all` — crippling, expected, open.

THE WHOLE ARCHITECTURE IS DOWNSTREAM OF ONE SENTENCE, AND THE SENTENCE WAS FALSE. `nbr-the-driver-that-performs-the-spawn` said the receiver reads and cannot act. Every scenario the declared architecture addresses is addressed on the assumption that publishing reaches somebody who can use it, and it does. CORRECTED 2026-08-20: the party is the walking agent, which hands a step it should not take itself to a subagent on a stronger hand.

IT IS SPIKEABLE AND CHEAP. `se-start.ts` spawns the lane and proves it answers before launching an agent; `se-pty.ts` runs an agent inside a pseudo-terminal with a live read-write channel. The probe is whether a published rung can reach either path and change what is started, inside a timebox, once.

AND IT SETTLED THE OWNER'S RULING WITHOUT ANYBODY ARGUING ABOUT IT, THE OTHER WAY. A party can act on a model name, so the roster is not a file maintained for nobody and the ruling wins on merit. WHAT THIS STATE GOT RIGHT AND WHY IT IS WORTH SAYING: the pick was correct and its stated reason was inherited from a node that was wrong. Seeding a spike on the load-bearing sentence is the right instinct even when the sentence is believed rather than checked — that is what a spike is for.

#### 2. What the record can honestly say about who acted

`raid-ar-the-actor-is-recorded-where-the-call-is-served` — corrosive, CERTAIN, open.

A CERTAIN ENTRY IS NOT A RISK, IT IS A CONSEQUENCE, and this one was mis-stated for most of this milestone. It said the answering driver can never be recorded; it can, self-reported, as on every line. What this architecture gives up is the CROSS-CHECK.

THE SPIKE IS ABOUT THE MARK RATHER THAN THE FIELD. `req-every-call-records-the-model-that-answered-it` demands the value be "marked as self-reported wherever the lane cannot obtain the value independently", and no design on the chart writes that mark. The probe is whether the lane can obtain any part of it independently today — from the transport, the harness registration, or the process it spawned — because that decides whether the mark is permanent or temporary.

WHY IT IS WORTH A TIMEBOX rather than a reading: the record has now twice asserted what the transport carries without opening it, and both assertions were wrong in the same direction.

#### 3. Whether a declared number stays honest

`raid-risk-a-hand-declared-rung-drifts-upward-and-nothing-ever-says-so` — corrosive, expected, open.

THE DECLARED ARCHITECTURE TYPES 308 FIGURES BY HAND — 154 active cells, two figures each. The scheduler literature says declarations nobody updates drift: sixty-nine per cent of requested CPU unused across more than twenty-three thousand production clusters, with the cause given as exactly that.

IT IS SPIKEABLE ON THE CORPUS WE HAVE. Rate a small sample of cells twice, by two hands or twice by one at a distance, and measure the disagreement. THAT IS THE ONLY ONE OF THE THREE THAT NEEDS NO NEW MACHINERY.

THE PICK METHOD'S THIRD FILTER APPLIES. It also concerns a roster the winner does not hold; it survives as an assumption under the owner's ruling rather than under the design.

`raid-risk-the-weaker-model-asymmetry-has-nothing-enforcing-it` — crippling, plausible. NOT SEEDED because the architecture answers it by construction: a record carrying neither a named driver nor a stated reason is not a valid record. That is a build task at M7 rather than an unknown.

THE FIRST SPIKE IS THE ONLY ONE THAT CAN KILL THE DESIGN. The second changes what a requirement can honestly demand. The third changes how much the design costs to run and nothing about whether it works.

A MAJOR WITH ZERO SPIKES IS LEGAL AND THIS IS NOT THAT CASE. Three is the number the filters leave, not a number chosen for shape.
