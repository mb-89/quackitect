# Evidence form / the-compile-carries-it-onto-the-step

## current_situation

A cell can carry a difficulty and the loader reads it. What nothing did was get it to the party that needs it: `el-sizing` has two inbound interfaces and both hand it a COMPILED MACHINE, and neither hands it the matrix.

## built

`StateDecl` CARRIES A `complexity`, and `compileColumn` copies the cell's value onto the state it compiles.

THAT IS WHAT MAKES `obtain-a-step-s-difficulty` A FIELD READ AND NOT A JOIN. A join would make the answer depend on the matrix as it stands NOW rather than on the machine the walk is running, so a rating edited mid-walk would change what a running record is being sized against.

A STEP WHOSE CELL DECLARED NONE CARRIES NONE. The field is absent rather than defaulted, and one case asserts that an unrated fixture produces no state claiming a rating.

TWO CASES HOLD IT: the value survives the compile for every rated cell, and nothing invents one where the cell was silent.

## follow_up

THE STEP NOW KNOWS HOW HARD IT IS AND NOTHING ASKS YET. `difficultyOf` reads the field in the next chunk, and the pull publishes it in the one after.

THE MECHANICAL STATES CARRY NOTHING AND OWE NOTHING. `start` and `end` are every machine's, no matrix row declares them, and nothing machine-specific belongs in them.
