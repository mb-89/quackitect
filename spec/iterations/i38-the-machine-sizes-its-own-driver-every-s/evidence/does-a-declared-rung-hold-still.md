# Evidence form / does-a-declared-rung-hold-still

## current_situation

`exp-two-hands-rating-the-same-six-cells` — HOLDS, and it found a class it was not looking for.

### The run

TWO READERS WITH NO SHARED CONTEXT were given the same five-rung ladder, the same six rows, and told to rate the `major` column of each from the row's own text. Neither saw the other's answer. Both reported that none of the six carried a pre-existing rating.

### The result

| row | first hand | second hand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1_40A scope-non-goals | C3 | C3 |
| M2_90 gate-inputs | C1 | C1 |
| M4_10B partition-functions | C1 | C1 |
| M5_20 reverse-sensitivity | C2 | C2 |
| M6_10 rank-unknowns | C2 | C2 |
| M7_40 build-steps | C0 | C2 |

FIVE OF SIX IDENTICAL. On a five-rung ladder chance agreement is one in five.

AND THE FIVE AGREED ON THE SAME EVIDENCE. Both quoted "The engine proposes; you confirm or move" for partition-functions, both quoted "the judgment the counts cannot make" for gate-inputs, both quoted "THE PICK METHOD, three filters in order" for rank-unknowns. THE LADDER REPRODUCES THE SENTENCE THE NUMBER RESTS ON, not only the number.

### The one disagreement, and both hands predicted it

`M7_40 build-steps`: C0 against C2, two rungs apart and the widest gap available.

BOTH READERS NAMED THAT ROW, UNPROMPTED, AS THEIR LEAST-SURE RATING, and both gave the same reason in their own words: whether the rung covers the DISPATCH the row performs or the AUTHORING inside the chunks it delegates to.

THE ROW SAYS BOTH THINGS. Its major note reads "the seeded chunk machine runs, parallel where independent, every fill recorded", and the row is described as "the placeholder the seeded chunk machine fills".

### What it settles and what it does not

IT SETTLES THAT THE DECLARED ARCHITECTURE'S BET IS SOUND FOR ORDINARY ROWS. 308 hand-typed figures is a large surface, and on rows that are one act two independent hands land on the same rung from the same sentence.

IT DOES NOT SETTLE THE RISK IT PROBES. `raid-risk-a-hand-declared-rung-drifts-upward-and-nothing-ever-says-so` is about DRIFT OVER TIME. Two readers in one session measure agreement, not drift. THE RISK IS NARROWED AND STAYS OPEN, and the node says so rather than claiming otherwise.

### The class

A PLACEHOLDER ROW THAT SEEDS A SUB-MACHINE HAS NO COMPLEXITY OF ITS OWN. `M7_40 build-steps` is one; so are `M4_25 run-candidates` and `M6_15 run-spikes`, both of which this record has walked as placeholders a drawn machine fills.

RATING SUCH A ROW FORCES A CHOICE THE ROW DOES NOT MAKE, and two careful readers made it differently. That is a figure that will be wrong for one of two readers however carefully it is typed.

## follow_up

THE PROMOTION IS NOT "RATE MORE CAREFULLY". The loader should refuse a complexity on a placeholder row, or the rating should attach to the states the sub-machine seeds — where the work actually is. Three such rows already exist.

WHAT WOULD ACTUALLY TEST THE RISK is the same six cells rated again by a hand that has not seen these ratings, after the work under them has moved. That is an iteration apart, not a session apart, and it is the only shape that measures drift.

AND THE SAMPLE IS SMALL AND SAYS SO. Six cells of 154 active ones, all at `major`. A wider sample could find the disagreement rate is worse at `patch`, where rows inherit and a rung is easiest to type without thinking.
