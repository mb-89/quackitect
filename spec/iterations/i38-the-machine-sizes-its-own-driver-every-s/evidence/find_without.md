## follow_up

- THE PER-STATE TRIM IS THE BIGGEST FINDING OF M4 SO FAR and it should be carried to the Pugh round as a serious candidate rather than a curiosity. Naming per state removes the reduction, the maximum rule and its requirement, one flow, a standing crippling-graded risk, and an option minted two states ago to repair the same mismatch.

- THE REASON PER-MILESTONE WAS ASSUMED IS WEAKER THAN IT LOOKED, and naming it is the useful part. Per-state naming sounds churny — a new driver every few minutes. But THE MACHINE ONLY NAMES; publishing a value costs a field on a pull, and it is ACTING that costs. Acting is outside the boundary by this design's own rule, so the churn lands entirely on a receiver that is free to ignore it. A receiver given per-state names can still switch per milestone; a receiver given only per-milestone names can never do better.

- THE SECOND TRIM IS NOT DECIDABLE YET AND SAYS SO. Removing the rung and the mapping is right only if the rung is a spelling rather than an abstraction, and that is answered by the first rating pass: if the ratings cluster on two or three values the indirection has almost no fan-out. It should be re-read at that moment, not dismissed now.

- ONE THING WAS ASKED AND FIRMLY KEPT. The publish function cannot go, because removing it means the machine acts on its own decision. That is the boundary the whole design is built around and it is the one trade this iteration will not make — worth recording as a refusal rather than as an omission.
