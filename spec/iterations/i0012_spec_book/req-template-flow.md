## Rationale (not load-bearing)
Owner ruling (i12 M2): straying from a template is not forbidden - it is QUESTIONED at the docs-complete review, with the reason recorded. The flow is bidirectional: template improvements reach the documents; document improvements teach the templates. This is the retro discipline applied to structure.
