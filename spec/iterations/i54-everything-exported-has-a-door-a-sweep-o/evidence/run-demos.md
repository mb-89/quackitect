What a DEMONSTRATION adds is that somebody watched it happen rather than read that it passed. The two things worth performing are the two refusals, because a refusal is the only part of this record a person meets directly.

### What a demo has to show here

- A write that turns a quiet module into one that reaches a governed conversation is REFUSED, and the refusal hands back a patch that applies.
- A departure line carrying a path and no reason is REFUSED, and so is a bullet the parser cannot read at all.
- The sweep reports every undeclared reach across the tree, and reports UNCHECKED rather than green on a tree it never looked at.

The third is the one no unit case could have caught, and it was a real defect this record shipped and then fixed.
