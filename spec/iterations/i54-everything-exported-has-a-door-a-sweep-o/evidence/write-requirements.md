- consistent: NO TWO ROWS CONTRADICT, AND ONE PAIR WAS CHECKED CLOSELY. req-absence-from-the-exemption-list-means-not-exempt and req-no-setting-disables-every-rule-at-once could appear to conflict, since the first says everything not listed is in breach and the second forbids turning the rule off. They agree: the answer to a rule that is too wide is deleting the rule, never suspending it.
- affordable: FIVE OF THE SIX COST ALMOST NOTHING BECAUSE THE SHAPE EXISTS. The widget guard already runs one rule, one registry, one hatch and two callers. The sixth, the reachability sweep, is the seeded record's own work and was sized before this record was widened.
- bounded: EVERY ROW NAMES A FINITE THING. The one row that could have grown without limit is the reachability guard, and it is bounded by what the source tree exports, which is enumerable.
- comprehensible: A READER WHO KNOWS NEITHER THIS RECORD NOR THE PATTERN CAN READ THE SIX. The carving row carries a table of names that pass and fail rather than a definition. The blanket-switch row names the three real products that ship one.
- no_tbd: ZERO. No row carries TBD, TBC, TBR or a question-mark marker in any field, and the lane's own marker check refused nothing on any of the six writes.
- behaviour_modelled: TWO ROWS CARRY A MODEL AND FOUR SAY IN ONE LINE THAT THEY DO NOT. The exemption row carries a lifecycle, because its first line — nothing to refused — is what proves no path mints an exemption silently, which is precisely how dependency-cruiser's generated baseline comes to have no author. The reachability row says none: one condition, one response, and a diagram of that would restate the statement in a second notation.
- quality_groups_swept: ALL NINE ISO 25010 CHARACTERISTICS WERE WALKED AND NONE BECAME A SCENARIO. Maintainability is the one this record serves and it is served by functional and constraint rows rather than by a measured scenario, because the demand is that a departure be recorded, not that recording it be fast. Functional suitability, performance efficiency, compatibility, interaction capability, reliability, security, flexibility and safety are each untouched by this box. Writing scenarios for them would produce confusion rather than coverage, which is what the method card warns against in as many words.
