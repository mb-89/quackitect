## gap_claim

THE CLAIM: every surface we have tells the truth about a record only while that record is still moving.

WHAT THE ALTERNATIVES SHED. A project board shows state and sheds the reasoning, so a closed item is a coloured box. A document trail keeps the reasoning and sheds the state, so nothing says whether it still holds. A build dashboard keeps the checks and sheds everything that is not a check.

OURS SHEDS SOMETHING TOO, and that is the gap. It keeps state and reasoning together while the walk is live, then loses both at the close: an archived record is a wall of grey with no way in. It also keeps them twice, in two renderers that drift apart with nothing to catch them.

SO THE GAP IS NOT A MISSING FEATURE. It is that the surface stops being true at exactly the moment the record becomes history, which is when a person most often comes back to it.
