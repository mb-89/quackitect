# Evidence form / draft-vision

## current_situation

The kickoff gate is blessed at major and the machine below is compiled to match.

The record's scope was reset at that gate. It now holds the four panel pieces it opened with, plus everything i23 had already ruled, and it holds nothing that needs the owner's judgment.

## big_idea

THE RESIDENT VISION IS INHERITED WHOLE. This change does not bend it, and nothing here re-derives the axiom.

WHAT THE PRODUCT IS, unchanged: a machine that walks engineering method for you, so rigor costs attention rather than paperwork. A person aims it and adjudicates; the machine routes, checks and records.

WHY THIS CHANGE DOES NOT BEND IT. Every piece in this record makes an existing surface tell the truth about state the machine already holds. An archived record that cannot be opened, a bless that needs a reload, a grey state with no reason, and two renderers that disagree are all the same failure: the machine knows and the surface does not say. Fixing that serves the resident idea rather than changing it.

## to_be_world

The engineer opens the panel in the morning and the board is legible without asking anything.

A record that shipped last week is in the archive. They click it and it opens as it stood at the close: green where it passed, thumbs on the gates that were blessed, every deck and form readable. Nothing about it is a mystery because it is finished.

They bless a gate and the surface repaints under their hand. No reload, no wondering whether the thing they are looking at is current.

A state sits grey. They hover it and it says why it is grey, in the state's own detail, instead of leaving them to guess.

And when a control changes in the engine, it changes everywhere at once, because there is one surface drawing it rather than two that drift apart quietly.

## goal_system

THE GOALS, in order, with the conflict named.

- THE SURFACE TELLS THE TRUTH ABOUT WHAT THE MACHINE HOLDS. This is the goal every piece here serves.
- ONE SURFACE, NOT TWO. A value wired into one renderer and missing from the other is the defect class this record ends.
- A FINISHED RECORD STAYS READABLE. Closing a record must not make it opaque.

THE CONFLICT IS SPEED AGAINST COMPLETENESS. Reading an archived record from its own branch is slower than reading the live tree, and the one-second rule binds every surface a person touches.

IT IS RULED IN FAVOUR OF COMPLETENESS, and the record already said so: a loading screen past a second is accepted for that one case. A fast surface that cannot show a closed record is the failure this record exists to fix.
