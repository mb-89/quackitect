# Report — one call lists every piece of work out of sight

## What was set up

TWO PIECES OF WORK RAN AT ONCE, and they came from different tables on purpose.

- A test run, started by asking the engine a scoped question.
- A shell command, started by the engine to carry that run.

NEITHER WAS ASKED ABOUT DIRECTLY. Every observation below comes from the account
riding the answer to some other call.

## What was observed

ONE ANSWER HELD BOTH PIECES. The test run and the shell command stood together
in the same account, on a call that had nothing to do with either.

EACH NAMED A TIME OR SAID WHY NOT.

- The test run: a time remaining, with the basis "a linear projection over 9 of
  179 finished on this run, dependable in neither direction".
- The shell command: no figure, with the basis "no measurement of comparable
  work exists, so no time remaining is given".

THE FIGURE MOVED AS THE RUN PROGRESSED, and the basis moved with it. Later
answers on the same run read "a linear projection over 145 of 179 finished".

THE BASIS TOLD THE READER WHEN TO STOP TRUSTING IT. When nothing had finished
for a while, the basis said so in words: "179 of 179 finished, and nothing has
finished for 64s, so this figure is not advancing". That sentence is what let
the wait end in a decision rather than another poll.

NOTHING SETTLED RODE ALONG. Acknowledging cleared 656 settled entries, and the
next answer carried only the two pieces that were actually running.

## Verdict

PASS, on all four pass lines.

- Both pieces stood in one answer.
- Each named a time remaining or said plainly why no figure was given.
- Each named what the figure rested on.
- Nothing settled rode along, and every record stayed reachable by its own id.

## What this report does not claim

THE POLLING HABIT WAS NOT MEASURED ACROSS A POPULATION. This is one walk, one
agent. What it shows is that the answer carried enough to decide with; it does
not show what every agent would then do.

ONE HONEST FAILURE IS RECORDED IN ITS SIBLING. A run reported itself as running
long after its last case finished, which is a defect in settling rather than in
reporting. It is written up in the report beside this one.
