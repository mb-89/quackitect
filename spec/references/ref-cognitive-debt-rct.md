Randomized trial, 52 junior developers: users with STRUCTURED SCAFFOLDING matched unrestricted users' build speed and then failed later maintenance-without-AI at 39% versus 77%. Process scaffolding causally halved downstream failure at equal velocity — the strongest single witness for input-and-process over output.
