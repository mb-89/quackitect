The tightest definition of the word in force anywhere, from the US Federal
Aviation Administration. Primary text read from the Government Publishing
Office on 2026-08-25.

## Overhauled — six steps, and the last one is the one we skip

Nothing may be described as overhauled unless it has been:

- disassembled
- cleaned
- inspected
- repaired as necessary
- reassembled
- TESTED against approved standards and technical data

A CORPUS SWEEP THAT DOES NOT END IN A TEST AGAINST THE CURRENT STANDARD has
not met the definition of the word it is using.

## Rebuilt — a strictly stronger claim

The same six steps, plus: tested to the tolerances and limits of a NEW item,
using new parts or used parts that meet new-part tolerances.

TWO VERDICTS, NOT ONE. It is worth having both, because they claim different
things about the result.

## Why this reference is here at all

The method page uses "overhaul" as its own name. A word with a legal
definition and a test step in it costs nothing to adopt and settles what the
name promises.
