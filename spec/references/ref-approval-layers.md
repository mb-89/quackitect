## What the category is

A 2026 product category: approval infrastructure routing high-stakes AGENT
ACTIONS through human review. What such a product sells:

- gate types
- RACI mappings
- escalation SLAs
- audit-trail schemas

The demand comes from the EU AI Act, ISO 42001 and the NIST AI RMF, which
all want demonstrable human oversight. Representative pages besides the
primary link: an agentic-workflow gate framework (digitalapplied.com),
approval-workflow guides (waxell.ai), architecture guides (stackai.com).

## The prior-art verdict

THESE GATE ACTIONS such as payments, deletions and escalations, not
engineering process. Four things are absent.

- no drawn method
- no evidence forms
- no forced design input
- no decision record as spec

The regulatory push for demonstrable oversight is a tailwind for the record
half of the vision.
