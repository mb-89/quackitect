The horizontal international standard for instructions for use. Its scope covers application software and sanctions electronic, interactive delivery. It defines three information types (conceptual, instructional, reference) and seven quality principles (completeness, minimalism, correctness, conciseness, consistency, comprehensibility, accessibility). Added at i0027 M2: the normative anchor for the IFU deck concept; the quality principles are the recorded gap the IFU requirements answer to.
