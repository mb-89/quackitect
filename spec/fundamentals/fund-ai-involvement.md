Every prose paragraph in this document carries a recorded AI-involvement value. Three means a full AI draft. Zero means the author's own words. A person's rework lowers the value; any AI touch resets it to three.

The measure answers one question only: how much of this text did the AI write? It says nothing about correctness or review. The author owns everything published, whatever the share. Field evidence on AI in delivery pipelines: [DORA on gen-AI](ref-dora-genai).
