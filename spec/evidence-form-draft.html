1Description

A retro looks back over the interval since the last one. The field gets asked first. Then every pending note is judged and drained. The call log is mined for what kept refusing. The waste in the record is named. What the interval taught leaves as rules and as steps.

2Motivation

Lessons expire. A stray nobody judges rots into noise, and a mistake nobody names repeats. The retro turns what happened into rules while it is still cheap, and it empties the inbox so the next decision starts on a clean slate.

3Current situation template: free-form

What stands right now, in a few lines — the inbox count, the open work, the interval's bounds. Filled from the survey before the sweep begins.
