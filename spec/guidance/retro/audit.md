# Actionables

1. Walk the checklist the audit step carries, one auditor a group of items. *
2. Walk what the window lands: the commits in its span, and the record where an item asks about conduct. *
3. Answer each item: held, broken with its evidence, or untested with the reason. *
4. Write `findings/audit-<group>.md` in the ten rows: a broken item under stop, a held one under keep.
5. Name a rule nobody follows as a check to build or a rule to cut. *
