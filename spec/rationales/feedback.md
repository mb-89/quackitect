# Why

The owner reads the tree between retros, and what they meet there is
evidence no transcript holds. So the retro asks for it by name, as a step of
its own.

## 1. Feedback before collect

Collect moves the private folder into the retro's input. A note written
before collect rides along with the transcripts and the log, so every later
step reads it from one place. A note written after collect lands where no
step of this retro looks.

## 2. One point at a time

The owner walks the tree and names what they meet. A hand racing ahead
answers a point the owner has yet to make, and the owner spends a turn
pulling it back.

## 3. Say it back

A point written wrong turns into a ticket built wrong. Saying it back costs
one line, and the owner catches the misreading while the point is still in
their head.

## 5. Discuss first

A note carrying an open question hands the question to a later hand that
lacks the owner. So the question goes to the owner now, and the note carries
the answer.

## 6. Notes, no changes

A change made mid-feedback reshapes the tree while the owner is still reading
it. The owner then reviews a moving target, and the retro loses the order
between what the owner asked and what a hand changed.
