# Scope

The owner rules three things on top of [[spec/funnel/a-button-makes-a-vehicle]], and asks for the steps, each with its proof, and the branches an agent works.

| ruling | what it settles |
|---|---|
| a vehicle's source stays in the vehicle | the method half holds the plugin, the doors, the verbs and the tools, and a stub reaches them by resolution, never by copy |
| the folders are `method/` and `project/` | a project holds its spec and its source; a vehicle holds both halves in one tree and works on its own method |
| the stub's link carries the vehicle's upstream repo | a cloud box with no vehicle clones that repo, installs it, and attaches to it |

This note stops at the steps and the branches. The design output of each branch says how its step is built.
