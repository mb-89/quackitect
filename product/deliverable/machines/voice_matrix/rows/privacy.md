# People and privacy

The only row that is `must` in every column, because the cost of a miss
does not scale down with the length of the text.

- No personal data in anything stored or published. That covers the spec,
  evidence docs, trace nodes, reports and entry files.
- Use the stakeholder ROLE: the owner, the adjudicator, the driving agent,
  the maintainer.
- Do not write "human vs agent" in prose. Say people, or name the role.
  The engine's actor stamp is a recorded metric with a fixed vocabulary.
  Prose is not.

## Why tier one

Email addresses, phone numbers and the banned prose forms all have shapes.
Names do not, and this row does not pretend to catch them — it catches the
mechanical leaks and leaves the judgement to a reader.

## Worth stating plainly

This row will never be complete. A lint that catches an email address does
not make a document safe to publish. It removes the careless half.
