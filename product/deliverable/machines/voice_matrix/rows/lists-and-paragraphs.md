# Lists and paragraphs

Already shipped and already refusing: SE-C-125 for a prose wall, SE-C-120
for a brief that chains three separator-joined parts.

- Three or more items is a Markdown list, one item per line.
- No compound sentences inside an item.
- Long prose carries real line breaks. Every surface renders them.

## brief

`must@write` is live today. SE-C-120 is the lane's most-hit refusal, which
is the argument for keeping it mechanical rather than written down again.

## Why the whole row is tier one

Counting separators and blank lines is string work. It is the cheapest
check we have and it catches the most-broken rules.
