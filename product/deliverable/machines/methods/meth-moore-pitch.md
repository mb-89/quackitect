## Situation
Closing move of the vision draft (M1). One blockquote that forces the five hard choices: audience, need, category, benefit, and the displaced alternative.

## Procedure
- Fill every slot. An empty <unlike> means the alternative was not faced.
- The <who> must match a value-prop audience; the <difference> must survive the prior-art scan.
- Read it aloud against the goal system: if the pitch and the top goal disagree, one of them is wrong.
