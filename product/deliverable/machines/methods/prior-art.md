# Prior art — somebody shipped this already

**What.** Deliberate research before invention: search the web, the
project's own corpus, and the standard literature for existing
solutions, patterns, and post-mortems. Reuse beats rediscovery.

**When.** ALWAYS at least briefly — and fully when the problem feels
general (caching, undo, scheduling, review flows all have decades of
art).

**Steps.**
1. Name the problem in the field's vocabulary — finding the right TERM
   is half the search (se_web_search earns its keep here).
2. Sweep three angles: products that do it, patterns that describe it,
   failures written up about it.
3. For each find: what would adopting it look like HERE? What does our
   context break?
4. Cite everything — an idea with a source is checkable, one without is
   a rumor.

**Output.** Idea stubs with sources, plus a short "considered and
rejected" list — tomorrow's reader needs both.
