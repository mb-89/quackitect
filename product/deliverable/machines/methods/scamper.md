# SCAMPER — seven systematic twists on what exists

**What.** Osborn's checklist, compressed: take the existing thing and
force seven transformations onto it. Mechanical creativity — no muse
required.

**When.** Something exists and should be better, cheaper, or different.
Weak on green fields, strong on redesign.

**Steps.** Apply each letter to the subject, one honest minute each:
- **S**ubstitute — swap a part, a material, a mechanism.
- **C**ombine — merge with another feature, tool, or step.
- **A**dapt — what does a neighboring domain do here?
- **M**odify — magnify, minify, exaggerate an attribute.
- **P**ut to other use — who else could use this as-is?
- **E**liminate — remove the part; does the need disappear too?
- **R**everse — invert the order, the roles, the direction.

**Output.** Up to seven idea stubs, each tagged with its letter.
