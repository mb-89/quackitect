# Analogy transfer — steal the shape from another domain

**What.** Ask how OTHER domains solve the abstract version of the
problem — other industries, other sciences, nature (bionics). Transfer
the mechanism, not the surface.

**When.** The domain's own conventions are exhausted, or the problem
smells like something with a famous solution elsewhere.

**Steps.**
1. Abstract the problem one level: not "archive rendering" but "showing
   a large history without drowning the reader".
2. Name three domains that face the abstract problem — libraries, air
   traffic, forestry, immune systems.
3. Describe each domain's mechanism in ITS terms, honestly.
4. Transfer: what is the mechanism's equivalent here? What breaks in
   translation?

**Output.** Idea stubs each naming the source domain and what survived
the translation — and what deliberately did not.
