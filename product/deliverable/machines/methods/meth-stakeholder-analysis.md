## Situation
Guidance for M2 map-stakeholders, the depth [[meth-stakeholder-tensions]] builds on. A stakeholder provides input, expects results, or participates - different groups matter for requirements, for architecting, and for portfolio decisions.

## Procedure
- Identify EARLY and by ROLE, never by name (one person may hold several roles; the role outlives the person).
- Type each role - the DICET set: Decider (formal power, holds the budget), Influencer (informal power, the eminence grise), Customer (provides requirements, internal or external), Expert (know-how), Team (will work on the thing).
- Place each on the portfolio: INTEREST x INFLUENCE, with ATTITUDE marked per cell (++ wins-if-you-win ... 0 neutral ... -- loses-if-you-win). High interest plus high influence: take care of them. Antagonists: find the root cause; the goal is turning them into protagonists, not routing around them.
- Mark adaptability (innovator vs conservative) where change is part of what ships.
- Check the CONTACT intensity each role needs (necessary / helpful / on-demand) against the contact that exists - a contact gap around a decider is a project risk, name it.
- The tensions then fall out: pairs whose interests pull apart become [[meth-stakeholder-tensions]] entries, and requirements source to the roles at M3.
