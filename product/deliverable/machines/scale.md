# The autonomy scale

Uniform 0.2 bands with a 0.01 floor — the floor keeps autonomy 0 blocking
even mechanical steps. Edit the lines below; the mirror's notches and the
level help follow on the next reload. A state's own gate weight stays its
authored `priority:` — calibrate against these anchors.

## Levels

- 0.01 | M | mechanical
- 0.2 | R | routine
- 0.4 | E | everyday decision
- 0.6 | C | consequential
- 0.8 | K | killer / milestone
- 1 | I | ideation — the agent finds its own work (behavior ships later)
