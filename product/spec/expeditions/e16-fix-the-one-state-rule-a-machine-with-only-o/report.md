# e16-fix-the-one-state-rule-a-machine-with-only-o — expedition-leave

## What was the goal

Bake the owner's one-state rule: a machine with only one working state
is a state, not a sub-machine. Convert the front desk and the retro,
give the desk real awareness through tag-pulled required reading, render
subtitles on drawn nodes, and record the rule durably.

## What was done

THE CONVERSIONS: front_desk and retro are plain states drawn directly
on main — the desk's talk and the retro's drain each keep their own
visit, guidance, and legality (se_note_drain rides retro's legal_tools;
the method rides its entry read). The four retired files (two canvases,
two inner state notes) are deleted. Boot stays a machine — it has
several states.

THE DESK'S AWARENESS: product/guidance/method/front-desk.md is pulled
by tag (front-desk) and therefore REQUIRED READING at entry. It orders
the live sweep (doors, containers, notes — never answer from memory),
carries the vehicle judgment and the execute rules, and deliberately
holds no door list and no vocabulary — those are read live so the desk
stays current when lanes land.

SUBTITLES: states may carry subtitle: in frontmatter — rendered as a
small gray second line under the drawn node's name and served in the
packet's next entries. The front desk says "In doubt, go here."; the
retro says "Drain the inbox."; ideation's flourish was dropped.

THE RULE'S HOME: product/guidance/method/machine-authoring.md — the
one-state rule, the subtitle and door-statement conventions, and the
standing canvas cautions.

## What settled it

The suite in this worktree: preflight green, 97/97 — the retro tests
walk the one-step entry (method hashes demanded at the door), the door
test proves both conversions (no submachine, subtitle present, tag
present, legality preserved), and the ideation shape still compiles.

## What was not done

The pull's "tag: query" generalization beyond tags (arbitrary queries as
reading lists) waits for a real need — tags cover the desk. The retro
method doc itself was not rewritten. Pruning stays a note.
