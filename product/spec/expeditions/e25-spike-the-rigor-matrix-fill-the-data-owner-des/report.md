# e25 — the rigor matrix, from data to a walking machine

## What was the goal

Fill the rigor matrix: the full-battery process as data, tailored by
change size, seeding the iteration machines and the documentation.

## What was done

The matrix stands at 50 rows and 250 cells, and it WALKS end to end:

- The owner's 48 review comments worked in; four design questions
  settled in discussion (criteria are promoted requirements, ADRs are
  own files in the RAID view, evidence is frontmatter data, functions
  are design input).
- Evidence moved to frontmatter YAML on every row; the reader refuses
  body echoes and evidence-less states.
- The kickoff seeds the iteration: change_size blessed, the column
  compiled LIVE and pinned with the matrix hash; escalation only grows
  and REOPENS exactly the grown steps (comparison, no numeric scale).
- The container expands a pinned iteration into its walk; the walk
  descends into candidates, spikes and build chunks — authored as small
  drawings, refused when absent, an explicit none passing.
- Realization kinds become tags, so the pull serves each builder its
  discipline's guidance.
- NO GATE PASSES WITHOUT ITS MILESTONE REVIEW REPORT — scaffolded from
  the gate's own fields plus verify/validate/red_team; the BLESS is the
  separate passing act, stamped durably with version and hand.
- Nine method cards harvested (test design, testability, stakeholders,
  doc quality, build strategies, RBT among them); the decision-matrix
  form ports v1's field-tested shape.
- Demo readiness: RUNME --export (fresh single-commit repo, no
  history), the launch check that refuses over a live session, the
  clean end (no strays), the ping channel (se_panel pulses a surface
  yellow), the tour method, the desk greeting.

## What settled it

The suite, 220 passing — including the end-to-end walk: seed, bless,
pin, descend, gate held without a report, passed on the blessed one.
Every piece landed on trunk through the lane; the export proven twice
against a temp target.

## What was not done

- The forms pass: human-usable filling surfaces, the form-and-method
  item-by-item review (scheduled for the first big iteration).
- True walk resume across restarts (re-walk is quick; position is not
  yet durable).
- Per-discipline guidance content for the realization tags.
- The v3 book representation (accepted as not-now by the owner).
- The Copilot cage stays unproven until run on a machine that has it.
