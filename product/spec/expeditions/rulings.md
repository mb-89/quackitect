# Rulings — the retro's writable surface

Closed expedition records live in git (their `exp/*` branch), not on the
tree. This ledger is where an adjudication lands AFTER close. One bullet
per expedition. The listed ruling overrides the branch-frozen one.

Format: `- <short id>: <ruling> — <why, optional>`

Rulings: `applied` and `dismissed` are stamped at close; a retro may
override with `approved` or `dismissed`.
