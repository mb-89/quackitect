id: se.raid-the-guidance-join-buries-the-instruction
kind: raid
statement: "Joining every applicable rule into every packet risks burying the step's actual instruction under a wall of law - the G1-vs-G3 conflict. A packet that arrives carrying twelve laws, four methods and a guidance note is not more binding than one carrying two; it is less, because the agent skims it. The failure is quiet: nothing refuses, the rules are technically present, and they stop being read."
provenance:
  iteration: i12-tool-surface
  ai_involvement: agent-drafted
raid_kind: risk
raid_owner: agent
trigger: "A packet whose guidance exceeds its instruction in length. RULED: relevance is the mechanism, not volume - applies_to must be narrow enough that a packet carries the rules binding THIS state, and a rule that applies to everything belongs in the contract rather than in a join. Measurable tripwire to set at M3: cap or at least MEASURE guidance size per packet, so the join's cost is visible rather than assumed harmless. Fallback: rank by specificity and carry the rest as named pointers the agent can pull."
