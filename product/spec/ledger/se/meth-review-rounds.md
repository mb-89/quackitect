statement: "THE INCREASING-SCRUTINY REVIEW a gate gets before it is blessed. Ported from v1's guides/milestone-review.md, which v2's meth-gate-review cited as 'rubric-cited' without v2 ever carrying the rubric.\n\nA gate is NEVER blessed on procedure alone. Each review covers TWO SETS: the gate's own acceptance items, AND every input state feeding it - the dependency cone since the last gate. Reviewing only the gate's own fields is the common failure and it is not a review.\n\n1. VERIFY - built it right. Did each input state deliver? Read the evidence or the referent; confirm the work EXISTS and MATCHES ITS CLAIM. A bless is not proof. Concretely: open what the evidence points at rather than trusting its description of itself.\n\n2. VALIDATE - built the right thing. Does this meet the original intent - the frame, the vision, the REQUIREMENT REGISTER - and not merely its own plan? List what is missing, wrong, or out of scope. WATCH FOR ASKS THAT NO CHECK COVERED: a requirement no acceptance item happens to test is exactly where a design drifts from its register (se.law-requirements-are-never-weakened).\n\n3. RED-TEAM - argue the opposing case BEFORE you endorse. Cite a rubric, not vibes: the criteria, the register, the goal system. A significant decision carries a KILL-CRITERION - name what would have to be true for this to be the wrong call, then look for it. Frame open questions so they can be FALSIFIED rather than agreed with. An OVERRIDE blesses past an unmet criterion: it is legal, and it is logged WITH ITS DISSENT, never as a clean pass.\n\nRISK-WEIGHTED: deepest scrutiny first on the riskiest, most central items - risk rises with graph centrality, reversal history, and human-judgement verifiers. Trust computed marks; they are deterministic. SCALE TO SIZE: do not red-team a trivial gate.\n\nTHE AGENT NEVER SELF-CERTIFIES A KILLER. v1's answer was a human adjudicator. v2 wants unattended runs, so the substitute must be mechanical or adversarial rather than absent - and until one exists, a self-blessed killer is an OVERRIDE, logged as one with its dissent.\n\nVERDICT: PASS, PASS WITH NOTED OVERRIDES, or REOPEN with named states and reasons. No silent pass.\n\nNOT PORTED FROM v1, deliberately and recorded rather than dropped: the M4 DIAGRAM hard-rule and its boilerplate-stamp question. v2 reviews the MATRICES instead - the owner ruled that v1's diagrams never really worked and that the matrix review IS v2's replacement for both the diagram and the view selection.\n\nWHY THIS EXISTS: i12 found that meth-gate-review had required verify/validate/redteam/verdict since it was written, that no evidence form ever collected them, and that consequently NOT ONE had been filled in any gate of any iteration. The verify round alone would have caught R3 being violated by the very design claiming to satisfy it; the red-team round would have surfaced the owner's counter-argument before the decision rather than after."
