## Situation
Two dimensions, two cadences. SOLUTION: every gate carries the scan field for its milestone's artifact (idea at M1, requirement set at M3, reference architectures at M4). PROCESS: a standing retro question - has our method gone stale against current practice.

## Procedure
- Name the comparables: what exists, what failed, what the standard checklist for this artifact kind demands.
- Position: where ours differs, what it adopts, what it consciously rejects (with the reason).
- Record misses as additions or as recorded rejections - never silently.
- Scale to the gate's risk; a scan that adds nothing is a recorded skip with reason.

## Effect
The gap-as-a-claim form (every alternative sheds X) is only honest after this scan; the scan is what makes the claim checkable.
