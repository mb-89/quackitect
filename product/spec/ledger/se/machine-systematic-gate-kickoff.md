## Guidance

Review per [[meth-gate-review]]. The kickoff handover is ONE brief carrying everything: the drained retro, the iteration goal, the scope as pulled-in/left-out, and the rigor as a single field (strikes and reasoning named when pruned). The agent bakes scope and rigor into the brief - no separate confirmation rounds before the gate. One bless sets the iteration; a rejection names what to redo.

## Evidence form

- retro_drained | (killer) every inbox note has a recorded disposition | required
- goal | the confirmed one-line iteration goal | required
- pulled_in | what this iteration absorbs, each item with its origin | required
- left_out | what explicitly stays out, and where it went | required
- rigor | the rigor for this iteration with reasoning; strikes named when pruned | required
