# The autonomy scale

FIVE RUNGS, plus blocked. Each rung names a KIND of work, and the rung above
it is the one that decides that work needs doing: ideation commissions
strategic work, strategy commissions tactical, tactics commission
operational, operations commission mechanical.

Set the control to a rung and the agent may do work at that rung and at
every rung below it. The rungs are cumulative on purpose — you cannot hand
over a strategic decision while withholding the mechanical work it implies.

NO STATE IS EVER AUTHORED AT 0, and that is what makes a full block
possible. The gate refuses when a state's priority is GREATER than the
autonomy, so 0.01 is the least a step can weigh and 0 admits nothing at all.

BLOCKED IS A CONTROL POSITION, NOT A PRIORITY ANCHOR. The other rungs are
both: positions to click, and anchors to calibrate a state's `priority:`
against. Zero is only a position. Authoring a state at 0 would let it run at
the blocked setting, which is the one thing that setting promises cannot
happen.

IDEATION IS THE DANGEROUS ONE, and it is marked. Every other rung delegates
work that already exists; ideation delegates the CREATION of work, so it has
no natural stopping point and it spends budget until something stops it. The
owner's standing experience is that taking the person out of the loop
degrades the result.

next reload.

## Levels

- 0 | B | blocked — nothing moves without the human
- 0.01 | M | mechanical — carry out what is already decided
- 0.2 | O | operational — run the work, choose the order
- 0.4 | T | tactical — decide how a piece of work is done
- 0.6 | S | strategic — decide which work is worth doing
- 1 | I | ideation — the agent finds its own work
