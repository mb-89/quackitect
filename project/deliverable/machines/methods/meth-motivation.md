# The motivation — method

A motivation convinces a stranger that the product should exist. It is
the stop-or-continue filter: if it does not carry alone, nothing
downstream matters.

## The order

1. Open with the PAIN, as scenes. Two to four short situations the
   reader recognizes themselves in. Concrete, second person, no
   abstractions.
2. The as-is, in two parts. What is hard today. What JUST changed in
   the world to make a solution possible now.
3. The delta. What nobody offers, in one paragraph. Name the existing
   alternatives and the essential thing each gives up.
4. The to-be world. A working day after the product exists. Present
   tense, people named by role, a scene the reader can picture.
5. The goal, as an OUTCOME. What a person ends up WITH — never a
   feature list.
6. The goal system. Goals listed, conflicts named openly, priority
   ruled. The method: [[meth-goal-system]].
7. The pitch, last. Moore's five slots, verbatim shape. The method:
   [[meth-moore-pitch]].

## The rules

- Write for the user. They do not care which attempt this is — no
  history, no lineage, no method words (owner law, 2026-08-04).
- A claim about the world carries evidence or is marked as belief.
  Dated sources beside dated claims.
- Ask the falsifying question and keep it in the packet: what
  observation would make this vision wrong?
- State what the alternatives do BETTER before what ours does — a
  motivation that survives that order is real.
- If a section bores its own author, it will bore the stranger. Cut it
  or sharpen it.
