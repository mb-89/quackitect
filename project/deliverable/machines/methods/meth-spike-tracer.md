## Situation
M6's seeded spikes. rank-unknowns picks the targets: RAID entries, the M5 tripwires, and any doubtful verify_method.

## Procedure
- Timebox every spike. The box is the budget; running out IS a result.
- Pick the cheapest form that yields the evidence: a calculation, a simulation, a throwaway script, a tracer bullet through the real stack, a walking skeleton (basic functionality plus the architecturally significant qualities, end to end thin).
- The riskiest examples get scripted here - their first real run.
- Evidence updates the design: fold-back ripples requirements and architecture; the suspect mechanics reopen exactly what the evidence invalidates.
- Promotion into the build is legal THROUGH THE GATE: this spike is downstream of the whole process. A free expedition idea that bypassed the process enters at the top instead.
