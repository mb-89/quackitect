## Situation

ONE CARD, TWO USES (owner ruling 2026-08-08). A probe and a spike are the
same act — build the cheapest runnable thing that settles a question — so
they share this card and an improvement to either improves both.

- M4 `find_by_probing` builds to DISCOVER. The question is whether an option
  exists at all, and the answer is a new option node or a recorded dead end.
- M6's seeded spikes build to DERISK. rank-unknowns picks the targets: RAID
  entries, the M5 tripwires, and any doubtful verify_method.

## Procedure

- Timebox every run. The box is the budget; running out IS a result.
- Pick the cheapest form that yields the evidence.
  - A calculation.
  - A simulation.
  - A throwaway script.
  - A tracer bullet through the real stack.
  - A walking skeleton: basic functionality plus the architecturally
    significant qualities, end to end and thin.
- The riskiest examples get scripted here — their first real run.
- Evidence updates the design: fold-back ripples requirements and
  architecture; the suspect mechanics reopen exactly what the evidence
  invalidates.
- Promotion into the build is legal THROUGH THE GATE: this spike is
  downstream of the whole process. A free expedition idea that bypassed the
  process enters at the top instead.

## PROBING AS A FINDER — WHAT COULD NOT BE DONE BEFORE

This is the one genuinely NEW generator of the AI era, and it is new for a
boring reason: cost.

A spike used to cost days, so it was rationed to the riskiest unknown and
never spent on "is this even an option". Now the cheapest runnable version of
an idea is an hour, so building the thing becomes a way of FINDING OUT
whether it belongs on the chart at all.

Every other finder reasons about options. This one runs them.

THE OUTPUT IS THE SAME EITHER WAY. An option node with its source, or a
recorded dead end with what killed it. A probe that failed is evidence
nobody has to pay for twice, and it is worth more than an option nobody
tried.

THROWAWAY MEANS THROWAWAY. A probe's code is not a head start on the build,
and treating it as one is how a prototype ships by accident. What survives is
the finding.

## WHAT MAKES A PROBE HONEST

- IT HAS ONE QUESTION, written before it starts. A probe that discovers its
  question afterwards discovered a rationalisation.
- IT HAS A TIMEBOX, and running out is a verdict rather than a failure.
- IT NAMES WHAT IT FAKED. Every probe stubs something, and an unnamed stub is
  where a false positive lives.
