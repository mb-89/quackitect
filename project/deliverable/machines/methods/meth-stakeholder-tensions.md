## Situation

Two stakeholder roles pull against each other. One wants what the other pays
for.

That is a risk. It has an owner, a trigger, and something you would do about
it. Log it in the RAID register at log-risks, like any other risk.

## Why it is not a table of its own



v1 modelled a tension as a connection node of kind `conflicts-with` and had a
table ready to render them. It minted zero across 27 iterations.

v2 made it a required field on map-stakeholders. The field WAS filled, and
every entry it collected read as a risk with a mitigation attached. One
pointed at a RAID entry outright.

So the content is real. Its home was wrong, and a second home for one kind of
risk breaks the single-source rule.

## What this means in practice

- map-stakeholders carries no tensions field. Its guidance is
  [[meth-stakeholder-analysis]].
- A tension found while mapping roles goes to the register as a risk.
- The decision criteria at M4 read the register, which is where the tension
  now is.
