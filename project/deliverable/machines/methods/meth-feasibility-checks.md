## Situation
M4's candidate composition (rough checks per candidate) and M6's spikes (deep checks on the winner's riskiest unknowns). Keep checks proportional - prove what the decision needs, no more.

## Procedure
- Resource estimation, early and rough: energy, material, performance, volume, transport, storage, timing, lifetime - expert estimate or quick calculation.
- Worst-case calculation or simulation where a number decides viability.
- Risk analysis where failure modes decide: FMEA for the modes, FTA backward from the feared top event.
- Make / reuse / buy per major element; strategic suppliers involved early - their know-how changes solutions.
- Post-development effectiveness sanity: manufacturable, testable, serviceable, disposable.

## Sources
SyA FRAME feasibility group, re-expressed; Pahl & Beitz evaluation criteria.
