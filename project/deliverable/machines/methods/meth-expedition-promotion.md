## Situation

Two different origins, two different doors.

An M6 spike is the result of every step before it. Its promotion enters M7 as
a pre-verified starting chunk, recorded as promoted.

A free expedition is an idea explored on the side. It proved nothing about
needs, requirements or architecture.

Its keeper feeds design INPUT, and walks the milestones like any other claim.

## Procedure

- Explore in isolation. The record stays untouched by anything not promoted.
- On return, name what the expedition actually established.
  - Name separately what it merely made attractive.
- Promotion is gated and recorded. Laundering exploration in as finished work
  is the named failure.
