# Pyramid and progressive disclosure

A TLDR or abstract on top, then the high-level view, then detail. The fully
detailed discussion sits at the bottom. Roughly five paragraphs and up.
Method card: `project/deliverable/machines/methods/progressive-disclosure.md`.

## Already live

`se_lint` has a `pyramid` rule today. It fired on the draft of the answer
that proposed this matrix — twenty paragraphs with no structure — which is
a fair demonstration that the rule earns its place.

## Why tier one

Counting paragraphs and looking for headings is structural, not semantic.
Whether the summary is a GOOD summary is tier two, and not worth chasing.

## brief, refusal

Struck. Neither is long enough to have a shape.

## note

`could`, because a note is captured mid-walk and the point of a note is
that capturing it stays cheap. Contract rule 4 says note it and keep
walking; a structure rule that slows capture defeats the rule it serves.
