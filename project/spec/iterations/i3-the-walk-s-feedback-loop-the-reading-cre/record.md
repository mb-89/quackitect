# i3-the-walk-s-feedback-loop-the-reading-cre

## Goal

The walk's feedback loop: the reading credit survives a reload, a red objective serves its fill, and one verb answers why a state is grey.

## Rough vision

Three parked leads, one theme - the walk explains itself and stops re-charging for unchanged reading.

- The reading credit keys to DOCUMENT CONTENT HASHES and survives a reload; only changed documents re-serve (note-61b267004f20 - three reloads on 2026-08-11 re-owed fifteen unchanged documents each).
- A wait at a RED objective serves the claim's fill instead of "the target is where the walk already stands" (note-1f2cabfb9d0a).
- The introspection verb: the objective, the first grey state and its problems in one call, replacing the grey-probe se_run cluster (note-936e7a2d67d5).

Independent of every sibling iteration; touches session.ts route and the read-condition machinery.
