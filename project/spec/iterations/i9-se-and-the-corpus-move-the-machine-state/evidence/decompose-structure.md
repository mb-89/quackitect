## allocation

THE ALLOCATION IS ON THE ELEMENT NODES, in each one's `implements` key, which is where the newer artefact points at what it derives from. What follows is what this delta CHANGED about it.

### The three new elements and what they realize

- el-state-declaration implements resolve-a-path and bring-the-product-up. Both need the same answer about the machine-state folder, and neither owned it.
- el-corpus-reader implements answer-a-structured-query and judge-a-claim. Both turn trace files into nodes, and until now each did it its own way.
- el-preflight implements stand-up-a-product and bring-the-product-up. The first install and every later open both need to know a precondition failed before anything moved.

### What moved on the standing set

- el-arrival is superseded by el-entrypoint. Its seven functions do not disappear; they are realized by the merged path, and whether a person is present becomes an input rather than a second element.
- el-entrypoint therefore widens to cover the unattended case, which is what the merge decision buys and also what it risks.

### Where the spread is information rather than noise

TWO FUNCTIONS ARE NOW IMPLEMENTED BY THREE ELEMENTS EACH. bring-the-product-up is realized by el-entrypoint, el-state-declaration and el-preflight, and stand-up-a-product by el-bootstrap and el-preflight. That spread is the shape of the winner: the open path is thin, and what it consults is owned elsewhere.

MANY-TO-MANY IS LEGAL AND THIS IS WHY IT MATTERS HERE. A single element owning the open would have to own the declaration and the preflight too, and those are exactly the pieces the other candidates arranged differently.

### What is NOT claimed

NO FUNCTION IS LEFT UNIMPLEMENTED BY THIS DELTA'S CHANGES, but the full check across all thirty-eight functions and twenty-two elements is review-class rather than computed, and this state's guidance says so in its own words. What is asserted here is the delta: three elements added, one superseded, and every function they touch named on their own nodes.

## follow_up

THE ARCHITECTURE EVALUATION INHERITS A STRUCTURE WITH ONE FOLD IN IT. el-arrival is superseded into el-entrypoint, and the scenario walk is where that fold either holds or shows its seam. The unattended path is the scenario to walk first, because it is the one the merge endangers.

THREE NEW ELEMENTS ARRIVE WITH NO INTERFACES YET NAMED. Where a flow's producer and consumer land in different elements, that pair owes one. The obvious owed pairs are el-state-declaration against el-resolution-seam, el-preflight against el-entrypoint, and el-corpus-reader against everything that used to read files itself.

ONE ELEMENT CARRIES AN OPEN DESIGN QUESTION ON ITS OWN NODE. el-preflight has to say where the line falls between what the installer installs and what it reports as missing, and the second hand found that tension rather than us.

THE SUPERSEDED ELEMENT IS MARKED RATHER THAN DELETED, deliberately. A reader who finds a missing element cannot tell a fold from a mistake, and this iteration has already had one incident where nodes vanished without a trace.
