## follow_up

THE PROFILE OUTRANKS EVERYTHING ELSE HERE. The winner's case is parallelism on many weak cores. Nobody has run a profile of a slow call, and IO-bound slowness would empty the case. M6 runs it first.

TWO MEASUREMENTS DECIDE THE COST.

- A satellite start with the engine module load INCLUDED. Every figure on the board excludes it.
- The price of a core-owned read becoming a channel hop.

THREE DECISIONS THE WINNER OWES AND HAS NOT TAKEN. They are named in record-adrs and repeat here because the deck's risks land on them.

- What a satellite is: a process, a worker thread or an isolate.
- What the core serves and over what channel, and whether that channel is bound to loopback.
- What a partial failure looks like, which is the in-flight call three risks hinge on.

THE THIRD LEVEL IS OWED. The engine delta resolves record then trunk. A vehicle overlay layered on the open engine is a third level, and two fatal-to-crippling scenarios hinge on it being composed rather than asserted.

THE BOOTSTRAP OWES A PROCESS START. It converges files today. Under this structure a greeting needs a core up and a satellite per open record.

raid-dec-thin-tree READS DECIDED AND IS UNBUILT, found by the probe of 2026-08-14. The winner rests on it. Either the status is corrected or the build is the architecture's opening cost.

OWED FROM THIS MILESTONE'S OWN RUN.

- The demand check as a mechanism, not a hand-built table.
- A verdict cell that carries the quote it rests on.
- Answering a clear jump in one call, deferred to specify-build.
- The bless path's latency, reported above one second by the owner.

That is both halves at once. The change reaches because every read is a trunk read; nothing unlanded crosses because a record's tree holds only its own folder.

req-acts-carry-role-and-channel. el-account: "every call logged with role and channel". The stamp is set where a call arrives, and el-core: "A call arrives naming a path." The core is that place for every call.
