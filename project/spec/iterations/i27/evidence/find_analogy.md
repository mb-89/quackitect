## follow_up

THE TWO TRANSFERS ARE ONE PROTOCOL IN THEIR OWN DOMAINS, and that is worth carrying to build_chart. Surgery runs the site mark AND the time-out. Aviation runs the readback AND the hearback. Neither field treats them as alternatives, and the chart should not either.

THE MARKING OPTION IS A GENUINELY NEW SHAPE. Prior art produced confine-the-root or judge-the-path. This produces resolve-once-early-and-stamp, which covers reads and writes alike without moving the root - and the read case is precisely what raid-iss-cheaper-alternative-never-compared names as the deciding question.

THE RECORD-LIFE ABSTRACTION IS OWED A SWEEP. req-archive-releases-worktrees is unmet, twenty-seven worktrees stood on this machine on 2026-08-13, and the leak-detection literature around memory ownership is the obvious first domain. Parked here rather than done, ready when this record reaches its own build.

FIVE DOMAINS ON ROW 1 ARE STILL OWED, and rail signalling's token block is the one most likely to pay - it is the only named mechanism that makes holding the right to write a physical object rather than a check.

## anything_else

WHY THE FAILURE MODE IS THE FIND, not the mechanism.

Aviation's own literature names the HEARBACK ERROR as the dangerous case: an uncorrected wrong readback that goes undetected until the controller sees the deviation on the display, when separation is already lost.

Put that beside raid-risk-a-write-lands-in-the-wrong-tree-silently, written here on 2026-08-13 with no knowledge of it: the work appears to land, and it is found at a merge, or never.

Two fields reached the same sentence independently. That is the strongest evidence this state can produce that our risk is real rather than anxious, and it cost one search.

THE SURGICAL FINDING IS ABOUT TIMING, WHICH IS THE PART USUALLY LOST. The protocol does not merely require a mark. It requires the mark before anaesthesia, because after that the one party who can confirm the correct side cannot speak. Our equivalent of anaesthesia is depth of walk: by the time a write happens, the caller who knew which record they meant is many states back.
