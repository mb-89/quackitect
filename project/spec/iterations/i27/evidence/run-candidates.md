## follow_up

THE FIFTH LINE IS INCOHERENT AS DRAWN. It takes the thin-tree pick, where a
record's worktree holds only that record's own folder and everything shared
is read from trunk. Under that pick the record holds no engine to change, and
the line's whole mechanism assumes it does.

ONE DECISION IS OWED UNDER THREE OF THE FIVE LINES and no chart row states
it: does a record's worktree carry the machine that record is allowed to
change. Answer no and self-hosting is impossible. Answer yes and the copies
drift. Nobody drew the middle.

THE ROUGH CHECKS THAT ARE STILL GUESSES. What N engine processes cost, for
cand-os-rooted. What a module swap costs inside a call's latency budget, for
cand-live-engine. Both are cheap probes and both belong to M6 rather than
here.

NO SPIKES WERE RUN, correctly. The rough checks are this state's; the deep
ones are M6's, on the winner alone.

run-candidates had both signed. Neither submachine can be re-entered: a
reopen refuses because a submachine carries no form of its own, and reopening
one of its substates leaves the parent's view of it met.
