## goal_system

THE VISION IS INHERITED, NOT RE-DERIVED. i17 does not move the big idea, the to-be world or the pitch, which is what the minor column drops mechanically. The resident vision stands and this state carries the delta only.

THE DELTA IN ONE BREATH: a raw capture is private and machine-local; an option is public and travels; and ONE act crosses between them, which is a rewrite.

FOUR CONFLICTS, each between goals that already stand, each ruled here because ruling them later is expensive.

### One: the pool must travel, and a raw note must never travel

THIS IS THE ITERATION'S WHOLE TENSION and the seed states both halves as absolutes. RAW NOTES NEVER ENTER VERSION CONTROL - they are dumps and can carry private data, and the seed calls it a hard line that does not bend. And the pool must be visible to any machine that clones the repository, or there is no pool.

RULED: BOTH HOLD, AND THE REWRITE IS WHAT MAKES THEM BOTH TRUE. Nothing is copied across the boundary. An option is AUTHORED at the drain, from the note, by whoever is draining it, and the note stays where it was. The two artifacts are different objects with different lifetimes on purpose.

THE PRICE IS ACCEPTED AND NAMED: two objects can drift. The rewritten option can stop describing the note it came from, and nothing will notice. Linear and GitHub Projects both avoid that by keeping ONE object end to end, and they can, because their capture is already inside the shared system (ref-triage-and-option-pools-2026). We cannot, so we pay the drift.

WHAT MAKES THE PRICE WORTH IT: the drain is the last moment anybody looks at the note with intent. A rewrite there costs one sentence; a leak costs the owner's privacy and cannot be undone once it is on trunk.

### Two: the rewrite is a privacy boundary, and it is also a quality filter

ONE ACT DOES TWO JOBS, and the seed says so deliberately: an option nobody can state cleanly for another machine was never an option, it was a stray.

THE TENSION IS THAT THE TWO JOBS WANT DIFFERENT STRICTNESS. As a privacy boundary the rewrite must be MANDATORY - a bypass, even a convenient one, is the whole hole. As a quality filter it wants to be REFUSABLE: some strays genuinely cannot be stated cleanly yet, and forcing a sentence produces a worse artifact than admitting the gap.

RULED: PRIVACY WINS ON THE MECHANISM, QUALITY WINS ON THE VERDICT. The rewrite is always required and never skippable, so there is no path from a raw dump to trunk. What the author may do is state plainly that the option is not yet expressible, and that IS a clean statement - the pool carries it as an open question rather than as a guess. The seed already rules the same way for the migration: what cannot be stated cleanly goes back to the owner as a list rather than being guessed at.

THE LOSER IS THE BULK IMPORT. v1 refused one outright on the same reasoning: NOTHING ENTERS THE LEDGER WITHOUT JUDGMENT. This iteration inherits that refusal rather than re-arguing it.

### Three: an unattended agent must be able to file, and nothing unjudged may land

THE CLOUD CASE IS THE OWNER'S OWN ADDITION to this record, 2026-08-17: an unattended walk needs somewhere to put a finding that survives the box being released. Today it has nowhere - the i15 run's debt note is simply gone, and this session's three findings would have gone the same way had the box been released before the retro drained them.

THE TENSION: filing must be cheap enough that an agent mid-walk actually does it, and judged enough that trunk does not fill with an agent's unreviewed prose.

RULED: THE FILING IS CHEAP AND THE JUDGMENT IS THE REWRITE, WHICH THE AGENT PERFORMS. It is the same act a person performs, not a lighter one. An agent that cannot state its finding cleanly files it as an open question, exactly as rule two says.

WHAT IS NOT RULED HERE, and it is deliberately left: whether an agent-filed option needs a different STATUS from a person-filed one. That is a question for the requirements, and inventing an answer at the vision is how a mechanism gets a field nobody needs.

### Four: the pool wants to be complete, and the corpus wants to stay walkable

EVERY OPTION IN THE POOL IS A NODE, and every pass over the corpus walks every node. 205 parked options against 1304 existing nodes is a sixth of the corpus arriving in one act.

RULED: COMPLETENESS WINS, AND THE COST IS MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSUMED. A pool that drops options to stay small is a backlog with extra steps. The mitigation is that the migration is a separate act with its own before-and-after timing, which is where raid-asm-a-migrated-pool-does-not-drown-the-corpus puts it.

THE PRICE IF THE ASSUMPTION FAILS: the pool needs a store of its own, and this iteration is not a minor. That escalation is visible by construction, because the probe is a timing rather than an opinion.

### What no conflict was found between

TRAVEL AND SURVIVAL do not compete, though the seed names them as two separate arguments. A pool on trunk is visible to every clone AND survives a discarded experiment, because both properties come from the same fact - it is committed. One mechanism answers both, and no trade-off sits between them.

THE PRIORITY ORDER, for every later criteria weighting: privacy first, then survival, then travel, then completeness of the pool. Privacy is first because it is the only one whose failure cannot be undone.
