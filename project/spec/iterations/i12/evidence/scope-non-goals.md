# Evidence form / scope-non-goals

## current_situation

The delta says the machine must stop spending the person's time on waiting. Scope says how much of that this record takes on.

The kickoff already ruled the record-level scope at minor. This state states the same boundary at vision level, so a reader who never opens the kickoff still knows what was left.

## scope

THIS EFFORT TAKES ON THE MACHINE'S OWN ANSWER TIME, and the instrument that measures it.

- The instrument first. A scoped run must record its timings, or nothing here can be judged.
- The surfaces that break the one-second rule and are measured breaking it: opening the state machine, and opening an evidence form.
- The battery's wall clock, attacked at its tallest pole rather than across the suite.
- The pull's answer, which pages rather than overflowing.
- The survey's window, which must govern every list it prints.
- The comparison walk, cached rather than recomputed per call.
- The routing defects that cost this walk an escape, pulled in on the owner's ruling of 2026-08-15.

THE BOUNDARY IN ONE LINE: everything here is the machine answering faster while proving exactly what it proves today.

## non_goals

- THE PERSON'S SCREEN. A toast and a progress bar for anything past a second is the surface half of the rule. It is screen work, it belongs with i23, and the owner runs that one at the screen.
- THE TICKET DESK. Submitting long work and polling a handle is a build of its own. The record says that round wants a walk that starts on it, so it is not cut from here, it was never in here.
- PRELOADING ON A GUESS. Preloading is sanctioned and it is not a non-goal in itself. Preloading anything that has not been measured is, because that moves work rather than saving it.
- THINNING THE BATTERY. Ruled at draft-vision and recorded as raid-dec-speed-never-buys-from-the-guard. The wall clock falls without the case count falling, or the win is not a win.
- A FASTER ANSWER THAT SAYS LESS. Same ruling. Paging keeps the whole answer reachable; cutting does not, and a cut answer is worse than a slow one because nothing announces what went missing.
- THE CLOUD, THE SHELL AND THE ENGINE'S OTHER DEBTS. Every named item here is answer time or the instrument that measures it. Nothing else earns a place because it happens to be nearby.

## follow_up

- The non-goals are not soft. Three of them are backed by a register decision, so a later state contradicting one is superseding a ruling rather than making a fresh call.
- The binding system-level exclusion list is owed at draw-context in M2, and this vision-level list feeds it.

## anything_else

ON WHY THE FIRST SCOPE ITEM IS AN INSTRUMENT RATHER THAN A SPEED-UP.

A performance record whose first item is a measurement fix reads like a detour. It is the opposite.

Every other item in the scope is chosen by a ranking, and the ranking comes from battery numbers that carry twenty-way contention. Nothing separates a case's own work from its queueing.

So the ranking may be right and may be an artefact, and today there is no way to tell. Fixing that costs one reporter argument on a spawn.

If it turns out the ranking was right all along, the cost was one call and the record gains certainty. If it was wrong, it saved the iteration from landing work where it changes nothing.
