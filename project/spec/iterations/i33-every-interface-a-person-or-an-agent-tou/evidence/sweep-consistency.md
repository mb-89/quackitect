### The four that changed

COMMAND AND TOOL DOCS. se_amend's `chain` argument described behaviour the tool does not perform — it is accepted and ignored. The description now says so. It is documented rather than removed, so nobody rebuilds it from the same wrong premise.

ENGINE-SERVED STRINGS. Two taught an act that had stopped working. The stale-claim message and the fallen-input remedy both told a reader to amend a claim that is down, which cannot clear it. Both now name se_reopen and explain the recheck. A third, a comment above the claim-time call, asserted the reversed rule four thousand lines from its own correction.

GUIDANCE CHAPTERS. refusals.md gained the amend and reopen rule under SE-C-112, which is where the contract says every clause's rule must stand ahead of time. That entry was generic, and the two paths under it are the ones that surprise people: a stale claim cannot be amended back, and a field another form reads cannot be amended at all.

README AND ENTRY DOCUMENTS. The status block was three stages out of date and named two commands that never existed, which a fresh-eyes tester found. It is rewritten to what a reader can check, and it deliberately does not list what is missing — a list like that goes stale exactly the way that block did. The register carries that instead.

### The five that did not, and why

METHOD CARDS. Checked rather than assumed: a search across guidance for the amend rule returns five hits and every one is historical prose about an earlier iteration, not a teaching of the semantics. The cards say what a step is FOR. The two acts on a standing claim are the engine's vocabulary and are taught in the refusals.

MATRIX ROWS. The goals list and both new rounds are compiled INTO every gate by the machine compiler rather than written per row, which is why the change reached all three gates of this column without a row being edited.

TEMPLATES AND SKELETONS. Both new rounds use templates that already stood, per-item for the goals and the bounds. No template gained or lost a field.

BOOK CHAPTERS. Nothing this iteration changed alters what the book teaches about how work is driven. The goals list makes a gate ask one more question; it does not change what a gate IS.
