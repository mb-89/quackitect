## business_case

THE DELTA'S NEW NEED, written in full with its pass lines, and added to vp-rigor-without-toil rather than to a new prop.

NEED: a slow interface says so, and a person is never left guessing whether it is working.
OUTCOME: silence stops being an acceptable answer from anything a person or an agent touches.

PASS LINES, both zero-target and both checkable once milestone one supplies the list:

- Modelled interfaces that breach the one-second rule without telling the person they are working. Target: zero.
- A control that declines a person's input and reports nothing about why. Target: zero.

WHY THIS IS A NEW NEED AND NOT A RESTATEMENT. i12 added the SPEED half of the owner's framing and not the HONESTY half. Their words carry both in one breath: "everything that is over one second needs to be non-intrusive... Either that fast, or very transparent about how slow it is." Under the criterion as it stood, an interface could miss the rule silently and fail nothing.

IT IS ALREADY BREACHED, MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED. AND THE DELTA REPAIRS i12'S OWN CRITERION WITHOUT REWRITING IT. That criterion asks for a SHARE of surface requests answered inside a second. Until interfaces are modelled as nodes there is no list to take a share over, which is why the rule shipped and never held.
