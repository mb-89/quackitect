## current_situation

The product's first vision packet, drafted from the settled talk with the owner: the corpus mined (the founding sebot motivation with the Venn, the two research briefs, v1's front door), the market checked as of August 2026, and every field redlined line by line in chat before this submit.

## big_idea

Architecture decides whether technology is good — and a wrong architectural decision made early costs a thousand times more to correct later. Architecture, in turn, is only as good as its design input: give a model thin input and it fills the gaps with plausible slop. quackitect forces the chain to happen properly — proven engineering method drives the agent through design input and architecture FIRST — and then hands the output end to the tools of the discipline: for software, the crowded field of LLM coding harnesses; for mechanical, electrical or control engineering, their own. The output end stays open by design.

## to_be_world

You draw the process in Obsidian, set a target, and leave. The agent walks all evening — every act logged, every claim landing in a form. In the morning the drawing stands green up to a gate; you read the evidence, press approve, and it walks on. Nothing moved unrecorded.

Two years later a new colleague asks why a decision went the way it went. Instead of a zombie discussion, the record answers: the decision, its evidence, its alternatives, and who approved it — one click away.

## goal_system

- Proper architecture, forced. Architecture decides whether technology is good, and a wrong early call costs a thousandfold later. The machine refuses to skip ahead to the output — that refusal is the product.
- Design input before architecture. Nobody architects well on thin input, and a model fills thin input with slop. The forms, the gates and the questions exist for this.
- Proven method, carried by the machine. Forty years of systems engineering — the agent is taught it, never asked to rediscover it.
- Agent strength respected at the output end. Design and generation are what agents are genuinely good at — governed lightly there, guidance instead of cage. For software output the many LLM coding harnesses plug in; other disciplines bring their own tools. The control conflict is ruled by phase: strict at input and architecture, light at output.
- Adjudication and the record, supporting. The person decides at gates; the trace and documentation fall out of the walk and kill a team's zombie questions. Conflicts with speed — priced openly by the change size and the dial.
- The frame: the governing machine is deterministic code wherever code can carry it; the method is discipline-agnostic — software, electromechanical, control, any technical product; and the non-goals are named — not replacing coding harnesses, not another output generator.

## moore_pitch

FOR engineering teams driving work with AI agents WHO need good architecture and not just fast output, quackitect IS A design-input and architecture harness THAT drives agents through proven engineering method — input first, architecture second, output last — with a human decision at every gate. UNLIKE the output-end tools — the many LLM coding harnesses for software, and each discipline's own generators — it forces the input end, where quality is decided, and composes with all of them.
