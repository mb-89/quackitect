## hostile_faq

- Why now and not two years ago? => Agents only recently became genuinely competent at the output end, and the damage of leaving them ungoverned became measurable at ecosystem scale.
- Why you and not the platform vendors? => They ship the output end and the fleet runtime; nobody ships forced design input, forced architecture and recorded approvals. The middle is open and we work in it daily.
- What breaks first? => The agent-steering scaffolding — model generations obsolete it. Priced in: the record and the ledger are the durable half; the steering lives as replaceable data.
- What does it cost? => Walltime at gates and discipline at the input end, priced openly by the change size and the autonomy dial. A patch walks a short machine.
- What was given up? => The whole output end: no code generator, no CAD engine, no commercial ambition. Composition instead of competition.
- Does the rigor not just produce faster slop? => Only if quality can fall, and it cannot: forms refuse incomplete claims, gates refuse unsigned inputs, and quality only ratchets up.
- Can one person really adjudicate all of it? => The honest open question. Adjudication erodes the skills it needs; the register owns this with a hands-on cadence — watched, not solved.
