# Evidence form / scope-non-goals

## current_situation

Scope drawn directly from the signed delta: the input-and-architecture middle is taken on in full, the output end deliberately left open. The binding system-level exclusion list follows at draw-context.

## scope

What this product takes on, at vision level:

- The INPUT end of engineering with agents: design input, architecture, and the decisions between them — driven through proven method, carried by drawn machines.
- The governance of agent behavior while that happens: the walk, the forms, the gates, the recorded approvals — strict at input and architecture.
- The record that falls out: decisions with evidence and approvers, traceable years later.
- The whole chain for ANY technical discipline — software, electromechanical, control — the domain lives in the drawn machine, not the engine.
- Light guidance at the output end: our own lane exists, and the walk hands off cleanly to whatever output tool the discipline uses.

## non_goals

What this product deliberately leaves, at vision level:

- Not another output generator: it does not compete on producing code or CAD — the crowded output field does that, and it plugs in.
- Not a coding-harness replacement: agents keep their runtimes; the walk composes with them.
- Not a commercial product: internal and strategic, full stop.
- Not a documentation tool: the record falls OUT of the walk; writing documents is never the point, deciding well is.
- Not a people-process: it governs agents, not humans — people aim, judge and approve; nothing here schedules or measures them.
