# Use-case seeds — the eighteen capability statements written as stories by mistake

These were authored in the story folder on 2026-08-06 and ruled the wrong
shape the same day (note-1e271dce1d72). They describe WHAT THE SYSTEM CAN DO,
which is the use-case question, not the story question. A story is a journey
with an order; these have none.

They are kept because the content is sound. generalize-use-cases mines them
and gives each one a Cockburn shape: actor, goal, main success scenario as
numbered steps, extensions off those steps.

Each line is `<capability> — <the proposition it was filed under>`.

## The lane and the cage

- The machine refuses to open a step until its input is earned — systematic engineering.
- A read is proven by the document's last words, which truncation cannot supply — systematic engineering.
- A refusal is typed and carries the exact call to make instead — rigor without toil.
- The method for a step is handed over before the step opens — rigor without toil.
- An artifact's shape is one click from the field asking for it — rigor without toil.

## The walk and its records

- An iteration's unfinished work lives in its own worktree, off the tree you demo from — systematic engineering.
- A test run answers one question; the full battery is earned at the gates — systematic engineering.
- The ceremony matches the change size, chosen once by a person at the kickoff — rigor without toil.
- The desk reads the live system and recommends one vehicle — rigor without toil.

## The record

- A stray finding is captured in one call and survives without the detour — the ledger.
- Progress is readable without interrupting the work — the ledger.
- A decision answers its own why from the record, years later — the ledger.
- The trace graph answers what serves what by looking — the ledger.

## The dial

- At the top, an unattended walk stops only where it must — autonomy range.
- At zero, the whole system runs with no AI in the loop — autonomy range.
- The setting changes mid-walk without restarting anything — autonomy range.

## The vehicle

- A builder overlays their own method without forking the engine — vendoring.
- The first upstream update is a replace, not a merge — vendoring.
