## set_criteria

- complete: Every pass line from gate-motivation has a row, and every row traces to one. One tree, status as the open flag, the archive staying on disk, and the container binding nothing uninvited. The two remaining pass lines — the stubs surviving and the battery green — are not requirements: the first is a migration step's acceptance, the second is a standing law of every iteration.
- consistent: No row contradicts another, and one pair was checked deliberately. Keeping a closed folder on trunk and deciding open from status could conflict if status were derived from the folder — it is not. The folder is presence; the status is the claim. That is exactly the separation the second row demands.
- affordable: Every row is verifiable with what already exists. Three by test, one by inspection. No new instrument is needed, which is why no row asks for one.
- bounded: Each row states ONE demand. The one that was tempted to state two — resolving in one tree AND deleting the seam — keeps only the demand; which symbols must not exist is in its Detail, where it belongs.
- comprehensible: Each statement names the actor and the response without a mechanism. The one that comes closest to naming a mechanism is the selection row, and its statement says what must not happen rather than what state must exist — the shape is in the Detail.
- no_tbd: No row carries TODO, TBD or a placeholder. Every verify_method is named, every damage grade chosen, every refines resolved to a standing use case.
- behaviour_modelled: NO MODEL WAS DRAWN, and that is a judgment rather than an omission. The method's test is whether a model says something the statement and the Detail cannot. Three of these are one condition and one response; a diagram would restate them in a second notation that can then disagree. The fourth, the selection row, is the only candidate — it is a missing transition — and its transition is named in one line: entering the container lands on selection, and selection binds only on a choice.
