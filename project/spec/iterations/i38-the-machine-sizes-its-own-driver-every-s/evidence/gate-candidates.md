## reasons_hold

EACH CUT, CHECKED AGAINST THE CANDIDATE RECORDS RATHER THAN READ.

Five rows were struck on their own merits: trace-source-never-mixes, overlay-resolution, overlay-survives-update, resume-needs-no-person, newcomer-leaves-able-to-ask. The reason given in each cell was that all four candidates meet them identically. That is checkable and was checked mechanically: the words trunk, trace view, overlay, engine version, vendored, panel, returning, newcomer, tour and onboard appear in none of the four candidate records. Not "about the same" — absent. The cuts hold.

THE HUNDRED AND NINE ROWS BELOW THE CUTOFF ARE OUT BY POSITION AND OWE NO REASON, and the gate should still ask what went with them. The one thing that could have hidden there is a cost axis, and it is not there to hide: the whole pool of 119 was searched for cost, spend, budget, waste, price and latency, and exactly one row matched — a process rule about an option carrying a cost entry. The position cut lost nothing that measures spend, because nothing in the pool does.

THE ONE MOVE, JUDGED AGAINST THE QUESTION THE GATE ASKS: does the reason hold, or does it favour a candidate?

req-comparison-carries-both-sides moved from 52 to 10. The reason recorded was that it is the sharpest discriminator on the chart. THE REASON IS OVERSTATED AND THE MOVE FAVOURED THE BASELINE. Scored, the axis returned 4, 3, 3, 3 — a one-point spread, and the single 4 went to cand-the-seed-made-total, the candidate composed first and described in this record as the baseline. "Sharpest discriminator" was a prediction about a scoring that had not happened, and the scoring did not bear it out.

WHAT SAVES IT IS THAT IT CHANGED NOTHING, AND THAT IS MEASURED. Recomputing the Pareto front over the four remaining axes gives the same three candidates: cand-the-seed-made-total, cand-the-reader-beside-the-walk, cand-the-receiver-decides. Recomputing without the all-zero axis gives the same three again. The front is stable under removing either row, so a move that advantaged one candidate did not move the outcome.

THE HONEST RECORD IS THEREFORE: the move was made on a prediction, the prediction was wrong in degree, the beneficiary was the composer's own baseline, and the result was unaffected. All four of those belong in the record together. A gate that reported only the fourth would be reporting that nothing went wrong.

THE FRONT IS THREE OF FOUR, NOT ONE, so the space did not collapse upstream. The single elimination is disputed in evaluate-set's own reading and the dispute is recorded beside the score rather than over it: cand-the-derived-ladder is dominated because its record is silent on the call record, and it is silent because at build_chart the record-cell axis carried one distinct value and no line was required to pick from it. That is a defect in how the lines were drawn, not a property of the architecture, and M5 is where it is repaired if it is repaired.

## bound_breaches

- if-agent-harness-to-entrypoint: BREACHED, measured in this session, and the first figure this gate wrote was wrong. The interface declares `bound: 1 second`. Of 1520 lane calls carrying a duration, 89 exceed it — 5.9 per cent — and 87 of those 89 are se_pull; se_run contributes one and every other verb contributes zero. THE FIRST FIGURE SAID 181 OF 1513, 12.0 PER CENT, AND IT COUNTED THE WRONG POPULATION: the log also holds `mirror_slow` records, 92 of them, every one over a second because a mirror_slow record IS the report of a slow call, so each breach was counted twice. Same error class as the pinned-record miscount this record made at M1: a population that looked like the one being measured. THE PULL IS THE WHOLE BREACH. Across 460 pulls the median is 357 ms and the tail is far outside the bound: p90 1686 ms, p95 2761 ms, p99 16285 ms, worst 18377 ms. A pull carrying a form breaches 26 per cent of the time (62 of 239) against 11 per cent for a bare pull (25 of 221). THE BOUND HAS NO PERCENTILE AND THAT IS ITS OWN FINDING: read as a hard ceiling the interface fails outright, read as a median it passes with room, and nobody has written which, so a 5.9 per cent breach rate cannot be judged against it. M4 did not cause this and is not innocent of it: two of the three candidates on the front put a per-step read on this interface, and a design adding work to the one verb that carries the entire breach owes the measurement, and now has it.
