THE SEAM IS A FILE FORMAT, NOT AN INTERFACE. The reader and the walk share the call record and nothing else, and the record is also the publication. That is the cheapest seam of the four and the most brittle: a change to the record shape silently changes what the reader computes, with nothing failing loudly.

ONE JOIN IS UNCHOSEN AND IT MATTERS. A reader deriving difficulty from reopen counts produces a continuous rate; a mapping checked for totality wants a closed domain. Bucketing the rate into rungs is the join, and nobody has picked the boundaries — which is where a recommendation quietly becomes an opinion.
