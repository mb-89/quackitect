## current_situation

The six test specs are signed. This state says HOW the thing is designed below the architectural line, then says WHAT is built in which order.

TWO DESIGN SPECS AND TEN CHUNKS. The specs are two because the iteration touches two elements: `el-sizing`, which is new and is the whole of its cluster, and `el-account`, which stands and grows three fields.

### Why the account side gets a new spec rather than an edit

`dsp-call-log` already realizes `el-account` and it is about the log's SHAPE — what is appended, what is capped, how it rotates. The three coordinates are a different design concern: WHO the record can name, and which of the three names the server can see for itself.

ONE OF THE THREE IS OBSERVABLE AND TWO ARE CLAIMED, and that asymmetry is the whole of the new spec. The state is known where the call is served. The model is not, because the transport hands the engine a client name and no model. The part played is not either, and for a sharper reason: the dispatcher stamps every agent the same, and a relayed answer never reaches the dispatcher as the delegate's at all.

### The chunk drawing is two chains that meet once

THE SIZING CHAIN runs from the ledger guard to the pull. THE ACCOUNT CHAIN runs from the three fields to the log query. They are independent and can fan out, and they meet at `a-weaker-walk-carries-its-reason`, which needs a named strength from one and a record from the other.

THE LEDGER GUARD GOES FIRST AND ALONE. `req-the-complexity-value-is-read-live-and-never-pinned` is graded fatal, wants one assertion, and guards three records open right now. It is also the cheapest red in the set, which is the argument for putting it first rather than for putting it last.

### What is deliberately not a chunk

RATING THE 154 ACTIVE CELLS. The build makes a difficulty declarable and refusable; it declares none. Doing both here would put fifty-three unreviewed judgements in the same commit as the mechanism that reads them.

ANYTHING THAT SPAWNS. The lane may not, and the party that does act already exists outside the box.

MAINTAINING THE MODEL LIST. There is no roster in this tree under the declared design, and the maintenance role still has no node.

### Ten crossings in the corpus were realized by no spec, and this state's law found them

THE CHECK READS THE WHOLE CORPUS, not this iteration's slice. It named ten interfaces no design spec realizes, one of them minted by this iteration and nine standing from earlier ones.

NONE NEEDED A NEW SPEC. Each was placed on the spec whose design already carries the crossing, with a paragraph saying why that spec is its home rather than a bare edge added to satisfy a check.

- FOUR ON `dsp-the-state-declaration`, which already says every consumer is GENERATED from the declaration rather than written beside it. A generated consumer is a crossing.
- FOUR ON `dsp-unattended-entrypoint`. Three inbound are the same shape — something that ran once handing to the path that runs every time — and the fourth is outbound, carrying the harness identity, because the entrypoint is what identifies the harness.
- ONE ON `dsp-call-log`, which already carries every other `*-to-account` crossing, because what a record holds is one design and not five.
- ONE ON `dsp-the-producing-acts`, beside its sibling crossing from the same act.

THE CONVENTION IS NOT UNIFORM AND THAT IS WHY EACH ONE IS ARGUED. Some specs carry their inbound crossings and some their outbound. The rule the template gives is the one applied: a link is a CONTRIBUTION, and a spec names a crossing only where its design actually serves it.

## follow_up

THE TWO PROMOTED SPIKES ENTER AS ASSIGNED CHUNKS AND NEITHER IS A STARTING ONE, which is worth saying because the guidance describes a promotion as a pre-verified STARTING chunk.

`exp-two-hands-rating-the-same-six-cells` enters at `the-sizing-block-answers`. Its promotion is a rule about which rows may carry a rating, and the rule has nowhere to hold until a cell can carry one.

`exp-can-anything-act-on-a-published-driver` enters at `the-call-record-grows-three-fields`. Its promotion is the acting party itself, and the party is outside the box — what changes INSIDE the box is that a delegated hand must be recordable.

TWO FILES NAMED IN THE SPECS DO NOT EXIST YET AND ONE IS NEW. `engine/sizing.ts` is the element's whole realization and is written at `the-sizing-block-answers`. Planned names are legal here and get their teeth at `trace-design`, after the build.

THREE CLAIMED FILES ARE ALREADY CLAIMED BY OTHER SPECS — `rigor-matrix.ts`, `pull.ts`, `tools.ts` and `calllog.ts` between them. That is the file grain working as intended: a spec claims the files its design lands in, and a file serving two designs is claimed twice.

THE ORDER INSIDE THE ACCOUNT CHAIN PUTS THE VOCABULARY AFTER THE FIELDS, and that is a bet worth naming. Growing a part field with a two-value vocabulary and widening it later is exactly the shape that produced the defect this iteration is fixing. The chunk statement carries the demand explicitly so the widening is not a follow-up.
