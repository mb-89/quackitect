## why_these

FOUR CANDIDATES, DRAWN ALONG THE ONE AXIS THAT REORGANISES EVERYTHING ELSE: whether our tree holds a roster and names a driver, and whether the thing deciding sits inside the walk.

- The seed made total keeps both. It is the baseline every requirement in this record was written against, so it starts with the fewest standing claims to reopen and the others have to beat it rather than replace it.
- The derived ladder keeps both and removes the declaration. It is the only line on the chart that cannot drift, because there is no typed number to raise.
- The reader beside the walk keeps the roster and takes the decider out of the walk entirely. It is the only line that is useful on the day it runs.
- Whoever holds the hands decides keeps the decider in the walk and gives up the roster. It is the only line under which two standing assumptions become unnecessary rather than merely unproven.

WHAT THE SPACE HOLDS THAT THESE FOUR DO NOT. Fifteen axes multiply out to 1080 combinations. Four is a sample of roughly one in two hundred and seventy, and the sampling rule was: take one line per position on the roster-and-decider axis, then fill every remaining axis with the cell that candidate's own logic implies.

NINE CELLS ARE ON NO LINE, AND THEY FALL INTO THREE KINDS.

- FOUR ARE ALTERNATIVE FORMULAS RATHER THAN ALTERNATIVE ARCHITECTURES, so they belong to run-candidates as variations inside cand-the-derived-ladder: deriving the rung from what will judge the output, scoring the work at dispatch, a rung naming an intensity as well as a worker, and the row naming the driver with no mapping at all.
- THREE ARE MOVES ANY LINE COULD TAKE WITHOUT CHANGING ITS ARCHITECTURE: one driver per record at kickoff and splitting a submachine where the spread is wide, both granularity, and running cheap and escalating on a failed check, which is a correction rather than a decision.
- TWO ARE PROPERTIES EVERY LINE COULD CARRY AND NONE TURNS ON: the decision being fixed within a run, and the driver reading the difficulty and deciding whether to take it. The second is the interesting omission. Two finders reached it independently, from prior art and from a SCAMPER reversal, which is one of the two independent two-finder convergences on the chart — CORRECTED at gate-candidates, which called it the strongest: find_analogy records the other, contradiction and analogy reaching opt-the-decision-is-fixed-within-a-run-and-revised-between-them from different directions, and nothing in the record ranks the two — and it is on no line because today's receiver cannot refuse, or accept, or act at all.

WHAT IS NOT A CANDIDATE AND SHOULD STILL BE SCORED. The null — change nothing, name no driver, keep spending whatever the person started — is not on the chart, because it is not a value on any single axis but the absence of the whole block. evaluate-set has to score against it anyway, and the number that would settle it is the one probe 3 could not get: whether a weaker hand actually does worse work.
