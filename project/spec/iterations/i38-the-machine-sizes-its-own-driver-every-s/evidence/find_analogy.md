THE ABSTRACTION THAT PAID: something is graded before it is dealt with, the grade decides the spend, and nothing afterwards says whether the grade was right. Old, high-stakes, and well-practised in several fields — every one of which treats the grade as a claim to be checked rather than as a fact.

## follow_up

- THE TRIAGE TRANSFER IS THE CHEAPEST THING IN THE WHOLE OPTION SET and the only one needing no error signal at all. Triage holds under-triage near zero and PUBLISHES the over-triage rate it accepts as the price. Naming our accepted over-driving rate in advance makes drift computable from the rating distribution the day the ratings are written, with nothing measured at runtime. Every other answer to the drift risk waits on attribution.

- THE FREIGHT TRANSFER SHOWS THE RECONCILIATION NON-GOAL WAS DRAWN TOO BROAD. Reconciliation was ruled out as a whole mechanism needing signals this iteration does not add. Carriers do not weigh every parcel — they spot-check a fraction and re-rate the mis-declared. A SAMPLE audit needs only the state and the model on the sampled calls, which is exactly what is already in scope, so the expensive version is out of scope and a cheap version may not be.

- THE AVIATION ROW CONFIRMS A BREAK REACHED INDEPENDENTLY and mints nothing. Frozen in use, revised between revisions, is what the contradiction finder derived from the matrix and what maintenance-interval practice has run for decades. Two finders reaching one mechanism from different directions is worth more than either alone, and the option node is shared rather than duplicated.

- THE FOURTH ROW CONFIRMS RATHER THAN CORRECTS and is listed anyway. Clinical notes, flight recorders and chain of custody all specify who records and when, at the moment of the act, and forbid reconstruction afterwards — which is why our call record is stamped where the call is served. A finder that reports only corrections implies everything unmentioned went unchecked.
