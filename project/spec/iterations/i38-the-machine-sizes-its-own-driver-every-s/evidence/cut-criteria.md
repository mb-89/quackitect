ONE MOVE IN A HUNDRED AND NINETEEN ROWS, and it is recorded as a move rather than smuggled in as a re-sort. The whole project has made two before this one.

A deeper cut would have produced a hundred judgements of which ninety-five read "identical under all four", which is the shape a comparison takes when it is measuring nothing.

THE MUSTS ARE NOT ABSENT FROM THE COMPARISON, they are prior to it. That half is right and the next clause was not.

It named three of them — `req-the-machine-names-a-driver-and-starts-nothing`, `req-a-machine-decision-repeats` and the live-read rule — which is the closest anything came to running the check.
