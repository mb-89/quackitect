## follow_up

FIVE ROWS SURVIVE THE CUT AND EACH ONE SEPARATES THE FOUR DIFFERENTLY.

- req-a-wrong-act-never-passes-silently divides prevention from detection. The totality check and the record-validity rule prevent by construction; the seed's sampled audit finds out afterwards.
- req-walk-survives-host-swap divides the lines that hold a model roster from the one that does not. It is raid-asm-one-model-list-serves-every-host-the-engine-supports expressed as a criterion.
- req-acts-carry-role-and-channel and req-the-actor-is-recorded-where-the-call-is-served are the account cluster's two rows, and they part the candidates by what the call record ends up holding.
- req-comparison-carries-both-sides is the one that had to be moved, and it is the row cand-the-receiver-decides fails structurally.

ONE MOVE IN A HUNDRED AND NINETEEN ROWS, and it is recorded as a move rather than smuggled in as a re-sort. The whole project has made two before this one.

WHAT THE SHALLOW CUT MEANS FOR evaluate-set. Five criteria over four candidates is twenty judgements, and every one of them has to be answered from a candidate's own text with a quote. A deeper cut would have produced a hundred judgements of which ninety-five read "identical under all four", which is the shape a comparison takes when it is measuring nothing.

AND THE MUSTS ARE NOT ABSENT FROM THE COMPARISON, they are prior to it. req-the-machine-names-a-driver-and-starts-nothing, req-a-machine-decision-repeats and the fatal live-read rule are checked as constraints at gate-design, and raid-iss-the-live-read-rule-forbids-more-than-its-own-reason-needs says the last of those three needs ruling on before two of the four candidates are buildable as written.
