## current_situation

A COORDINATE A CALLER CANNOT SEND IS ALWAYS THE DEFAULT, and a log of defaults answers nothing.

## built

THREE ARGUMENTS NOW RIDE EVERY LANE TOOL, for the same reason `update` does: the answer is about the CALL rather than about any one verb, and a coordinate a caller can only supply on some verbs is a coordinate the log cannot be grouped by.

- `as` — which part you are playing, from the closed vocabulary. Omit it and the record says `walker`.
- `relayed_by` — who is filing work somebody else did. Send `as` naming the AUTHOR and this naming yourself.
- `answered_by` — what actually served the call. Omit it and the record says `unreported`.

THE DESCRIPTIONS CARRY THE DESIGN AND NOT ONLY THE SHAPE. `as` says why the default is `walker` and not `guide`: a default of `guide` would let the strong hand's work hide in the weak hand's count. `relayed_by` says when to use it — whenever you carry a delegate's answer back — because the case that loses attribution is the one nobody thinks to declare.

THE OFFERED ENUM IS THE ENFORCED VOCABULARY, and a check holds the two together. An offer wider than the check refuses honest callers; one narrower hides parts nobody can name.

### Two cases were added and one of them is about reach

`every lane tool accepts the hand, the relay and the answering model` walks the served `tools/list` and asserts all three keys on every tool. It is the case that would have caught this chunk being skipped: the record would still validate, refuse and mark, and nothing could reach it.

`the hand vocabulary offered to a caller is the one the log enforces` compares the offered enum against the log's closed set. Two lists of the same words in two files drift the day one is edited.
