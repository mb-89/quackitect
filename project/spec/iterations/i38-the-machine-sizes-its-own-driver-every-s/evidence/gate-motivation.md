## vision_scope_stated

THE PACKET IS COMPLETE AND IT IS AN INHERIT, which is what the state's own guidance asks a major to do where the vision holds.

BIG IDEA. The machine should know how hard each of its own steps is, and say so, so the strength of the hand doing the work is a decision rather than an accident of who started the session.

TO-BE WORLD. A milestone opens by naming the driver it needs. An agent arrives at a state knowing whether the work is a lookup or a judgment. A finished record can be asked who did what. The owner maintains one list by hand and reads the same answer everywhere. The machine argues in one direction only — stronger is free, weaker costs a recorded sentence.

GOAL SYSTEM. Five goals with three conflicts named and ruled: determinism over fitness, correctness over cost inside a submachine, and recommendation over order. A priority order is given for when they cannot all be had, and it puts the out-of-lane spawn first because a mechanism that reaches outside is a worse thing to get wrong than a coarse rating.

PITCH. All five slots filled, and it names what is given up in the pitch itself rather than in a footnote.

SCOPE AND NON-GOALS. Six items in, eight out, each out-item with its reason and its home.

WHAT THE PACKET DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO: re-derive the axiom. There is no vision file in this repository and no vision folder in the trace — the product vision lives in the records and is inherited. This packet points at it, states the delta, and rewrites nothing.

## problem_agreed

THE DELTA IS REAL, AND IT IS MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSERTED.

Nothing in project/deliverable declares a complexity. No matrix row carries a rating, no engine file reads one, and no model list exists anywhere in the repository.

The call record ends at `actor` and `se_version` (calllog.ts:19-23). There is no model field to fill.

And the second coordinate is missing too: grouping the call log by state or by visit puts every record in one bucket, live, today, while grouping by actor separates cleanly. So a walk is unattributable on both axes.

THE GOAL IS WORTH HAVING, AND THE ARGUMENT IS ABOUT WHERE THE BUDGET GOES. What caps how much work gets sent is the token bill, not the hours — that is already a standing value proposition rather than something this iteration invented. A process that spends frontier capability transcribing decisions somebody already made is spending its cap on the cheapest half of its own work.

THE ARGUMENT'S HONEST WEAK POINT, put here rather than buried. The saving cannot be quantified from inside this system today, because nothing can attribute a call to a state. The first honest number arrives after this iteration, not with it. Anyone wanting a figure before committing will not get one, and the alternative — quoting a router's published savings in support of a design that has no router — would be borrowing another design's evidence.

WHAT WOULD MAKE THE PROBLEM NOT WORTH SOLVING: if the ratings turned out to cluster, so that forty-eight of fifty-three states sat on one rung. Then the machine would be saying something it already knew. That is a real possibility and it is not knowable until the ratings exist, which is why they are M3's work with evidence per state rather than a table filled in one sitting.

## prior_art_positioned

SCANNED AT THE KICKOFF GATE AGAINST PRIMARY SOURCES, and the field splits in two with us in neither half.

WHAT EXISTS AND WORKS: the runtime routers. Cursor's router computes a continuous complexity score per request from a predictor trained on live traffic and labelled by whether the user proceeded or corrected. Bedrock's prompt routing predicts per-request response quality against a threshold the operator tunes. OpenRouter's auto router classifies into task types and ranks models on a rolling spend index. All three fit the individual item; all three are corrected by outcomes; all three publish real cost reductions.

WHAT THEY SHED: reproducibility and disclosure. Cursor hides the routed model by default. Copilot's review path does not disclose it at all. Both accept the model varying between turns. For a system whose entire output is an auditable record, that is the wrong thing to give up.

WHAT ALSO EXISTS AND IS MORE LIKE US: the agent frameworks. Claude Code's subagent model is frontmatter defaulting to inherit. The OpenAI Agents SDK makes it a constructor argument and offers no routing. LiteLLM's router strategies are about infrastructure state rather than task difficulty. They are static as we are, and none of them derives the declaration from anything — a human types it and it stands.

WHERE WE SIT: our declaration attaches to a STATE the machine already understands, rather than to an agent somebody configured. That is what makes it derivable in principle and auditable in practice, and it is the only strip between the two halves worth standing on.

WHAT FAILED, AND IT IS THE PART THAT SHOULD WORRY THIS GATE MOST. Declared resource classes are decades old and the lesson runs against us. Kubernetes refuses to let anyone declare a QoS class and computes it from requests and limits instead. Google measured hand-managed jobs at roughly twice the slack of machine-corrected ones. A 2026 survey across more than twenty-three thousand production clusters found sixty-nine percent of requested CPU unused, cause given as declarations nobody updates. The structural reason is the same everywhere: under-declaration fails loudly and over-declaration fails silently, so the drift only ever runs one way. Our design has exactly that shape.

AND THE C2/C3 SEAM IS NOVEL, WHICH CUTS BOTH WAYS. Rating work a priori by whether a checker could catch a wrong answer is mirrored by no shipped system the scan could find. Production does the same judgment AFTER generation — cascading runs the cheap model, verifies the output, escalates on failure. The nearest published proposal routes on computed code-health metrics and its own author calls it an idea not yet proven. Novel means the burden of proof is ours.

WHAT THE SCAN COULD NOT ESTABLISH, said rather than left blank: whether one named router product still ships, whether a named vendor still powers a competitor's auto-router, and whether four named observability platforms carry a distinct requested-versus-served model field.
