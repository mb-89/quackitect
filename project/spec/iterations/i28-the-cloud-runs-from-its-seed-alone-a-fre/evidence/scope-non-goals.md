## scope

### The lifecycle, which is the owner's five points

GIT IS THE TRUTH AND THE DISK IS A WORKSPACE. A folder means somebody is working that iteration right now, on this machine, and nothing else.

- ENTERING MATERIALISES the folder at that moment, and at no other moment.
- THE CLOSE REMOVES the folder, committing or refusing first, never removing blind.

### The bootstrap, which is the seed's own list

- AN ENTRYPOINT THAT RUNS, beyond PowerShell. It verifies node, installs, starts the lane with the panel suppressed, waits for the health check rather than racing it, fetches the refspec, adopts the record, launches the caged walker, and exits non-zero with ONE sentence naming the step that failed.
- THE SERVER SURVIVES BACKGROUNDING, so stdin EOF stops meaning shutdown while it is serving.
- itAdopt BECOMES A LANE VERB, so a peer picks up a pushed record without a shell.
- THE FETCH REFSPEC IS THE LANE'S, so a fresh clone sees `refs/heads/it/*` without somebody knowing to ask.
- THE LANE GETS A LIFECYCLE: a starting instance refuses a held port and says whose it is, and a health answer reports pid, parent, listener, uptime and the tail of the engine log. It reports and never kills.
- A WORKTREE VERB IN THE LANE, so retiring one is not five shell commands under an exemption.
