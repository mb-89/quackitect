## why_these

WHAT THEY ARE A SAMPLE OF: the six decisions above, which have roughly forty combinations once the empty cells are struck. Four is a sample and it is chosen to span rather than to cluster.

- A IS THE DESIGN WE ARRIVED WITH, drawn so it competes rather than presiding. Every other candidate is measured against it.
- B IS THE COHERENT MAXIMUM. If the claim owns the folder's lifetime, then a lease is what makes a dead holder ordinary, and putting the claim on the record's own branch follows. Three options that each arrived from a different finder turn out to compose.
- C IS THE NULL OPTION, and the method says it is regularly the best on the chart. It removes the folder lifecycle rather than perfecting it, and this product already runs that way by i27's ruling.
- D MOVES THE BOUNDARY rather than the mechanism. If the environment stands the product up, four of the entrypoint's seven steps and the runtime assumption go with it.

WHAT THE SPACE HOLDS THAT THESE DO NOT:

- ANY COMBINATION WITH THE INCUMBENT DISK TEST. Struck deliberately: the disk answering what exists is the defect this iteration exists to remove, and keeping it would make every other row cosmetic.
- A LEASE WITHOUT THE CLAIM OWNING THE FOLDER. Legal and uninteresting - it fixes the crashed-walk hole and leaves the folder lifecycle exactly as fragile.
- THE RECORD-ONLY FOLDER COMBINED WITH C. Incoherent: there is no folder to thin.
- IMAGE-ONLY, WITH NO SCRIPT. Struck because uc-install-quackitect's actor is an engineer on a laptop, and telling them to run a container to get an editor extension is worse than what they have.
