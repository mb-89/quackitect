### The row that did not dissolve, and why that is the useful one

REMOVING THE FOLDER AT CLOSE improves Loss of substance and degrades Loss of information. No separation touches it.

- IN TIME fails: the work is uncommitted at exactly the moment the folder must go.
- IN SPACE fails: it is one folder in one place.
- IN RELATION fails: nobody observes it differently; the work is gone for everybody.
- IN LEVEL fails: the part and the whole lose the same thing.

SO THE GRID IS THE HONEST NEXT STEP, and the principle it points at is 11, Cushion in advance — add the fallback before the failure. Committing before removing is that principle, and it is what the design already does.

THE VALUE IS NOT THE ANSWER, WHICH WE HAD. It is that the method reached the same answer from outside our own reasoning, and named the four separations that fail so nobody proposes them again.
