## built

[[cand-no-folders-at-all]], at project/spec/trace/candidate/cand-no-folders-at-all.md.

THE NULL OPTION, REACHED BY TWO INDEPENDENT FINDERS. Trimming asked what if the cluster does not exist; SCAMPER's Eliminate arrived separately. That is the closest this method gets to a second opinion.

THE METHOD RATES THE NULL OPTION HIGHEST IN VALUE. It also rates it lowest in proposal rate, of any option there is.

HOW IT WORKS: the machine has one tree. Entering claims and checks trunk out at that point. Leaving is checking out something else, after committing. Parallelism moves from one machine holding several trees to several machines holding one each, which is what this iteration's goal argues is now cheap.

THE SEAMS ARE FEW AND THAT IS THE ARGUMENT. Entry is a checkout rather than a worktree add, so there is no new mechanism at all. The claim ledger already prevents collision across machines, and this candidate stops trying to prevent it within one.

WHAT IT COSTS is written as plainly. Three costs.

- One record at a time, per machine.
- Work in progress must be committed before switching.
- A checkout costs what the tree costs to write.

ITS STRONGEST EVIDENCE IS THAT THIS PRODUCT ALREADY RUNS THIS WAY, by i27's ruling of 2026-08-14. The candidate generalises a standing exception rather than inventing a mechanism, so it REMOVES a special case rather than adding one.

### Why this candidate is uncomfortable

IT MAKES THE ITERATION'S WHOLE SUBJECT UNNECESSARY. Materialise-on-entry, close-time removal, the sweep, the stale-folder class and the second definition of open all stop existing rather than being fixed.

AN ITERATION THAT ARGUES ITSELF DOWN TO A SMALLER PIECE OF WORK is doing the method's job. The uncomfortable part is that this line was reached by two finders and would have been reached by neither if the fan had not been run whole.

### What it does not answer

THE CRASHED WALK, same as candidate A. There is no folder to go stale, but the CLAIM is still held by a machine that no longer exists.

ONLY THE LEASE ANSWERS THAT, and the lease is independent of whether folders exist.

SO THE CLAIM'S EXPIRY AND THE FOLDER'S LIFETIME ARE SEPARATE DECISIONS. A shortlist could take this candidate AND the lease together.
