- bounded: every row names one condition and one response, and the two that carry several facets say so in a Detail table that binds rather than in a longer sentence
- comprehensible: each row's breaks_if_removed says what concretely fails without it, in one line, and none of them says a version of "quality would suffer"
- no_tbd: zero markers in the seven new rows — no TBD, TBC, TBR or question marks; the standing 197 are not re-swept here and the gate's own count covers them
- behaviour_modelled: TWO ROWS CARRY A MODEL AND FIVE DO NOT, and the look was taken on all seven — the worktree row carries a state model because its first line, `(nothing) -> seeded`, is what forces the question of what creates a folder, and that is exactly the hole seven prose rows about claiming failed to show; the console row carries one because the two port cases are a missing transition rather than a condition and a response; the other five state one condition and one response, so a model would restate them in a second notation and give two things to drift apart

### The fan-out was checked and the fold was made

FIFTEEN STEPS AND EXTENSIONS BECAME SEVEN ROWS. The method's heuristic is that a use case refined by more than five rows is a clustering candidate, so the look was owed and taken.

TWO FOLDS WERE MADE, each a facet family sharing one kind, one verify_method and one concern.

- THE FOLDER LIFECYCLE. Seeding, entering and closing were three candidate rows. They are one concern — when a folder exists — and they fail together, so they are one row with a three-line table and a state model.
- THE LANE'S PROCESS LIFECYCLE. Surviving backgrounding and refusing a held port were two candidate rows. Both are the lane staying alive with nobody watching, both verify by test, and the port case only makes sense beside the backgrounding case.

WHAT WAS NOT FOLDED, and why. The git-list row and the offline row look like one concern and are not: one is about which source answers a question, the other about what happens when that source is unreachable. They fail independently and they would be tested differently.

SEVEN IS STILL ABOVE FIVE. The threshold is a smell rather than a rule, the look was taken, and the remaining seven each read as one concern. That look is recorded here rather than skipped.
