Four lines drawn across the morphological chart at build_chart, chosen to span
the six decisions rather than to cluster near the design we arrived with.

Each compose state fills three sections of its own candidate note: how it

## Why these four

THE SCOPED FIX competes rather than presides. It is the design this iteration
arrived with, drawn as a line like any other so the others are measured
against something rather than assumed better than it.

THE LIFECYCLE IS THE CLAIM is the composition no finder proposed. Three
options arrived separately — the folder hanging off a live claim, the lease,
and the claim on the record's own branch — and they compose into one coherent
position.

NO FOLDERS AT ALL is the null option, which the method says is regularly the
best on the chart and has the lowest proposal rate of any option there is.
This product already runs that way by i27's ruling.

THE HOST IS DECLARED moves the boundary rather than the mechanism, and it is
the only line that shrinks the entrypoint instead of improving it.
