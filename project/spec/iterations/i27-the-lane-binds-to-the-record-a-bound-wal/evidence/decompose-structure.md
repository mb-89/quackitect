# Evidence form / decompose-structure

## current_situation

THE WINNER BECOMES STRUCTURE: five new elements and twenty-two new interfaces, joining ten elements that already stand.

THE FIVE, and each exists because a pick demanded it rather than because a diagram wanted a box.

- el-core knows the whole state and owns what cannot be split - trunk, the
  mirror, the claim ledger, the note inbox, the call log, and the routing.
- el-satellite serves one agent's work on one record and knows nothing of any
  other.
- el-resolution-seam is the one place a path is resolved, refused and named,
  which no verb may go around.
- el-satellite-supervisor starts, watches, replaces and reaps satellites, and
  never lets a half-ready one serve.
- el-engine-delta resolves engine and method files record-first then
  trunk-second, so a record holds only what it changed.

ONE INTERFACE IS THE PRICE OF THE SHAPE and the other twenty-one are its consequences. if-core-satellite crosses a process boundary, which every other line on the chart avoided by having only one process. Every core-owned read becomes a hop across it, and nobody has priced the hop.

THE OTHER TWENTY-ONE CARRY THE CROSSINGS THE FIVE NEW ELEMENTS OPENED. Fifty-one owed crossings stood when this state opened. Narrowing two over-broad allocations took it to twenty-one, and minting the interfaces took it to zero.

THE TEN STANDING ELEMENTS ARE BROWNFIELD AND JOIN AS THEY ARE. el-mirror, el-claim-ledger and el-record-store move under the core's ownership; el-walk-engine is what a satellite runs. None is rewritten.

WHY el-engine-delta IS AN ELEMENT AND NOT A NOTE ON ANOTHER ONE. It is the resolution that makes the thin tree and a changeable machine both true at once, and it is the piece the winner borrowed from its runner-up. Naming it separately is what lets a reader see that borrowing.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY ABSENT. No element for holding a record's work in memory. That refinement is adoptable by every line and deferred pending the profile, so building structure for it now would be structure for an undecided choice.

## anything_else

WHAT THIS DECOMPOSITION DID NOT DO, said plainly because the method warns
about it.

IT DID NOT DECOMPOSE FIVE STRUCTURES AND COMPARE THEM. The machine compares
at pick grain and decomposes once, after the winner. The recorded price is
that the scores judged mechanisms rather than structures, and that price is
real here: cand-live-engine and cand-core-satellite share the delta, and
nobody knows whether their structures would have scored the same way.

IT DID NOT INVENT THE OPEN DECISIONS. What a satellite actually is - a
process, a worker thread or an isolate - is undecided, and every element
above is written to survive all three. Naming one here would have been
deciding by drawing.

THE ONE PLACE THE STRUCTURE ALREADY ARGUES WITH ITSELF is worth flagging for
the evaluation. el-satellite-supervisor owns four all-or-nothing acts, and one
of them - what happens to an in-flight call when a satellite dies - is the
question no single-process line ever had to answer. It is written as an open
question rather than as a solved one.

HOW THIS FORM CAME TO BE WRITTEN. The walk stood at record-adrs and would not
route to this parallel branch, so the evidence was written directly. That is
the same wedge recorded as note-bb725251735e, hit on a different join.

## allocation

THE MATRIX IS COMPUTED AND IS NOT REPEATED HERE. This section carries only the argued spread, which is what template-element-matrix asks for. An earlier fill wrote the counts down and they went stale under a signature within the hour.

WHY FOUR FUNCTIONS SIT IN MORE THAN ONE ELEMENT. Every one is an owner-and-doer split rather than duplication, and each is a place a change to the function lands twice.

- fn-run-a-governed-walk: el-core routes a governed walk and el-walk-engine runs it. That split IS the architecture.
- hold-the-method: el-engine-delta holds a record's override, el-method-compiler holds and compiles trunk's.
- hold-the-work: el-record-store holds it on disk, el-satellite-supervisor holds it in a live process.
- serve-a-step: el-satellite is where a step is served, el-walk-engine computes it.

MANY-TO-MANY IS LEGAL and the matrix is where the spread shows. This row's own guidance says so.

WHY THE SPREAD SHRANK WHILE THIS STATE WAS OPEN. el-core and el-satellite first re-declared functions their contained elements already realize. Narrowing them to what each uniquely realizes is what took the owed crossings down, and it is why an earlier reading of this section named different numbers.

ONE PAIR EXCHANGES IN BOTH DIRECTIONS: el-record-store and el-test-runner. It predates this iteration. Landing asks for a fresh green, and the runner reads the tree it is asked about.

ONE CROSSING IS THE ARCHITECTURE'S OWN. el-core and el-satellite are different processes, so every flow between them crosses a boundary. [[if-core-satellite]] names both ends and carries all ten: the instruction, the stamped claim, the compiled machine, the open record, the position, the worktree, the resolved target, the dispatched call, the evidence form and the call log.

THAT ONE INTERFACE IS THE PRICE OF THE SHAPE, stated plainly. Flows that were function calls in every single-process line are now channel hops, and nobody has priced a hop.

if-core-satellite IS FLAGGED AS UNDEMANDED and it stays. The matrix derives a crossing from flows between differently-allocated functions, and a process boundary is a deployment fact no flow expresses. Both elements name it as their boundary, and it is where the design's whole price is written down.
