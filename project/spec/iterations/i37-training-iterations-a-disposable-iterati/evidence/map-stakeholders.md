## coverage

THE SET IS UNCHANGED BY THIS DELTA, and the argument is below rather than asserted.

EVERY VALUE PROP'S AUDIENCE RESOLVES. This iteration authors on exactly one proposition, vp-rigor-without-toil, whose audience is stk-engineer-driving-agents. The new criterion sits under that same audience and introduces no second one.

WHAT CHANGES FOR EACH RESIDENT ROLE.

- stk-engineer-driving-agents. Gains a reading they could not take before: whether a change to the machine helped. Nothing about their working day moves, which draw-context already claimed.
- stk-agent. Gains a new kind of walk. An agent may now be handed a rewound tree and told the work is discarded. That is a real change in what the role is asked to do, and it is why the honesty assumption on the register names the agent's behaviour rather than the engineer's.
- stk-vehicle-owner. Unaffected. A benchmark run never touches a driven project, and the vehicle guard already refuses a write to the tree a vehicle came from.
- stk-newcomer. Unaffected by the mechanism, and worth watching for one reason. A newcomer reading the archive will now find benchmark reports beside iteration records, and a report that does not say plainly what it is could be mistaken for one.

THE ALWAYS-ON CLASSES, each present or ruled out.

- THE OPERATOR of the running system is stk-engineer-driving-agents in this product. There is no separate operations role, because the same person who drives the agents runs the benchmarks.
- THE MAINTAINER of the machine is the same role again. This product is self-hosting, so the person improving the machine and the person using it are one.
- A REGULATOR or AUDITOR class is ruled out. Nothing here is customer-facing, and the AI-involvement obligations the project already carries attach to published content rather than to a discarded walk.
- AN ACQUIRER is ruled out, and frame-delta already recorded why: there is none, and the business case is a cost statement instead.

THE ONE ROLE I LOOKED FOR AND DID NOT MINT. Something like "the benchmark reader" or "the machine maintainer" as a role of its own. It fell for the same reason the past tree fell as a neighbour at draw-context: it is an existing role wearing a new hat. Minting it would split stk-engineer-driving-agents in two and leave every existing criterion ambiguous about which half it serves.

## anything_else

ONE THING ABOUT stk-agent IS SHARPER HERE THAN ANYWHERE ELSE IN THE ITERATION.

Every other role reads a benchmark result. The agent role IS the instrument. It is the only stakeholder whose behaviour is the thing being measured, and the only one that cannot be asked whether the measurement is fair.

THAT IS WHY THE HONESTY ASSUMPTION IS OWNED BY THE OWNER RATHER THAN BY THE MAINTAINER. It is a question about what the role does under a condition nobody can verify from inside it.
