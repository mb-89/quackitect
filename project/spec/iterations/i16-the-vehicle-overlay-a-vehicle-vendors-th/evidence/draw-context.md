## boundary

THE SYSTEM IS THE WHOLE PRODUCT, AND IT PLAYS TWO ROLES WITHOUT CHANGING SHAPE. That is the fact this boundary exists to state, and it is what the previous version got wrong.

### The box is the same box in both roles

THIS REPOSITORY IS A PARENT. It has no ancestor and nothing to pull from.

A COPY OF IT IS A DESCENDANT. It has an ancestor and may pull from it.

EVERYTHING ELSE IS IDENTICAL. Same engine, same lane, same method, same forms, same machines. A descendant is not a smaller box, not a wrapped box, and not a box with something else around it. THERE IS NO CONTAINER, and a boundary that draws one invents a relationship nobody asked for.

### What is inside

EVERYTHING THE PRODUCT NEEDS TO RUN, in one tree.

- The engine and the lane — the tools, the refusals, the call log.
- The method — guidance cards, machines, rigor rows, form templates.
- The corpus — spec, records, evidence, the trace nodes and the register.
- The identity — the brand fact at the root, which is the one file a rename writes.
- The surface — the extension, its actions and its panel. INSIDE, and that is worth saying because the affordance arriving in scope makes it easy to read as a separate thing sitting outside and calling in.
- The overriding content a descendant writes over what it carries. Its LOCATION is unchosen and deliberately not named here.

### What is outside

EVERYTHING ELSE, and the neighbour nodes name the ones that matter. Three properties of the outside are boundary facts rather than neighbour facts.

THE OUTSIDE IS NOT REACHABLE BY WRITING. No write, link, mount or install step a descendant makes may resolve outside its own tree. That is the isolation law, and the path jail already enforces it at one resolver.

TWO EXCEPTIONS ARE NOT EXCEPTIONS, and the second is new.

- Work the engine does on a DRIVEN PRODUCT lands in that project's tree. Legal because the driven project is the SUBJECT of the work and somebody pointed at it on purpose.
- A SPAWN WRITES A WHOLE TREE THAT DID NOT EXIST, at a path somebody named. Legal for the same reason and for one more: the produced tree is not a neighbour the system then keeps talking to. It leaves.

AND THE OUTSIDE CANNOT SEE IN. Nothing outside can reach into a running descendant. Today that is true because no path exists rather than because anything forbids it, which is a gap write-requirements carries.

### The one line that replaces the old boundary

A DESCENDANT CONTAINS EVERYTHING IT NEEDS AND TOUCHES NOTHING IT DID NOT AIM AT.

## intended_use

WHAT THE SYSTEM IS FOR, at the boundary, in the five uses this iteration touches. Each says who does it and what crosses.

### One — driving its own development

SOMEBODY WORKS ON THE PRODUCT ITSELF, through the lane, with the method in the same tree. Every use the system has ever had, unchanged.

### Two — spawning a descendant

SOMEBODY MAKES A COMPLETE NAMED COPY that runs on a machine with nothing of the parent's beside it. The name is one file.

WHAT CROSSES OUT: the whole product, once, renamed.

WHAT DOES NOT CROSS: any structure through which a later operation could reach back. No symlink, no junction, no hardlink, no mount, no install step that writes to the source.

AND THE PARENT LEARNS NOTHING. No registry, no callback, no list.

### Three — taking an update from where it came from

A DESCENDANT'S OWNER RUNS AN UPDATE and gets what the parent changed since, without losing changes they did not choose to lose.

WHO DECIDES A COLLISION: a person, at the descendant. Never the parent, and never silently.

AND ONE THING NO MECHANISM OFF THE SHELF CAN DO belongs here: when an update moves an identity the descendant's overriding content claims, the system SAYS SO rather than quietly serving its own card.

### Four — driving a project that is not itself

SOMEBODY POINTS THE SYSTEM AT SOMEBODY ELSE'S PRODUCT and works on it, with the method coming from the system's own tree and the descendant's overriding content on top.

WHAT CROSSES IN: the driven project's state — its spec, records, evidence and source.

WHAT CROSSES OUT: everything written on the work's behalf, into that project's tree.

WHAT THIS SPLITS: where the method comes from stops being the same place as where the work lives.

### Five — reaching both of those from the surface. NEW AT THIS REOPEN.

SOMEBODY PRESSES SOMETHING RATHER THAN RUNNING SOMETHING. The extension offers two acts, each with the same shape: ask what is needed, produce a tree, open a window in it.

- CREATE A VEHICLE. It asks what the export asks, produces the descendant, and a new window opens in it.
- START A PROJECT. Same shape, and the window opens in the project's folder.

AND THE THIRD PART IS NOT A BUTTON AT ALL, which is why this use is not decoration. The system must COME UP in the tree that window opened — including a driven project, which is not its own tree and carries none of its method. That is the crossing this use adds, and it is the one the path jail refuses today.

WHAT CROSSES OUT: a produced tree, and a request to the editor to open a window on it.

WHAT CROSSES IN: nothing new. The produced tree is not a neighbour the system keeps talking to.

### And one use that is not new but is now load-bearing

WORKING ON ITSELF WHILE DRIVING SOMETHING ELSE. A descendant hits a fault in its own machinery mid-job, records it, and repairs it in its own next iteration. No new mechanism — it is a note — but the boundary has to allow two subjects in one session, and this states that it does.

## excluded_use

BINDING AND SYSTEM-LEVEL, sharper than M1's vision-level non-goals. Each line says what is excluded and what it would break.

### Excluded because it breaks the isolation law

USING A DESCENDANT AS A ROUTE TO ITS PARENT. No operation started in a descendant may resolve, write, link, mount or delete outside its own tree in the parent's direction. BREAKS: raid-dec-an-import-is-read-only-and-a-vendored-copy-is-yours, graded fatal, with a repository already destroyed by exactly this.

SPAWNING BY REFERENCE RATHER THAN BY COPY. A descendant may not be created as a symlink, junction, hardlink, mount, or any install step that writes to the source. BREAKS: the same law, at the moment the copy is made. AND THE CHECK IS PER PLATFORM: a junction fires on Windows where a symlink cannot even be created.

ANY REGISTRY, CALLBACK OR TELEMETRY THAT TELLS THE PARENT A DESCENDANT EXISTS. BREAKS: the privacy goal, and the promise that what a descendant's owner writes never has to leave their building.

### Excluded because it breaks the overlay

EDITING THE PARENT'S CONTENT AS THE WAY TO OVERRIDE IT. A descendant MAY edit anything it carries. What is excluded is making the edit the MECHANISM of overriding, because then every override is a merge conflict waiting for the next update.

CARRYING WORK IN THE OVERRIDING LAYER. It holds method. Spec, records and evidence are work and live where work lives.

SERVING A DEFAULT SILENTLY WHERE AN OVERRIDE NO LONGER RESOLVES. BREAKS: raid-dec-serve-the-overlay-and-report-the-drift. The quiet fallback is the one outcome that ruling names as unacceptable.

### Excluded because it is somebody else's job

MERGE LOGIC OF OUR OWN. Existing mechanisms do it, and the probe on 2026-08-18 measured one doing it. BREAKS: the schedule, and it is the register's headline risk.

AUTOMATIC PROPAGATION UPWARD. Nothing a descendant changes travels to the parent on its own. What may travel is a PROPOSAL somebody reads and decides on.

### Excluded because it is a different shape

INSTALLING THE SYSTEM INSIDE SOMEBODY ELSE'S PRODUCT. A driven project never contains the engine. It is pointed at, not wrapped around.

REQUIRING BOTH A PARENT AND A DESCENDANT ON ONE MACHINE. BREAKS: goal 1, and the owner's own constraint that colleagues will not have both installed.

SUBSTITUTING BRAND NAMES AT RENDER TIME. BREAKS: the trust chain — v1 rejected this because rewriting text the ledger hashes hides content from it.

### Excluded from the affordance specifically. NEW AT THIS REOPEN.

THE REST OF THE BEGIN-A-PRODUCT EXPERIENCE. Two acts and the ability to run in a driven project is the whole of item three. req-scaffold-from-template, req-begin-touches-nothing-existing and req-fresh-product-starts-empty stay out. BREAKS NOTHING if left out; adding them would break the scope, which is why the line is here rather than in a wish list.

AN ACT THAT WRITES OUTSIDE THE TREE IT PRODUCED. A spawn or a start may create a tree at a named path and may write inside it. It may not touch anything else on the machine, and it may not modify the tree it was launched from beyond what a normal run would. BREAKS: the isolation law, at the one moment the system legitimately writes somewhere new.

OPENING A WINDOW THE PERSON DID NOT ASK FOR. The act produces a tree and asks the editor to open it. It does not close, replace or reconfigure the window it was launched from. BREAKS: nothing structural — it breaks trust, which is the thing an act that writes a whole folder most needs.

## anything_else

WHY THE SURFACE IS INSIDE THE BOX, since the affordance arriving in scope makes it easy to draw the other way.

THE EXTENSION IS PART OF THE PRODUCT. It ships with it, it is copied with it, and a descendant carries its own. Drawing it outside would say a descendant depends on something it does not contain, which is the sealed model returning through a different door.

WHAT IS OUTSIDE IS THE EDITOR, and that neighbour has stood since i1 as nbr-vscode. The system asks it to open a window; it is not the system's own surface.

SO THE NEW CROSSING IS SMALL AND THE NEW CAPABILITY IS NOT. What crosses is a request to open a folder. What is hard is that the system must then COME UP in that folder, which for a driven project means coming up somewhere carrying none of its method — and that is the crossing the path jail refuses today.

### What the affordance did NOT change

THE BOUNDARY ITSELF. A descendant still contains everything and there is still no container. Scope grew twice today and this drawing moved once, when the sealed model fell this morning. That is the difference between a change to what the system DOES and a change to what it IS.
