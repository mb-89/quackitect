## why_these

WHAT THE FOUR ARE A SAMPLE OF: the axis that runs from cheapest to strictest, held against one question — who pays for the copy's freedom to change things.

- WHAT SHIPS TODAY pays nothing and gets nothing. The copy is free and terminal.
- NOTHING BUT A CHANNEL pays one cell and gets a merge base. Everything else is the predecessor's, which ran for a year.
- THE PROGRAM ROUTE pays upstream, permanently, by hand-writing a migration per breaking change, and buys a copy that owns everything and still receives work.
- EVERYTHING DECLARED pays the COPY'S OWNER, at the moment of every edit, and buys a record that cannot rot.
