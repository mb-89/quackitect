### What running the catalogues whole actually bought

- Subtraction on the incumbent reached a method-only bundle, which the owner's self-sufficiency ruling forbids. Recording it means the next person meets the ruling rather than the idea.

### The two that bit, and why they are opposite in shape

REVERSE PRODUCED THE ONLY UPWARD MECHANISM ON THE CHART. Everything else moves work from the source to the copy. This moves it the other way, and the transform is what made the asymmetry visible: downward needs a merge and therefore lineage, while upward needs a reader and therefore attention. One is expensive to build and the other expensive to sustain.

MULTIPLICATION PRODUCED A SPLIT NOBODY HAD PROPOSED. One overlay becomes two, differing in exactly one property. It matters because the owner has said colleagues will hold copies on machines that never had the source, which is the only condition under which a shared-versus-personal distinction pays.

### One honest limit on this sweep

THE SUBJECT LIST OFFERED EVERY OPTION IN THE CORPUS, roughly a hundred and forty of them. Twelve operators against all of them is not what the card means by running whole, and doing it would have produced a table nobody reads.

SO THE SUBJECT WAS CHOSEN AND THE OPERATORS WERE NOT. Both catalogues ran entire against the incumbent, which is what SCAMPER and SIT are for — they transform something that exists. Three extra rows apply an operator to a specific option where that option was the sharper target.

THAT IS A JUDGMENT AND IT IS RECORDED AS ONE. A later pass wanting more could run the operators against the twelve options in this iteration's cone rather than against the incumbent alone.
