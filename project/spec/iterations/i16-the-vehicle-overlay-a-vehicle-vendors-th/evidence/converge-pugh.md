### The three reservations, none of which overturned the arithmetic

ONE. THE WINNER'S CENTRAL CLAIM IS ESTABLISHED FOR THE EASY HALF OF ITS SPACE. Its probe faked exactly the hard case: a migration expressible as one text substitution. The interesting migrations are the ones that must understand structure rather than match text, and nothing has tested whether they can be written at all. The trigger is cheap and already named: write one migration that must reorder a document's sections, and see.

TWO. ITS TAX IS PERMANENT AND FALLS UPSTREAM. Somebody hand-writes a migration for every breaking change, forever. That is why the mechanism is rare rather than obvious, and it is the reason to doubt this line rather than any doubt about whether it works. A source that stops writing migrations does not degrade gracefully; it silently stops delivering and no copy learns.

THREE. ITS FAILURE MODE IS A MIGRATION THAT RUNS, SUCCEEDS AND PRODUCES SOMETHING WRONG. Nothing refuses. The diff is left unstaged for a person, and a person who does not read it has no signal at all. That sits directly against the criterion this iteration ranked first.

REASONS. This is the one thing the field cannot do both of, and the runner-up is the only candidate that has it. A derived inventory says a difference EXISTS; it cannot say the copy's owner meant it, or why. The winner's inventory is recomputed from the merge each time, which is why it can never be false and also why it carries no meaning.

AND req-overlay-drift-reported HAS TWO CLAUSES. Both candidates score 4 on it, and they satisfy different halves.
