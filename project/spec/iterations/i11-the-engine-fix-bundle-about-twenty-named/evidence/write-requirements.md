## set_criteria

- complete: Every scoped item traces to a row, and every new row traces to a scoped item. The dozen fixes divide cleanly: four mint a new demand, the rest violate rows that already stand. The payload trim answers req-the-answer-never-exceeds-its-bound and req-call-answers-in-one-second; the amend and the argument names answer req-call-answers-in-one-second; the router and reentry defects answer req-walk-branches-at-waypoint and req-a-reopen-stands-where-it-can-work. Naming a new row for any of those would have been minting a duplicate.
- consistent: One pair was checked deliberately, because it is the pair most likely to conflict. req-a-harmless-finding-is-carried-not-stopped-on permits carrying a defect; contract rule 4 says a hole in the work under your hands IS the work. They do not conflict because the permission is bounded by "blocks nothing downstream" and guarded by req-a-harmless-finding-names-an-open-entry. Where the guard is absent, rule 4 wins, and the requirement says so.
- affordable: All four are verifiable with what exists. Four by test, none needing a new instrument. That is why none asks for one.
- bounded: Each row states ONE demand. The battery row came closest to stating two — refuse the full run, and answer a scoped one — and they are kept together because they are the same demand about who decides when tests run. Splitting them is the failure the row exists to prevent, recorded at raid-dec-blocking-and-the-battery-refusal-ship-together.
- comprehensible: Each statement names the actor and the response without naming a mechanism. The bucket rows say what must happen to a finding, never that a checklist carries an `[owed]` token — that lives in the Detail, where a design may change it.
- no_tbd: No row carries TBD, TBC, TBR or a placeholder. Every verify_method is named, every damage grade chosen, every refines resolved.
- behaviour_modelled: NO MODEL DRAWN, and that is a judgment rather than an omission. The test is whether a model says something the statement and Detail cannot. Three of the four are one condition and one response. The fourth, the bucket pair, is the only candidate — it has a lifecycle: found, owed, carried, cleared or refused at the close. That lifecycle is four lines in the Detail and a diagram would restate it in a second notation that can then disagree with the first.
