The standard for software design descriptions. The attributes the
design-spec template borrows: a component's definition and purpose, its
responsibilities, its interface, behavior with constraints (pre and
postconditions, invariants, timing), and the design rationale against
alternatives. Read via the mirrored PDF; searched 2026-08-11.
