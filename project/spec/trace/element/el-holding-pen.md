The pen holds what is not yet work: notes captured where they land
([[raid-dec-stray-log]]), drained only at the retro; ideation that records
the problem before the options and keeps the losers.

AND IT IS WHERE A STRAY STOPS BEING MACHINE-LOCAL. The mint is the pen's last
act on a note: the author's statement crosses into a durable option and the raw
note stays behind, marked drained. The pen owns the ACT and its demands - that
the statement is authored rather than pasted, that it says what the option is
and when it comes back, that there is no lighter path for an agent.

THE OPTION LANDS WHERE THE PEN PUTS IT, and that is the pen's realization
rather than a second element's job. Allocating the mint to the record store as
well was tried and backed out: it made the function span two groups, and the
element matrix then owed four interfaces that do not exist. Owing a new
interface is the architecture moving, and nothing here moves it - the pen
already writes durable things and already reaches the desk.

Boundary: the interfaces the element matrix mints for its flows.

Realization: the notes store, the options pool it mints into, and the ideation state.
