IT IS KEYED TO A COLUMN, not to a row. A probe at i38's probe-assumptions found `draft-vision` spanning three rungs across its change sizes and not monotonically — accepting a standing artifact at major, framing one from nothing at product. A flow carrying one value per row would have been carrying the wrong number for three columns in four.

IT IS DECLARED RATHER THAN OBSERVED, which is the whole trade this iteration makes. A difficulty computed at runtime would fit the work better and could not be replayed.

IT NEVER ENTERS A RECORD'S DEMANDS. It is a hint about who should drive, not a claim anything rests on, so nothing needs it frozen.
