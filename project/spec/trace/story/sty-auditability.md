## Deck

I am answerable for output I did not write and did not watch happen.
|||

---

An iteration moved overnight: agents walked, a gate was blessed, and the call log and the trace sit in the repository.
|||

---

I ask the log for the night's calls; every dispatch answers with one line, and each refusal carries its clause.
|||

---

I ask which grants were recorded; each names the role that granted and the channel it arrived on, with no username or hostname anywhere.
|||

---

I follow the blessed gate back through the trace to the evidence and the requirement rows it proved.
|||

---

I state what happened, who ruled and on what evidence, from the record alone.
|||
