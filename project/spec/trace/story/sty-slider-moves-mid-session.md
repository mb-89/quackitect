## Deck

Trust is not a constant. The same engineer wants to watch the step that touches the schema and wants to leave the room for the one that renames a field.
|||

---

The slider sets how much the agent may do alone. It sits beside the drawing, and it moves at any moment.
|||

---

Raised, the agent walks every hop whose weight fits under it, and stops only where the weight goes over.
|||

---

Lowered mid-walk, the next heavy step stops instead of running. The agent names which step waits and says that a message resumes it.
|||

---

The setting changed the walk without restarting it. Nothing the slider does moves the machine by itself — it only says how far the next pull may go.
|||
