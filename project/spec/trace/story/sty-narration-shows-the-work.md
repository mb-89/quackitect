## Deck

An agent working for twenty minutes is a black box. The engineer either interrupts and breaks the flow, or waits and hopes.
|||

---

Before the first edit, the agent posts a checklist. The engineer sees the SHAPE of the work before any of it has landed.
|||

---

Items tick off as they land. Any call can carry one line saying what is happening right now, and the graph shows it.
|||

---

Go quiet too long and the engine refuses the next call until an update is paid. How long is too long sits on the person's own bar, not in the agent's taste.
|||

---

The engineer read the progress without asking for it, and a silence long enough to worry about could not happen.
|||
