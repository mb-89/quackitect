## Deck

The agent reaches for the tool it always reaches for. In this project that tool is caged. A bare "permission denied" would cost a turn of guessing and teach nothing.
|||

---

The refusal comes back typed. It names the clause, what was expected, and what actually arrived.
|||

---

It carries a remedy, and the remedy is not advice. It is the call to make instead, with its arguments already filled.
|||

---

The agent runs the remedy and is back on the walk in one turn. The refusal is logged, so a clause that keeps firing surfaces in the retro's ranking as a design defect.
|||

---

The cage taught instead of blocking. Being stopped costs one call, and the stop that keeps happening stops being common.
|||
