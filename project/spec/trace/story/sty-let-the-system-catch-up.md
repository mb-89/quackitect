## Deck

A run of fast days. Guidance edited in three places and consistent in none, a lint that fires on things nobody meant, prose written before a rule that now contradicts it.
|||

---

None of it is a bug and none of it belongs to a record. There is no feature to hang the fix on, which is exactly why it never gets done.
|||

---

The engineer takes the overhaul door. It is not a project and produces no deliverable.
|||

---

The sweep runs over what drifted: guidance against the rules it should follow, prose against the voice it should keep, artifacts against the templates that minted them.
|||

---

Each finding is fixed where it lives, not worked around. The ones that need a decision become notes rather than guesses.
|||

---

The battery runs at the end, because a sweep that touched everything has to prove it broke nothing.
|||

---

The method matches itself again. Nothing shipped, and the next fast day starts from something consistent instead of something that merely works.
|||
