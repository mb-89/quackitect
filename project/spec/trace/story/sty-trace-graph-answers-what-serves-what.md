## Deck

"Why does this exist?" gets asked about every line of every spec. The answer usually lives in one person's head, and it leaves when they do.
|||

---

The graph draws the corpus as rings. The vision sits at the centre, the value propositions around it, the stories that realize them further out.
|||

---

Filtering to one proposition redraws the whole layout around it. What is left on screen is exactly what serves that promise.
|||

---

Clicking a node shows its statement and its type, and its path opens the file itself in the editor.
|||

---

The question was answered by looking. A proposition nothing serves shows up as an empty arc, before anyone has to notice it in prose.
|||
