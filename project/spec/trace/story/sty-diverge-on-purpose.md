## Deck

The engineer has a problem and one solution already in mind. It is the first thing they thought of, which is the weakest reason to build it.
|||
The house rule against the default: meth-morphological-analysis - combinations span the space before anything is picked.

---

They take the ideation door rather than the desk. Nothing here is a record and nothing here commits.
|||
FINDING: the ideation door is not shipped as its own surface. The scale's notch 1 exists (machines/scale.md) and its idle behavior is declared later work. The diverge machinery below is real.

---

The machine asks for options, not an answer. One is not enough and the obvious one does not count as two.
|||
Walked in i1: enumerate-space ran its parallel finders and built the chart - the evidence stands signed in the record.

---

They write three. Two are worse than the first and that is the point — a comparison needs something to compare against.
|||
The i1 candidate set: run-candidates elaborated the derived-house candidate against the chart's alternatives, each an option node with found_by.

---

Each gets what it costs and what it sheds. The one that was obvious now has to argue for itself against the others.
|||
The scoring machinery: evaluate-set and converge-pugh, walked and signed in i1; the criteria fell out of the requirements per meth-derive-criteria.

---

They leave with a choice and the reasoning behind it. Nothing was seeded, nothing was committed, and the losing options stay on the record so nobody re-proposes them in a month.
|||
The losing options stand as option nodes with their scores; declare-winner's signed form carries the choice and its why.

---

The obvious answer won, or it did not. Either way it was chosen rather than defaulted to, and the difference is written down.
|||
The i1 decision trail: gate-candidates through declare-winner, each form signed, the ADRs in the register.
