## Deck

A builder already has a product and a way of working they will not give up. They want the machine, not the method that ships with it.
|||
The promise is vp-vendoring; the general form is uc-vendor-and-overlay.

---

They vendor the engine into their own repository, under a folder of its own. Nothing of theirs goes inside that folder, and the rule is written down where they will find it again.
|||
The folder rule stands at the desk (guidance/front-desk.md, commit 1b239b66); the house's own vendoring precedent is deliverable/vendor/mermaid with its README.

---

They run the script. Their product's front desk comes up, and it is quackitect's — no method of theirs is loaded yet.
|||
req-second-product-reuses-install and req-one-script-installs, in tsp-first-run's procedure.

---

They write their own guidance and their own method cards in their own folder, above the engine's. The resolution chain prefers theirs where they exist.
|||
The overlay seam is pinned by tsp-overlay-seam's suite, green in the 2026-08-11 battery.

---

They walk one iteration. Their cards arrive at the states they wrote them for. The engine's cards serve every state they did not.
|||
The placement verb se_prompt_place and the overlay resolution the tsp-overlay-seam suite covers.

---

Their rigor matrix row replaces one of the engine's outright. The compiled machine for the next iteration carries their step, not the original.
|||
Rows compile from the rows folder by name (engine/rigor-matrix.ts); an overlay row of the same name is the replacement mechanism the seam suite pins.

---

Three months on, a new engine version drops. They replace the folder whole. There is nothing to merge, because there was never anything of theirs in there.
|||
The pull-assets-never-lean-on-servers rule and the vendored-folder discipline in guidance/craft/software.md.

---

The next pull runs the resolution chain again, and their method comes through unchanged. They vendored a machine and kept their method, and the two never became one fork.
|||
The chain re-resolves on every pull by construction - the truth is read live (software.md).
