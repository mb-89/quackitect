## Deck

The reasoning behind a decision lives in a chat that is gone. What survives is the artifact, which says WHAT was decided and never why.
|||

---

Months later, a reader opens a stakeholder node and finds `dicet: decider` on a role nobody remembers agreeing to.
|||

---

They click the node. The details pane names the state that authored it, and the evidence form that state signed.
|||

---

The form carries the reasoning, the rounds that tested it, the alternative that was refused, and the hand that blessed it with its date.
|||

---

The question is answered from the record alone. Nobody was asked, nothing was reconstructed, and the answer is the one that was actually given at the time.
|||
