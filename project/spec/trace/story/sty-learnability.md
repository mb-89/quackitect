## Deck

Method-heavy projects greet me in words I do not have yet.
|||

---

A fresh clone, the README open, nobody beside me.
|||

---

I read the README top to bottom; every sentence is plain, and each method word carries a link.
|||

---

I follow one link and the definition is one click away.
|||

---

I take the entry chain's next pointer and reach the tour, which shows the project from its live state.
|||

---

Before the session ends I can say what the product is and where the work happens, with nobody helping.
|||
