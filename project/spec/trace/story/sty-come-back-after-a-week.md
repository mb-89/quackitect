## Deck

A week of other work. The engineer remembers there was an iteration and nothing else about it.
|||

---

They open the folder. The engine starts, the panel draws, and the machine is exactly where it was left — no session to restore, because the position was never in a session.
|||

---

The drawing shows one lit node inside an open iteration. The crumbs above it say which record, and which milestone.
|||

---

They open the node. Its evidence form is filled and stamped, and the one below it is empty. That is the whole answer to "where was I".
|||

---

The decision graph beside it shows the last checklist: what was planned, what was ticked, and the one item that was deferred to a state further down.
|||

---

They open the notes inbox. Six pending, oldest four days. Nothing was lost while they were away, and nothing was silently decided either.
|||

---

A fresh agent boots into the same position. It reads what it owes and stops. It remembers nothing from last week, and it does not need to — the repo is the memory.
|||

---

A week away cost one glance. The state of the work was on the screen, not in anyone's head.
|||
