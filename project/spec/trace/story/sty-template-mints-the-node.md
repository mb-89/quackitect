## Deck

A form that says "list the stakeholders" gets prose. Prose cannot be checked, counted, or drawn on a graph.
|||

---

The field says what it accepts — one story per line — and carries a link to that type's template beside the ask.
|||

---

The template is the whole instruction. Every frontmatter field that must be filled carries a comment saying what goes there, and so does every heading.
|||

---

Filling it means replacing comments with content. The engine checks the finished node against the template, so a missing section is caught where it was written.
|||

---

Nobody explained the format in prose, and every node that came out is one the graph can draw.
|||
