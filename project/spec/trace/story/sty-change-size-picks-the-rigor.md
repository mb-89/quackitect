## Deck

The same ceremony for a typo and for a new product is how process dies. One is theatre and the other is negligence, and a team that suffers both stops believing in either.
|||

---

At the kickoff the agent PROPOSES a change size — patch, minor, major or product — with its reasoning, and names the cells a smaller column would strike.
|||

---

The person picks. The bless IS the choice. Nothing was decided at seeding, so nothing is assumed here.
|||

---

The engine compiles the chosen column into this iteration's own state machine and pins it to the record.
|||

---

The walk a patch gets is short and the walk a product gets is long, and the difference was decided once, by a person, on the record.
|||
