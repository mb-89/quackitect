## Deck

A method that only works with an AI driving it is not a method. It is a product feature, and it dies with the vendor.
|||

---

The slider sits at zero. Nothing the agent pulls will move the walk one state. The mirror is open beside the editor.
|||

---

The engineer ticks the reading boxes themselves and presses through boot. Each tick pins the version of the document they read.
|||

---

They open an evidence form in the details pane, fill it, and press submit. The SAME checks run: the fields, the references, the templates.
|||

---

The walk stands where they put it, carrying the same record it would have carried with an agent — and no AI touched any of it.
|||
