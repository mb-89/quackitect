## Deck

An agent told to build will build. Nothing stops it starting from a one-line brief, and an architecture chosen on thin input costs orders of magnitude to undo later.
|||

---

The iteration stands at M1. Its vision packet, its as-is and its delta are written and signed. The engineer says "go to draw-context" and walks away.
|||

---

The agent aims at draw-context. The pull draws the route, walks the hops it may, and STOPS at gate-motivation — naming the clause, what it expected, and what it got.
|||

---

The engineer opens the gate's evidence form. The rounds are filled, the red team's kill-criterion is named, the verdict reads pass. They press the thumb.
|||

---

draw-context opens. Everything past the gate rests on a frame a person signed, and the record says whose hand it was and when.
