## Deck

An engineer has a day's work in mind and no idea what this system calls it. Every tool of this kind opens by asking which record type you want, which is a question only the tool cares about.
|||

---

They open the desk and say it in their own words: "the trace links are broken and I want to fix them today."
|||

---

The desk sweeps before it answers. One call lists every open record, every pending note, and the doors the machine actually offers this minute. Nothing comes from memory.
|||

---

It recommends the smallest vehicle that still honours the gates — the expedition already open — says why in two sentences, and names the second-best option with what it would cost.
|||

---

The engineer said one sentence and chose one word. They never learned what an expedition is, and the desk carried the paperwork.
|||
