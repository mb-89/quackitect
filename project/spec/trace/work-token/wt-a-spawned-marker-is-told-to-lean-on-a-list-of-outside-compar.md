## Why it stands

In one run, twenty-two of forty-four cells came back at the top two marks naming no comparison whatever, which the scale forbids, and the table had to be filled again from nothing. Two repairs. Let the form draw its permitted comparisons from the earlier step that gathered them. And refuse a submitted table whose top marks leave that column empty. A code comment already claims something verifies this, and nothing does.
