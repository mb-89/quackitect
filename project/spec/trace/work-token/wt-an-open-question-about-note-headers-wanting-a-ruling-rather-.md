## Why it stands

An open question about note headers, wanting a ruling rather than a repair. The engine's printer strips quotation marks from values that parse without them, while most authors here typed those marks deliberately. Nobody has said which side is right, and the churn alarm cannot tell a wrong printer from a wrong corpus. The tension is sharp: the printer removes exactly the protection that one refusal's own remedy recommends. A guard catches the break at the moment of writing, so the exposure is bounded meanwhile.
