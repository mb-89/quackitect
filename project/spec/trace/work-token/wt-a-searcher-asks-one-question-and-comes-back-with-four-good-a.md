## Why it stands

A searcher asks one question and comes back with four good answers. Only the answer fitting the current chart has anywhere to live. The rest land in a prose field nobody opens again. Wanted is a home that keeps a sourced finding across iterations, names who is expected to read it, and settles whether the search method itself should demand the capture instead of leaving it to whoever remembers.
