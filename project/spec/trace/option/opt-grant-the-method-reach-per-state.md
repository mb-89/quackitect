## Mechanism

The confinement is not one rule over the whole walk. It is a property of the
STATE, exactly as the legal tool list already is.

A work state reaches its own tree and nothing else. The retro, the overhaul
and any state whose subject IS the shared method carry a grant that widens
the reach, and the grant is on the row where anybody can read it.

THE SEPARATION IS IN RELATION - both demands hold, for different observers.
The conflict only existed because one rule was assumed to apply to every
state at once.

WHY IT IS NOT A SPECIAL CASE. The system already varies authority per state
and refuses on it: SE-C-110 names the state, the legal moves and the exact
next call. This adds one more thing to the same list rather than a second
mechanism beside it.

WHAT IT FIXES THAT NOTHING ELSE DOES. note-430f3af449b8: the retro exists in
two homes, and in the bound one it is mechanically barred from the step that
is its whole point, with nothing warning you until the write refuses.

WHAT IT COSTS. The grant is a new field on the matrix row, and a row that
grants too much is a hole nobody sees. It wants the same treatment as the
tool list - visible on the drawing, refused when undeclared.
