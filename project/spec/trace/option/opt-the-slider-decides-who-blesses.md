## Mechanism

THE CONTRADICTION. Requiring a person at every gate makes the judgment real.
It also stops the unattended walk dead, which is the thing the product sells.

Improving is 27, Reliability. Degrading is 35, Adaptability.

THE SEPARATION IS IN RELATION. Both demands were assumed to apply to one
observer. They do not. What the gate protects is that a VERDICT is recorded —
that somebody looked, said pass or fail, and put a reason on it. Who holds
the pen is a different question, and the slider already answers it for every
other step.

So the gate owes a verdict always. Below the slider it comes from a person.
Above it, from the agent. The land gate keeps its exception, because reaching
trunk is the act that leaves the record.

WHAT IT WOULD COST HERE. This was ruled and built on 2026-08-09, so the cell
is occupied rather than proposed. It is recorded because the SEPARATION is
the reusable part: `req-gate-needs-a-persons-verdict` had overstated its own
rule for months, and the same overstatement will happen again wherever a
demand names an observer it did not need to name.
