## The abstract problem it answers

Compare two machines fairly when the work they do is not identical by nature.

## What transfers

The insistence that the COURSE is the constant and everything else is the
variable. It is why the model, the effort and the size must be held inside a
pair, and why only the machine version may move.

## What it warns about, and it is already on the register

A course driven often enough gets tuned for. That is the Goodhart risk, and
here it is bounded by the agent having no memory between runs — but not by
anything else.

## Mechanism

One archived iteration is the course. A pair is two runs of that course under
two machine versions, everything else fixed.
