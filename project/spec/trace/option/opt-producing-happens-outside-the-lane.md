## Mechanism

THE JAIL IS NOT WIDENED BECAUSE THE ACT IS NOT INSIDE IT. A person runs a
script, which writes wherever they told it to.

THE SCRIPT BOUNDS ITSELF INSTEAD. Today's export refuses a destination that is
not empty, and demands a folder, a name and an abbreviation with no fallback,
because a forgotten argument would ship it to somebody else.

## What it costs

IT FAILS THE OWNER'S ASK DIRECTLY. They want a CREATE VEHICLE button in the
editor extension, which makes producing a copy something the running system
does. An act reachable from inside the system is inside the lane by
construction.

AND THE SCRIPT'S OWN GUARDS ARE NOT THE JAIL'S. They are hand-written checks in
one script, reimplemented in whatever produces the next kind of tree, rather
than one rule at one resolver.

## Why it is on the chart

BECAUSE IT IS WHAT SHIPS, and a chart that omits the incumbent gives every
candidate nothing to be measured against.

AND BECAUSE IT IS CHEAP AND IT WORKS. Nobody has been harmed by it. The reason
to leave it is the button, not a defect.
