## Mechanism

THE SUBJECT is the evidence form, and the operator is TASK UNIFICATION: give
an existing component a second job.

THE FORM TODAY records what a state did, and REFERENCES the standing nodes by
id. Two artifacts, and the form is the weaker one: it freezes at sign-off
while the nodes keep living.

THE SECOND JOB. The form becomes the editing surface for the nodes it names.
A cell in the table writes a frontmatter key on the node. There is no second
copy, so there is nothing to drift.

WHAT IS ALREADY BUILT. The node-table and dsm templates do exactly this —
`writes: cluster` on partition-functions put a key on sixteen function nodes
this session. So the option is partly occupied, and the cell is whether it
becomes the RULE rather than two templates that happen to work that way.

WHAT IT COSTS. A form that edits the corpus can damage the corpus, and a
signed form then means something different: not "this is what I wrote" but
"this is what I wrote it INTO". The signature would have to cover the nodes
as they stood, which is the evidence-hash problem the gates already have and
solve by recomputing rather than storing.
