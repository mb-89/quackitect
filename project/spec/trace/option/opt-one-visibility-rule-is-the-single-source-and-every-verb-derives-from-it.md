## The evidence it rests on

Three lists decide this today. paths.ts EXCLUDED_DIRS serves list and glob.
search.ts carries two entries of its own. se_file_read consults nothing, and a
lane read of .se/reading.md returned the file and its hash.

## What it buys beyond this iteration

The conditional concealment needs one rule that four verbs honour. Written per
verb it will disagree per verb, exactly as the three standing lists already do.

## Mechanism

One predicate over a path and the current binding. Every path-taking verb calls
it. Nothing else decides visibility.
