## Mechanism

THE HOST KEEPS THREE THINGS AND THE CARGO KEEPS TWO.

- The platform holds a recombinase, a defined insertion address, and THE
  PROMOTER — the thing that makes anything run at all.
- The cassette holds a payload and one recombination site. It carries no
  promoter and no replication origin.

SO THE CARGO IS INERT BY CONSTRUCTION. Detached, it cannot express and cannot
persist. It becomes live only by landing at the address, downstream of a
promoter the host owns.

INSERTION DOES NOT BREAK ANYTHING. The address is dedicated, and that is the
design's chief economy: capture costs nothing that was already there.

AND POSITION IN THE ARRAY IS MEANINGFUL. The slot next to the promoter is the
live one. The rest is a reservoir held at low cost — one chromosomal array
carries a hundred and seventy-nine cassettes across three percent of the genome.
Promoting a dormant one is excision and reinsertion at the front.

ACQUISITION AND ACTIVATION ARE SEPARATE EVENTS, which is the property no
software override mechanism in this iteration's survey has.

## What transfers

THE HOST OWNS THE ONLY WAY TO RUN. An override that cannot invoke itself cannot
surprise anybody, and the resolution order stops being a rule the copy has to
learn — it is the array.

THE DECLARED ADDRESS. Overrides land at a place the base named for them, so
capture never damages what was already there. That is the opposite of every
path-keyed scheme, where an override occupies the same coordinates as its target.

THE ORDERED RESERVOIR. A copy can hold an override it is not currently using,
cheaply, and promote it later. Nothing on this chart separates "I have this
change" from "this change is in force".

AND THE GATING. The capture machinery sits under stress control rather than
running continuously — it reshuffles when the organism is in trouble. An update
is exactly such a moment.

## What breaks in translation

THE SINGLE SHARED PROMOTER HAS NO ANALOGUE, AND IT IS DOING MOST OF THE WORK.
The economy comes from the cargo being unable to run itself. Anything imported
into a software system carries its own entry points. Strip those and you have
made it inert rather than cheap. Keep them and the property that made this sharp
is gone.

THE CHEAP RESERVOIR DOES NOT SURVIVE EITHER. A cassette a hundred positions deep
costs a few kilobases. A dormant override costs a version constraint, a build,
and surface area.

AND BIOLOGY ITSELF CORRECTED THE TIDY PART OF THE MODEL. The textbook claim was
that expression falls with distance from the promoter, giving a loud head and a
silent tail. Testing a hundred and thirty-six cassettes in 2024 found the
distance effect NEGLIGIBLE. What actually governs downstream expression is the
TRANSLATION RATE of the cassettes upstream, because that sets the stability of
the shared transcript.

SO THE CASSETTES ARE NOT INDEPENDENT, EVEN THERE. A slow one degrades everything
behind it. The clean reservoir was a better model than it was a fact, and a
design copying the model rather than the finding would inherit a false premise.

## What the platform cannot do, and says so

THE INTEGRON IS NOT MOBILE. It encodes no mobility functions at all and rides on
transposons and plasmids. Most clinical ones cannot even self-mobilise, carrying
incomplete transposition modules.

THAT IS THE HONEST SHAPE OF THE TRANSFER. This mechanism answers how an addition
is held and made live. It says nothing about how the addition ARRIVES, which is
a different cluster's question and a different option's job.

## The numbers, because the adjective was doing too much work

A CROSS-CHECK READING SUPPLIED WHAT "NEGLIGIBLE" WAS HIDING. Measured across
all six orderings of a three-cassette array (Souque, Escudero & MacLean, eLife
10:e62474, 2021):

- Position ONE to position TWO: a 6- to 24-fold drop in the resistance the
  cassette confers.
- Position TWO to position THREE: under 2-fold.
- Transcription declines STEADILY with position. The steep first-to-second drop
  is therefore mostly TRANSLATIONAL, caused by short reading frames inside the
  insertion site that boost translation of a cassette lacking its own ribosome
  binding signal, and only in the front slot.

SO THE GRADIENT IS A CLIFF AND THEN A PLAIN. Slot one is privileged for a
specific mechanical reason; everything behind it is roughly equivalent. That is
a better design to copy than a smooth decay, and it is the opposite of what the
textbook model implies.

## What the shuffling actually buys, measured

THE SAME EXPERIMENT RAN THE COUNTERFACTUAL. Populations with a working
recombinase survived a rising antibiotic ramp significantly better than an
otherwise identical strain with the recombinase deleted. Around ninety percent
of surviving recombinase-positive populations showed rearrangements; the deleted
strain showed none, and neither did recombinase-positive populations kept under
mild pressure.

BUT THE SURVIVORS REACHED THE SAME CEILING. Final resistance did not differ
between the two groups. THE MECHANISM CHANGED THE PROBABILITY OF GETTING THERE,
NOT WHAT WAS REACHABLE.

THAT IS THE HONEST CLAIM FOR ANY REARRANGEMENT MACHINERY HERE. It does not make
a better configuration possible. It makes finding one more likely, under
pressure, and it is worth exactly that.

## And it is switched off where it is not needed

ALMOST A THIRD OF THESE RECOMBINASE GENES ARE INACTIVATED IN THE WILD, by
frameshifts, nonsense mutations and deletions. Lineages that have specialised on
one niche turn the shuffling off.

THE COST IS REAL ENOUGH TO BE WORTH PAYING TO AVOID. A copy that has settled
wants the array, not the machinery that rearranges it, and a design offering
both should let the second be disabled without losing the first.
