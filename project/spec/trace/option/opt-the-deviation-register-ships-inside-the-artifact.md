## Mechanism

THE REGISTER IS NOT BESIDE THE DOCUMENT. It is a chapter of it.

CENELEC PUBLISHES EUROPEAN STANDARDS that member countries must implement. Where
a country's own law conflicts, it may file an A-deviation — and the rule for
where that lands is the transferable part. Its own rulebook: information that
the provisions conflict with national regulation "shall be included in an Annex
to the European Standard".

WHAT THE ANNEX CARRIES, in the domain's own words: the countries having such a
deviation, "clear identification of the national regulation concerned and of the
normative clauses of the standard in conflict with the regulation". The foreword
must warn the reader that deviations exist at all.

SO EVERY HOLDER OF THE BASE DOCUMENT HOLDS THE COMPLETE FORK LIST. No separate
system, no subscription, no lookup, no wiki page that rots.

## What transfers

THE PLACEMENT, which is the whole idea. A copy's departures are a generated
section of the artifact they depart from, keyed to the clause identifier.

THE COUNTING. CENELEC's Technical Board monitors deviations per member, per
standard, and per piece of legislation. Divergence nobody measures is divergence
nobody can argue about.

AND THE PUSHBACK BEFORE ACCEPTANCE. The drafting body "should make every effort
to amend the draft in order to avoid the conflict", especially where several
countries want the same deviation. A deviation many copies want is evidence the
base is wrong, and the mechanism routes it that way rather than accumulating
forks.

THE PERIODIC SWEEP. Validity is re-asked on the five-year systematic review, and
a deviation no longer valid must be removed by amendment. Deviations never
re-examined become permanent by accident.

## What breaks in translation

CENELEC'S DEVIATIONS EXIST ONLY FOR CONFLICTING NATIONAL LAW. Its rulebook is
explicit that a deviation "is not a privilege granted to a CENELEC member". Our
copies deviate because they want to, which removes the filter that keeps the
annex short.

AND THE OBLIGATION SURVIVES THE DEVIATION THERE, NOT HERE. "A-deviations do not
release CENELEC members from their obligation to implement the relevant European
Standard." A copy of this product owes nobody implementation of anything.

THE DIRECTION IS ALSO INVERTED. CENELEC's annex is compiled by the PUBLISHER,
who sees every member. A copy of this product is meant to be independent of its
source, so nothing here sees every copy. The register can only be per-copy: what
THIS copy departed from, shipped inside this copy.

THAT IS A REAL LOSS AND WORTH NAMING. The cross-copy view — where two copies
made the same departure, which is the signal that the base is wrong — cannot
exist without a central body. This transfer keeps the placement and gives up the
aggregation.

## The counter-example, which is the same idea done badly

UNCITRAL'S MODEL LAW STATUS TABLE lists 127 jurisdictions with a year and a
single version bit, then says in its own words that "the legislation of each
State should be considered in order to identify the exact nature of any possible
deviation from the model in the legislative text that was adopted".

THAT IS A REGISTRY OF WHICH VERSION YOU ARE ON AND NOTHING ABOUT YOUR CHANGES.
It hands the reader 127 forks and tells them to diff each one by hand. A
provenance record without a deviation record is that.
