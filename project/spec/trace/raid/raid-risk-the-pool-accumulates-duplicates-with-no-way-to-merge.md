## Where it was found

THE PRIOR-ART SCAN AT M1 (ref-triage-and-option-pools-2026). Linear Triage
offers four actions and one of them is mark-as-duplicate, which merges into a
canonical issue rather than closing beside it. Our four dispositions map onto
the other three and leave that one unanswered.

## Why expected

DUPLICATES ARE WHAT A CAPTURE INBOX PRODUCES. The same irritation is noticed on
three different days by whoever is walking, and each time it is a fresh note.
The i17 seed itself carries seven pulled notes that overlap on the criteria
machinery.

## What would reduce it

A FIFTH DISPOSITION that drains a note INTO an existing option rather than
minting a new one - the note is marked drained, the option gains a source. It
is small, and it is named in the reference node as the first thing worth
stealing.

WHY IT IS A RISK AND NOT SCOPE: the mechanism works without it, and adding a
disposition before the first one has been used is designing against an
imagined shape.
