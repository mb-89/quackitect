## The shape of it

meth-gate-review, in its own words: SEARCH. Actually search the web, at the
gate. `se_web_search` and `se_web_fetch` are the lane for it.

`M0_90_gate-kickoff.md` grants: se_survey, se_note_drain, se_file_read,
se_file_write, se_file_patch, se_file_search, se_file_glob, se_file_list,
se_log_query, se_answer.

Neither web verb is there.

## Why the honest answer is not good enough

THE METHOD ALREADY RULES ON THE GAP: an unmade comparison is a finding, write
"not scanned" with the reason. That is the right rule for a scan somebody chose
not to make. It is the wrong rule for a scan the state made impossible, because
the reader cannot tell the two apart - and the kickoff is the gate where a
shape gets fixed, so it is the worst one to leave unscanned.

## Repayment

Grant the two web verbs at every gate whose review demands a scan, or move the
scan to the state before the gate and have the gate read its result. Then a
check that every state naming meth-gate-review can reach a scan.
