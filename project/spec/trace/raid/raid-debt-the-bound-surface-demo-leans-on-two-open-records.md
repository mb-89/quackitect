## What is owed

ONE DEMONSTRATION, FOUR STEPS, AND A WATCHER. Two records open, the
walk standing in one, and every link opened from the other's form
landing in the other's tree.

## Why it cannot be observed today

THE SPEC SAYS SO ITSELF, in "What it leans on": two records can be open
at once with satellites serving both, and until that build stands the
procedure cannot run.

IT ALSO NEEDS A SCREEN. The pass is a person seeing that the page they
reached is the page the form was about. A URL is assertable; the landing
is not.

## Why a row rather than a blank

THE SPEC ALREADY SAID THE HONEST THING and nothing carried it. So every
iteration reached this box, read the same clause, and re-derived the
same conclusion.

THIS IS THE SIBLING OF raid-debt-human-observed-demonstrations, which
carries four demonstrations for the same reason and was itself written
because re-adjudicating them every iteration was the waste. That row's
own impact line says re-adjudication must not happen. This spec was
outside it and got re-adjudicated anyway.

## Repayment

THE DEBT CLOSES when the two-record build stands and a watcher runs the
four steps. Closing it hands the claim back, and somebody answers the
box again.
