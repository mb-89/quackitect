## What is happening

THE DEMONSTRATION IS SPECIFIED AND UNPERFORMED. Its six steps are written, and
nobody has walked one of them.

BOTH REQUIREMENTS SAY WHY A TEST CANNOT STAND IN. [[req-one-command-produces-a-complete-copy]]:
"the pass is a machine with nothing of the source on it. That is observed end to
end rather than measured under instrumentation, and a test running inside the
source's own tree cannot establish it."

## What the tests DO establish, so the gap is exact rather than vague

THE HERMETIC CHAIN IS PROVED. A vehicle is produced into a temporary directory,
a project is produced from that vehicle, the project carries none of the method
and one record naming its vehicle by identity, and the engine is unchanged by
having produced. Eleven cases in `tests/produce.test.ts`.

WHAT IS NOT PROVED IS EVERY STEP THAT LEAVES THIS MACHINE:

- step four, the vehicle brought up where nothing of the engine is beside it,
  on its own shipped method, with zero files edited by hand
- step five, that vehicle opening a project tree and coming up able to work
- step six, a tree with no record refusing rather than guessing
- the sixth slide, the desk greeting under the vehicle's own name

TWO OF THOSE ARE KNOWN TO FAIL TODAY for reasons already recorded rather than
for reasons the demonstration would discover. Nothing reads the driven record at
startup, and the copy needs a network install before it runs.

## Why it is the owner's rather than the agent's

IT NEEDS A SECOND MACHINE AND A PERSON WATCHING. That is the definition of a
demonstration, and it is the one form of evidence an agent cannot produce alone.

## What would close it

ONE SITTING. Press the button, move the result to a machine with nothing of this
on it, bring it up, point it at a project, and open a tree with no record. Six
observations, written down as they happen.
