## What is being assumed

THAT ONE RULING CONSTRAINS ONE THING. Session state belongs to the machine
rather than to a branch, so switching branches must not switch which state you
are looking at.

AND THAT DEPTH IS FREE. Nothing in that ruling says the folder must sit at the
top of the tree rather than one level down.

## Why it is an assumption and not a fact

IT IS A READING OF INTENT. The ruling's words are about branches. Whether its
author also meant the location has not been established, and the person who
made it can settle it in one sentence.

THE RECORD ASSERTS THE READING RATHER THAN CHECKING IT. Its exact words are
that the old ruling is not in the way, followed by the reasoning above. That is
an argument, and a good one, but it is not the same as having asked.

## Why it is graded crippling

EVERY GOAL IN THIS ITERATION SITS ON IT. If the reading is wrong, the move is
not a tidy-up. It is a proposal to change a standing guarantee, which is a
different iteration at a different size.

## Probe

THE RECORD ALREADY NAMES THE PROBE and it is in scope: one test asserting the
folder resolves to one place while a record is bound. That turns the guarantee
from trusted into checked, whichever way the reading goes.

AND ONE QUESTION SETTLES THE INTENT HALF, which no test can reach. It is
cheap, and it belongs to whoever made the ruling.
