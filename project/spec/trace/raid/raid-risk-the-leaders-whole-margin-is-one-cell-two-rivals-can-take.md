## The fallback

DECLARE THE WINNER WITH THE GRAFT ALREADY APPLIED, or declare it knowing the
margin is one cell. graft-onto-the-winner is the state that takes what a rival
does better; here the traffic runs the other way, and the rivals would be taking
what the leader does better.

THE CHEAPEST HONEST MOVE IS TO REDRAW THE LINES FIRST. Two rivals are silent on
an axis they could answer, and they are silent because the chart did not ask.
Redrawing them before declare-winner costs one edit each and removes the
artefact; declaring first and finding out later is a reopen of the winner, which
is the most expensive reopen this machine has.

IF THE GRAFT IS NOT APPLIED, the fallback is to record in declare-winner that the
winner led on two axes that no rival was required to contest, and that the finding
stands against it rather than beside it.
