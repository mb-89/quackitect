## Where it comes from

THE THIRD GOAL CONFLICT RULED AT THIS ITERATION'S draft-vision. The owner's
framing asks for both halves in one breath: everything over a second must be
non-intrusive, and must be very transparent about how slow it is.

RULED: honesty wins over quiet, because the owner's own wording says a person is
never left guessing. Non-intrusive constrains the FORM of the telling and never
whether it happens.

THIS ENTRY IS THE COST OF THAT RULING. Winning the tie for honesty buys a
different failure, and it is worth naming before the first surface is built
rather than after five of them shout at once.

## Why the register already has evidence for it

THE MACHINE HAS DONE THIS ONCE ALREADY, in the other direction. mirror_profile
recorded a phase breakdown over a second, 324 times, for days, and no state
read it. An instrument nobody reads is not instrumentation.

A PROGRESS MESSAGE NOBODY READS IS THE SAME FAILURE with the audience changed
from a state to a person.

## What would make it fire

- Every slow call announcing itself identically, so the announcements carry no
  ranking.
- A progress signal that appears for a call finishing in 1.1 seconds and for
  one taking 30, with nothing distinguishing them.
- Telling the person a thing is slow without telling them whether it is
  progressing.

## What holds it off

THE TELLING EARNS ITS PLACE BY SAYING SOMETHING THE SILENCE DID NOT. What is
running, how far along, and whether waiting is the right thing to do.

AND THE PRIOR ART AT THIS ITERATION'S GATE POINTS THE SAME WAY: percentile
targets rather than a flat threshold, so a surface that is occasionally slow
and one that is always slow do not produce the same signal.
