The ladder, low to high, as the owner ruled it at the cut-over:

- none — no rung pressed; nothing moves without the human.
- mechanical — what mainly the engine does. Transitional anchor 0.2.
- operational — single steps and submitting. Anchor 0.4.
- tactical — running iterations and expeditions, seeding and delegating
  sub-work. Anchor 0.6.
- strategic — seeding iterations, retros, overhauls and planning;
  delegating tactical and below; draining the backlog. Anchor 0.8.
- ideation — filling the backlog and producing the new; everything but
  the axiomatic vision of the product; delegation to all others.
  Emergency rides with it. Anchor 1.

The 1.5 tier is never reached and ignored. The dictation transposed
mechanical and operational; the owner confirmed mechanical sits below
operational, and the anchors ascend with the ladder.

The words are the truth (machines/scale.md); the numbers are the
transitional carriers while the weighing still compares numerically.
