## Probe

OWED, AND IT IS CHEAP. The check is written against a belief about human and
agent behaviour, not against a measurement, and the measurement exists as soon
as the pool has any content.

THE PROBE: after the first fifty mints, take every minted option and its source
note and measure the longest verbatim run they share. A distribution clustered
near zero says authors are writing; a fat tail near the note's own length says
they are pasting and the check is doing real work; a hump in the middle - long
shared runs that the check let through - is the falsification.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT: leaks that got through as near-copies. One found by the
corpus sweep is enough, because the row it defends is fatal.

WHY plausible AND NOT conceivable. An author under time pressure who knows a
paste will be refused will reword the paste, and a reworded private sentence is
still a private sentence. The check makes the lazy path illegal; it does not
make the honest path easier.

## What the measurement found, 2026-08-19

TWO OF THE THREE BLOCKERS WERE GONE. The check is built and refuses a run of six
or more. The pool holds 24 tokens, which is short of the fifty this entry asked
for and is not nothing.

EVERY TOKEN JOINED TO ITS SOURCE NOTE. Twenty-four of twenty-four, because each
token names its note in its own frontmatter.

### The numbers

THE LONGEST SHARED WORD-RUN, real pairs: max 5, median 4. Twenty-three of the
twenty-four sit in the 3-to-5 band. Not one reaches 6.

THE CONTROL, and without it those numbers mean nothing. The same statistic
against 72 pairs of a token and a note it did NOT come from: max 3, median 2,
and 62 of the 72 at two words or fewer.

### What that says

THE CHECK CATCHES THE PASTE. Confirmed. Nothing is anywhere near the note's own
length, and the fat tail this entry feared does not exist.

THE BELIEF UNDERNEATH IT DOES NOT HOLD. Independent writing about the same
subject produces two or three shared words. These produce four or five. The
minted statements sit at double the background and one word below the refusal.

THAT IS THE HUMP THIS ENTRY NAMED AS THE FALSIFICATION, moved. It predicted a
hump in the middle of long shared runs the check let through. What is there is a
hump pressed against the threshold, because the threshold is where it is.

THE ENTRY'S OWN WORDS CALLED IT: "An author under time pressure who knows a
paste will be refused will reword the paste, and a reworded private sentence is
still a private sentence."

### Why this matters more than the number suggests

A FOUR-WORD RUN SOUNDS HARMLESS AND IS EXACTLY THE DANGEROUS LENGTH. What the
FATAL row protects is private text, and private text is short: a name, an
employer, a figure, a date. A leak does not need a sentence.

SO THE CHECK ENFORCES THE LETTER AND NOT THE SPIRIT. It makes the lazy path
illegal, which is what it was built to do, and it cannot tell a rewrite from an
authoring.

THE RESIDUAL HAS A HOME ALREADY:
`raid-risk-the-rewrite-carries-the-private-sentence-across`.

### What this measurement cannot say

TWENTY-FOUR MINTS, NOT FIFTY. The shape is clear and the tail is not measured.

ONE AUTHOR. Every token in the pool was minted by an agent working from the same
guidance, so this measures one behaviour rather than a population.

ONE METRIC. A genuine paraphrase that shares no run at all scores zero here and
could still carry the private thing. The word-run sees copying, not meaning.

SO THE TRIGGER STANDS. Re-run this at fifty, and again the first time a person
rather than an agent mints one.
