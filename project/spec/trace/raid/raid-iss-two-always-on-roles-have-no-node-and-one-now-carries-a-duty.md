## The two are not the same case

COMMUNICATOR IS AN HONEST STRIKE. Nobody explains this product to others yet,
the surfaces that would serve that role are unbuilt and stand as their own open
iterations, and minting a role no artifact serves fills the register rather
than reading it. It is listed here for completeness and needs nothing.

PROJECT-OWNER IS A HOLE THIS ITERATION WIDENED. The ruling that the owner
maintains the model list by hand, one sitting at a time, is a STANDING duty
with a cadence. It is the thing the whole fixed-list design rests on — a list
nobody updates is the drift risk arriving by a different door — and there is no
node it can be written on.

## Why it was not simply fixed at the state that found it

MINTING A PROJECT-OWNER NODE FOR THE WHOLE PRODUCT inside M2 of a sizing change
would be re-doing the product's stakeholder map under cover of an unrelated
iteration. The state struck it deliberately and recorded the strike rather than
absorbing the duty into a role that does not hold it.

## What closes it

A PRODUCT-LEVEL STAKEHOLDER PASS, which is somebody's iteration and not a
paragraph. When it happens, the maintenance duty above is the first concern the
project-owner node should carry, and this entry is the pointer to it.

UNTIL THEN THE ANSWER TO "who keeps the list current" IS NOBODY, ON THE RECORD,
which is better than an answer that reads as settled and is not.
