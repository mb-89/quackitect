The machine is built so one person can drive many agents. The same
arrangement removes the hours of hands-on work that built the judgment the
driving depends on.

It is a risk rather than an assumption because it has not happened yet, and
rather than an issue because nothing is presently hurting. The decay would be
slow and would not announce itself.

WHAT MAKES IT WATCHABLE: the hands-on cadence. If the owner goes a long
stretch without building something unaided, the trigger has fired whether or
not anything looks wrong.
