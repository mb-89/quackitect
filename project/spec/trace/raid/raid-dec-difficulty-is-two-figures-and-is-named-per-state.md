## The choice

THE CORPUS HOLDS BOTH EXTREMES AND THAT IS THE ARGUMENT. A finder state reads one
method card and asks for deep original judgement. A partition state reads
forty-nine function nodes and their flows and renders a table. Collapsing both to
one word says they need the same hand, and nothing supports that.

THE MILESTONE REDUCTION GOES WITH IT. Once every state carries its own figures
there is nothing a maximum would be computing, and
`raid-risk-a-submachine-maximum-drags-easy-items-onto-an-expensive-walker` has
nothing to bite on.

## Rejected options

- ONE FIGURE, WHICH IS THE SEED'S SHAPE. Rejected on the measurement rather than
  the argument: at major the declared rigor word reads `full` on 49 of 53 rows and
  ranks nothing, while the field count spreads over seven classes. One figure is
  either uninformative or wrong depending on which one you take.
- THE MAXIMUM OVER A MILESTONE, `req-a-milestone-takes-the-maximum-complexity-over-its-rows`.
  The requirement stands and this architecture never reaches it. Probe 1 measured
  the spread it would hide: at major, ten rows ask no evidence field at all and one
  asks six.
- A FORMULA THAT DERIVES THE FIGURES, `opt-the-difficulty-is-computed-from-the-row-s-own-field-count`.
  REJECTED AND REVISITABLE, and the reason is recorded at graft-onto-the-winner:
  nothing here says where the figures come from, so a derived formula would sit
  under this decision without contradiction. It is not taken because nobody has
  designed the formula, and adopting an undesigned one imports a risk this
  comparison never scored.

## Consequences

THE RUNG MAPPING BECOMES TWO-DIMENSIONAL AND NEEDS A RULE FOR THE CORNER where
the axes disagree. `raid-asm-the-model-ladder-is-a-total-order` was already
unproven; this makes the disagreement explicit rather than creating it, and under
`raid-dec-the-block-names-a-rung-and-never-a-model` the corner is the receiver's
to rule on rather than ours.

AND EVERY ACTIVE CELL NOW CARRIES TWO FIGURES RATHER THAN ONE. Probe 1 counted
154 active cells across the four change sizes. Whatever the source of the figures,
that population doubles.
