## What crosses

- the trace nodes, as markdown with frontmatter
- the machines, as canvases
- the guidance and the method cards

## Measured 2026-08-17

Listing the whole trace tree: 16 ms. The corpus sweep reads and parses 1222
nodes in about 400 ms, printed by the sweep itself on every run.

NEITHER IS A BOUND BEING MET, because this edge has none. They are recorded
so the next reader knows the scale rather than guessing it.

## Why this edge has no bound

NOTHING IS SERVED HERE. The product writes files and the vault opens them; no
call crosses, so there is no answer to be fast or slow. This is the one modelled
boundary whose bound is honestly absent rather than relaxed.

THAT IS WORTH A NODE ANYWAY, because it is the reason several other things are
true: `.se/reading.md` is the same thing as a file for a person to open, and a
person can read the whole corpus with the product switched off.

## The demand it does carry

THE FORMAT IS THE CONTRACT. A frontmatter change that a vault cannot parse
breaks this edge silently, from the product's side, with nothing on the
product's side failing. The lane's own frontmatter guard is what stands here,
and it refuses a parse break before anything lands.
