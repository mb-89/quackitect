The one door, and its whole value is that there is no second one. Every lane
verb inside a satellite reaches storage through here, including the shell.

## What goes in

The call, its path, and the record the satellite is bound to.

## What comes back

One of two things, and never a third.

- THE RESOLVED STORE, which the answer then names. A write is followed by a
  read from that same store, so the landing is proved by what came back
  rather than by the call's own verdict.
- A REFUSAL, naming the path and why it falls outside the record.

## What it refuses

Anything resolving outside the record the satellite serves.
A relative path climbing out, an absolute path into a neighbour. The working
directory makes the common case free and this catches the case the working
directory would happily serve.

## What it does not refuse

A call naming trunk. That names a different OWNER,
not a bad resolution, and it goes up through if-core-satellite to the core.
Confusing the two would refuse the door method changes and commits both use.

## The shell is inside this boundary

A satellite's child process
inherits its working directory. That closes the bypass the i8 field report of
2026-08-12 recorded, where a guard covered five write verbs and could not
watch a shell.
