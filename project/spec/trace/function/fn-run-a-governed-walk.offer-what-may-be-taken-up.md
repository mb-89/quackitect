## Rationale

IT IS NOT route-the-work, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS WHAT THE KILL CRITERION RESTS
ON. route-the-work turns a sentence about wanted work into the right vehicle to
hold it. It is indifferent to where the wanted work came from, and it would
pass every check unchanged over a pool nobody could see.

THIS FUNCTION IS THE SOURCE BEING COMPLETE. It is the only thing in the tree
that consumes flow-standing-option, and without it the mint produces an output
nobody consumes — which is one of the four holes, and the one the gate named as
this iteration's kill criterion.

THEY SHARE A CLUSTER because they are two halves of one conversation: what may
be taken up, and what vehicle takes it.

## Solution-neutral

The statement names no surface, no query language and no store. "Wherever it
was parked" is the demand — the machine that parked it must not matter — and
that holds for a rendered panel, a lane answer, or a printed list.

## The flow that is NOT here

An UNDRAINED capture. It is deliberately not an input: an undrained note has
not been judged, and this function offers options rather than everything
anybody typed. The pending count stays a separate signal, served by
show-where-it-stands, and the two must not merge.
