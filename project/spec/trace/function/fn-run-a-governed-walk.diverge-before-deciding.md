## Rationale

DIVERGENCE IS A FUNCTION, not a mood. Something has to refuse the choosing
step while one option stands, and something has to keep the losers after the
winner is picked.

It opens no record and commits nothing, which is what separates it cleanly
from holding the work. Thinking is not a vehicle, and giving it one would
make every idea a piece of accounting.

The recorded ORDER is here rather than in the log because it is the evidence
that the options were not written to justify a decision already made.
