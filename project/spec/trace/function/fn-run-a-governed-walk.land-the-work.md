## Rationale

SPLIT OUT ON THE OWNER'S RULING, 2026-08-07, and it has the strongest case
of the four for standing alone.

LANDING IS NOT A PHASE OF THE RECORD'S LIFE. req-landing-needs-no-close says
work reaches trunk while the record stays open, so a record may land many
times before it closes. That is a different rhythm from opening once and
closing once, and a function that held both would be describing two clocks as
one.

ALL OR NOTHING IS THE WHOLE DEMAND. A landing that half-succeeds leaves a
trunk nobody can reason about, which is why the requirement says one merge or
none. The green battery is the precondition rather than a nicety: what makes
the trunk trustworthy is that nothing red ever reached it.
