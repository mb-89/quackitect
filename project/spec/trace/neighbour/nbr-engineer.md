## Interface

The mirror's controls, and nothing else: the target, the autonomy dial, the
reading checkboxes, and the thumbs on a gate's form. They read the drawing
and the evidence forms; they press submit, bless or dismiss.

Every one of those acts is recorded with their hand on it. No control reaches
past the record — the person aims and adjudicates, and the walk advances only
on the agent's pull.
