## Interface

The vault is the project folder itself — plain markdown and `.canvas` files
on disk, with no import and no export.

A state machine's truth IS its Obsidian canvas. The engine reads what a
person naturally draws, and a mechanism that depends on metadata Obsidian
will not surface to its editor is a defect in the mechanism.
