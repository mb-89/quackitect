## Concerns

- The front door is written in a vocabulary they do not have. Method words
  like suspect, bless, cone and gate mean nothing on first contact.
- They cannot tell what the product IS from what it DOES. A tour that walks
  the machinery answers the second question and not the first.
- Nobody is beside them. Whatever the entry documents fail to say, they have
  to work out alone or give up.
- They arrive at a system that is mid-flight, and everything they read is
  dated by work they were not present for.

## Notes (not load-bearing)

WHY THE WEIGHT IS ABOVE THE INFLUENCE. This role changes nothing and decides
nothing, and two owner laws exist for it anyway: entry documents carry no
method jargon (2026-07-12), and the tour runs from the live state rather than
a script. Both were forced by findings — the i17 red team and the i19 cold
read — so the role has already cost the product real rework.

It is a served role, never a consulted one. Nobody asks a newcomer what they
need; the product finds out by watching one fail.
