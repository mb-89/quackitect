## Concerns

- Its context is finite and it loses everything at a compaction. Anything the
  product needs it to remember has to live in the repo.
- A refusal it cannot recover from in one turn costs a round and teaches it
  nothing.
- Guidance it cannot reach is guidance it does not follow. What is not pulled
  at the state it works in may as well not exist.
- Bookkeeping spends the same attention as the work. Every protocol element
  the engine could carry and does not is taken out of the thinking.
- It cannot tell a rule it must obey from a rule it may weigh, unless the
  wording says which.
- It now works BESIDE PEERS it never talks to: another machine's agent holds
  claims it must honor sight unseen, and its own claim is all that keeps its
  iteration its own.
- IT NOW BOOTS ON MACHINES NOBODY PREPARED, unattended, with nobody to ask.
  A first step it cannot pass is not a degraded experience, it is a closed
  door, and weaker models already fail the boot reading proof outright
  (added at i28, 2026-08-15).

## Notes (not load-bearing)

THE ONE ROLE WITH NO STAKE, and that is why it is here. Its disposition is
neutral because nothing about the outcome matters to it, so the product may
never rely on its goodwill, its memory or its care. Every guard exists because
this role cannot be trusted to hold a rule it merely read.

Its influence and weight are high for the opposite reason: it does the work,
its failures are the product's failures, and most of the design is shaped
around what it can and cannot be relied on to do.

DISTINCT FROM nbr-agent-harness. The harness is the runtime outside the
boundary, and this is the role inside the product's concern. The product
serves this role — the lane, the typed refusals, the reading loop are all
built for it to consume.
