## Setup

THE CUT STRUCK NINETY-NINE OF ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN ROWS as
non-differentiating. Twelve survived and became the axes three designs were
scored on.

A GATE HAD ALREADY RE-READ TEN, chosen as the ten most likely to be wrong, and
found two failures. Seventy-nine had never been read at all.

THE STATEMENTS ARE ONE FRONTMATTER KEY, so the whole set can be pulled in a
single search rather than in ninety-nine reads. That is what made the timebox
possible.

## Result

FIVE MORE ROWS WERE STRUCK WRONGLY.

- `req-scaffold-from-template`, rank 14, above the line.
- `req-extension-replaced-reported`, rank 58.
- `req-method-reuse-is-vendoring`, rank 86, and the weakest of the five.
- `req-purpose-recorded-at-begin`, rank 91, and the strongest on merit.
- `req-begin-says-own-window`, rank 108.

ALL FIVE SHARE ONE BLIND SPOT. Nobody checked the bootstrap cluster. The third
design picks a cell that deletes the installer AND the scaffold, and every row
describing scaffolding was struck as untouched.

THE STRONGEST OF THE FIVE ARGUES AGAINST ITSELF IN THE RECORD. The third
design's own text says a clone "loses the purpose the scaffold used to record",
and the cut called that row untouched.

## A second defect, found on the way

THREE ROWS CARRY THE REASON "vehicles, untouched" AND NONE IS ABOUT A VEHICLE.
Three genuine vehicle rows sit nearby carrying the same reason.

A REASON WAS COPIED DOWN A BLOCK OF ADJACENT ROWS. That is worse than judging
from a name, because a name-based judgment at least looks at the row.

## What it does not change

THE SEAT CANNOT MOVE ON THESE ROWS. All five separate the third design only.
Both leading designs keep the installer and the scaffold, so they tie on every
one.

## What it makes worse

THE TWELVE SURVIVING AXES SEPARATED THE TWO LEADERS ON TWO. A thirteenth they
tie on makes it two of thirteen.

THIS SPIKE CANNOT SAY WHICH DISEASE THAT IS. It only looked at rows that were
cut. Nobody has asked whether the twelve that survived were the right twelve,
and that is a different question with a different probe.
