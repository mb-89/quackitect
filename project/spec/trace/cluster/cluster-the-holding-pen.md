## Rationale

THE WEAKEST CLUSTER IN THE PARTITION, and saying so is part of it. Its two
members share no flow. They share a rule.

Neither may open a record. Holding a stray must not move the walk, and
diverging must not mint a vehicle for thinking. Both refuse the thing every
other function in the tree does freely, and both hand what they caught to the
account rather than acting on it.

WHY IT IS NOT A BIN. A bin is what is left over. These two were placed by a
rule that can be stated and argued with, and the rule is the same one twice.

If a later reading finds a shared policy too thin to cluster on, they
separate and nothing else in the partition moves.
