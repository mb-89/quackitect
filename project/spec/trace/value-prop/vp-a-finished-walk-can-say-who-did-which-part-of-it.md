## Success criteria

- THE MODEL THAT ANSWERED IS ON THE CALL, not the model that was asked for.
  The two differ in practice and the difference is the interesting case.
- THE STATE THE CALL WAS MADE IN IS ON IT TOO. Either coordinate alone answers
  nothing, and today neither is there.
- A QUESTION LIKE "WHICH STATES DID THE EXPENSIVE MODEL WALK" IS ONE QUERY,
  not a reconstruction.
- IT SURVIVES THE BOX. Attribution that only exists while the machine is alive
  is not attribution.

## Unlike

[[vp-the-machine-says-how-strong-a-hand-each-step-needs]] is the forward half
and this is the backward one. That one decides before the work; this one reads
after it. They are separable — a machine could route perfectly and record
nothing, or record everything and route by accident.

THE BACKWARD HALF IS WHAT MAKES THE FORWARD HALF CORRECTABLE. A declared rating
with no record of what it produced can never be shown to have been wrong, which
is the standing risk this proposition would eventually retire.

## What this is not

IT IS NOT A CLAIM THAT THE RECORD IS TRUSTWORTHY YET. Today the only party who
knows which model answered is the party being measured, and the harness can
move a session to a different model without saying so under machine-readable
output. Until the value comes from something other than self-report, this
proposition is a want with an open assumption under it, and it says so rather
than pretending.
