## Success criteria

- THE MODEL THAT ANSWERED IS ON THE CALL, not the model that was asked for.
  The two differ in practice and the difference is the interesting case.
- THE STATE THE CALL WAS MADE IN IS ON IT TOO. Either coordinate alone answers
  nothing, and today neither is there.
- THE PART THE CALLER PLAYED IS ON IT AS WELL, added 2026-08-20. A walk is
  driven by two hands and not one: a WALKER does the daily work, and a GUIDE
  is asked for the occasional step the walker will not take alone. Counting
  them apart is the whole question "was the split worth it".
- AND A RELAY CARRIES ITS ORIGIN. Either hand may work the lane. Where the
  walker carries a guide's work back instead, the record says it was the
  guide's. A walker filing a guide's judgment under its own name defeats this
  proposition completely.
- AND THE MODEL DOES NOT STAND IN FOR THE PART.
  `project/guidance/method/subagents.md` § Which model says judgment work
  INHERITS the session model. So a guide can carry the same model name as the
  walker that asked it, and grouping by model returns one bucket for two hands.
- A QUESTION LIKE "WHICH STATES DID THE EXPENSIVE MODEL WALK" IS ONE QUERY,
  not a reconstruction.
- IT SURVIVES THE BOX. Attribution that only exists while the machine is alive
  is not attribution.

## Unlike

[[vp-the-machine-says-how-strong-a-hand-each-step-needs]] is the forward half
and this is the backward one. That one decides before the work; this one reads
after it. They are separable — a machine could route perfectly and record
nothing, or record everything and route by accident.

[[vp-a-cheaper-model-does-the-mechanical-work]] is the ancestor both of these
descend from, and this node is the one that would let anybody check it. That
proposition asks that a record walk to its gates on a small model with the same
states signing. Nothing today could tell you whether it did, because nothing
records which model walked which state.

AND THE ANCESTOR IS WHAT MADE THE THIRD COORDINATE NECESSARY, 2026-08-20. That
proposition asks that a record walk to its gates on a small model. The owner's
arrangement is narrower and better: the small model walks EVERYTHING and asks a
strong one for the occasional step. Checking that arrangement means counting the
walker's calls against the guide's, and neither the model coordinate nor the
state coordinate can do it.

THE BACKWARD HALF IS WHAT MAKES THE FORWARD HALF CORRECTABLE. A declared rating
with no record of what it produced can never be shown to have been wrong, which
is the standing risk this proposition would eventually retire.

## What this is not

IT IS NOT A CLAIM THAT THE RECORD IS TRUSTWORTHY YET. Today the only party who
knows which model answered is the party being measured, and the harness can
move a session to a different model without saying so under machine-readable
output. Until the value comes from something other than self-report, this
proposition is a want with an open assumption under it, and it says so rather
than pretending.
