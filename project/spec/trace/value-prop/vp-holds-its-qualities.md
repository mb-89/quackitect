## Success criteria

- Recovery — Metric: turns from a typed refusal to an accepted call. Target: 1.
- Audit — Metric: declared accountability questions answerable from the record alone. Target: every one, with role and channel.
- Orientation — Metric: first-time readers who can say what the product is within one session, unaided. Target: 2 of 3.
- Fresh machine — Metric: steps outside the published entry to a verified working state. Target: 0.
- Overlay safety — Metric: forced overlay edits per engine update, and cross-tree writes. Target: 0 and 0.

## Unlike

Unlike rigor tooling that proves itself only on the happy path, each quality
here is demanded with a pass line and walked as a journey. A stressed product
fails a named row instead of a mood.

## Notes (not load-bearing)

Five qualities picked from the ISO 25010 walk: recoverability, auditability,
learnability, portability, maintainability. Qualities not picked stay with
the functional batches where their mined demands already land (response
time, for one).
