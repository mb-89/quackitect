<!-- ai:3 -->
How much structure and gating a project uses, chosen once at start.
- vibe: no gates. Build it, and record one line why.
- lean: light structure. One review gate per concern.
- systematic: full rigor. Every concern is gated, and killer checks always reach the adjudicator.
