Internal Siemens learning material on system architecting. Not publicly available - cited from the owner's course notes. The template takes the architecture-versus-design rule (system-wide impact or an important quality, after Bass), the seven-part architecture content spine, the context views, the partitioning forces, the block description fields, the governance rules, and the mandate that every design decision names the requirement it addresses.
