## detail

THE OWNER RULED ON wk-e318448216. Their words: this shouldn't throw an error, can you just not put them into additional properties.

That token silenced the refusal by declaring claimed_by and claimed_at as properties, in the schema and in both process files. It works, and it says the wrong thing. A declared property is part of what a token author writes: it reaches completion, the field descriptions and the editor as though somebody were meant to fill it in.

Nobody fills these in. se claim writes them, the way the engine writes began and ended. The schema already carries a comment saying the holder is not a field here because it is the engine's, and this is the same argument for the two that are.

So the three declarations come out and the fields are exempt from the additionalProperties refusal instead, named once beside the check that refuses. The schema then says what an author writes, and the engine's own bookkeeping is not judged as token content.
