## detail

A new branch in a switch inherits the state change and none of the refusals, which live inside the sibling branch. On wk-6428f18b01 the third verdict accept_with_findings sets ImpOpen like the accept case in judgeSpec in src/engine/pull.go, and the accept case carries somethingWasRewatched before it. For a new member of a dispatched set, the criterion checks that it is refused for every reason its siblings are refused. Drive each refusal as its own case. Where two or more branches must share a guard, lift it in front of the switch rather than copy it. Related: wk-6428f18b01.
