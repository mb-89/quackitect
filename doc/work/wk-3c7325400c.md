## detail

Backlogged tokens are minted and nothing is obliged to consume them. pull.go says backlogged work holds nobody, and the retro drains the log and the scratchpad but not the backlog. Three parts close it. The retro triages the backlog: every learned token is promoted into guidance or a check, merged into a sibling, or aborted with a reason. The pull or the retro reports the backlog count and the age of the oldest entry. A mint that finds a sibling extends it instead of adding a twin. Prior art: https://github.com/gastownhall/beads
