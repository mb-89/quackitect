## detail

Putting a tracked token down frees the hold and leaves the claim standing under the name that put it down. The queue then hands that token to somebody else, and every verb refuses the new holder.

Measured here in September 2026. main worked wk-c6e5db7c51 and put it back, the record saying it did so because main is on another token now. Four minutes later se pull handed that token to worker-chagall and wrote that name into its holder.

The claim was still main's. se claim with take answered that it is claimed by this box under main, and se run on it answered that the token travels and this box holds no claim. So it sat in a hand that could neither work it nor claim it, and the only move left was to put it down again.

This is not the case wk-dab7999f44 names. There the box holds no claim at all. Here it holds one under another actor, so a claim exists and the guards still refuse, which the claim-and-take call cannot mend.

Two shapes to choose between. A put-down releases the claim it holds, which is what a worker leaving a token means. Or the queue passes over a token claimed by an actor other than the one pulling, the way wk-dab7999f44 asks it to pass over an unclaimed one.


## proposed action

My best attempt: the queue passes over a token claimed by an actor other than the one pulling, beside the check wk-dab7999f44 asks for. Releasing a claim on every put-down would also drop one a worker meant to keep across a short break, so that half is the riskier of the two.

## done when

- a token put down by one actor is not handed to another while that actor's claim stands, decided by a test that puts one down and pulls as a second actor
- a token whose claim is released is handed out again, decided by the same test after the claim is released
- the token says which of the two shapes was chosen and why
