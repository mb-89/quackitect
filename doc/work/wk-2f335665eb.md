## detail

The hold goes stale faster than the review method's own measurement takes, so careful reviews get their token reclaimed mid-review. StillPulling in src/engine/arrival.go counts PullsBeforeHoldIsStale, default 10 in src/engine/config.go, in pulls by anybody. A reviewer running util/checks/battery.sh or go test over src/engine is not pulling, falls behind, and loses the token. Once reclaimed, the write gate refuses every Bash call, so the reviewer cannot finish an injection. Three shapes to weigh: count the holder's engine calls instead of pulls, refresh the hold on any holder call, or use a wall-clock window. Check, red today: hold a token, make ten pulls by other actors while the holder makes non-pull engine calls, and require the hold to survive.
