## detail

CLASS: HALF A MECHANISM SHIPS, AND THE HALF LEFT OUT IS THE ONE THAT AUDITS THE
OTHER.

A detail names two parts and says in as many words that neither is enough alone.
The part that PRODUCES gets built, checked and evidenced. The part that CHECKS
the first does not, and nothing downstream is visibly missing, because the
producing half fills the field, writes the record and turns the gate green. The
absence has no symptom until the producing half is wrong, which is the exact
case the second half existed for.

WHY IT IS THE AUDITING HALF EVERY TIME. It is the half with no output of its
own. You cannot demonstrate it in the evidence by showing a thing it made, the
tests for it are about somebody else's mistake, and it always looks like it can
follow later. So it is deferred, and being deferred it is not written down as
deferred, because nothing was decided -- it simply did not come up.

MEASURED ON wk-7f0b46d99f, "criteria are seen red", round 2, whose evidence is
otherwise the most reproducible I have read in this queue: six watched reds each
citing a line that is exactly where the assertion is, and three counts that all
recount. Its detail says "TWO PLACES IT IS OBSERVED, and they are different
observations. The worker sees it before the work, with the fix absent. The
reviewer sees it again, by putting the defect back or by building the tree
without the fix, and the verdict says which it did. Neither one alone is enough:
the worker's proves the check can fail, and the reviewer's proves the check is
still the one that guards the behaviour after the work landed." The worker's
half is built: two fields, a refusal at the save, a gate at the submission and a
gate at the draft. The reviewer's half is built nowhere. Payload carries no field
for it, the accept path takes no evidence at all, nothing is written to the note,
no refusal asks for it, and no section of the evidence mentions it.

AND THE COST ARRIVED THE SAME DAY. Commit 694def52 loosened the spec gate so
that a criterion which passes is agreed if it carries **red without** and **red
said**, on the sound ground that a recorded observation answers the same question
as the engine seeing it red. That trade moves the entire weight of the gate onto
the recorded string, and the reviewer's second look is what would hold it. In one
sitting of reviewing I found one token whose observation cited drain_test.go:201
where the assertion is at :241, and another whose row cited nudge_test.go:35 for
a property guarded at :158 by a different test. Both were caught because a person
happened to re-run them. Nothing asked either reviewer to.

WHAT TO DO INSTEAD. WHEN A DETAIL NAMES TWO HALVES, PUT BOTH IN THE EVIDENCE,
even if the second one's answer is nothing yet. The reviewing method already
allows that answer -- named artefacts, or nothing yet naming what still owes it
-- and the answer that fails is silence, because silence cannot be told from
having judged it out of scope.

AND ASK WHICH HALF HAS NO OUTPUT. Read the detail for the part whose job is to
notice that another part is wrong. That is the one that will be missing, and it
is missing quietly, so look for it by name rather than by symptom.

## lesson 1 · round 1 · by reviewer9

**the class:** A CRITERION THAT NAMES A PROPERTY AND NO SUBJECT, ON A TOKEN WHOSE DELIVERABLE GOES INTO MORE THAN ONE PLACE. The first criterion names file A and the second names file B, and every criterion after those two opens with the word it. The pronoun has two referents, so whoever writes the check picks one, and the one picked is the copy that is easier to read. The list is green while the second copy is a heading and one sentence. It reads as complete on the page, because the sentences cover every property and two lines on the list carry a path. No line says which copy the properties are asserted of, and a reader supplies the missing subject from the criterion above. The place with no check of its own is the place whose content nobody decided.

**instead:** Give every criterion a subject, by path, even where the criterion above it already named one. A pronoun is not a subject, because the check is written from the sentence and the sentence is what the worker reads. Where the deliverable goes into several places, the content criterion walks them and fails naming the one that is short. Where the sentence names a section as well as a file, say that the check reads that section, because a whole-file search is green with the text under any heading.
