## detail

A claim with a good reason is argued with twice before it is granted, and the owner does not want it.

challenge.go sets claimsBeforeAStopIsGranted to three. When an agent holds open work, the engine pushes back twice and grants the third claim. So an honest stop costs three turns.

THE OWNER'S WORDS: I do not want you to have to stop two times. If you claim, you can stop next. If the reason is valid, you stop.

THIS REVERSES A MEASUREMENT, named here rather than left for a reader to find. The header on challenge.go carries the owner's earlier words: if he has a reason three times, then it stops. It was built because naming a reason cost nothing, and any of the five words did. The owner has weighed that against what it costs in turns and decided the other way.

What the argument protected is not lost. A stop still needs a claim, and the claim still names one of five sanctioned reasons. A false blocked is still refused at the claim itself. What goes is only the count.

## proposed action

Delete the argument. A standing claim with a sanctioned reason is granted at once.

## approach

Delete the argument. A standing claim naming a sanctioned reason is granted at once, whatever is in hand.

TheChallenge and claimsBeforeAStopIsGranted go, with their file, because nothing else uses them. decideStop stops counting refused stops. The guidance loses the actionable and the paragraph describing the argument. The two notices lose the sentences explaining a rule that no longer exists.

What stays is the claim itself, the five sanctioned reasons, the blocked check at the claim, and asked being granted whatever is held.
