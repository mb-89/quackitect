## detail

Folding a table of many groups is one press per group, and there is no way to open or shut all of them. The owner asked for an expand all button and a collapse all button in the generic editors, drawn as icons to save space. Expand all opens every group and every folded row in the pane, and collapse all shuts every group and every row with children. They act on the pane they sit in, and the icons come from util/icons.json. They go in the .bs-bar at src/extension/editor.ts:151. Check in the driver: press collapse all and assert every group is shut and every parent row folded. Press expand all and assert none is.
