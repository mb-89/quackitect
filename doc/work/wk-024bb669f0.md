## evidence: the wipe is real, the recovery is not

Both targets do lack their step 1 and step 2 tables, and .se/work is untracked: git cat-file -e ea12ce0b:.se/work/wk-641e205a7a.md answers that the path exists on disk but not in that commit. So the git route that restored wk-526ac833fb and wk-c93aac62be, both under doc/work, cannot work here. The only other store is .se/undo, and it keeps a file's pre-write content under was and only a sha256 of the result under applied. The text an author applied is never stored.
