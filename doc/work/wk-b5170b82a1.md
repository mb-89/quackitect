## detail

The retro collect copies the transcripts and drains the log into one folder as separate files with separate clocks. Make the collect verb also write a merged timeline file into the retro folder. Log records and transcript turns are interleaved by timestamp, each line naming its source and actor. The sources stay in the folder untouched. A timestamp the transcript lacks is interpolated between its neighbours and marked as interpolated. Rides on the collect half of se retro. doc/guidance/retro.md carries the method sentence for it.
