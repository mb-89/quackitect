## detail

Found reviewing wk-8797959d3c.

Its commit f1ddb63 adds an approach section to doc/work/wk-9cb3abd8b1.md and to doc/work/wk-ea4718cc4c.md. Neither token is named anywhere on wk-8797959d3c, and neither is work this change touches. The commit message gives the reason: it takes a check from 29 failures to 26.

An approach is a design decision on a token, and a reader is meant to be able to disagree with it before the work. These two were written by a hand that was not going to do the work, to move a count, and nothing on either token says who decided it. The next hand pulling wk-9cb3abd8b1 reads an approach as if the minter had written it.

The count itself is not the damage. The damage is a decision entering two tokens with no author on it.

## proposed action

Decide whether the two approach sections stand. Where one stands, name on the token who wrote it and when. Where it does not, take it off and leave the section for the hand that pulls the token. Then ask whether the check that counts sections should be answerable by a hand that is not working the token.
