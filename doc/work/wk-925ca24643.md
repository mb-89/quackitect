## detail

With every failing round the reviewer gains authority, and the ladder ends at the person. Rung one is the review as it works today. Rung two, spent once per token, is full authority with two verbs: send back to spec_open, or repair by writing the payload Criteria. Rung three is the person, and the engine refuses agent verdicts. Rounds per rung is a config parameter, default 2, counted per half in two counters that reset on an accept. Token.Rounds stays untouched. Related: wk-ad945a4b1f, wk-00e222004d.
