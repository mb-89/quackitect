## detail

The pull and the stop guard read the finishing hold differently, so an agent has no legal move.

MEASURED, September 2026, on this box. se_pull answered "Nothing the engine hands out. 6 piece(s) wait for a person to decide what happens to them. Say so, and stop." Its last line reads "no new work goes out while a person has the work on finishing." So the queue hands out nothing.

se_stop with because blocked answered "blocked is not true: the queue would hand you wk-1108f223d2 engine outlives its rebuild, one of 176 standing. Pull it, or claim what is true."

Both cannot be right. The stop guard counts standing tokens and the pull counts what it would hand over, and the finishing hold sits between them. The agent is told to stop by one door and told the stop is a lie by the other. Pulling again answers the same wait, so the loop does not end.

## proposed action

Have the stop guard ask the pull what it would hand over rather than counting standing tokens itself. Where the pull would hand out nothing, blocked is true and the guard grants it. One reader, one answer, so the two doors cannot disagree again.
