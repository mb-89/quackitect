## detail

The engine refuses a criterion that is green before the work is done, and nothing asks whether a criterion can ever go green. Add a validator on the transition into spec_in_review that refuses a criterion carrying every, all, any or no with no named enumerator, quoting the criterion. The enumerator must be something other than the check itself, not a pattern, a hand list or a walk that recognises shapes. Closed sets the engine holds, such as every declared flag or every projection in projections.json, name their enumerator and pass. Add a test that agrees a criterion naming its enumerator and refuses the same sentence with the enumerator removed. Related: wk-1412093cd8, wk-24be1c06ae, wk-f3559efa68.
