## detail

A bucket never draws as a group, so a drop has nowhere to land. util/views/work.base declares a group per status under groups:, and a declared group takes its rows before the grouping level runs. So if(bucket, bucket, status) never makes a group of its own. A declared group takes no drop, since dropping onto a status is a write the engine refuses, so the page has no drop target. Proposed shape, to agree before building: a row carrying a bucket goes to its bucket's group and not to the declared group its status matches. The check, red today: file two rows into a bucket through se work --file and draw the view. Require a group named after the bucket carrying both rows and sets.
