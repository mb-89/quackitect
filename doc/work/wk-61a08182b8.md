## detail

The sidebar's minter carries a process picker. panel.ts:475 reads the picked value and posts it as process on the run message.

Every mint that is not a note now has to say tracked or not tracked. So the picker leaves the panel with two ways out: ask the person a second question, or mint one kind.

The owner ruled it mints notes and nothing else. A note is what a person writes down before anything is decided, which is what the box is for.

The picker, its options list, its open and close handlers and the process it posts all become dead once that holds.

## proposed action

Take the process out of the message the panel posts, so the mint reads its default.

Delete the pick control, its markup, its styling and the handlers at panel.ts:481 and :499, unless another control uses the same class.

Say in the box's own words that it writes a note. Its placeholder at util/parameters.json:157 becomes title / the note body.
