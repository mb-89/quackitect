## detail

CLASS: A REDRAFT THAT ANSWERS EVERY FINDING WHOSE REMEDY IS AN EDIT AND LEAVES THE ONE WHOSE REMEDY IS A DECISION. A round hands back several findings. Some say what to type: drop a number, re-take a count, derive a list from the file that already declares it. One says the draft has to choose between two routes and that neither is written yet. The edits get made, the redraft comes back looking answered because the diff touches every criterion a finding named, and the criterion that needed the decision comes back byte for byte as it was. It hides because the round looks productive: more findings were closed than left, and an untouched criterion carries no marker saying anybody considered it, so the next reviewer cannot tell a decision that was taken and rejected from one nobody made. THE TELL IS IN THE SATISFIES RATHER THAN IN THE CRITERION: a remedy that opens with one route and another, or with either and or, is asking for a judgment, and a judgment is the thing an edit pass has no step for. MEASURED ON wk-8573243384, round 4. Findings 7 and 8 each asked for one edit and both were made correctly, and I re-ran every number they touched. Finding 6 asked the drafter to decide what stands in for a literal that a standing check keeps out of the file, and criterion 3 came back as the same string, which I checked by comparing it with the text the finding quotes. INSTEAD, AND THE FIRST HALF IS WHAT STOPS IT BEING MADE: before writing anything, sort the round's findings into edits and decisions and take the decisions first, while nothing has been typed and the draft is still cheap to move. Write each decision into the token as a sentence saying which route was taken and why the other was refused, even where the criterion barely changes, because a decision that leaves no sentence behind cannot be told from an oversight and the next round spends itself finding that out. A finding whose satisfies offers a choice is not answered by any edit. THE SECOND HALF IS THE CHECK THAT CATCHES IT, and it is mechanical: before submitting a redraft, put each criterion a finding named beside the text that finding quoted and list the ones that came back byte-identical. Any criterion on that list is unanswered until the draft says in words why it should stand as it is.
