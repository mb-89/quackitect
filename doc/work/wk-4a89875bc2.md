## detail

A guard counts what is owed over a filtered set and then accepts an answer keyed to the unfiltered one. In somethingWasRewatched in src/engine/pull.go, says[c.Says] is filled for every criterion before the branch that decides what is owed. A reviewer can satisfy a refusal raised about one criterion by keying the observation to another that has no command. Add the test where the answer names a member outside the filtered subset and require the refusal to stand. The fixture needs one member inside the filter and one outside.
