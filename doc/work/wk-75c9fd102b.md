## detail

The queue hands a token over and does not write the claim that makes it the taker's. take says it claims what it hands out, and its condition is exactly the lapsed case.

Measured 2026-09-06. The pull offered wk-1da1791ba0 three times running. After each, the token still carried claimed_by aeaf7bd9/worker-ravel-two at 06:48:59. That claim lapsed at 09:48:59 under claim_hours 3. The next write on it was refused for want of a claim.

An earlier reading of this said the queue offers work another box holds. That was wrong. WouldHandOut already refuses a claim held elsewhere, and offering lapsed work is correct. The clock, not the rule, made three hand-outs look like a double-claim.

## proposed action

Find out whether take writes the claim it says it writes, by driving take over a token whose claim has lapsed. Where it does, the loss is elsewhere and this token says where. Where it does not, the hand-out and the claim become one write that cannot half happen.

## done when

- take over a token with a lapsed claim leaves it claimed by this box, read back off the record by a test.
- The same token then answers nothing at the claim gate, and the same test asks NoClaimHere.
- A token another box holds unlapsed is still refused, and a test reads that box's claim standing.
