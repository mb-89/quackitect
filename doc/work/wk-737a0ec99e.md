## detail

NOT READY. This is a design under discussion and it is not work to pick up. It is written down so the thinking survives the conversation it came from. More discussion is expected before anything here is built, and the open questions are listed at the end.

THE PROBLEM, MEASURED. Of 156 tokens, all 156 carry a detail section and 20 carry a done-when section. There are more than 200 distinct evidence headings, every one a hand-written sentence used as a section name: 'evidence: what I cannot verify', 'evidence: the undefined the owner kept seeing', 'evidence: two changes, either of which would have been enough'. A token has no schema, so each agent invents the shape of its own submission and the reviewer has no fixed place to look. It reads prose and forms a judgment, and judgment varies. That is the churn in wk-12edb3522e seen from its cause rather than its cost.

TWO PROCESSES, AND THE NAMING SETTLES ITSELF. A work token follows a PROCESS. A walker walks a MACHINE. Level 2's document is already titled the state machine and Level 1 already forbids that word in a Level 1 implementation, so no vocabulary is invented.

THE MODEL. SIPOC plus enablers is the inspiration and it may not survive contact. Each step names its suppliers, inputs, outputs, customers and enablers.

  Inputs and outputs are the payload: what the step consumes and what it produces.
  Suppliers and customers type the EDGE. The supplier of step N+1 is the customer of step N, and that is what says who may do the next step. A step whose customer is a reviewer cannot join to a step whose supplier is not one.
  The enabler is what makes the step possible without being consumed, which is where a check lives. See wk-f1f0ea41d7.

They look like padding in a single-actor process, which is why a sandwich diagram repeats one name in nearly every cell. Level 1 exists for drafter to reviewer to actor to reviewer, and there the edge type is the whole content of the handoff.

THREE CHECKS FALL OUT, RUN ONCE PER PROCESS DEFINITION rather than once per token, before a single token is minted with it:

  every output has a customer, so no product has nobody to receive it
  every input has a supplier, so no material arrives from nowhere
  at every join the customer of N and the supplier of N+1 are the same role, or the process is malformed

A PROCESS IS A GRAPH OF STEPS, with loops and with and-or branches. Minting a token chooses which process it follows. An ephemeral fix is a one-step process: fix the thing, done. The spec-then-implement flow that exists today is one process among several rather than the only shape work can take. New process definitions are new shapes a work token can have.

THE CLASS AND THE INSTANCE. The process is the class and the checklist is the instance. Bake the instance into the token, which is this token's chapters and its answers. Reference the class, with a digest, so a token knows which version of the process it was minted under. The precedent is already in se work: --guidance inline for small, --guidance-ref for shared, chosen by the minter for the same reasons.

The graph and the table are RENDERED from the definition and the answers rather than stored. Nobody has to write a markdown table if it is generated, which disposes of the only objection the owner had to using one.

Each checklist entry is a chapter. Each chapter has two subchapters, submission and reviewer.

THAT IS WHY IT CLOSES SILENCE. An unanswered finding is an empty chapter. The engine counts chapters against entries the way UnmetCriteria already counts criteria, so wk-be358bd3f3 is answered structurally with no new refusal concept.

STATUS IS NOT HARDCODED ANYWHERE. The statuses a token may carry are the union of the step names across every defined process, the way Obsidian lets the keys that exist inform what may be entered. The evidence that this is the right direction is already in the tree: token.go carries eleven status constants and a wasCalled map beside them, mapping spec to spec_open, open to imp_open, closed to imp_done, spec_ready to spec_submitted and three more. That map exists because the names were renamed in Go and the files on disk were not, and 30 tokens still carry the old spellings. It is the class this project already names, a hand-written list standing in for a set the language can enumerate.

WHAT THE UNION CANNOT CARRY, AND IT MUST BE DECIDED. Eight engine files ask a status question and the sharpest is Ended, which is written as ImpDone or Aborted. Whether a token has ended decides what a retro may take, what the drain moves, what pull hands out, what abort may act on and what the views drop. So each step declares a ROLE alongside its inputs and outputs, and the engine reasons about roles. Ended becomes 'this step's role is an ending'. The views filter on role rather than on a status name, so a process with new step names appears in here and in yours without editing eight filters.

The step names are the vocabulary and they grow from the data. The roles are the alphabet and they stay in the engine. The rule against hand-written lists bites on the set that grows, not on the alphabet.

THE CONSEQUENCE NOBODY CLAIMED. pull.go is 1121 lines answering what is open, whether it is yours, and whether you may take it. With customers typing the edges, a pull becomes: which step's customer is my role, on a token that has reached that step. The process says who is next and the engine reads it. Most of the queue stops being code.

AND IT IS THE SEAM TO LEVEL 2. A spike's step can name a machine state as its customer. Level 1 never learns what a machine is, because a customer is an opaque identifier the way a scope already is. That is how a token travels into the layer above without the layer below importing its words.

WHAT IS NOT DECIDED

  Whether all five columns survive, or only inputs, outputs and enablers plus the two edge roles.
  Which roles the engine knows. The alphabet has to be small and it has to be enough.
  Where a process definition is stored, and in what. util/ already holds views, projections and parameters.
  Whether the eleven statuses that exist become the first process definition, and what happens to the 156 tokens minted under them.
  Whether the graph is stored or rendered, and in what notation.
  The migration. This changes the core type: token.go, pull.go, lane.go, retro.go, abort.go, nudge.go, spec.go, store.go and the eight filters in util/views/work.base all ask a status question today.
