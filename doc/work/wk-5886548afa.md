## detail

A guard written as a deny list over forms nobody can enumerate widens by one form per round and never closes. Before writing a guard, write both lists, what may be here and what may not. If the may-not list is open and the may list is closed, assert the closed one and fail on anything not on it. After watching a guard go red, write the same defect two more ways, a variable, a concatenation, another quoting, and require it red on each. A reviewer reads the token history for a pattern widened once per round. Related: wk-1412093cd8.
