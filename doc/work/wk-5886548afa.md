## detail

CLASS: A GUARD WRITTEN AS A DENY LIST OVER A SET NOBODY CAN ENUMERATE, WHERE THE PERMITTED SET IS SMALL AND FINITE. A rule says a place may not contain a kind of thing, and the check goes looking for that thing. The looking is a pattern, the pattern matches the forms the author has seen, and the forms are unbounded because a language can make the same value a dozen ways. Every round then buys exactly one more form, which is why this class is invisible from inside a round: each fix is correct, each is watched red, each closes the case the finding named, and the reviewer who checks the fix finds it sound. THE TELL IS THE SHAPE OF THE HISTORY RATHER THAN OF THE CODE. If the last three rounds all widened the same pattern by whatever the last finding happened to write, the check is enumerating the forbidden. Ask what the place is ALLOWED to contain, and if that answer is a short list while the forbidden one is open, the test is the wrong way round. MEASURED ON wk-1412093cd8 over four rounds: seven call sites, then one spawn shape, then the converse, then two quote characters, and after all four a flag written as a local variable or as two strings added together still passes, on a check whose whole subject is a flag written at a call site. INSTEAD, AND THE FIRST HALF IS WHAT STOPS IT BEING MADE: before writing a guard, write both lists, what may be here and what may not. If the may-not list is open and the may list is closed, invert the test and assert the closed one, failing on anything not on it. That is one decision at the start and it removes the whole family, where a deny list removes one member per round. It also changes what a reviewer can check, because a short allow list is something two people can read and agree about, and a pattern is not. THE SECOND HALF IS THE CHECK THAT CATCHES IT, and it is a habit rather than a test: when you have watched a guard go red on the defect, write the SAME defect two more ways before trusting it, choosing the ways from the language rather than from the tree, a variable, a concatenation, another quoting, an alias. If one of them passes, the guard is matching a form and not a fact, and the remedy is not another pattern. AND WHEN YOU ARE THE REVIEWER, read the token's own history for the shape: a pattern widened once per round is the signal, and it is visible from the findings without opening the code.
