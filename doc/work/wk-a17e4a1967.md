## detail

Deleting the review flow took about twenty rounds of go vet, one undefined symbol each. Measured on five planted errors: go vet reports 1 and takes about 500 ms. go build -gcflags=-e reports all 5 and takes about 1020 ms. So vet is twice as fast per call and still the wrong tool. The cost that matters is the round trip and not the call. Twenty rounds of vet is twenty turns and about 10 s of tool time, where two rounds of build is two turns and 2 s. The flag exists because Go's type checker stops after a batch by default.

## done when

- Build and lint verbs pass -gcflags=-e, and five planted errors come back in one run.
- A check fails when the build command lacks the flag.
