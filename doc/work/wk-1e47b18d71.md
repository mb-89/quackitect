## detail

A destructive sweep guards what it keeps with a typed list of fields, and the list goes short when the record grows a field. In citedInOpenWork the keep reads five fields of a token and not the evidence, the re-watch, a lesson or a finding clause. When a list is an exception to something destructive, do not type a list, walk the record type by reflection. Where a typed list must stay, write beside it what makes it complete. Add a test that enumerates every field of the record that can hold prose and asserts the keep sees a citation in each. Related: wk-02e17b9eb4, wk-339068932c.
