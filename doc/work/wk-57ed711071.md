## detail

When a finding lands on one member of a set the note carries many of, re-run its test over every member before answering. Put the count in the submission. For recorded observations, apply each red without in a copy of the tree and require the run to print the red said. Where two criteria carry the same without word for word, apply it and see which of the two goes red rather than declaring it shared. Case in point: wk-339068932c round 8. Criteria 11 and 17 both record a retro.go removal that is in no file, and both stay green with it applied. Three of sixteen observations were wrong and only the one the finding named was fixed.
