## approach

A rationale is a kind like any other: src/schemas/rationale.schema.yaml, notes in doc/rationale/, read by the lint the way guidance is. The shape to disagree with:

- frontmatter carries kind, title, and explains, a list of the files or notes that hold the instruction this argues for.
- the body runs decided, why, costs, revisit when. Four sections, none optional.
- the walk goes both ways. The rationale names what it explains, and the thing explained names the rationale back by link and does not repeat its sentences. TestARationaleIsNamedNotRepeated drives that through every note: the link is there, and no sentence of why appears verbatim in the file it explains.

Two cases rather than a sweep: src/mcp/lane.go, where a turn pays the cost, and src/schemas/work-token.schema.yaml, which carries the heaviest standing argument. The ALL-CAPS blocks left in schema.go, gate.go and store.go are ordinary work afterwards, one token each.
