## detail

The standard process declares approach a required section. Its open state reads "written with its approach, and waiting to be taken". The mint writes neither, so a standard token is open and failing the check a second after it is minted.

Measured at d1aa9e3, with node util/checks/open-tokens-carry-their-sections.mjs over the tree: 201 open tokens read, 31 failed. Every one is a standard token with no approach. wk-f353de1280 counted twenty-two when it was written, so the class grows while it is worked.

The mint already has the gate for the neighbouring rule. token.go refuses a mint where the process requires done when and the token carries no criterion. It asks nothing about approach.

The ask step is the other half. The standard process says its ask activity ends at open and writes the approach. The engine mints straight into open and hands step 1 out afterwards, so between the two the token is open and red.

Two doors close this. The mint refuses a standard token with no approach, the way it refuses one with no done-when. Or a token is not open until its ask is answered, which is what the state's own description says. The first is small, the second is the honest one, and the taker decides which and says why.

Until one is closed, every backlog this clears fills again.

## proposed action

Close one of the two doors at the mint, and say on the token which one and why the other was left.

## done when

- a standard token minted with no approach is not left open and failing, decided by: mint one, then run node util/checks/open-tokens-carry-their-sections.mjs . and read its last line
