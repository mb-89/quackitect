## detail

From the verdict on wk-40abb881a7, which moved src/engine/log.go to src/engine/internal/logbook/logbook.go.

The move took the log's fields out of reach of two tests that had been reaching into them from the flat package, and the answer was to export two methods for them:

- logbook.RotateAt has one caller in the tree, src/engine/log_test.go:18.
- logbook.NameSession has one caller in the tree, src/engine/owed_test.go:219.

Neither is called by any non-test file. So the package's exported surface is wider than the job it does, and a reader of logbook.go cannot tell which of its exports the engine actually uses from which exist so a test in another package can poke it. That is the cost the split was meant to remove: the point of a package under internal is that what it holds is its own.

The two tests are logbook's own tests. They belong in package logbook, next to the fields they set, and then the two methods go.

The rest of the move is sound and this does not undo it.

## proposed action

Move the one test in src/engine/log_test.go that calls RotateAt, and the part of src/engine/owed_test.go that calls NameSession, into src/engine/internal/logbook as package logbook tests, setting the fields directly. Then delete RotateAt and NameSession from logbook.go. Where a test cannot move because it needs the engine around it, say so on this token and leave the method with a comment naming its one caller.
