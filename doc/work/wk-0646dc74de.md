## detail

Measured on this box at 19:10. git status over doc/work showed 112 note files deleted from the working tree and not committed. 91 of them had a row in doc/work/archive.jsonl, so those deletions are the close doing its job and were committed. The other 21 had no archive row and no archive tag. Twenty of them still existed in git HEAD, every one status open with no disposition. So twenty pieces of open work had been taken off the disk with nothing saying who did it or why. Nothing refuses this and nothing reports it. A close deletes a note and writes an archive row, and those two are meant to move together. A deletion with no row is either a close whose archive write failed, which NotArchived is supposed to surface, or a hand or a sweep removing live work. Either way the queue silently shrinks. I restored all twenty from HEAD. Had anyone committed those deletions first, twenty open tokens would have left the backlog and nobody would have known.
