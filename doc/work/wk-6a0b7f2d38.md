## approach

The reader becomes a module of its own, which the engine and the viewer both require. It comes out of internal, because internal is the marker saying no other module may import it.

The syntax of the log is the one that survives. A person types it, and the Level 0 rulings are written against it. The callers of expr move over to it.

The extension stays a caller rather than an importer. It builds its arguments in engineargs.ts and reaches the reader through the engine, so there is still one implementation and one syntax.
