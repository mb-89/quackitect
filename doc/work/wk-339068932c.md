## detail

Everything loose in the scratchpad goes, and a retro keeps what an unfinished token cites by path, reading every word the record holds. The checks live in util/checks so the drain cannot take them. A retro refuses while another actor holds work, read from the tokens and StillPulling in src/engine/arrival.go, and names who.
