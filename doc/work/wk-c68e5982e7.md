## detail

A token carries no record of what changed under it, so a reviewer reads the files as they stand rather than the diff. Put two shas on the token, the one it started at and the one it ended at, written by the engine. Owner to decide when the first sha is taken and whether the engine commits at all or only records where the tree stood. Check: name a token, make a commit, submit the token, and require a range holding that commit and not the one before it. A token that spans no commit says so rather than naming an empty range.
