## detail

CLASS: AN EXTENT INHERITED FROM THE FINDING THAT REPORTED IT, RATHER THAN
MEASURED AGAINST THE FILE.

A reviewer's finding names the places it happened to look. A drafter reads the
finding, writes those places into the criteria, and the criteria then say
"all of them" about a set the reviewer drew and nobody re-swept. The sample
becomes the extent, and it becomes it silently, because the draft now carries
the word every and a list, which reads exactly like a measurement.

THE TELL. The list stops at a struct boundary, or a package boundary, or the
boundary of whatever the reviewer had open. Ask what draws the boundary. If the
answer is "the parser", the list should have been every field that parser
touches; if the answer is "the finding", it is not an extent, it is a quotation.

AND THE REMEDY INHERITS IT TOO. A rule chosen for the shape of the instance --
refuse a newline, because this field is one line -- is applied to the whole set
the draft named, and the set turns out to hold fields of a different shape for
which the rule is wrong. A remedy is only as general as the extent it was
chosen against.

FOUND ON wk-24be1c06ae, whose own subject is a value that does not fit its line.
Its draft names four fields -- Says, Runs, Without, Red -- and says "All four
one-line fields ... not only the two the lesson was found on", which reads as a
sweep. It is the reviewer's sample. The parser those four live under,
src/engine/store.go, also reads a finding's Clause out of a heading split on the
middle dot, and a finding's Wrong and Satisfies line by line, truncating each at
the first newline. Measured on the record as it stands: of fourteen findings on
four tokens, the six written as one long paragraph survive at 900 to 2800
characters, and every one written in paragraphs is cut to its first -- 85
characters of a 1900-character finding on wk-7f0b46d99f, 196 of 3900 on
wk-c22f29af7b. Reviewers have been losing the body of their findings all
session, and the one-paragraph house style is the workaround nobody was told
they were using.

AND THE INHERITED REMEDY IS WRONG THERE. Refusing a newline is right for a field
that is one line by design. Wrong and satisfies are paragraphs by design, and
the writer already renders them as a block; only the reader is line-based. A
rule that refused a newline in them would make every review written this session
illegal.

WHAT TO DO INSTEAD. When a draft turns a finding into criteria, re-sweep the
file the defect lives in before writing the word every, and say in the draft
what was swept and what the sweep answered -- the function, the file, the count.
One pass over the parser, not over the finding. Then group what you found by
shape before choosing a remedy: fields that are one line by design, fields that
are a block by design, and fields that go into a heading with a delimiter in it
are three different problems and want three answers. A reviewer's finding is a
sample of the defect and never its extent, which the method already says to a
reviewer; the same sentence belongs to whoever drafts from it.

THE CHECK, RED TODAY: a test that walks every field store.go reads back and
requires a value written to it to come back byte-identical -- a newline, a
blank line, the lead itself, the delimiter, an empty string. Against the tree as
it stands that is red on Wrong, on Satisfies and on Clause as well as on the
four the draft names.
