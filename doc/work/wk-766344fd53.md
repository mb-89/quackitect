## detail

Found reviewing wk-120d7c9685.

aWrittenDay in src/engine/identity.go matches a number beside a month name, and several month names are ordinary English words. The first branch of the pattern ends at the month with no boundary and no year, so a sentence carrying no date at all is refused.

Run against the pattern, "The queue held 12 may be more than the box can run." matches "12 may" and is refused as a datetime. So does "It found 3 march past the gate", on "3 march". The refusal is a hard deny with no override, and its advice, to write a month and a year, answers a sentence that never carried a date.

A guard that refuses ordinary prose is one somebody turns off.

## proposed action

Hold the day-beside-a-month branch to a shape a date has and a sentence does not: a year beside it, or an ordinal, or a boundary the following word cannot be a verb across. The month-first branch already carries an optional year and is the shape to follow.
