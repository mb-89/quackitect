## detail

The evidence sections of a work token are capped at 200 words by src/schemas/work-token.schema.yaml, headerPrefix "evidence: ", maxWords 200, and overWords counts table pipes as words. wk-2b8f45c1e7 added two rows to the ask of both tracked processes, at src/processes/standard.process.yaml lines 48-51 and src/processes/trivial.process.yaml lines 44-47. The two rows cost 40 words unanswered and 61 as wk-2b8f45c1e7 itself answered them. Its own ask section now stands at 194 words of the 200 allowed.

What is gained by doing it is that a worker can answer every ask row in a sentence without the save being refused, which is the only way the two new rows get real answers rather than three words each.

What breaks if it is never done is that the ask table is now large enough to refuse its own honest answers. Measured over doc/work: 71 of 290 ask sections stand within 55 words of the cap. The same cap already refused every release of wk-963dbf6898, recorded at src/engine/investigate.go line 214. The class went invisible when the fixture at src/engine/standard_test.go line 105 stopped writing each criterion into its evidence cell, so no engine test now produces a section near the bound.
