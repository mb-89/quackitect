## detail

Emergency mode is a second door to what god mode already does. There is one way to take the refusals off, and it is the rung.

What emergency buys today is one branch. At src/engine/hook.go:903 an armed emergency lets a projection be written directly, which is otherwise the one refusal with no override. God takes every refusal off, that one included.

What it costs is a second concept for the same need, with its own file, its own flags, its own arming record and its own expiry. It is the only thing in the tree that ends on a timer, and a timer is not how a control here works: a control lasts as long as the session.

It also shows up in the answers. --config and the record verb both print an emergency block beside every parameter, so a reader meets a mode that is not the one the tree actually uses.

## proposed action

Take emergency mode out, and let the rung be the one way to lift a refusal.

## approach

The branch at hook.go:903 goes, and the refusal below it stands at every rung but god. NoGuardsAtAll already answers god, and that is what the projection refusal reads.

Then the type and its four functions go from config.go, with the file they wrote. The flags go from main.go, and the emergency block goes from the answers in main.go and recordverbs.go.

It waits on the session-scoped controls. Until the rung resets when the session ends, taking the timer away would leave nothing that ends.
