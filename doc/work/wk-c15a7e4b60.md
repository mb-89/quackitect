## detail

Cross-cutting deferred import, export, packaging, versioning and capability negotiation as one group, on the ground that none of them constrain what is built first. That has held.

They are grouped because they are one question underneath: what does this system look like to something that is not this system.

## what we gain

A door for work to arrive from somewhere else and to leave for somewhere else. Until then every consumer has to be given the whole tree and the method that reads it.

## what breaks without it

Everything stays inside one tree and one method. A second consumer has no way in that is not a clone, and a version of the method cannot be named or asked for.

## appetite

Large. This is a module rather than a change, and it is the kind that grows if it is started before it is needed.

## what we do not know

Which of the five is actually first. Packaging and versioning may be one thing. Capability negotiation only means something once there are two versions that differ.
