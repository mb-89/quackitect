## detail

When an old log line is deleted was deferred at Level 0, and nothing in the design has depended on the answer since. The record grows for as long as the tree lives.

The retro is what reads the log and turns it into something smaller. So the retro is what empties it, and a retention rule written before the retro drains anything would be a number nobody measured.

## what we gain

A record that stays the size somebody chose, rather than the size time made it. And a stated rule, so a reader knows what the log still holds and what it has stopped holding.

## what breaks without it

The log grows without bound. The day it matters is the day a box runs out of room. By then the lines worth keeping are mixed with the ones nobody read.

## appetite

Small. A rule and a sweep, and the sweep runs where the retro already runs.

## what we do not know

Whether retention is a count of lines, an age, or whatever the retro leaves behind after it has drained what it wanted. The third is the one that needs no number.
