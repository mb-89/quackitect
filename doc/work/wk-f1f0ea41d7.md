## detail

A finding that can be a check is written as one, by the reviewer, in the round that found it. The reviewer writes it ad hoc in .se/scratchpad/, not in util/checks/, which only a retro fills. The engine runs it once over the whole tree, and where it is red is the measured extent. The check goes onto the token under review as a criterion carrying the whole red set. The agent that gets it back fixes all of it. Small enough to do in the moment, the reviewer does it. At the retro each ad hoc check is promoted into util/checks/ as a real test or dropped with the scratchpad. Related: wk-b42c0e9a53.
