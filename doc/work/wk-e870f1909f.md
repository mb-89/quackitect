## detail

The submission gate has two states for a checklist line, ticked and not. It has no state for "answered, and the answer is that it is not met". src/engine/pull.go refuses on !done before it ever reads the evidence cell, and refuses separately when a line is ticked and says nothing. So a worker who answers a line honestly with a sentence saying why the criterion does not hold cannot close the token. se_pull refuses, naming that line, and goes on refusing one line per pull until every box is ticked.

This contradicts the guidance those tokens carry. doc/guidance/work-token.md rule 15 says "Answer each line rather than ticking it". Its chapter 15 says a line ticked without being read is the institutional knowledge thrown away and the tick kept. The gate forces that outcome: the only way out is to tick a line the worker has just written is false.

Smallest case, observed on wk-437137c7a1. Two do-checklist lines were answered with sentences saying why they were not met. se_pull refused twice, once per line, clause "the checklist", wrong "step 2, do: this is not ticked: ...". Both were then ticked with the qualification left in the sentence and the same submission was accepted. Only the boxes changed.

The cost is that a reader of any closed token cannot tell a line that was met from a line the gate forced.

## proposed action

Refuse an UNANSWERED line rather than an unticked one. Where the evidence cell is non-empty, the line counts as answered whether or not it is ticked, and the token may close. An unticked line with a non-empty answer is carried to the reviewer to rule on rather than blocking the worker. Where the evidence cell is empty, refuse as today, and word the refusal "this is not answered" rather than "this is not ticked". Both existing protections survive: a line neither ticked nor answered is still refused, and a ticked line saying nothing is still refused.

## done when

- A token whose do-checklist carries one unticked line with a non-empty evidence sentence closes through se_pull with a disposition. A test in src/engine drives the gate over such a note and asserts no rejection, seen red first against today's refusal text.
- A token whose do-checklist carries one unticked line with an EMPTY evidence cell is still refused, and the refusal names that line. The same test asserts the rejection, so the guard is not merely removed.
- The refusal a person reads says the line is not answered rather than not ticked: the test matches on that wording.
