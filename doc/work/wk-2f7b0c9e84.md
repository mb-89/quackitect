## detail

At every moment a person is shown the next most useful thing to do, and it is marked blue. Somebody who does not know the system follows the blue line and makes progress. With the engine down the start button is blue. With it up, a pull button for the person is blue, and that button does not exist yet.

For an agent the blue line is the pull. The pull already decides what comes next and already carries a notice saying why. So the mechanism is built for one audience and not for the other.

The two lines differ in one place. With nothing left to do, the person is pointed at ideation and the agent is told to stop. Under ideation mode the agent is pointed at ideation as well, and does the work otherwise reserved for a person.

That makes ideation a loop. Nothing to do, so ideate. Ideas reach the funnel, the funnel is drawn, the tokens are worked, and there is nothing to do again.

## what we gain

A person who does not know the system can still work it. They follow the colour rather than learning the order, and every step they take is one the engine already believes is next.

One answer serves both audiences. The engine says what is next and why, the pull returns it and the panel paints it. What an agent is handed and what a person is shown cannot then disagree, because neither is composed twice.

## what breaks without it

A newcomer meets a panel of controls with no order to them and picks wrong. The knowledge of what comes next stays inside the pull, where only an agent ever benefits from it. And an agent with nothing left stops, which is right today and leaves a box idle that could be thinking.

## appetite

Large. The highlight and the person's pull button are small on their own. The state machine the line walks is Level 3 work, and Level 3 does not exist.

## what we do not know

Whether needs_human means one thing. Some tokens carrying it wait for judgement an agent could supply. Others wait for the owner because the decision was reserved. Ideation mode cannot tell those apart, and one field carries both today.

Whether an agent should draw from the funnel. The draw is the owner's step by design, and the rule against a graveyard rests on it. An agent that both fills the funnel and drains it turns it into a queue with more steps.

Where the loop stops. Hold and finishing up are stops, and ideation mode is an anti-stop with no terminus of its own. It ends when the owner holds, finishes up, or turns the mode off, and nothing else ends it.

Which colour it is. The panel already draws its buttons blue, so the hint and the ordinary state would look the same. The name can stand while the colour moves.

How the line is programmed. The owner has said that part is for later.

## prior art

The pull already answers what comes next and carries a notice saying why, so half of this is built and spent on agents alone.

The rationale one tree holds every parameter already rules that the panel is a view over what the engine owns. A view holding a value of its own is two copies with no rule for which wins. That is the shape this needs, applied to the answer rather than to a value.
