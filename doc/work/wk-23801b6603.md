## detail

SPIKE. Level 0 ruled that the standing layer is small and its size is budgeted, and left the number open: the budget must be stated and measured, and it is not invented here.

WHERE IT STANDS. AGENTS.md is 20073 bytes today. That is what every agent carries on every turn, and it is the projection of doc/guidance/voice.md and doc/guidance/behaviour.md, which are 7599 and 12351 bytes. The corpus behind it is larger: reviewing.md and specifying.md are about ten thousand bytes each and ride the token rather than the turn.

The layer has been growing by accretion. Each lesson that reaches guidance adds a section, and nothing takes one out. That is exactly the failure the ruling was written against: the standing prefix tax, one reasonable addition at a time.

WHAT TO MEASURE. What the standing layer costs per turn, in the unit the host charges. What it buys, which means whether a rule in it changes behaviour at all. The second is the hard half and it is the one that decides the number, because a rule that is paid on every turn and changes nothing is the whole of what a budget is for.

WHERE THE ANSWER GOES. To the person, for discussion. A budget is a decision and not a measurement, so this ends in a conversation and not in a commit. Bring the measurement and a proposal, not a number on its own.
