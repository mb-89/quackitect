## detail

From the verdict on wk-887e9a126a, the engine archives.

se find --words means FTS5 over the index, and the flag's own help says so: terms in FTS5 syntax, a AND b, a NOT b. se find --archive --words means something else. src/engine/archive.go FindArchived does want = regexp.QuoteMeta(p.Words), so the words are matched as one literal string, operators and all.

Measured on this tree. se find --words 'undo AND pops' answers two hits. se find --archive --words 'undo AND pops' answers zero, because no archived line carries the characters "undo AND pops". The archived token wk-00b78575d6 is titled "undo pops anothers apply" and is right there under refs/tags/archive/wk-00b78575d6.

The damage is that the archive door is the whole reason the archive is not write-only, and it answers nothing for a query the tree answers. A searcher reads zero hits as "that work does not exist" rather than "that flag means something else here", and closed work is exactly the work nobody remembers well enough to doubt the answer.
