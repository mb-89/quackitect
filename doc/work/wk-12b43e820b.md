## detail

Only what belongs in the record goes to doc/work. Everything else goes to the
scratchpad.

THE OWNER SAID THIS BEFORE AND I DID NOT DO IT. Every token I have minted is
traced, so doc/work holds thirty files, most of them about a defect in a check
nobody will care about next week. That is a mess I made rather than a gap in
the design.

WHAT THE RULE ALREADY IS: a token is traced or ephemeral, the minter decides,
and .se/work is private and never travels while doc/work is the record and is
in version control. The mechanism exists. Nothing applies it.

WHAT TO SETTLE:
- what belongs in the record. A decision, a rule, something a reader in six
  months needs. Not every round of a check.
- who decides. The minter, with a default that is right more often than not.
- what happens to the thirty already written. Some of them belong in the
  record and most do not, and moving a closed token is a rewrite of history
  rather than a tidy.

THE DEFAULT IS THE WHOLE QUESTION. Traced by default gave this. Ephemeral by
default would lose things that mattered. It probably turns on who minted it: a
person's token is the record, and an agent's breakdown of its own work is not.
