## detail

The queue handed wk-13bfca8541 to main for a verdict, and main is the hand that wrote it. Reviewing rule 14 exists to stop exactly that: an evaluator recognises its own output and favours it. The guard compares the author's name, and that token's frontmatter carries no author field at all. Other tokens carry one, such as wk-dffbf05720 with author: main. So the comparison is against nothing and cannot refuse. A guard that reads a field nobody wrote is a guard that passes everything, and it passes silently, which is worse than absent.

## proposed action

Find where author is written and why it was not written here, then refuse a verdict when the field is missing rather than when it merely differs.

An absent author is unknown provenance, and unknown provenance is the case the rule is about. Treating it as nobody lets every such token through to whoever asks.
